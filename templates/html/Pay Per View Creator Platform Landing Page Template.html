How are views counted?

Unique, human views with minimum watch time and scroll depth. Bots and repeated rapid refreshes are filtered out.

Payouts are processed every Friday once your balance crosses the minimum threshold.

Can I set my own RPM?

Yes. You can use suggested brackets by category or set a custom RPM for each piece.
