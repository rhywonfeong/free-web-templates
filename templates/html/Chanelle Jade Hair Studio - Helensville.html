FAQ

Find answers to the questions our clients ask most.

What is your cancellation policy?

We understand cancellations/no shows happen. We have a 24-hour cancellation policy, this means you will be charged 80% of your booked in service if you do not turn up or cancel within 24 hours. By booking in you agree to these terms and conditions.

How long are appointments?

Most appointments run between 15 minutes for toners and 45 minutes for cuts or colours. We build in time to discuss what you want and make sure the work is done right.

Do you offer services for children?

Yes, we offer cuts for children aged 0-9 years and 10-15 years.
