Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to KYC to trade on Marqet?
No. Marqet is a fully decentralized protocol. You simply connect your Web3 wallet to start trading global assets instantly without permission.
How are stock prices determined on-chain?
We utilize a decentralized oracle network (DON) that aggregates price feeds from premium off-chain institutional sources and pushes them on-chain with sub-second latency.
What is the maximum leverage available?
Leverage varies by asset class to ensure protocol solvency. Crypto pairs offer up to 100x, while FX and Commodities offer up to 50x, and Stocks up to 20x.
Which network is Marqet built on?
Marqet is currently deployed on Layer 2 scaling solutions to minimize gas fees and maximize transaction speed, ensuring a trading experience comparable to CEXs.
