First time? Welcome.

Here’s how to have a cozy, confident first visit.

1) Start gentle

Tell your bartender how you want to feel. We’ll guide you to a lighter bowl and you can add from there.

2) Sip slow

Give it 10–15 minutes. Kava builds a mellow. You’ll know when you’ve hit your sweet spot.

3) Pair with snacks

We’ve got light bites to keep things grounded. Ask about dairy-free or gluten-free options.

4) Safety & interactions

Kava is non-alcoholic. If you take medications or have liver concerns, chat with your clinician. Our team can share lab results.
