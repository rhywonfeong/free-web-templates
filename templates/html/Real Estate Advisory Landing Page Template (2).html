Leadership

Corbin Rich

Founder & Principal

Corbin Rich founded Playa Advisors to bring institutional-quality real estate expertise to private investors.

Corbin earned a Master’s in Real Estate Finance from the University of Denver in 2008 and began his career in commercial real estate lending and investment sales brokerage. In 2013, he joined a multifamily office as an investment associate, where he was later promoted to Managing Director and, in 2023, appointed Head of Real Estate.

Over the past 15+ years, Corbin has completed over $510 million in acquisitions, financing, sales, and leasing transactions. His experience spans multiple property types (including multifamily, industrial, retail, and office) across markets nationwide.

At Playa Advisors, Corbin leads all aspects of client strategy, acquisitions, and portfolio management, supported by a network of trusted industry partners and leading edge technologies.

Case Studies

Business Sale to Investment Portfolio

A client retained a 52k sq ft cold storage facility after selling their business. When the tenant relocated, we stepped in.

Strategy & Execution:

Backfilled the building at significantly higher rent (+58% NOI), managed the sale for an attractive exit, and executed a 1031 exchange into multiple lower-risk assets.*

Next-Generation Portfolio

A third-generation family office sought to create a US commercial real estate portfolio but lacked domestic assets.

Strategy & Execution:

Served as strategic investment advisor to envision and build a multi-property US portfolio. Acquired multifamily and retail properties in Colorado, Texas, and Virginia.*

Industrial Value Creation

A former business owner sought long-term cashflow. We secured an off-market industrial property with upside.

Strategy & Execution:

Placed bridge financing, executed a long-term lease, then secured 10-year interest-only financing to cash out equity while generating significant returns.*
