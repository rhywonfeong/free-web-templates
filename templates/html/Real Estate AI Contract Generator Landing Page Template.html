Real Estate Purchase and Sale Agreement

Effective Date: October 24, 2024

1. PARTIES. This Real Estate Purchase and Sale Agreement (this "Agreement") is made and entered into by and between Oak Stone Capital, LLC ("Buyer") and Meridian Holdings Group ("Seller").

2. PROPERTY. Seller agrees to sell and convey to Buyer, and Buyer agrees to purchase from Seller, the real property located at 1240 Mapleton Ave, Austin TX 78702 (the "Property"), together with all improvements and fixtures situated thereon.

3. PURCHASE PRICE. The total purchase price to be paid for the Property is $1,250,000.00 (the "Purchase Price"), payable as follows:

(a) Earnest Money: $25,000.00 deposited with Title Company within 3 days.

(b) Seller Financing: The sum of $850,000.00 shall be financed by Seller pursuant to the Promissory Note attached hereto as Exhibit A.

(c) Cash at Closing: The balance of purchase price.

4. FINANCING TERMS. The Seller Financing shall bear interest at a rate of 5.5% per annum, amortized over 30 years, with a balloon payment due on the 60th month|
