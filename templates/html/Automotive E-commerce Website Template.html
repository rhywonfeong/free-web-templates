The Plug & Play Ecosystem

Engineered specifically for DIY installers. Our modular system links together instantly to give your older chassis the functionality of a 2024 model without a single spliced wire.

1. Chassis T-Harness

Connects directly between your factory ignition switch and wiring harness. Perfectly matched OEM connectors.

2. Smart Control Brain

The central hub. Processes proximity key entry, remote start logic, and coordinates security protocols.

3. Ghost Immobilizer

Digital lock-out preventing engine start even with a cloned key. Requires a custom button sequence or paired phone.

4. Push-to-Start

Replaces the key cylinder hole with a premium illuminated momentary button for that modern feel.
