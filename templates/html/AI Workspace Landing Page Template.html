It depends on what you mean by "better."

Common ways to compare LLMs:

  • Coding: Claude 3.7 Sonnet, GPT-4.1, Gemini 2.5 Pro are often strong
  • General reasoning: GPT-4.1, Claude 3.7 Sonnet, Gemini 2.5 Pro
  • Long documents/context: Gemini 2.5 Pro and Claude are often strong
  • Speed/cost: smaller models like GPT-4o mini, Claude Haiku, Gemini Flash
  • Creative writing: Claude and GPT models are often preferred
  • Multimodal: GPT-4o and Gemini are strong for image/audio/video tasks
  • Open-weight/local use: Llama, Mistral, Qwen, DeepSeek models are popular

A practical summary:

  • Best all-around premium: GPT-4.1 / Claude 3.7 Sonnet / Gemini 2.5 Pro
  • Best value: GPT-4o mini or Gemini Flash-class models
  • Best for local/self-hosted: Qwen, Llama, Mistral, DeepSeek depending on hardware
