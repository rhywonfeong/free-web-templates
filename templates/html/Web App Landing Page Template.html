Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Nebula.

How does the pricing work?
We charge based on resource usage. You only pay for the compute time and storage you actually use. There are no upfront fees or long-term contracts.
Can I deploy Docker containers?
Yes! Nebula supports deploying any Docker container. Just provide your Dockerfile and we'll handle the build and deployment process automatically.
Do you offer SLA guarantees?
Enterprise plans come with a 99.99% uptime SLA. We have a globally distributed infrastructure designed to withstand regional outages.
How do you handle secrets?
Secrets are encrypted at rest using AES-256. They are injected into your application environment at runtime and are never exposed in your build logs.
