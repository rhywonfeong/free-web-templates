Patient Instructions

Important information to help you prepare your pet for procedures, surgery, and rehabilitation at Erivet. Following these guidelines helps ensure the safest treatment and faster recovery.

DIAGNOSTICS

Diagnostic Procedures Under Anesthesia

Many diagnostic procedures require general anesthesia. Please follow the instructions carefully before arriving.

  • Fasting guidelines: No food for 12 hours prior to the procedure.
  • Water intake: Allow free access to water up until arrival.
  • Medications: Continue regular medication unless instructed otherwise by our team.
  • Health information: Please inform us of any recent changes in your pet's health.
  • Previous test results and imaging.
  • Complete medical history from your primary vet.
  • Your identification and pet passport/documents.
  • Duration: The entire process typically takes 3-4 hours.
  • Anesthesia: Carefully monitored by specialized anesthesiologists.
  • Pickup info: We will call you as soon as your pet is awake and ready to go home.
SURGERY

Surgery Preparation and Recovery

Proper preparation and careful wound care are essential for a safe procedure and smooth recovery.

  • Ensure your pet is clean before arriving.
  • Walk your pet shortly before the visit.
  • Follow the 12-hour fasting instructions strictly.
  • Bring any necessary medical information requested.
  • Prevent licking: Keep the recovery collar on at all times.
  • Keep dry: Ensure the wound remains clean and completely dry.
  • Monitor daily: Check stitches for excessive redness, swelling, or discharge.
  • Open stitches: If stitches open, cover the area lightly and contact us immediately.
  • Signs of complications: Severe lethargy, refusal to drink, vomiting, or pale gums.
  • Emergency cases: Call us or the nearest 24/7 animal hospital without delay.
REHABILITATION

Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation

Consistent therapy helps pets recover strength and mobility. Follow these guidelines for successful rehabilitation.

  • Ensure the pet is fully clean prior to the session.
  • Walk your pet briefly before arriving for therapy.
  • Bring specialized diapers if required for your pet.
  • Our team will guide you on what to expect during the session.
  • Home exercises: Perform the prescribed movements gently.
  • Frequency: Usually 2-3 times daily, or as instructed.
  • Recovery tips: Keep sessions positive and reward your pet.
