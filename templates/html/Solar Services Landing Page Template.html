Frequently Asked Questions

Do you deliver panels and equipment nationwide?

Yes, S-TECH Solar Solution provides secure delivery for solar panels, inverters, and batteries across major cities in Pakistan with proper handling.

What is the warranty on Lithium Batteries and Panels?

Tier-1 N-Type Panels typically come with a 12-15 year product warranty and 25-30 year linear performance warranty. Lithium batteries (ZETARA, AILINK, etc.) come with official 5-year warranties and 6000+ lifecycle guarantees.

How does the Net Metering process work?

We handle the entire A-Z net metering process including documentation, NEPRA application, testing, and bi-directional meter installation directly with your local DISCO.
