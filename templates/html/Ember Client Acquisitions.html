Questions

Straight answers.

How does pricing work?

There's no fat retainer. You cover a monthly tech fee that only pays for the infrastructure — domains, inboxes, and software — which I make nothing on. Beyond that, you only pay when a qualified call actually shows up.

What counts as a qualified call?

A call with a real decision-maker who fits your ideal client profile and has shown genuine interest. We define exactly what qualifies together, up front.

Do I have to do anything?

Just show up to the calls and close. I handle the strategy, tech, list, writing, sending, follow-up, and booking.

How soon will I see results?

From kickoff to campaigns going live is about two weeks. Results then build as we launch, read responses, and optimize toward what converts.

Who is this for?

High-ticket B2B service firms with a proven offer that want another reliable client acquisition channel.

What if it doesn't work?

Then I don't get paid. The performance model means the risk sits with me, not you.
