LOCATION

Plai Laem, Koh Samui

Plai Laem is one of Koh Samui's most desirable residential areas, located on the island's northeast coast and known for its peaceful atmosphere, natural beauty, and upscale villa living. The area is surrounded by quiet beaches, lush tropical landscapes, and prestigious resorts, offering a sense of privacy while remaining highly convenient.

With close proximity to the international airport, Choengmon, marinas, temples, wellness centers, and fine dining, Plai Laem perfectly balances relaxed island living with modern lifestyle comforts, making it ideal for both long-term living and investment.
