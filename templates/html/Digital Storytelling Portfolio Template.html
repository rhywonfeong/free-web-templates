What I Do

I’m a multidisciplinary digital creative working across design, motion, and storytelling. I focus on shaping and maintaining creative direction for story-led digital work, ensuring that motion, visuals, and interactions support the narrative and stay aligned with your brand.

I’m often brought in to help teams shape and implement motion-heavy pages and storytelling sections, ensuring the work feels cohesive, and true to the brand.

One-Line Summary

I help teams create story-led digital work where motion, video, and design come together clearly and cohesively.

About

Hi, I’m Jason, a creative director and designer originally from Ireland. I come from a family of architects, which shaped my early fascination with thoughtful design. I chose to specialise in digital work because of its disruptive nature and the DIY ethic it encourages.

Today, I collaborate with studios, agencies, and small teams on projects that values craft, intention, and creative ownership over templates and trends.

"I’m drawn to projects that feel thoughtful, human, and creatively ambitious; work that respects both intelligence and emotion, and takes craft seriously."

Ireland — Foundations

Obtained dual degrees in both computer science and digital media, developing a mix of technical thinking and creative practice.

Canada — UI & Early Agency Work

Moved to Toronto, and worked primarily in UI design, alongside internships with digital agencies and startups. This period shaped my approach to systems, usability, and real-world production constraints.

New York — Media, Studios & Agencies

Relocated to New York and worked across media and studio environments, including Vox, CIO, Unreasonable Studios, The Bloc, and D3 NYC, among others. Here, my work leaned more heavily into motion, storytelling, and media-driven digital experiences.

Independent Practice — Lord Lyon & Co.

Founded Lord Lyon & Co. two years ago — a small, subscription-based studio focused on branded documentaries and media-heavy digital projects. This has allowed me to combine creative direction, motion, and design systems into cohesive, story-driven work while collaborating closely with other creatives and production teams.
