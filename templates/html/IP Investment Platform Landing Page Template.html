The Structural Framework

A transparent, advisory-led process for institutional-grade IP investment.

1. Brand Assigns IP

Brand registers specific intellectual property into a legally ring-fenced Protected Cell.

2. Shares Issued

Fractional Tracking Shares are minted, correlating directly to the royalty revenue of the IP.

3. Participation

Investors allocate capital via advisory mandate to acquire yield-bearing Tracking Shares.

4. Royalty Distributed

Quarterly dividends are paid directly to shareholders based on audited brand gross sales.
