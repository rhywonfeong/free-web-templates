Our Mission

Mbanza d’Afrique exists to promote and empower African visual artists, giving them a global platform to showcase their talent. We aim to preserve and innovate African artistic traditions while inspiring the next generation to embrace and celebrate their culture.

A Community Beyond Art

We are more than a talent agency — Mbanza d’Afrique is a family. Our mission is to create a comforting space where African artists can express their creativity freely and connect with a supportive network. Through collaboration and mentorship, we help turn passion into purpose.

Finding the Next Generation of African Creatives

We seek emerging talent through art schools, cultural festivals, online platforms, and community art projects — ensuring every gifted artist has the chance to shine.

  • Partnerships with art schools and academies
  • Cultural festivals and local showcases
  • Online platforms and digital submissions
  • Community art projects and mentorship

Building Bridges Across Continents

Our partnerships with African museums, cultural centers, and international galleries ensure that African art reaches audiences worldwide. We are creating an online marketplace to make African art globally accessible and appreciated.

Coming Soon: The Mbanza Marketplace

A digital space where collectors, curators, and art lovers can explore and purchase authentic African artworks directly from talented artists across the continent.
