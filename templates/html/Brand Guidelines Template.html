07 & 08 — Visual Rules

Design Do's & Don'ts

Our interface relies on clean geometry, soft shadows, and purposeful warm whitespace.

  • Soft shadows only

    Use gentle elevation for cards and modals. Never use hard or dark drop shadows.

  • Thin line-style icons

    Icons should be crisp, 1.5px stroke width, typically in Charcoal or Sage.

  • Never use Terracotta for body text

    Low contrast makes it illegible. Stick to Charcoal (#3B3733) or Stone for reading.

  • No flashy animations

    Motion should be natural, smooth and purposeful, not distracting.
