#1 South Island Destination

Where the crater hums

Lyttelton is New Zealand's most eccentric port town. Dive into a maritime cauldron of underground art, legendary live music, and bohemian grit.

Explore the Scene Hear the Port

Curated Chaos

Discover the studios, stages, and secret spots that make Lyttelton a haven for creators and misfits.

Legendary Venues

From the intimate wooden floors of The Loons to the quirky, vaudeville vibes of the Wunderbar. Live original music is the lifeblood of the port.

Hidden Studios

Wander up steep volcanic streets to find ceramics, painters, and sculptors operating out of heritage cottages.

Dark Roasts & Dives

Fuel up at fiercely independent roasteries by day, and slide into moody, nautical-themed dive bars by night.

The Volcanic Basin

Perched on the edge of a collapsed volcano. The dramatic landscape fuels the art, traps the sound, and creates an atmosphere unlike anywhere else in Aotearoa.
