Daily Lesson

Grammar focus: Present Simple vs. Present Continuous

Concept

  • Use Present Simple for routines: I work every day.
  • Use Present Continuous for actions now: I am working now.
  • Signal words: always, usually, now, at the moment.

Examples

I read news every morning.

She is reading a book right now.

They play football on weekends.
