Our Technology

Advanced System Features

Rain Sensors

  • Saves water by 30-35% over basic irrigation controllers.
  • Prevents over watering during or after sufficient rainfall.
  • Includes convenient bypass features for manual control.

WiFi Clocks

  • Control your system from anywhere in the world.
  • Controller receives data from local weather stations and automatically adjusts your irrigation system.
  • Controller sends alerts if the system is not functioning properly.
  • Have ELS manage your system from our office.
  • Monitor correct water being used. In the event of broken lines or heads, the sensor will send out an alert (must have flow sensor installed).

Backflow Testing

Recommended highly by the DEP every year on the opening of your irrigation system. Done to protect any back siphonage from chemicals, toxins or pesticides into your drinking water.

SHOULD BE DONE EVERY YEAR
