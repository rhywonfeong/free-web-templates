Orchestrated Intelligence

Meet your new specialized research department.

Unlike general-purpose LLMs, Lexion uses a swarm of narrowly focused agents. They divide complex legal questions, research independently, and synthesize findings into actionable memorandums.

Case Law Agent

Scours millions of federal and state rulings in seconds. Identifies controlling authority and distinguishing facts with high precision.

Statute Agent

Monitors legislative updates and parses complex statutory language to ensure your arguments are anchored in current law.

Precedent Agent

Connects securely to your firm's internal DMS. Surfaces past briefs and memos to leverage your institutional knowledge.

Synthesis Agent

The orchestrator. Reviews findings from all other agents, resolves conflicts, and drafts cohesive, citation-ready analysis.

The Process

From query to draft in minutes, not days.

1

Input Natural Language Query

Describe your legal issue, jurisdiction, and desired output format just as you would to a junior associate.

2

Agent Delegation

Lexion automatically breaks the query into sub-tasks and assigns them to the appropriate specialized agents.

3

Parallel Research & Verification

Agents retrieve sources, extract key arguments, and perform self-correction loops to ensure hallucination-free results.

4

Final Output Generation

Receive a structured memo with hyperlinked citations, ready for human review and refinement.
