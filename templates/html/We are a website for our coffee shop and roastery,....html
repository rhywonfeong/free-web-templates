Our Coffee Story & Records

From farm to first sip

We started with a borrowed roaster, a pop-up bar, and a promise: buy transparently, roast to highlight sweetness, and share every step openly. Today our records track every batch, profile, and cup score so we keep learning—together.

2016
First roast, first pop-up

Sold out in two hours; we knew we had to keep going.
