The Ancient Art of Honey Hunting

For centuries, the Gurung people of Nepal have risked their lives scaling sheer cliffs to harvest the world's most precious honey.

Dawn Preparation

Hunters gather before sunrise, performing ancient rituals to seek blessings from the forest spirits.

The Climb

Using handmade rope ladders, hunters descend hundreds of feet down sheer cliff faces.

Smoke & Harvest

Burning grass creates smoke to calm the giant Himalayan bees while the precious honeycomb is carefully extracted.
