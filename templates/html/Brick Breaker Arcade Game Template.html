About this game

A streamlined brick breaker designed to feel at home on any modern device.

The interface is intentionally minimal: a single play area, clear status indicators, and quick access to settings. Everything else stays out of the way.

The game is fully client-side, runs in your browser, and preserves high scores locally. You can safely reload and continue to chase better runs.

Use the theme toggle in the header to switch between light and dark modes. Controls are optimized for both keyboard and touch.
