[ THE PROBLEM ]

Good founders die waiting for conviction.

The traditional venture model is broken by consensus. Funds form committees, require unanimous votes, and wait for social proof from other investors before deploying capital. They call it diligence; we call it pattern matching.

While they deliberate, you burn runway. The structural inability of large funds to generate independent conviction means the best founders are penalized for building things that look non-obvious to an analyst.

Speed is the only real alpha in early-stage venture. We engineered our entire firm to go from zero to term sheet before the rest of the market even understands the category.

MOAT DEPTH

[ QUESTIONS ]

The things you want to know before applying.

What is your average check size?
We typically invest between €2M and €5M for initial Seed checks, and €8M to €15M for Series A rounds. We reserve an equal amount for follow-on allocations in our winners.
Do you lead rounds?
Yes. We lead 95% of the rounds we participate in. We prefer to set terms, take a board seat, and actively assist in scaling operations.
What geographies do you cover?
Our primary mandate is Pan-European, with a focus on London, Paris, and Berlin ecosystems. We selectively deploy up to 20% of the fund in US-based companies with European technical founders.
How fast is your decision timeline?
Initial review takes 48 hours. If we move to diligence, our process from first partner call to term sheet is engineered to take exactly 3 weeks. We do not drag founders through months of exploratory meetings.
