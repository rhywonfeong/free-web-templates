FAQs — Everything you need to know.

How long does it take to see results?
Your first video goes live in 10 days. Most clients start seeing calls within the first 30–60 days. But YouTube is a long-term play. The more videos you have, the more calls you'll get. By 90 days, you should have 10+ booked calls.
What if I don't have time to film?
You only need two hours per month. That's it. Film 4–5 videos in one sitting or split it up into two 1-hour sessions, and you're done for the month.
What if YouTube doesn't work for my niche?
If your buyers are researching before they buy — and they are — then YouTube works. We've seen this work in coaching, consulting, agencies, SaaS, professional services, and more. If you sell something that closes on a call, YouTube works.
What if I already tried YouTube and it didn't work?
Then you didn't do it right. Most people post random videos with no strategy, no scripting, no optimization. They wonder why they get 47 views and zero calls. This is different. We're targeting specific search terms your buyers are typing in. We're scripting videos that hook people in the first 30 seconds. We're optimizing for clicks and watch time. This isn't “post and pray.” This is a system.
I already have other client acquisition channels working. Why do I need YouTube?
YouTube makes everything else work better. When prospects research you after a referral, LinkedIn post, or ad click, finding 20–30 videos of you solving their problem increases your close rate by 30–50% and makes your other channels convert higher.
