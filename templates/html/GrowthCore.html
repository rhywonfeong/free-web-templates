Privacy Policy

Educational Application Data Security

1. Introduction

At GrowthCore.in, we are committed to protecting the privacy and security of all data processed through our educational software applications. This policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and protect data, particularly for students and educational institutions.

2. Data Collection

Our educational applications collect only the information necessary to provide the intended services. This may include:

  • User account information (name, email, educational institution)
  • Academic performance data
  • Learning progress metrics
  • User preferences and settings
  • System usage statistics for improvement purposes

3. Data Security Measures

We implement comprehensive security measures to protect all data, including:

  • End-to-end encryption for data transmission
  • Secure data storage with encryption at rest
  • Role-based access controls
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Compliance with industry security standards
  • Continuous monitoring for suspicious activities

4. FERPA & COPPA Compliance

Our applications are designed to comply with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA) and the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), ensuring proper handling of student data and appropriate parental consent mechanisms.

5. Data Retention

We retain educational data only for the period necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in our service agreements or as required by law. Institutions can request data deletion at any time, and we provide tools for account closure and data removal.

6. Third-Party Access

We do not sell educational data to third parties. Any third-party services integrated with our applications undergo rigorous security assessments and are bound by strict data protection agreements. We maintain transparency about all third-party services used in our applications.

7. User Rights

Educational institutions, teachers, parents, and eligible students have rights regarding their data, including:

  • Access to all stored personal data
  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Data portability in standard formats
  • Deletion requests (subject to legal and contractual obligations)
  • Opting out of certain data processing activities

8. Updates to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or regulatory requirements. Significant changes will be communicated to users and institutions through our application notifications, email, or website announcements.
