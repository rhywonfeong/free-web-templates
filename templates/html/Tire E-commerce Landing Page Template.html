Wheel and Tire Specs, Made Simple

Wheels and tires work as a system. Learn the key specs so you can shop confidently—or let us match everything for you.

5x114.3

Bolt Pattern

The number of lugs and the circle diameter. Must match your vehicle for proper mounting.

ET35

Offset

Controls how the wheel sits in the fender. Correct offset helps avoid rubbing and brake clearance issues.

245/40R18

Tire Size

Width, sidewall, and diameter. We ensure your tire size matches your wheel width and overall diameter targets.
