Profile and behavioral analysis for primary system users

Kavita Reddy

Lead Risk Adjustment Auditor

Bio

Kavita is a meticulous and experienced auditor with over 8 years in healthcare compliance. She manages a team reviewing medical records for Medicare Advantage compliance. Managing large volumes of data daily, she relies heavily on streamlined workflows to ensure her team meets strict turnaround times without ever sacrificing coding accuracy.

Goals

  • Ensure 95%+ coding accuracy consistently across all team audits.
  • Drastically reduce the turnaround time for bulk record approvals.
  • Maintain seamless communication and reduce friction with healthcare providers.

Frustrations

  • Repetitive manual data entry that increases the risk of human error.
  • Navigating through dozens of separate screens just to update a single batch of statuses.
  • Inconsistent or missing documentation files originating from external providers.

Motivations

  • Discovering new, efficient system workflows that save time for her entire department.
  • Leading her team to achieve compliance mastery without burnout.
  • Providing clean, unchallengeable audit reports to executive management.

Behavior

  • Highly organized; relies on strict daily routines (checks queues at 8 AM and 4 PM).
  • Power user who prefers keyboard shortcuts and data-dense list views over visual cards.
  • Batches similar tasks by provider to maintain deep focus and context.
