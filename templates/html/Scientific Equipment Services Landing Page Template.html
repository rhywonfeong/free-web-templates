Operational Excellence

Precision services for critical environments.

From urgent repairs to audit-ready validation, we ensure your lab infrastructure performs with absolute reliability and compliance.

Repair Services

When equipment goes down, response time determines recovery. We combine fast arrival with structured troubleshooting to restore reliable operation.

  • Root-cause diagnostics: electrical, mechanical, refrigeration, airflow, and control-systems.
  • Correction of temperature instability, CO₂ faults, and sensor drift.
  • Component-level repairs for motors, boards, and mechanical assemblies.
  • Post-repair validation with clear documentation.

Calibration & Inspection

Accurate performance and documented compliance are essential. Our proprietary workflows verify critical operating parameters for audit readiness.

  • Proprietary inspection workflows refined over 20 years.
  • Verification for Temp, CO₂, RPM, and Airflow using NIST standards.
  • Performance adjustment to meet strict accuracy requirements.
  • Certificate of Inspection for QC and audit teams.

Preventative Maintenance

Strengthen reliability and extend equipment lifespan. Our PM procedures identify early-stage issues before they lead to costly failures.

  • Comprehensive assessment of motors, bearings, sensors, and gaskets.
  • Refrigeration evaluation: compressors, fans, and coil performance.
  • Safety testing for alarms, shutoffs, and system responses.
  • Compliance documentation detailing findings and actions.

IQ/OQ Validation

Ensure equipment is installed correctly and performing within required specifications—critical for regulated environments.

  • Installation verification including utilities and configuration.
  • Operational testing for stability, uniformity, and safety functions.
  • Structured documentation aligned with QA expectations.
  • Support for clinical, research, and production compliance.
