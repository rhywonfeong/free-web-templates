Why Choose Us

Most luxury listings in Boston fail for one reason: they were launched as a marketing exercise rather than a sales strategy.

A marketing exercise broadcasts to the largest possible audience and waits for a response. A sales strategy identifies the right buyer often before the listing is public and creates the conditions for that buyer to act.

Campion & Company has been executing sales strategies in Boston's luxury market since 2007. Before that, Tracy Campion was doing it at R.M. Bradley for twenty years. The approach has never changed: know the market surgically, know the buyers personally, and price from data rather than aspiration.

This is not a large firm. It is a selective one. Every property we represent receives the full weight of Tracy's network, her negotiating experience, and her forty years of relationships in this city. That is not a promise any national brokerage can make — because their attention is divided across forty cities. Ours is not.

If you own a significant property in Back Bay, Beacon Hill, the South End, the Seaport, Brookline, or the surrounding neighborhoods, you deserve to know what this kind of representation actually looks like in practice. That is what the consultation is for.

Tracy Campion: Founder & Principal, Campion & Company

Tracy Campion has been Boston's number-one selling residential real estate broker for twenty-one consecutive years. Since founding Campion & Company in 2007, she has personally overseen $9.3 billion in residential sales across Back Bay, Beacon Hill, the South End, the Seaport, and Boston's most sought-after suburbs.

Before founding the firm, Tracy served as Senior Vice President and Manager of R.M. Bradley and Company's residential division for over twenty years. Her career began not in real estate but in door-to-door sales restaurant equipment, cable TV, retail experience she credits for the tenacity and client instinct that have kept her at the top of this market for four decades.

Named one of Boston's 150 Most Influential People by Boston Magazine, a Women Who Mean Business honoree by the Boston Business Journal, and a two-time Pinnacle Award recipient for Real Estate Entrepreneurship, Tracy is also a Trustee of Emmanuel College, her alma mater.

She is not Boston's #1 broker because of the size of her firm. She is #1 because of the depth of her relationships, the precision of her market knowledge, and an uncompromising commitment to doing right by her clients at every price point.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of properties does Campion & Company represent?▼

We specialize in luxury residential real estate in Boston and the surrounding suburbs — primarily Back Bay, Beacon Hill, the South End, the Seaport, Waterfront, and Brookline. Single-family homes, condominiums, and historic properties. We also serve as exclusive marketing agents for residential developers.

Is there a minimum price point to work with Campion? ▼

We work across price points, though our deepest expertise and strongest buyer network is at the $2M+ level in Boston's core neighborhoods.

How quickly do properties typically sell? ▼

A correctly priced, correctly positioned property in our market finds a buyer. When a property sits, the cause is almost always a strategic issue — not a market issue. We diagnose that clearly before a listing goes live.

Do you handle both buying and selling? ▼

Yes. We represent buyers seeking homes in Boston's luxury market — including access to off-market inventory that never appears publicly.
