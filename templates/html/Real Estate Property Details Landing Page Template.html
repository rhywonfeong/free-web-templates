Property Description

Wake up to the sound of waves crashing against the cliffs in this architect-designed villa that blends seamless modern luxury with the raw beauty of the Sozopol coast. Featuring floor-to-ceiling windows and a wraparound infinity terrace, this property offers unobstructed panoramic views of the Black Sea.

The residence spans 240 sq.m of functional living space. The open-plan living room features Italian porcelain flooring and a custom fireplace, flowing naturally into a state-of-the-art kitchen equipped with premium appliances.

Each of the three bedrooms includes an en-suite bathroom and private balcony access. The master suite boasts a freestanding bathtub with ocean views. Outside, the landscaped garden includes mature fig trees and a BBQ area perfect for summer entertaining.
