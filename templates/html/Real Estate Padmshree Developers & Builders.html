About Us

Engineering Excellence in Real Estate

Padmshree Developers and Buildersis a professionally managed real estate development firm specializing in well-planned residential plotting layouts in and around Solapur. With a strong foundation in civil engineering, we focus on delivering legally clear, infrastructure-ready plots that meet modern living standards.
