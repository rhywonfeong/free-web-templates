Why Choose Sharp Detail?

The business of car detailing is often treated by others as seasonal work, with many services disappearing within months. Sharp Detail has been the leading mobile car detailing service in the Washington DC Metro area for over 10 years.

Our longevity comes from our commitment to quality. We use a tried and proven hand-washing method that demands care and attention to detail. We are fully mobile—whether you are at home, an apartment, or your office, we come to you fully equipped with our own water and electricity.
