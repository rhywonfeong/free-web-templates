Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions. Don’t see yours? Call us anytime.

How long does AC installation take? ⌄

Most standard replacements take a single day. Complex installs or ductwork modifications may take 1–2 days.

Do you handle permits and inspections? ⌄

Yes. We pull required city permits and coordinate inspections on your behalf.

What size AC do I need? ⌄

We perform Manual J load calculations and consider insulation, windows, and sun exposure to right-size your system.

What warranties are available? ⌄

Manufacturer parts warranties plus our workmanship coverage. We'll register the equipment for you.

What causes my AC to blow warm air? ⌄

Several issues can cause warm air, including low refrigerant, a dirty air filter, or a compressor problem. Our technicians can quickly diagnose and fix the problem.

How often should I service my HVAC system? ⌄

Service your HVAC system twice a year—spring for cooling and fall for heating. Regular maintenance helps prevent breakdowns and extends equipment life.

Will replacing an old system reduce my energy bill? ⌄

Yes. Modern HVAC systems are significantly more efficient than older models. Upgrading to a high‑efficiency system can lead to substantial energy savings.
