Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common employer questions

What happens if an employee delays Medicare?

Delaying Medicare enrollment can result in lifetime penalties and gaps in coverage. The rules vary based on company size and whether the employee or spouse is working. Misunderstanding these rules can lead to denied claims and significant employee exposure.

Does this cost the employer anything?

No. Exact Benefits provides this service to employers completely free of charge. We're compensated by insurance carriers when employees choose to enroll in Medicare plans, similar to how health insurance brokers are paid.

What if we're a small group and Medicare pays first?

For employers with fewer than 20 employees, Medicare typically pays first, making enrollment critical. We help navigate these rules and ensure employees understand their obligations to avoid claim denials and out-of-pocket costs.
