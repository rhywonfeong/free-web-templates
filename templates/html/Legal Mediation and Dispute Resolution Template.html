A deliberate path toward resolution.

Successful mediation rarely happens by accident. It requires a neutral environment, rigorous preparation, and a practical approach to navigating impasse. Our process is designed to bring clarity to complex matters.

01

Prepare

Thorough review of briefs and pre-mediation conferences ensure all parties arrive ready for meaningful dialogue, minimizing surprises on the day of mediation.

02

Convene

A structured, impartial environment where core issues are addressed candidly. We prioritize pragmatic risk assessment over posturing.

03

Resolve

Guiding parties through impasse toward actionable settlement terms. If complete resolution isn't reached, we establish clear next steps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who may request mediation?

Counsel for any party, or unrepresented parties directly, may initiate a mediation request. All participating parties must agree to the mediation and the selection of the mediator before scheduling is finalized.

What information is needed to schedule?

Basic case information, names of all parties and counsel (for conflict checks), venue, and preferred dates. Confidential briefs are submitted closer to the mediation date.

Can mediation be held virtually?

Yes. We offer fully remote, hybrid, and in-person mediation options depending on the preferences and logistical needs of the parties.
