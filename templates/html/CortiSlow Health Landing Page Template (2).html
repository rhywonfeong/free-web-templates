The Mechanism

The problem isn't you. It's what nobody told you about stress.

When you're under constant pressure — work deadlines, the kids, the mental load — your body releases cortisol. But when stress becomes chronic, cortisol stays elevated.

Elevated cortisol at night disrupts sleep, stores fat, and crashes your energy. It's a loop. And willpower can't break it.
