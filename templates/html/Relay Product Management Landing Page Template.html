Why this exists

I built Relay because I was running a product brand and drowning in the admin of it. Buying in one sheet, stock in another, budget in a third, assets across a dozen folders, the calendar in my head. Six places for one business.

So I built one place instead. First for us — rough, internal, exactly what we needed. Then it got good enough that I decided to build it properly.

Now I'm opening it up to a handful of people running similar businesses, before anyone else. That's the whole plan for now.
