How It Works

Installed in 6 Simple Steps.

1

Define Your ICP

We clarify exactly who you should be targeting. Industry, company size, role, revenue band, geography, buying triggers — we narrow your focus so every email goes to someone who actually fits your offer.

No guesswork. No spray-and-pray.

2

Build a Verified List

We source high-quality prospects using premium data providers and multi-layer email verification. Every address is validated before it enters your campaign.

The result: Higher deliverability. Fewer bounces. Cleaner domain reputation.

3

Write the Sequence

We craft a personalized, multi-step outreach sequence designed to start conversations — not blast promotions.

  • Custom positioning based on your offer
  • Unique first-line personalization
  • Strategic follow-ups spaced intentionally
  • Clear, simple calls to action

Professional. Human. Direct.

4

Launch the Campaign

Once approved, we deploy your campaign through a properly warmed sending environment. Everything runs automatically.

  • Sends scheduled
  • Follow-ups triggered
  • Health monitored

No manual chasing. No daily management.

5

Route Replies to You

When a prospect responds, it goes directly to your inbox.

Interested leads. Objections. Meeting requests.

You take over the conversation when it matters — after the heavy lifting is done.

6

Optimize Weekly

Outbound isn't “set it and forget it.” We monitor open rates, reply rates, positive response rates, and deliverability health.

Then we refine messaging, targeting, and sequencing to improve performance over time.

This is controlled scaling — not random volume.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are emails verified?
We use multi-layer verification tools that check domain validity, mailbox status, spam trap risk, and catch-all detection before a contact enters your campaign. Only verified emails are uploaded, protecting deliverability and sender reputation.
How personalized are the emails?
Each campaign includes custom messaging tailored to your offer and ICP. We also generate unique opening lines per prospect based on publicly available data, so outreach feels relevant and intentional — not templated or automated.
What happens when someone replies?
Replies go directly to your inbox. Positive responses, objections, and meeting requests are handed off to you so you can close the deal. We generate the conversation — you convert it.
Can you use our domain or yours?
We can configure campaigns using your domain (recommended for long-term scaling) or deploy through a dedicated outbound domain set up specifically for cold outreach. We'll guide you on the best option based on your goals.
How long to launch?
Most campaigns go live within 5–7 business days after intake form completion and copy approval. Larger campaigns may require additional list-building time.
Can we approve copy before sending?
Yes. You'll receive your sequence for review and approval before launch. Nothing goes live without your sign-off.
