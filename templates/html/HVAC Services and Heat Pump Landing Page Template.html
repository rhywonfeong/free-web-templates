Gas Furnace to Heat Pump · Retrofit Services

Gas Furnace Retrofit to High-Efficiency Heat Pumps

If you currently heat with a natural gas or propane furnace, a gas furnace retrofit to a high‑efficiency heat pump can significantly reduce your energy costs and emissions. Our technicians design retrofit solutions that connect to your existing ductwork so you can enjoy year‑round comfort with minimal disruption to your home.

We assess the condition and sizing of your current ducts, electrical panel capacity, and vent locations to determine the best approach. Where needed, we coordinate with licensed electricians and make targeted duct modifications to keep air flowing quietly and efficiently to every room.

Benefits of converting from gas to a heat pump

  • Lower utility bills and reduced exposure to volatile gas prices
  • Heating and cooling from a single high‑efficiency system
  • Cleaner indoor air without combustion by-products
  • Reduced carbon footprint and alignment with CleanBC goals
  • Access to stacked rebates for eligible retrofits

What’s included in a typical retrofit project

  • On-site assessment of your furnace, ductwork, and electrical system
  • Selection of a cold‑climate heat pump sized for your home
  • Safe decommissioning of the gas furnace and related components
  • Installation, start‑up, and fine‑tuning of the new system
  • Guidance on warranty registration and rebate documentation

We provide clear, upfront pricing and explain your options in plain language. Whether you want to fully remove your gas line or keep it as backup, we’ll help you choose the path that makes the most sense for your home.

24/7 Emergency HVAC Service · Metro Vancouver

Emergency Heat Pump & Furnace Repairs

Heating or cooling failure rarely happens at a convenient time. Our team provides 24/7 emergency repairs for heat pumps and furnaces in Metro Vancouver , so you’re not left without comfort when you need it most.

When you call, you speak directly with an experienced technician who can triage the issue, offer safety guidance, and dispatch service to your home as quickly as possible. We carry common parts on our vehicles to complete most repairs in a single visit.

Common emergency issues we handle

  • No heat or no cooling from your system
  • Breaker tripping or electrical smells from HVAC equipment
  • Unusual noises, vibrations, or burning odours
  • Frozen outdoor units or leaking indoor coils
  • Thermostat failures or error codes on your system

For your safety, we’ll always let you know if it’s better to shut down equipment and wait for service rather than continue operating a failing system.

Preventative Maintenance · Heat Pumps & Furnaces

Seasonal Maintenance & Performance Tuning

Regular maintenance keeps your equipment running efficiently and helps prevent unexpected breakdowns. We offer seasonal maintenance for heat pumps and furnaces across Metro Vancouver, tailored to your system and manufacturer requirements.

Our technicians complete a detailed multi‑point inspection, clean critical components, and verify that your system is performing within specifications. We also note any developing issues so you can plan repairs before they become emergencies.

What’s included in a typical maintenance visit

  • Check filters, indoor and outdoor coils, and blower assemblies
  • Verify refrigerant levels and inspect for visible leaks
  • Inspect electrical connections, sensors, and safety controls
  • Test heating and cooling performance and thermostat operation
  • Provide a written report with recommendations, if needed

For many manufacturers, proof of regular maintenance is required to keep warranties valid. We document each visit so you have a clear history of professional service on your system.
