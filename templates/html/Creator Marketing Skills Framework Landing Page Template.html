Core Competencies

The framework is divided into four primary pillars, covering the entire lifecycle of creator marketing operations.

Strategy & Planning

Aligning creator initiatives with broader business objectives and target audiences.

  • Goal Setting & KPIs
  • Budget Allocation
  • Platform Selection

Sourcing & Outreach

Identifying the right talent and establishing initial communication and interest.

  • Profile Vetting
  • Pitch Crafting
  • Negotiation Basics

Campaign Execution

Managing the end-to-end process from brief to content publication.

  • Brief Development
  • Content Review
  • Logistics & Seeding

Measurement & ROI

Analyzing campaign performance and translating data into actionable insights.

  • Performance Tracking
  • ROI Calculation
  • Reporting & Insights

Why use this framework?

Creator marketing is maturing. This framework provides a standardized language and progression path for teams to scale effectively.

Standardized Hiring

Create clear job descriptions and assess candidates against defined industry benchmarks rather than guesswork.

Career Progression

Provide your team with a clear roadmap for skill development from junior coordinators to strategic directors.

Team Structuring

Identify skill gaps within your current organization and structure your team for maximum impact.
