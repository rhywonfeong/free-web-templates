Our Impact Operating System

Powered by B1G1, we make giving a natural part of doing business. It's an automated, verified loop that turns everyday actions into extraordinary impacts.

1. You Commit

Link a routine business action—selling a product, booking a client, sending an email—to a cause.

2. We Connect

Our system automatically routes a micro-contribution to a verified B1G1 project, like providing 30 days of clean water.
