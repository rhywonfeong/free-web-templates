Frequently asked questions

Clear, straightforward answers to common concerns.

How long does it take to get my auto insurance quote?
Most drivers can complete the form in about 90 seconds. Once you submit your basic information, you'll instantly see quotes from multiple providers.
Will checking my rate affect my credit score?
No. We perform a "soft pull" to generate your quotes, which does not impact your credit score in any way.
Why should I use AutoQuote instead of going directly to insurance companies?
Going direct limits you to one price. We compare dozens of top-rated carriers simultaneously to ensure you aren't overpaying for the coverage you need.
Is my personal information safe with AutoQuote?
Yes. We use bank-level 256-bit encryption to protect your data. We never sell your personal information to spam lists.
What types of vehicles can I insure through AutoQuote?
We support quotes for cars, trucks, SUVs, and motorcycles. We can also help bundle with home or renters insurance for extra savings.
Can I switch my current policy using AutoQuote?
Absolutely. You can switch at any time. Your new carrier will often help handle the cancellation of your old policy to ensure no lapse in coverage.
