Why Isn't Everyone Doing This Then?

Great question. Because private villa development in Dubai's ultra-luxury market has some very real challenges. Here's what trips most investors up:

The Right Plot is Everything

Prime plots are nearly fully allocated. If you don't know who to call, the best opportunities are gone before they even hit the market. Sourcing the right plot is the key.

Construction Costs

A 25% cost overrun on a AED 25M build wipes out AED 6.25M in profit. This is almost guaranteed without the right contractors and oversight.

Bad Design = Lower Price

The difference between a villa that sells at AED 50M vs AED 70M is almost entirely design decisions made at the start, and they can't be undone.

Approvals

Dubai's regulatory process, RERA, DDA, and municipality, can take 3–4 months without the right guidance.

Selling Ultra-Luxury Network

AED 50M+ transactions don't happen through property portals. You need direct access to UHNWI buyers, family offices, and sovereign-adjacent capital.

The bottom line: the strategy works brilliantly but only with the right execution. Without it, the same mistakes that derail most investors will derail you too.
