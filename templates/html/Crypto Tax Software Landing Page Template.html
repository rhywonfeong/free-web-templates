Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to pay taxes if I didn't sell for fiat?

Yes. In most jurisdictions (like the US, UK, Australia), trading one crypto for another (e.g., BTC to ETH) is considered a taxable disposal. You must calculate the fair market value in fiat at the time of the trade to determine your gain or loss.

How secure is my data? Do you have access to my crypto?

We use bank-level encryption. We only use read-only APIs or public wallet addresses to fetch your transaction history. We cannot move or access your funds under any circumstances.

What if I made a mistake in previous years?

You can generate reports for previous tax years using Taxo to file amendments. Our software will recalculate your entire history to ensure your cost basis carries forward correctly.

Can my accountant use Taxo?

Absolutely. We have a dedicated CPA portal. You can invite your accountant to your account, allowing them to review your transactions, adjust settings, and download the reports they need.
