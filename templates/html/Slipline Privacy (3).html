02 / How it works

Private by design. Three steps, end‑to‑end.

Slipline breaks linkability using stealth routing and zero‑knowledge settlement. No accounts, no tracking pixels, no breadcrumbs.

STEP 1

Set your swap privately

Choose from 14+ chains. Your request is encrypted client‑side; relays never see your source address.

Encrypted intent No account Stealth routing
STEP 2

Route via encrypted relays

Your intent is split and shuffled across independent relays with cover traffic and timing noise.

Cover traffic Split routes Timing noise
STEP 3

Settle with zero‑knowledge

Funds are settled using zk‑proofs; verifiers confirm correctness without revealing linkable inputs.

Non‑custodial Chain‑agnostic Zero knowledge
