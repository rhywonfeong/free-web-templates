HEAD COACH

Professor Kolo Vida

Guam Roots.
World-Class Lineage.

Hélio Gracie → Ralph Gracie → Kurt Osiander → Professor Kolo Vida

Buena Vida means the good life . And for Professor Kolo, that started on the mats in Guam before he was a teenager. He brought that same love of the art to San Francisco, earned his black belt under Kurt Osiander out of Ralph Gracie's legendary lineage, then built this academy from the ground up in West Sacramento in 2019.

What he built isn't just a gym. It's a community where dads de-stress after long days, where kids grow into focused, confident young people, and where everyone — from day-one beginners to seasoned competitors — is treated like they belong.
