Common questions.

Everything you need to know about working with us.

What is your typical project timeline?

Timelines vary based on scope, but a standard end-to-end website redesign typically takes 6 to 10 weeks from discovery to launch. We provide a detailed schedule after initial scoping.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes, we offer monthly retainer packages for continuous design, development, and strategic support to ensure your digital product evolves alongside your business.

What technologies do you use?

We are tech-agnostic but favor modern, performant stacks. For web experiences, we frequently utilize React, Next.js, Tailwind CSS, and headless CMS solutions like Sanity or Contentful.
