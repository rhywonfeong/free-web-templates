FAQs — Everything you need to know.

How fast can we expect results?
Most clients begin seeing positive responses within the first few weeks and qualified meetings within the first 30 days. Results depend on your market, offer, and targeting, but our goal is to start generating opportunities as quickly as possible while building a sustainable pipeline.
What counts as a qualified meeting?
A qualified meeting is one with a decision-maker who matches your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP), has the problem you solve, and attends the scheduled call. We agree on these criteria together before launching.
Do you require long-term contracts?
No, we work on a month-to-month, performance-based basis. You aren't locked into any long-term commitments. You stay because we deliver results.
How much do you charge per meeting?
Our fee per qualified meeting depends on your industry and offer, typically starting around $300, plus a small one-time setup fee for infrastructure. You only pay for meetings that occur.
Who handles the email accounts and sending?
We handle everything end-to-end. We purchase the secondary domains, set up the inboxes, warm them up, and manage the sending infrastructure to completely protect your main domain's reputation.
Do I need to provide the leads?
No, we handle all the lead sourcing and list building using premium data providers to ensure we're reaching out to the right decision-makers at the right companies.
How do we get started?
Book a strategy call using the calendar above. We'll audit your current process, see if we're a fit, and if so, we can launch your custom campaign within 7-14 days.
