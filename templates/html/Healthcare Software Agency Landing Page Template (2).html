Frequently Asked Questions

How can requirements be shared before the project begins?

A discovery call is typically scheduled to walk through product goals, clinical workflows, technical constraints, and user journeys. If available, existing documentation, reference apps, or feature lists can be shared to guide scoping.

What is the typical timeline for developing a custom healthcare product or MVP?

Most full-featured MVPs are delivered within 8–14 weeks, depending on platform complexity, number of integrations, and design/development scope.

How is the total project cost estimated?

Cost is based on scope, team composition, timeline, and technology stack. After an initial discovery session, a fixed quote or estimate range is provided based on key features and technical requirements.

What engagement models are available (fixed, hourly, dedicated team)?

Engagements can be structured as fixed-price (for clearly scoped projects), time-and-materials (for evolving products), or dedicated teams (for longer-term builds or scale-ups).

Is it possible to speak with the actual developers or team members before signing off?

Yes. Stakeholders can meet developers, designers, or tech leads to assess skill fit and collaboration style before formal onboarding.

Who will be the single point of contact throughout the project?

A dedicated project manager is assigned as the primary point of contact to manage delivery, communication, and coordination across teams.

Is daily or weekly time zone overlap provided for syncs and reviews?

Yes. Projects are staffed to ensure at least 3–4 hours of daily working overlap with the client’s preferred time zone for effective collaboration.

What happens after the app is launched?

Post-launch support includes optional maintenance, performance monitoring, bug fixes, and feature expansion. Support scope can be tailored to ongoing business needs.

What does ongoing maintenance involve, and how is it priced?

Maintenance can include server monitoring, minor enhancements, security patches, and support SLAs. Costs vary based on app complexity and usage, and are billed monthly or by usage blocks.

Are there any third-party or recurring cloud costs to consider (e.g., hosting, APIs)?

Yes. Clients receive full ownership of source code, infrastructure, and technical documentation upon completion, with no vendor lock-in.

Can the development be transitioned to an in-house team later?

Yes. A structured handoff process ensures internal teams receive the full codebase, deployment guides, and access credentials. Technical onboarding support is also available if needed.
