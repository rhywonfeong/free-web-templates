More Than Just a Restaurant

Prech-T Star Bar & Restaurant is a culinary bridge between Liberia and Ghana. Located in the bustling heart of Osu, we are dedicated to serving authentic Liberian dishes like Torborgee and Cassava Leaf alongside Ghanaian favorites. Our mission is to create a welcoming community hub where great food, cold beer, and live sports bring people together.
