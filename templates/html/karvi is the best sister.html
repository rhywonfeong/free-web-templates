Core Technology

Vertical Axis Wind Turbine

Unlike traditional horizontal turbines, VAWTs catch wind from all directions without needing to reorient. Their omni-directional design and silent operation make them perfect for dense urban environments.

  • Operates in turbulent city winds
  • Low noise acoustic signature
  • Bird & bat friendly design

Integrated Urban Grid

Energy harvested from street-level VAWTs flows directly into the localized smart grid.

Smart Buildings

Direct power supply to IoT sensors, HVAC automation, and localized battery storage systems.

Adaptive Infrastructure

Powering AI-driven traffic lights and autonomous surveillance drones for city safety.

Street Illumination

Self-sustaining LED arrays that dim when streets are empty to conserve harvested energy.

CHARGING... 87%

Inductive Wireless Charging

Park and power up. Our smart road infrastructure embeds magnetic resonance coils directly into parking bays. Energy harvested by local VAWTs is transferred wirelessly to EV batteries with 94% efficiency.

Magnetic Resonance

Safe, high-speed energy transfer across air gaps.

App Integration

Monitor charge levels and payment via city app.
