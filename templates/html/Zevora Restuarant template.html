The Narrative

It begins with an obsession. Years spent in the quiet backstreets of Ginza, learning that what you remove is as important as what you add.

At KANSO, we deconstruct Edomae sushi to its molecular level. The rice is cooked in cast iron. The vinegar is aged for three years. The fish is treated not as an ingredient, but as a living artifact of the ocean. This is a philosophy of restraint—where silence speaks louder than noise, and simplicity is the ultimate sophistication.
