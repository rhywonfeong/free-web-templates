The Problem

Here's what we kept hearing from founders.

"We hired through an agency. The engineer was great in the interview, then disappeared two weeks in."

"We burned three months and $40k on code we had to throw away and rebuild."

"The agency said 'senior engineer.' We got a junior who was also working for three other startups at the same time."

"When things went wrong, the agency ghosted us. No support. No replacement. Just radio silence."

These aren't edge cases. This is what offshore hiring looks like when there's no real accountability.

Our Approach

How we do things differently

We built Enlighten Schola around three principles that traditional offshore models often miss.

Full-Time Embedded Engineers

Your engineer works for one client only — you. No juggling projects, no context switching. They become part of your team.

End-to-End Management

We handle sourcing, vetting, onboarding, payroll, and compliance. You get a dedicated Client Success Manager who stays involved.

Transparent Process

Weekly timesheets. Direct communication with your engineers. No hidden fees. You know exactly what's happening, every step.

The result? Offshore hiring that feels like hiring in house.

Our Values

What we stand for

Quality First

We reject 99% of applicants. We only place engineers we'd hire ourselves. Great talent, not available resources.

Accountability

Long-Term Thinking

Respect for People

Engineers aren't resources. They're professionals. We pay them fairly, train them continuously, and give them meaningful work.
