Roxie Hensley stands at the intersection of justice, compassion, and Wyoming grit. As a former youth guidance counselor, practicing attorney, and woman‑owned business leader, she brings unmatched experience to ensure our Albany County, WY community remains safe and strong.

A Life of Service

Roxie has spent her life helping people — guiding youth through difficult crossroads, advocating for justice in the courtroom, and building a thriving woman‑run business, in the heart of downtown Laramie, Wyoming.

Her leadership as Albany County GOP Chair reflects her dedication to transparency, responsibility, and community engagement. Roxie believes in a justice system that protects the innocent, holds offenders accountable, and strengthens the fabric of Albany County.

A Community
Cornerstone

Roxie Hensley isn’t just an attorney; she is a cornerstone of the Laramie community. Licensed to practice law in Wyoming for nearly two decades, Roxie brings a sharp legal mind and a deep understanding of our state's justice system.

But her resume goes far beyond the courtroom. As the proud owner of local staple a massive job creator, and the Chair of the Albany County Republican Party, Roxie knows exactly what it takes to build, survive, and thrive in Wyoming. She has stood on the front lines defending our conservative values and fighting for the hardworking citizens who form the backbone of our local economy.

Why Roxie is Running for Albany County Attorney

Roxie is running for County Attorney because Albany County deserves a leader who shows up, listens, and acts with integrity. She believes in a justice system that reflects Wyoming’s core values: personal responsibility, limited government, respect for the law, and strong communities. Roxie is committed to ensuring that victims in Laramie and across Albany County are heard, offenders are held accountable, and the law is applied consistently and fairly.

Her Vision for Albany County, WY

We are living in a critical time, and Albany County needs a chief prosecutor who won't back down. Roxie's platform is built on the values that make Wyoming great:

Unapologetic Law & Order

Roxie will fiercely prosecute the violent crimes and drug trafficking threatening our neighborhoods. She backs the blue and will ensure our law enforcement officers have the strong, reliable legal partner they need in the prosecutor's office.

Defending Your Rights

As a true patriot, Roxie believes the Constitution is not a suggestion. She will be a firewall against government overreach and will always protect the fundamental rights of Albany County citizens.

Justice with Integrity

Roxie brings 20 years of legal experience and the practical, no-nonsense perspective of a local business owner to the office. She will ensure the prosecutor’s office operates with total transparency, accountability, and a commitment to justice for victims.
