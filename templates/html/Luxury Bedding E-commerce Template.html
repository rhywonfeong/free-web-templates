Lowell.

A single, perfect line.

Lowell is the quintessential Matouk style. An elegant, 1-inch sateen tape detail traces the edges of our crisp, 600 thread count Milano percale. It is a study in restrained luxury, designed to frame your bed with architectural precision.

Crafted in Fall River

Every piece of Lowell is cut, sewn, and meticulously finished by our team of skilled artisans in our Massachusetts facility, ensuring flawless execution.
