Have Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the annual fee for membership?

Please contact our office directly for the most current membership fee structure. We believe in transparency and will discuss all costs with you before you make a commitment. Our fee is a single, annual payment that covers all the primary care services included in the membership.

Do you accept health insurance?

We do not accept health insurance for our membership fee. The annual fee is paid directly to the practice. However, we recommend all members maintain their health insurance plan to cover outside services such as specialist visits, hospitalizations, emergency room care, and complex imaging or lab work.

What is the difference between concierge medicine and my current primary care?

The key difference is the size of the practice and the level of access. A traditional primary care doctor may have over 2,000 patients, which leads to long waits and short visits. Our practice is limited to a small number of families, allowing for 24/7 physician access, same-day appointments, and deeply personal, proactive care.

Who is Dr. Abeer Aziz-Siddiqui?

Dr. Aziz-Siddiqui is a board-certified physician [American Osteopathic Association] with over a decade of leadership experience in some of South Florida's most respected hospitals. She founded Palm Beach Concierge Medicine with her husband to create a practice that restores the trusted, personal relationship between a doctor and their patients.

What happens if I need to see a specialist or go to the hospital?

We act as your healthcare quarterback. Dr. Aziz-Siddiqui will personally coordinate your referral to the appropriate specialist and communicate with them directly about your case. If you are hospitalized, she will work with the hospitalist team to advocate for you and help manage your care.

Is this practice right for me if I am healthy?

Absolutely. Our care model is built on a foundation of proactive and preventive medicine. For healthy patients, our focus is on keeping you that way through comprehensive wellness planning, advanced health screenings, and building a strong baseline for your long-term health.

Does concierge medicine replace my health insurance?

No. Concierge medicine is not health insurance. Your membership covers enhanced primary care services, including direct physician access, extended visits, and proactive wellness planning. You should maintain your existing health insurance to cover specialist care, hospital stays, prescriptions, and other services outside of primary care.
