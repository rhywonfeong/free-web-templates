Our Story

Good Food.

The Lost Yeti brings together bold flavours, creative cooking and a vibrant social atmosphere. Designed as a place to eat, drink and gather, it's the perfect destination for lunch, dinner, cocktails and celebrations.

From quick lunches in town to late-night drinks and social dining, The Lost Yeti is built around great food, great drinks, and unforgettable moments.

More Than Just a Restaurant

At The Lost Yeti, every visit is designed to be an experience. Whether you're stopping by for a quick bite, meeting friends for drinks, or celebrating something special, our space adapts to every moment.
