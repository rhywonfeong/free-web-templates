The Artist

Breathing life into the canvas

I'm Alex Rivera, a digital illustrator and concept artist obsessed with storytelling. I believe every brushstroke should serve the narrative.

My work bridges the gap between traditional fine art techniques and modern digital workflows. Whether it's a book cover, game asset, or personal piece, I aim for emotional resonance and visual fidelity.

Visual Development

Building worlds from scratch

Concept art is about solving problems visually. I help studios and authors visualize their worlds, setting the tone, mood, and architectural style.

From thumbnail sketches to fully realized matte paintings, I handle the entire visual development pipeline.

Character Design

Characters with soul

A great character design tells a story before they even speak. I focus on anatomy, costume history, and silhouette to create memorable figures.

I specialize in fantasy and sci-fi aesthetics, blending realism with stylized elements.

Traditional Roots

Digital tools, traditional heart

I started with oils and charcoal. That foundation in light, shadow, and composition informs every digital piece I create.

I often use custom brushes to mimic the texture of canvas and the flow of ink, ensuring the "human touch" remains visible.

My Workflow

From sketch to masterpiece

Collaborative and iterative creation process

1

Concept & Thumbnails

We start with loose sketches and thumbnailing to establish composition, pose, and value structure. We iterate here until the foundation is solid.

2

Line Art & Color Key

Refining the sketch into clean linework (if applicable) and establishing the color palette and lighting mood through color keys.

3

Rendering

The bulk of the work. Painting details, textures, skin tones, and atmospheric effects. Bringing the piece to a high level of polish.

4

Final Polish & Delivery

Final adjustments, post-processing effects, and exporting in high-resolution formats suitable for print or digital display.
