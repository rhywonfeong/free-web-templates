Our Roots

A family-run
garden retreat.

Hidden away in the vibrant heart of Kamala, 63 Ancient Tea is born from a love of sharing genuine local flavors. We specialize in brewing fresh, high-quality coffee and preparing delicate, handcrafted coconut treats from scratch.

Step onto our terrace and leave the rush behind. Surrounded by dense, lush foliage, our intimate café offers a quiet, affordable corner of Phuket where every guest is treated like family.
