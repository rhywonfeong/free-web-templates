About Me

I'm a UX researcher passionate about tackling ambiguity and turning complex problems into clear product direction. I thrive in the early stages of product development, where asking the right questions is often more valuable than finding quick answers.

Over the past 10 years, I've partnered with cross-functional teams across AI, enterprise software, education technology, and consumer products to uncover user needs, reduce product risk, and shape product strategy. My work spans everything from generative discovery to evaluative research, always focused on helping teams build experiences that are both useful and meaningful.
