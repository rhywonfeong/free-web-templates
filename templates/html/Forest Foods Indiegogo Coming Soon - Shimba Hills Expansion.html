Why Shimba Hills Matters

Shimba Hills is a place of extraordinary ecological value, but like many landscapes, it faces growing pressure from changing land use, degraded soil, climate stress, and fragile food systems.

Forest Foods sees an opportunity to help create something lasting: a regenerative model that restores soil health, supports local farmers, increases biodiversity, and produces food in a way that works with the land rather than against it.

This campaign is about helping that system take root.
