It started with one question.

"Why isn't someone cleaning these cars while everyone is golfing?"

Caddy Bath began with a simple idea between friends: golfers spend four or five hours enjoying a round while hundreds of vehicles sit in the parking lot doing nothing.

Getting a vehicle professionally detailed usually requires sacrificing additional time on another day. So we flipped the process.

Instead of asking customers to make time for detailing, we bring detailing to the time they already have. That's Caddy Bath.

Simple. Convenient. Premium.
