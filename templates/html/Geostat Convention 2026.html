Convention Theme

Contours: Bridging the World and Statistics

Geospatial insights and official statistics converge to reveal the shape of our landscapes, communities, and development paths. Explore how contours transform raw data into grounded, visual stories.

Contours · Data · Futures

Primary Theme

From Data Tables to Living Landscapes

Translate statistical series into contour maps that show elevation, risk, opportunity, and change over time.

From Data to Landscapes

Shaping contours through statistics—connecting socio-economic indicators, hazards, and infrastructure to real-world terrain.

Beyond Borders
