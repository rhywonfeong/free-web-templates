Required Document List
for Thailand Visa

Passport

Valid Passport with a minimum validity of seven months.

Photographs

Three copies, size 35x45mm. Must be a lab print on matte paper, white background, and not older than 6 months.

Financial Proof

Six months Bank statement (Personal & Salary) with solvency certificate. Minimum balance requirement is 1,50,000 BDT.
Must carry this balance until passport is received.

Occupational Proof

For Job Holders

  • NOC on office letterhead mentioning tentative leave duration.
  • Visiting Card copy (Two Copies).
  • Office ID card copy.
  • Salary certificate and last 3 months pay slip.

For Business Personnel

  • Updated Trade License.
  • Business Card (Two Copies).

Translation & Notary

All documents must be translated into English and printed on A4 paper. GoTrip provides translation services (extra charges apply).
