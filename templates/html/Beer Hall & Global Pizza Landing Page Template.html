Our Story

A Hall for the Curious

Godwin's Beer Hall was born from a simple idea: why travel the world when the world can come to you? We've curated an ever-rotating selection of beers from across the globe, paired with Executive Chef Rob Chiappone's inventive Global Pizza concept.

Whether you're savoring a German lager withtwurst or pairing a Belgian ale with Thai curry pizza, every visit is a new adventure.
