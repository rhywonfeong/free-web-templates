Popular Before and After Design Ideas

Sparking your imagination is the first step in the transformation journey. Whether your goal is a modest refresh or a gut-renovation, here are some of the most impactful ideas we have seen effectively elevate local residences.

1. The Cabinetry Overhaul: Custom vs. Refacing

Outdated, dark oak cabinets can make a room feel smaller than it actually is. The "before" often features poorly utilized vertical space and inaccessible corner units. The "after" introduces sleek, ceiling-height custom cabinetry that maximizes storage.

  • Pros of Custom: Total control over layout, integrated organizers, and exact hardware finishes.
  • Pros of Cabinet Refacing: A faster visual update if the existing box structures remain in excellent condition.

However, when significantly altering the layout to accommodate larger energy-efficient appliances or a new island, custom built-ins are often the required standard.

2. Upgrading Countertop Materials

Laminate or heavily grouted tile surfaces of the past are high-maintenance and visually dated. A striking "after" incorporates seamless slab surfaces. Quartz countertops have become incredibly popular due to their non-porous nature, resisting stains from wine, coffee, and oils without the need for annual sealing.

Pairing these durable surfaces with a dynamic tile backsplash creates a focal point that instantly modernizes the environment while providing easy cleanup behind high-traffic cooking zones.

3. Illuminating the Workspace

The dreaded single fluorescent ceiling box is a hallmark of older homes. A proper modern lighting design is layered. By combining ambient recessed lighting with targeted under-cabinet lighting and statement pendant fixtures over the island, the room transforms from a shadowy cavern into a bright, welcoming gathering space.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a kitchen remodel take in Sun Lakes?

A typical kitchen remodel takes between 4 to 7 weeks of active construction time. This timeline depends on the complexity of the project, such as whether you are simply updating surfaces or entirely reconfiguring the layout, moving plumbing lines, and installing custom cabinetry.

Should I choose custom cabinetry or cabinet refacing?

If your current layout functions perfectly and the internal cabinet boxes are structurally sound, cabinet refacing can be a viable option. However, if you want to optimize storage, integrate energy-efficient appliances, or change the room's footprint, custom cabinetry is the superior choice for long-term value.

What are the best countertop materials for an active cooking space?

Quartz countertops are currently the gold standard for active spaces. Unlike natural stone, quartz is engineered to be non-porous, meaning it resists staining and does not require periodic chemical sealing. It offers a premium look with drastically reduced maintenance.

How does a design-build approach save money?

The design-build model saves money by eliminating the "design-build gap." Because the interior designer, estimators, and construction crews are all on the same team, feasibility and budget are checked simultaneously during the planning phase. This prevents ordering incompatible materials and eliminates costly mid-project change orders.

Do I need to buy my own energy-efficient appliances?

You can purchase your own appliances, but it is highly recommended to do so in close collaboration with your design team during the planning phase. Exact dimensions and electrical/plumbing specifications are required before finalizing the custom cabinetry design to ensure a flush, built-in aesthetic.
