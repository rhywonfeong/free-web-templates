Frequently Asked Questions

How does anonymous chatting work?

You can chat with random people without revealing your real identity. Only your nickname and avatar are visible to others.

How do I report someone?

Tap the menu icon (three dots) in a chat and select "Report User". Describe the issue and submit.

Can I change my nickname?

Yes! Go to Profile > Edit Profile to change your nickname or avatar anytime.

Is my data safe?

We don't collect personal data. All chats are anonymous and messages are not stored permanently.
