Meet Jasmine Hajimomen

Founder and CEO

I started doing tax returns for friends out of an In-N-Out Burger in 2012. Not because anyone asked me to. I just couldn't stop learning about the tax code and figuring out how to help people keep more of what they earned. That curiosity turned into a Facebook community of over 15,000 self-employed individuals, a degree from USC, and eventually years of working hands-on with high-earning creators, business owners, and entrepreneurs who needed more than just someone to file their returns.

They needed someone who could think ahead and build a real strategy. Today, Momen Tax Services is a leading tax and accounting advisory firm that helps clients save tens to hundreds of thousands of dollars through complex, tailored tax planning. Every strategy is legal, proactive, and built around the full picture of our clients' financial lives.

Whether you're a W2 high earner, a business owner scaling fast, or a creator turning income into long-term wealth, we build the plan that makes sure your money works as hard as you do.
