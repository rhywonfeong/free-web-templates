Answers to common questions.

If you’re evaluating Hoodgen for yourself or your team, these details can help.

How is Hoodgen different from ChatGPT or other AI chat tools?
Hoodgen is a full content workspace, not just a chat box. You get project structure, promptless workflows, campaign organization, and unified access to multiple models—all tuned for shipping content across channels.
Do I need my own OpenAI or Anthropic API keys?
No. Access to multiple premium models is included in your Hoodgen subscription with fair usage limits. For advanced teams, we’re adding the option to plug in your own keys on higher‑tier plans.
Can I use Hoodgen with my clients or team?
Yes. You can invite collaborators into shared workspaces (with role control on Team plans) or share read‑only links to specific campaigns and projects on all paid plans.
What happens to my content and data?
Your projects, briefs, and outputs are yours. We don’t use your content to train our own models, and we follow strict security practices for storage and access.
Can I cancel or change plans anytime?
Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel directly from your billing settings. We’ll keep your workspaces intact so you can return when you’re ready.
