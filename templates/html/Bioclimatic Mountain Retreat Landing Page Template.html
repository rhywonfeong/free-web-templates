A Different Side of the Island

Forget the crowded beaches and busy resorts. Welcome to the heart of the island, where time slows down. At My Green Canaria, you are surrounded only by vegetation, nature, and silence. Located high in the mountains of Gran Canaria, we offer a retreat for those seeking peace, fresh air, and a deep connection with the earth.

Here, the only "traffic" you will see are the flocks of sheep and goats grazing on the opposite slope of the valley, and the hawks gliding on the thermal currents above. With over 300 days of sunshine a year, this is your private hideaway to disconnect and recharge.

The Magic of Living in the Rock

Staying in a Casa Cueva (Cave House) is a unique experience that dates back to the ancient inhabitants of the island. It is not just accommodation; it is a natural embrace.

Natural Air Conditioning

Our caves are bioclimatic wonders. The rock acts as a natural thermal regulator, keeping the interior temperature between 18°C and 22°C all year round. Cool in summer, warm in winter.

The Sound of Silence

The thickness of the mountain walls provides absolute sound insulation. You will experience a depth of sleep that is hard to find anywhere else.

Tradition Meets Comfort

While our 3 bedrooms are carved into the mountain following centuries of tradition, the rest of the house offers modern design, light, and all the comforts you need.

Your Mountain Home

Our property is designed for nature lovers who appreciate simplicity and beauty. We combine rustic textures with modern amenities to create a space that feels like home.

UNESCO World Heritage Site

Barranco Hondo:
A Living Museum

We are located in Barranco Hondo de Abajo, a place of immense ethnographic value. This area is part of the Risco Caído and the Sacred Mountains of Gran Canaria cultural landscape.

Hiking Paradise

Put on your boots and step outside. You are minutes away from some of the island's best hiking trails.

Local Community

Support our neighbors. Buy local cheese, visit the traditional potters (loceras), and taste authentic flavors.
