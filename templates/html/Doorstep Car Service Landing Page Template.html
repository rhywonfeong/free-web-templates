Frequently asked questions

Do the mechanics actually service the car at my home?
Yes, 100%. Our specialized mobile service vans are equipped with all necessary tools to perform complete periodic maintenance, deep cleaning, and battery replacements right at your parking spot in Hyderabad. No garage visits required.
How is pricing calculated? Are there hidden costs?
We believe in radical transparency. The price you see for our standard packages is the price you pay. If our pre-service inspection reveals additional parts are needed (like brake pads), we will send you a digital quote. We only proceed with extra work if you approve it on WhatsApp.
Do I need to be present during the service?
You only need to hand over the keys and provide access to the vehicle. You can watch the service happen or return to your work. Our technicians will update you via WhatsApp with photos and videos upon completion.
How do I book a service?
Booking is entirely conversational and frictionless. Simply click "Chat on WhatsApp" anywhere on this page, share your car details, and our automated assistant (backed by real humans) will share packages and available slots in your area.
