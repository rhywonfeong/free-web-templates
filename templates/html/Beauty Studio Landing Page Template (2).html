Common Questions

Does the procedure hurt?
Most clients experience very little discomfort. We use a high-quality medical-grade topical anesthetic before and during the procedure to ensure you are comfortable. Many clients even fall asleep during the process!
How long does it last?
Depending on your skin type, lifestyle, and aftercare, results typically last between 1 to 3 years. Oily skin may require touch-ups sooner. We recommend an annual color boost to keep them looking fresh.
Will it look dark immediately?
Eyebrows will appear slightly darker and sharper for the first 3-5 days. This is normal oxidation. Over the next week, they will soften by about 30-40% to reveal the final, natural color we designed.
Can I wear makeup after?
You should avoid applying makeup directly on the eyebrows for at least 10 days to allow them to heal properly. You can wear face makeup around the area, but keep the brows clean.

The Complete Guide to Eyebrow Tattoo Aftercare

The secret to perfect eyebrows isn't just the procedure—it's how you care for them in the 7 days following your appointment.

First 24 Hours

Gently blot your eyebrows with a clean tissue every hour to remove lymph fluid. This prevents thick scabs from forming.

Days 2-7

Keep them dry! Use the healing cream provided in your AmorJ aftercare kit sparingly. Do not pick or scratch if they itch.

Microblading vs. Traditional Tattooing

It's Not Forever

Unlike traditional tattoos which place ink deep into the dermis, semi-permanent makeup deposits pigment into the upper layers. This means it fades naturally over 1-3 years.

No Blue Brows

Traditional tattoos often turn blue or green over time due to the carbon content. Our premium pigments are formulated to fade true-to-color.
