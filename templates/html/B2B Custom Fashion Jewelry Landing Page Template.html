Frequently Asked Questions

What is the customized Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)?
MOQ 100pcs for regular products, 500pcs for special processes like custom boxes. Trial orders (100-500pcs) supported at a slightly higher unit price.
How long does proofing take and cost?
7 days regular, 3-5 days expedited. Fee 50-200 RMB depending on complexity. Fee refundable for bulk orders over 500 pieces.
How is price calculated and what is the payment method?
Price = Material + Process + Design + Packaging + Freight. Payment: 30% advance, 70% before shipment via T/T, PayPal, or L/C.
What about quality problems or copyright?
Free replenishment within 30 days for non-human issues. Customer owns copyright for their designs; original designs belong to us unless exclusivity is agreed.
