Why we started

In 2018 we started traditional car rental broker business Rentalmoose.com. The more operators we met, the clearer the bottleneck became: the rental industry didn’t have a reliable, end-to-end way to deliver true digital access—especially out of hours—without stitching together five different vendors.

So we built the missing piece: a digital key and platform designed for car rental, car sharing and corporate fleets.

From broker to infrastructure

2018

The beginning (Rentalmoose)

Kenneth Pohl and Agor Eiskop founded Rentalmoose to understand how customers choose rentals—and where the friction really sits.

2019

First launch, first scepticism

We took the "digital rental" question to World Travel Market (WTM). The feedback was blunt: "Nice idea, but the industry won’t move." That scepticism became fuel.

WTM London Market validation phase
2020

Commitment to build

COVID hit—and we doubled down. We secured product development funding and started building our first hardware prototypes.

2021–2023

R&D under real constraints

Supply chain disruption slowed hardware development, but it also forced us to design for reliability, simplicity, and manufacturability.

2023

First major validation

We signed our first contract with a Europcar franchise—moving from theory into operational reality.

Europcar Franchise Signed
2024

Rebuilt for what the market truly needs

We released a new hardware generation and, more importantly, shaped Movitronic 2.0: the platform infrastructure that powers digital rentals, sharing and fleets.

2025

Platform launch + traction

We launched the new platform, completed 1,957 digital rentals, and signed operators including Europcar, Carwiz, Hertz, Nordic Travel Group, Flandur and SwipNgo.
