Planned Maintenance Window: Service Upgrade and Performance Improvements

We are scheduling a brief maintenance window to roll out infrastructure upgrades that will deliver faster response times and improved reliability. During this period, brief interruptions may occur for a subset of features.

The maintenance is designed to be minimally disruptive and is part of our ongoing efforts to enhance stability. We appreciate your understanding and patience as we make these improvements.

Schedule

  • Start: Tuesday, Oct 7, 2025 at 09:30 UTC
  • Expected duration: 45–60 minutes
  • Impact: Short intermittent downtime for dashboards and webhooks

What’s included

  • Database engine upgrade to the latest minor release
  • Improved caching for high-traffic endpoints
  • Security patches and dependency updates

What you need to do

  1. Plan critical operations outside of the maintenance window.
  2. Monitor your integrations for transient errors and retry as needed.
  3. Subscribe to status updates for real-time notifications.

We strive for zero-downtime deployments, and this upgrade prepares our systems for the next phase of growth.

If you have any questions or anticipate significant impact, please contact support so we can help mitigate disruptions to your workflow.
