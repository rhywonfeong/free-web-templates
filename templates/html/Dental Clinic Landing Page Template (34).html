About IG Dental

Precision Meets Compassion

Founded in 1995, IG Dental believes that a visit to the dentist should be a restorative experience. Led by Dr. Alexander Georgiev, our team combines decades of clinical excellence with a philosophy rooted in patient comfort.

We are located in the ideal center of Sofia, near "Pet te Kyosheta" (Five Corners), providing easy access to premium dental care.

Mission

To provide world-class dental care with a focus on hygiene and comfort.
