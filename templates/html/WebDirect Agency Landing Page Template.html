Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our custom website development process.

Why choose custom code over WordPress?
Custom code offers superior performance, security, and flexibility. Unlike WordPress, which relies on heavy plugins, our solutions are lightweight and tailored exactly to your needs.
How can you deliver so quickly?
We use a component-driven development approach and modern frameworks like Tailwind CSS that speed up the styling process significantly without sacrificing quality.
Is the Design Preview really free?
Yes! We believe in showing value first. We'll create a mockup of your homepage so you can see our vision before committing to the full project.
Do I own the code?
While the site runs on our infrastructure for optimal performance, you own your content and design with clear offboarding options.
