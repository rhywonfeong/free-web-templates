09 / FAQ

ASK BEFORE YOU RIG.

Clear answers on setup, suitability, travel and what to consider before your first session.

DOES IT HURT?

It shouldn't. A hang should feel like a long, dull stretch through the spine and a bit of pressure where the harness sits. Sharp pain, pins and needles or numbness means come down and reassess — the first session in the course covers exactly what a good hang feels like.

I'VE GOT A HERNIATED DISC. CAN I USE IT?

Ask your doctor or physio first, and show them the product. Traction is used clinically for some disc presentations and is a poor idea for others, and that call depends on your imaging and your symptoms, not on a website.

WHAT DO I ANCHOR IT TO?

A pull-up bar, a fixed beam, a rafter, or a solid door frame using the supplied strap. It needs to be structurally sound and able to take your full bodyweight plus a margin. Nothing gets drilled and nothing gets damaged.

HOW LONG BEFORE I FEEL SOMETHING?

Most people feel the release during the first hang. Whether it sticks is a question of consistency — the change comes from doing fifteen minutes daily for a few weeks, not from one long session.

HOW IS THIS DIFFERENT FROM JUST HANGING OFF A PULL-UP BAR?

Grip. Dead-hanging from a bar means your hands, forearms and shoulders fail long before your spine gets any meaningful time under traction, and it loads your shoulders in a position a lot of people can't hold. The harness moves the anchor point so you can stay there for fifteen minutes with your arms doing nothing.

ARE THERE HEIGHT OR WEIGHT LIMITS?

Rated to 150 kg. It fits anyone from roughly 150 cm to 200 cm. You need about 20 cm of clearance between your feet and the floor at full hang.

HOW LONG DOES SHIPPING TAKE?

Yes. Under a kilo, packs into the supplied case, goes in carry-on. It sets up on most hotel gym pull-up bars.

WHAT IF IT DOESN'T WORK FOR ME?

Sixty days. Use it properly, and if it isn't doing anything for you, send it back for a refund. You don't need to explain yourself.
