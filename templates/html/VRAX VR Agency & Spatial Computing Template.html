[ 07 ] FAQ

Frequently
Asked

Answers to common questions about engagement models, timelines, and platform targets.

What does VRAX build?
We design and ship headset-native experiences: gameplay systems, diegetic UI, spatial audio, haptics, and realtime performance tuning across standalone and PC VR.
Which platforms do you support?
Quest-class standalone, PC VR, and emerging spatial devices. We scope targets based on performance budgets (GPU/CPU), tracking constraints, and distribution requirements.
How long does a project take?
Typical engagements range from 2–4 weeks for a prototype sprint to 3–6 months for a production release, depending on content volume and platform matrix.
How do we start?
Send a brief to hello@vrax.studio . We’ll respond with a scope outline, timeline, and a production plan aligned to your release goals.
