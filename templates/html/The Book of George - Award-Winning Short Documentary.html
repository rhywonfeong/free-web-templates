The film

A LIFE CAN CHANGE WHEN SOMEONE SEES POSSIBILITY BEFORE YOU DO.

The Book of George follows a path that did not begin with a conventional map. George McKenzie Jr.’s journey from Bedford-Stuyvesant to wildlife fieldwork reveals how chance encounters, difficult choices, mentorship, creativity, and the willingness to keep looking can change the direction of a life.

The film asks a larger question: what becomes possible when a person learns to recognize the value of their own perspective?

Beyond the screen

THE FILM ENDS. THE CONVERSATION BEGINS.

The Book of George is designed to travel beyond the screen. Screenings, Q&A programs, photography experiences, and workshops use George’s story to open conversations about identity, mentorship, nature, creativity, work, and the turning points that shape a life.

George McKenzie Jr.

FROM BED-STUY TO THE EVERGLADES

Guyanese-born and Brooklyn-raised, George McKenzie Jr. built an unconventional path into wildlife photography and conservation storytelling. Today he is a National Geographic Explorer, documentarian, educator, and mentor whose work helps audiences see the overlooked connections between wildlife, place, access, and possibility.
