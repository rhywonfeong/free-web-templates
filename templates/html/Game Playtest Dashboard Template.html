1. 3D fighting test level setup

2. Spawn fighters and hook up controls

In DBZ_BattleArena_Test, set up a two‑player fighting scenario:

Use my main character Blueprint BP_GokuStyle as Player 1 and BP_VegetaStyle as Player 2.

Make sure Player 1 is possessed by the local player controller when I press Play.

Give Player 2 a simple AI that walks toward Player 1 and randomly uses light, heavy, and ki blast attacks.

Ensure WASD (or left stick), jump, light attack, heavy attack, ki blast, block, and dash inputs are mapped and working for Player 1.

3. Dragon Ball–style camera and UI for playtesting

Configure a third‑person fighting camera for DBZ_BattleArena_Test:

Always frame both fighters in view, zooming out slightly as they move apart and in as they get close.

Lock the camera to a side‑view style (slight angle is okay) so the fight is readable, like a 3D arena fighter.

Add a simple HUD: health bars for both fighters at the top, and a ki/energy bar at the bottom for each fighter.

Show round win text when one fighter's health hits zero, then automatically reset both fighters to their spawn positions after 3 seconds.
