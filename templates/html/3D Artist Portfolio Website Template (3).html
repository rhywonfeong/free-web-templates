About Me

Ishkhan Melkonyan

Prop Artist • 2 Years Exp.

I'm a passionate 3D Artist based in Armenia, focused on creating storytelling props that add depth to game environments. My journey began 2 years ago, driven by a fascination with how small details can make a digital world feel lived-in.

I specialize in hard-surface modeling and texturing, utilizing industry-standard tools like Maya and ZBrush . I'm constantly learning new workflows to push the quality of my assets.

When I'm not modeling, I'm analyzing game art direction or studying traditional manufacturing to better understand how things are built.
