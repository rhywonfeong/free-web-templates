Frequently Asked Questions

What chains does AERIE support?
AERIE is EVM-compatible by default. We support Ethereum, Polygon, Avalanche, Arbitrum, and Optimism. Custom RPCs can be configured for private subnets.
Is AERIE only for NFTs?
No. While we have robust NFT tooling, AERIE handles general treasury operations, smart contract deployment, and extensively supports Verifiable Credentials (VCs) for identity management.
What are Verifiable Credentials?
VCs are a W3C standard for digital identity. They allow you to issue tamper-proof proofs (like "Employee of Corp" or "Member of Club") that users hold in their wallets, enabling trust without centralized databases.
Can we integrate via API?
Yes. Our API Manager module allows you to generate keys and programmatically mint assets, verify credentials, or read on-chain data to use in your own applications.
