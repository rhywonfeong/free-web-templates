How do you ensure the agents don't sound like bots?
Orwell uses advanced LLMs fine-tuned on months of chat history specific to each community. They adopt the slang, sentence structure, and timing of the group. They make typos, use emojis correctly, and never post "marketing copy".
Is this safe for my brand reputation?
Absolutely. You approve the "narrative playbooks" before deployment. Agents are instructed to be helpful, not pushy. If sentiment turns negative in a chat, the agent disengages immediately to protect the brand.
Can I target specific competitor groups?
Yes. This is one of our most popular use cases. We can deploy agents into communities discussing your competitors to offer your product as a superior alternative when users express frustration.
What is the pricing model?
We operate on a monthly retainer basis based on the volume of communities managed and guaranteed mentions. Access starts at $2,500/mo.
