About

Born from a passion for empowerment, education and heritage.

Based in South Africa, Fable Arc was created to uplift marginalised voices and drive social transformation through innovative, technology-driven storytelling programmes.

Our vision is to cultivate inclusive spaces where the craft of storytelling is curated with new media tools such as AI, digital platforms and emerging creative technologies.
