The experience behind the coaching

About Me

I started delivering newspapers at 15 and started my first business at 20. I built that business over 27 years, growing a multi-service company with 25 employees.

I managed multiple teams, vans and office staff, and worked on home-services and civil-engineering projects. I understand the daily pressure of balancing jobs, clients, staff, cashflow, and the work that still needs doing at the end of the day.

There came a point when I had to ask myself: “Do I own this business, or does the business own me?”

After an accident in 2010, I had to change my approach. I began working on the business rather than only in it. That change helped the company grow by almost 300%.

Practical support, not theory

Why Bother With a Business Coach?

When you are busy delivering work, it is difficult to step back and see what is quietly costing you time, profit, and momentum.

Advertising may be bringing in the wrong enquiries. Quotes may not be converting. Staffing can feel like a constant challenge. Cashflow can be tight while payment sits unpaid. Long working hours become normal because every decision and every job still comes back to you.

A business coach gives you space to look at the business properly, identify what needs attention, and put practical improvements in place.

A straightforward framework

Circle of Business

The coaching framework consists of three stages.

01

Stage 1

Get the Basics Working Harder

Focus on marketing, sales, and services. Make small changes, build practical systems, and create better results from the fundamentals already in your business.

02

Stage 2

People, Processes & Profit

Strengthen your team, processes, and numbers. Use the 80/20 principle to identify high-value work and build repeatable systems around it.

03

Stage 3

From Technician to Business Owner

Build on day-to-day improvements, move away from being the main technician, and spend more time working on the business as its owner.
