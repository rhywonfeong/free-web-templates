Frequently Asked Questions

How does the credit repair process actually work?
We start with a free consultation where we review your credit report and identify all negative disputable items. From there, we build a custom plan, submit disputes to the bureaus on your behalf, and monitor your file with regular updates until completion.
How long does it take to see results?
Every situation is different, but most clients begin seeing changes within the first 30 to 45 days after disputes are submitted. We'll give you a realistic timeline during your consultation based on what's on your specific report.
Do I have to pay my collections before you can help?
No. In fact, we advise against paying collections without consulting us first. Once a third-party debt collector buys your debt, you have a legal right to challenge and dispute it. Paying it doesn't always help your score, and in some cases it can reset the clock on how long the item stays on your report.
What types of negative items can you remove?
We work with charge-offs, collections, late payments, bankruptcies, student loans, medical bills, and more. If it's negative and disputable, we'll address it.
Is the consultation really free?
Yes. There's no cost and no obligation. We'll review your report, tell you exactly what we can do, and you decide if you want to move forward. No pressure.
