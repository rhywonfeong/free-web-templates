Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from our Austin patients.

Do you accept my dental insurance?

Yes, we are insurance-friendly and in-network with most major PPO plans. You can use our quick verification form below, or call our front desk to confirm your specific coverage details.

How do Same-Day Crowns work?

Using advanced CEREC technology, we take a 3D digital scan of your tooth (no messy impressions). We then design and mill a custom, high-quality ceramic crown right in our office while you wait, allowing you to leave with a permanent restoration in a single visit.

What should I do in a dental emergency?

Call us immediately at (512) 555-0198. We leave blocks in our schedule for urgent needs like severe pain, knocked-out teeth, or broken restorations. If it's after hours, follow the prompts on our voicemail for emergency instructions.

Do you see children?

Absolutely! We are a family dentistry practice. We recommend scheduling a child's first visit around their first birthday or when their first tooth appears to establish a comfortable, lifelong relationship with oral care.
