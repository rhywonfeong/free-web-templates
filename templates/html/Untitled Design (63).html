What the
signal goes
through.

Everything is analogue until the encoder. The chain has not changed since 2023 and we publish it because people ask.

  1. Reel to reel, half inchStuder A80
  2. Passive summing16 into 2
  3. Valve compressor2:1, slow
  4. Tape delay sendSpace Echo
  5. Converter48 kHz, 24 bit
  6. EncoderAAC 320
