Safety & Ethics

We prioritize physical safety and data privacy. The Grid is a space for exploration, not risk.

Read Full Protocol
Safe Havens

All physical clues are located in permitted, safe venues (libraries, museums, cafes) or well-lit public areas. No trespassing required.

Accessibility First

Non-AR routes available for all spatial clues. Transcripts provided for audio puzzles. High-contrast visual modes supported.

Data Privacy

No real personal data required to play. Minimal tracking with clear opt-out paths. Geofencing disabled for minors without consent.

Moderation

Community forums and Discord channels are actively moderated. Zero tolerance for harassment.
