Operating Principles

Code is truth.

Figma files don't ship, code does. I sit with engineering early to understand technical constraints, prototype in the browser when necessary, and ensure the final shipped product matches the strategic intent without creating tech debt.

Embrace ambiguity.

The most valuable design work happens before a single wireframe is drawn. I thrive in the messy space of unstructured problems, facilitating alignment between stakeholders to define exactly what we are building and, more importantly, why.

Outcomes over output.

Shipping features is easy; changing behavior is hard. I measure success not by the volume of screens produced, but by the impact on core business metrics and the alleviation of real user friction.
