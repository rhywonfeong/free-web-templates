Questions & answers

Frequently asked questions

Here’s what patients and clinics most often want to know about MediAxis. You can adapt or wire this section to a real FAQ component.

Is MediAxis a clinic or a software platform?

MediAxis is a digital platform that connects patients to independent clinics and doctors. We don’t provide medical services directly—we help you find, book, and manage them securely.

How much does it cost to use the platform?

Creating an account and booking appointments is free for patients. Consultation fees are set by each clinic or doctor and shown transparently before you confirm.

Is my health information secure?

Yes. We use industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest, strict access controls, and regular security audits. Only you and authorized members of your care team can view your records.

Can I switch between online and in-clinic visits?

Many clinics support hybrid care. If your doctor allows it, you can convert follow-up visits to telehealth or move online consults into in-person appointments through your MediAxis account.
