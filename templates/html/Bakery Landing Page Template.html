Baked with integrity

Since 2010, we've been dedicated to the art of slow fermentation and traditional techniques. No shortcuts, just honest, time-tested baking methods.

Local Ingredients

Sourcing our flour from regional stone-mills to ensure peak freshness and support our local farming community.

Slow Fermentation

Our sourdoughs cold-ferment for up to 48 hours, developing complex flavors, perfect texture, and better digestability.

Hand Crafted

Every single loaf, croissant, and tart is shaped by hand by our skilled artisans, ensuring unique character and quality.
