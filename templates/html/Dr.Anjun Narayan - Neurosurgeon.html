Frequently Asked Questions

When should I see a neurosurgeon?
You should consult a neurosurgeon if you experience chronic back or neck pain, unexplained severe headaches, numbness, weakness in limbs, seizures, or if you have been diagnosed with a brain or spine abnormality via an MRI or CT scan.
Is minimally invasive spine surgery safer?
Yes. Minimally Invasive Spine Surgery (MISS) utilizes smaller incisions, causing less muscle damage, reduced blood loss, and lower infection risk. This translates to significantly faster recovery times and shorter hospital stays compared to traditional open surgery.
What is the recovery time after spine surgery?
Recovery time varies depending on the specific procedure. With minimally invasive techniques, many patients are walking the same or next day and return to light activities within 2-4 weeks. Full recovery for complex fusions may take a few months.
What are the risks of brain surgery?
While all surgeries carry risks such as infection or bleeding, modern technology like 3D neuro-navigation and intraoperative monitoring significantly mitigates these risks. Dr. Narayan will discuss your specific risk profile in detail during consultation.
Do you offer second opinions online?
Yes, we offer comprehensive online second opinions. You can upload your MRI/CT scans through our secure portal, and Dr. Narayan will conduct a detailed video consultation to discuss your diagnosis and treatment options.
