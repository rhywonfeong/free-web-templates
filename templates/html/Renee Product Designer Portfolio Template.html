About me

I am a product designer with a background in landscape architecture and a focus on building clear, intuitive digital products. Over the past few years, I've worked across e-commerce, SaaS, and ERP systems, combining design thinking with React implementation to bring ideas into production.

My approach is rooted in systems thinking, data, and collaboration. I start by understanding user behavior and business context, then translate complexity into structured flows and simple interfaces. By working closely with engineers and stakeholders, I ensure each product is both technically feasible and aligned with user needs.
