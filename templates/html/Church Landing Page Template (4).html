A Pastor Who Understands

He knows what it's like to be far from home. Life can take us to unexpected places, and sometimes in those transitions, we lose our spiritual grounding.

He started this church with a simple conviction: no one should have to walk their faith journey alone, regardless of their zip code. Whether you're traveling for work, living abroad, or simply haven't found a local congregation where you fit, New Royal Ministry was built for you.

He isn't a distant figurehead. He's the kind of pastor who will learn your name, notice when you're missing, and pray with you personally. Here, you aren't a viewer; you are family.
