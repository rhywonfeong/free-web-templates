Our founders

We are a group of founders who believe businesses should be built to last, serve people, and give back to society. Triajna was created to provide a permanent, values-aligned home for ideas, teams, and communities to grow responsibly over time. We are patient in how we build, disciplined in how we grow, and intentional in how we create impact. Our focus is not short-term outcomes, but long-term value for the people who do the work and the communities around them.

"We build for people, not just progress."

Are we a fit?

We are thoughtful about where we focus our time, energy, and care.

What resonates with us

  • Purpose-led businesses with durable foundations
  • Founders who value continuity, integrity, and shared growth
  • Leaders seeking autonomy with responsibility

Where we may not be the right partner

  • Ideas without a clear path to real-world impact
  • Situations requiring short-term fixes over long-term care
  • Growth driven solely by speed, not sustainability
