Find a Plumber

WaterSafe is a free, industry-backed online directory that helps you find reliable, registered plumbing professionals in your area. Choosing a qualified WaterSafe-approved plumber ensures high quality work that meets Water Fittings Regulations, protecting your drinking water from contamination and costly mistakes.

Hiring an unqualified plumber can lead to serious water safety issues and expensive repairs. WaterSafe-approved contractors are registered, professional and qualified.

How It Works

1. Select Service

Use our free online search tool to find the type of contractor or service you’re looking for.

2. Enter Postcode

Input your full postcode. For example, entering “NP11” will display results for that local area.

3. Choose Contractor

Browse the list of WaterSafe-approved contractors and select one that best suits your needs.

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of plumbing services are offered?
WaterSafe-approved plumbers offer expert services for homes and businesses, covering everything from routine maintenance to emergency repairs. For household and commercial plumbing, they handle leak detection, tap and toilet repairs, and full system installations.
Why hire a plumber instead of doing it yourself?
Hiring a WaterSafe-approved plumber ensures your plumbing is done safely, legally and to the highest standards. DIY work can risk water contamination and costly repairs.
What qualifications should I look for?
A qualified plumber should have at least an NVQ Level 2 in Mechanical Engineering Services (Plumbing). WaterSafe membership means they have been vetted and trained to meet strict standards.
How do I make sure I hire a reliable plumber?
Choose one registered with WaterSafe. This ensures they are qualified, insured and follow industry regulations. They provide a Certificate of Compliance for their work.
