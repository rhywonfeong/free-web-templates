Three ways to learn

Different concepts require different approaches. Sapling adapts its teaching style on the fly.

Socratic Mode

Guided questions that help you reason through problems instead of just giving you the answer. Builds deep intuition.

Expository Mode

Clear, direct explanations when you are completely stuck or encountering a concept for the very first time.

Teach-Back Mode

Explain concepts back to the AI in your own words to test true understanding and expose hidden gaps.
