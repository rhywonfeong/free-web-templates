Design a notification routing system for a DevOps platform.

System Design B2B SaaS 60 min

The Problem: Developers are suffering from alert fatigue. Critical production incidents are being missed amidst thousands of low-priority warnings.

The Constraint: You cannot simply "turn off" alerts. There must be a logic layer that routes specific severities to specific channels (Slack, PagerDuty, Email) based on time of day and on-call schedules.
