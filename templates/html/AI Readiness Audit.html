Dear Mike Brown Services Team,

Thank you for scheduling your AI Readiness Audit with us. We're looking forward to learning about your business and identifying opportunities where AI and automation can save you time, capture more revenue, and reduce the daily grind of running your operation.

This document explains what the audit involves, who we'll need to speak with, and what to have ready so we can make the most of our time together.

Preparation Checklist

To get the most out of our time together, please have the following items accessible on audit day. You do not need to prepare anything in advance — just know where to find these items.

Phone & Communications
Access to your current phone system so we can understand how calls are routed
Your after-hours setup (voicemail greeting, answering service info, or forwarding rules)
A rough idea of how many calls you receive per day/week
Sales & Estimating
A sample estimate or bid document (any recent one is fine)
We'll use this to understand your format and process — not to review your pricing.
A sense of how many estimates you send per month and your approximate win rate
Any templates, forms, or checklists your team uses for new customer intake
Scheduling & Operations
Your current scheduling method (calendar, whiteboard, app, spreadsheet, etc.)
How crew assignments are communicated each morning
Financial & Accounting
Access to your accounting software (QuickBooks, etc.)
Just to see the general setup, not your numbers.
A sample invoice
General awareness of your payment terms and collection timeline
Technology & Software
A list of all software and apps your business uses
Accounting, scheduling, project management, Procore, email, etc.
Login access to your website backend (Hibu dashboard) if available
Your Google Business Profile (know the login or who manages it)
Documents & Compliance
General idea of where your important documents are stored
Contracts, insurance certs, licenses, training records, etc.
Your current plumbing license number and renewal date, if handy
