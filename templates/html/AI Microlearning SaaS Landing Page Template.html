Journey Mapping: Job Seeker Flow

Friction Opportunity
01

Landing & Value Prop

User lands via "Data Science Jobs" keyword.

Confusion
Issue: Hero section addresses "Companies" first, confusing Job Seekers. CTA "Start Now" is vague.

Recommendation: Split landing page above fold. "I am hiring" vs "I am looking for a job". Personalize CTA to "Check my Skill Gap".

02

Sign Up & Profile Creation

User asked to upload resume immediately.

High Drop-off
Critical Friction: Resume parser fails often. 12-step form is too long before seeing value.

Fix: Move resume upload to *after* the first "Aha!" moment. Allow manual entry of just 3 skills to generate immediate job matches.

03

First Skill Assessment

User takes Python quiz.

Good Engagement
Users who complete the quiz have a 3x higher retention rate. However, the quiz results page is a dead end.
04

Premium Upgrade Trigger

User tries to apply for a job.

Revenue Opportunity
Problem: Paywall appears too early. User hasn't seen enough value yet.

Strategy: Offer "Verified Skill Badge" (free) but charge for "AI Resume Tailoring" (Premium). Shift monetization to the application phase, not the viewing phase.
