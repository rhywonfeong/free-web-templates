Philosophy

Space is the ultimate luxury. We shape it with purpose.

Founded on the principle of reduction, Aura Studio strips away the unnecessary to reveal the essential. We believe that a well-designed space should evoke calmness and clarity. Our approach is deeply personal, focusing on tactile materials, natural light, and bespoke detailing to create homes that are not just seen, but felt.

Methodology

How we bring visions to life.

01

Discovery

Deep dive into your lifestyle, aesthetic preferences, and the architectural potential of the site to establish a cohesive brief.

02

Concept

Developing spatial layouts, material palettes, and initial 3D visualizations to define the mood and direction of the project.

03

Detailing

Producing rigorous technical drawings, bespoke furniture designs, and finalizing procurement schedules for execution.

04

Realization

Overseeing construction, managing artisans, and conducting the final styling phase down to the last curated object.
