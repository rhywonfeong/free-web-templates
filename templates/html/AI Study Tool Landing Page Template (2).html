Your Answer

Question:

Explain how catalysts increase the rate of reaction. [6 marks]

Catalysts provide an alternative pathway for the reaction with a lower activation energy.

This means more particles have the required minimum energy to react upon collision.

As a result, there are more successful collisions per unit time, increasing the rate of reaction.
