Common Questions

Do you accept my insurance?

We are in-network with most major PPO providers including Delta Dental, Cigna, Aetna, and MetLife. Our team will handle all the paperwork for you to maximize your benefits.

How long does a smile makeover take?

It varies by case. A simple whitening is 1 hour. Porcelain veneers typically require 2 visits spaced 2 weeks apart. Invisalign cases average 6-12 months. We provide a timeline during your consultation.

Do you offer sedation dentistry?

Yes. We offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas) and oral conscious sedation for anxious patients. Your comfort is our top priority.
