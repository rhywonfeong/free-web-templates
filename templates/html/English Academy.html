Common Queries

How do I determine my current English level?
We offer a comprehensive 45-minute online placement test free of charge. This assesses your grammar, vocabulary, and reading comprehension. After the test, an academic advisor will call you to assess your speaking level.
Are the certificates recognized globally?
Yes. Our certificates align strictly with the CEFR (Common European Framework of Reference for Languages) standards, which is the international benchmark for language ability recognized by employers and universities worldwide.
Do you offer visa assistance for international students?
Absolutely. Our admissions office has a dedicated visa support team that guides you through the application process for Short-term Study Visas and Student Visas, providing all necessary Letter of Acceptance documentation.
