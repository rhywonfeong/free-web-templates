Repair, Replacement and Installation FAQs

30+ years in the HVAC business have taught us a lot, including the importance of sharing our knowledge and experience to answer your questions about HVAC service. Here are answers to some of the frequently asked questions about HVAC services: repair, replacement and installation.

Aire Serv offers HVAC repair service, routine maintenance, energy-efficiency upgrades, emergency services, and full HVAC installation and replacement solutions for residential and commercial properties.

HVAC repair service involves fixing specific issues, such as faulty parts or system malfunctions. A full HVAC replacement service is needed when a system is outdated, inefficient, or beyond repair.

If your system is under 10 years old and only has minor issues, an HVAC repair service may be enough. Older systems with frequent breakdowns may benefit more from a complete HVAC replacement service.

Most HVAC installation jobs can be completed in one day, but larger or more complex systems may take longer. Your local service professional will provide a detailed timeline.
