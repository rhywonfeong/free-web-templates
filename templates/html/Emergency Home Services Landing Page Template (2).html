Frequently asked questions

How quickly can a technician arrive?
Our average response time is under 60 minutes for emergency calls. For scheduled maintenance, you can pick a specific time slot that works for you.
Are your technicians licensed and insured?
Yes, absolutely. Every professional on the RapidFix platform undergoes a rigorous background check, skill assessment, and must provide proof of valid licensure and insurance.
How is pricing determined?
We use standard flat-rate pricing for common jobs, so you know the cost before work begins. For complex diagnostic work, we charge a fixed dispatch fee which is waived if you proceed with the repair.
