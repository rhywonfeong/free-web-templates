Brand Guidelines

Cultivating deep connections for the modern, discerning woman.

Soulful Love provides luxury education, supportive communities, and transformative retreats tailored for women aged 30-60. Our branding reflects elegance, maturity, warmth, and the profound depth of human connection.

Primary Logo

Letters only, utilizing extremely tight tracking to represent closeness, intimacy, and a sophisticated high-end editorial feel.

Clear Space

Maintain a minimum clear space equal to the height of the letter 'S' around the logo to ensure visibility and impact.

Minimum Size

The logo should never appear smaller than 120px in width on digital screens or 1.5 inches in print to maintain legibility.

Usage

Do not stretch, distort, or apply unapproved colors to the logo. Use only the provided master files.

Color Palette

A sophisticated red palette. Moving away from harsh primary reds towards luxurious wines, warm blushes, and grounded neutrals to appeal to a mature demographic.
