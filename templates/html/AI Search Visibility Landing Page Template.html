How We Build Your Presence Across AI Search

This is more than SEO. It is a multi-layered visibility strategy for the AI era.

Step 1

Audit Your Current AI Visibility

We review how your brand currently appears across search, AI generated answers, branded queries, content ecosystems, and trust based platforms. This helps us identify where you are strong, where you are missing, and where competitors are gaining ground.

Step 2

Expand Your Presence Across High Signal Platforms

We work to increase your visibility on the platforms LLMs and buyers pay attention to, including forum and discussion pages like Reddit and Quora, along with other third party sources that strengthen brand awareness, relevance, and authority.

Step 3

Publish Content That Strengthens AI Discovery

We create and optimize content that helps AI systems better understand what your company does, who it serves, and why it matters. This can include website content, supporting articles, structured topic pages, and YouTube videos with transcript-rich content.

Step 4

Strengthen Reviews, Reputation, and Trust Signals

We help improve the signals that build confidence around your brand, including company reviews, external mentions, credibility assets, and reputation factors that support stronger visibility in both traditional search and AI led discovery.

Step 5

Improve SEO Presence On-Page and Off-Page

A strong GEO strategy still depends on strong SEO foundations. We improve your on page structure, content quality, internal relevance, and technical clarity, while also strengthening off-page authority signals.

Step 6

Measure, Refine, and Expand

A lot goes into GEO, and it is not a one-time setup. We continuously refine the strategy, expand your brand footprint, strengthen authority signals, and improve the assets that increase your likelihood of being recommended inside AI answers.
