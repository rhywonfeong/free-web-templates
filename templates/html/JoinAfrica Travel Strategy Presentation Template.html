The best safaris don’t start with a place, they start with a guide.

Organic, ICP & Meta Strategy mapping for the new standard in African travel.

Flipping the Model

For too long, safari travel has been sold around lodges and locations. In reality, it’s the guide who determines what you see, how you experience it, and what you take away.

The right guide does not take over the safari, they unlock it and make it richer.

Key Angles & Hooks

  • Two people. Same safari. Two completely different stories.

    Dramatise the radical difference a guide makes.

  • Safari booking has been backwards.

    Challenge the entire industry norm.

  • The "False Sense of Control"

    People plan meticulously, but leave the most important variable to random chance upon arrival.

Content Strategy

The Four Pillars

01

The Reframe

Shift the mental model: "Start with the guide, not the place". Challenges current booking methods and builds belief.

Funnel Role: Top > Awareness & Belief
02

The "For You"

Make it personal. Emphasises how an unaligned guide ruins trips, demonstrating flexibility and customisation.

Funnel Role: Mid > Relevance & Desire
03

The Trust

Make guides feel real and desirable. Humanises the process showing expertise, personality, and emotional connection.

Funnel Role: Mid/Lower > Trust Buy-in
04

Testimonials

Reduce risk and validate the approach. Real outcomes that reinforce "I wish I knew this earlier".

Funnel Role: Lower > Conversion Confidence

Platform Execution

Confident, insightful, and quietly authoritative tone. Focus on human experience, helping travellers feel less overwhelmed while challenging their preconceptions.

Carousels

  • Stop scrolls with quick, simple insights.
  • One clear message per post to shift perspective.
  • E.g., "You're thinking about safari wrong".

Reels

  • Sell the feeling: effortless, ease, trust.
  • VO-led storytelling: uncertainty > guidance > solution.
  • Talking heads of guides sharing expertise.

Stories

  • Consistent, reliable presence. Social proof.
  • Interactive tools to drive site traffic.
  • Split-story visuals: JoinAfrica ease vs Traditional overwhelm.
Target Audience

Ideal Customer Profiles

Affluent First-Time Planners

Excited but don't know where to begin. US & UK markets are strongest for long-haul East Africa trips. Reaching those already considering a trip.

Interest-Led Travellers

Seeking a highly specific experience: photography, birding, conservation, luxury. Distinctive story rather than just "a safari".

Family & Multi-generational

High-consideration, high-value trips. Need extreme confidence in planning. JoinAfrica makes milestone trips feel simpler and trusted.

Paid Meta Strategy

  • Testing & Audience

    Start in US market. Test messages to see who moves down the funnel.

  • Creative Approach

    Keep it simple. Explain "Guide First" vs traditional.

  • Success Metrics

    Measure deep intent: guide views and match journey starts, not just traffic.

  • Retargeting

    Re-engage interested users with social proof and a push to start matching.
