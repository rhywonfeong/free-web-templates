Our Legacy: Trusted Insurance Advisory Since 1997

Karen Direct Insurance Brokers Ltd was founded in 1997 in Karen, Nairobi, with a clear purpose: to offer custom-made insurance solutions that truly reflect each client’s needs. From our earliest days, we have focused on understanding our clients’ businesses, families, and ambitions before recommending any cover.

Insurance Products & Services

We provide a comprehensive suite of insurance solutions designed for both businesses and individuals. Each programme is custom-made based on your risk profile, industry, and objectives.

Health Insurance

Medical cover for individuals, families, and employee groups, ensuring access to quality healthcare when it matters most.

  • • Flexible options for outpatient, inpatient, maternity, and dental.
  • • Network-based and reimbursement options.
  • • Tailored benefit limits aligned with your budget.

Fire & Peril

Covers loss or damage to buildings and contents caused by fire, lightning, explosions, and related perils.

  • • Protection for commercial and residential premises.
  • • Optional extensions for riots, strikes, and malicious damage.
  • • Can be integrated into a broader property programme.

Aviation Insurance

Specialist cover for aircraft hull, liability, and related aviation exposures.

  • • Protection for aircraft operators and owners.
  • • Tailored liability limits for passengers and third parties.
  • • Structured with leading aviation insurers and reinsurers.

Pet Insurance

Medical and liability protection for your pets, supporting responsible pet ownership.

  • • Cover for veterinary expenses arising from illness or accident.
  • • Optional third-party liability cover.
  • • Flexible plans for different breeds and ages.

Contractors All Risks Insurance

Comprehensive cover for construction projects, protecting works, materials, and third-party liability.

  • • Covers damage to contract works and materials on site.
  • • Includes third-party property damage and bodily injury.
  • • Suitable for contractors, developers, and project owners.

Group Personal Accident

Financial protection for employees against accidental death or disability, providing peace of mind to both staff and employers.

  • • Lump-sum benefits for death and permanent disability.
  • • Optional medical expense extensions.
  • • Can be integrated with WIBA and medical plans.

Domestic Package

Combined cover for your home building, contents, domestic employees, and personal liability.

  • • Fire, theft, and accidental damage to your home and contents.
  • • Cover for domestic workers as required by law.
  • • Personal liability for incidents occurring at the residence.

All Risks

Broad cover for specified items such as electronics, jewellery, and portable equipment against a wide range of risks.

  • • Worldwide protection for listed items.
  • • Ideal for laptops, cameras, and valuables.
  • • Flexible sums insured and deductibles.

Public Liability

Protects your business against legal liability for third-party injury or property damage arising from your operations.

  • • Covers legal defence costs and compensation awards.
  • • Suitable for offices, retailers, manufacturers, and events.
  • • Limits tailored to your risk exposure.

Fidelity Guarantee

Protects your organisation against financial loss arising from dishonest acts by employees.

  • • Cover for theft of money, stock, or assets by staff.
  • • Can be arranged on named or blanket employee basis.
  • • Supports internal control and risk management frameworks.

Work Injury Benefits Act (WIBA)

Mandatory cover for employees against injuries, disabilities, or death arising out of and in the course of employment.

  • • Compliant with Kenyan labour legislation.
  • • Provides benefits for medical expenses and compensation.
  • • Often combined with Group Personal Accident for enhanced protection.

Goods In Transit

Covers loss or damage to goods while being transported by road within Kenya and neighbouring territories.

  • • Suitable for manufacturers, distributors, and transporters.
  • • Cover for theft, collision, overturning, and accidental damage.
  • • Options for single transit or annual declarations.

Marine Insurance

Protection for goods transported by sea, air, or inland waterways, from port of origin to final destination.

  • • Covers loss or damage due to marine perils and handling.
  • • Suitable for importers, exporters, and logistics firms.
  • • Single voyage and open cover arrangements available.

Motor Commercial

Motor insurance for commercial vehicles such as trucks, vans, and fleets used for business purposes.

  • • Third-party only, third-party fire & theft, or comprehensive options.
  • • Fleet and single-vehicle arrangements.
  • • Extensions for windscreens, accessories, and special perils.

Professional Indemnity

Protects professionals against claims arising from errors, omissions, or negligence in the course of their professional duties.

  • • Suitable for lawyers, doctors, consultants, and other professionals.
  • • Covers defence costs and compensation awards.
  • • Limits and deductibles tailored to your practice size and risk.

Business Interruption

Covers loss of income and additional expenses when insured events disrupt your operations.

  • • Works alongside property/fire policies.
  • • Helps maintain cash flow during downtime.
  • • Customisable indemnity periods and limits.

Loan Guard

Protects lenders and borrowers by covering outstanding loan balances in the event of death or disability.

  • • Peace of mind for both financial institutions and borrowers.
  • • Can be integrated with credit facilities and mortgages.
  • • Flexible benefit structures and terms.

Motor Private Insurance

Motor cover for privately used vehicles, offering financial protection against accidents, theft, and third-party liabilities.

  • • Comprehensive, third-party only, and third-party fire & theft options.
  • • Optional covers for windscreens, radios, and special perils.
  • • No-claim discounts for safe drivers.

Endowment Policies

Long-term savings and protection plans that pay a lump sum on maturity or in the event of death during the policy term.

  • • Combines life cover with disciplined savings.
  • • Suitable for education planning and long-term goals.
  • • Flexible premium payment options.
