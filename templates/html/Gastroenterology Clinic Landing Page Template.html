Article I
Real Property Assets

The Grantor confirms that the real property commonly known as 1240 Bel Air Road, Los Angeles, CA 90077 (Assessor's Parcel No. 4369-021-015) has been duly conveyed to this Trust by grant deed recorded with the Los Angeles County Recorder on October 14, 2025, as Instrument No. 20251014-0442991. The Trustee is authorized to manage, lease, encumber, or sell said property in accordance with the terms of this Trust and CA Probate Code § 16220.

*Title vesting confirmed. LA County Recorder search completed January 12, 2026. No liens noted. CA R&T Code § 62(d) exclusion filed.

Article II
Assignment of Business Interests

The Grantor hereby assigns, transfers, and conveys to the Trustee one hundred percent (100%) of the membership interest in Harrington Capital Ventures LLC, a California limited liability company (CA SOS File No. 202560415891), to be held as part of the Trust Estate. The Trustee is authorized to exercise all management rights, receive distributions, and execute documents to effectuate this transfer, pursuant to CA Corp. Code § 17704.01.

*CA SOS confirmation pending — provisional pending state acknowledgment. Operating agreement amendment required.

Article III
Federal Estate Tax & Generation-Skipping Provisions

This Trust is structured to qualify for the federal estate tax marital deduction under IRC § 2056. The Successor Trustee shall, upon the Grantor's death, divide the Trust Estate into a Survivor's Trust, a Bypass Trust (Credit Shelter Trust), and, if applicable, a Marital QTIP Trust, to maximize the applicable exclusion amount under IRC § 2010 — currently $13,610,000 per person (2026).

Any generation-skipping transfer tax exposure arising under IRC § 2601 et seq. shall be reviewed by the Successor Trustee and reported on IRS Form 706-GS(D). The Grantor's unused GST exemption shall be allocated per the Trustee's election on IRS Form 709.

Article IV
Successor Trustee Powers

The Successor Trustee shall have all powers granted to trustees under CA Probate Code §§ 16200–16249, including the power to invest Trust assets under the Uniform Prudent Investor Act (CA Prob. Code § 16045 et seq.); to retain or sell any Trust asset; to borrow funds on behalf of the Trust; and to make distributions to Beneficiaries in cash or in kind at the Trustee's discretion.

Article V
Spendthrift Protection

No interest of any beneficiary in the income or principal of this Trust shall be subject to assignment, anticipation, pledge, attachment, execution, or bankruptcy proceedings before actual receipt by such beneficiary. This spendthrift protection is absolute and shall not be waived by any beneficiary, pursuant to CA Probate Code § 15300.

Article VI
No-Contest Clause

Any beneficiary who directly or indirectly contests the validity of this Trust, or any provision thereof, or who opposes, challenges, or seeks to impair any of its provisions, shall forfeit any and all interest they would otherwise receive under this Trust, pursuant to CA Probate Code § 21310 et seq.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the Grantor has executed this Trust Agreement on the date first written above, and the Successor Trustee has accepted the trusts herein created.
