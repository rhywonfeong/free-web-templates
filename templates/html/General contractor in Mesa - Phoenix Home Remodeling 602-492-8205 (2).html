Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a general contractor in Mesa charge for a kitchen remodel?

Depending on the scope, a high-quality kitchen remodel in Mesa typically starts around $40,000 for a smaller space and can go upwards of $100,000 for luxury transformations involving custom cabinetry and structural layout changes.

How long does a typical bathroom remodel take?

A standard primary bathroom remodel takes about 4 to 6 weeks once construction begins. A smaller guest bathroom or a simple bathtub-to-shower conversion generally takes about 2 to 4 weeks. This timeline assumes all materials are pre-ordered and on-site before demolition starts.

No, you do not. With a design-build firm like Phoenix Home Remodeling, you collaborate closely with our in-house designer. They will help you select all finishes, design the layout, and generate 3D renderings, keeping the entire process unified under one roof.

Why should I choose a design-build contractor over a standard builder?

A design-build contractor handles both the planning and construction phases seamlessly. This model eliminates finger-pointing between separate designers and builders, reduces costly change orders, and allows for a finalized, fixed price before construction begins.
