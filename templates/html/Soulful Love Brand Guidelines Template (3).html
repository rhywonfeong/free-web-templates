Usage Guidelines

The primary logotype should be used in the majority of applications. It relies solely on elegant typography and tight tracking to convey luxury. Maintain ample negative space around the mark.

The Monogram

The 'SL' monogram is reserved for compact spaces, social media avatars, and subtle brand accents (like custom retreat stationery or wax seals).
