02. TUTORIAL

Protocol Architecture.

A four-step decentralized execution layer designed for high-frequency prediction markets and instant settlement.

STEP 01

Market Discovery

Filter real-time events across Crypto, Macro, and Sports sectors using on-chain data feeds.

> fetch_markets(active=true)
STEP 02

Connect & Deposit

Link your non-custodial wallet and deposit USDC/ETH into the smart vault for instant liquidity.

> approve_token(USDC, amount)
STEP 03

Execute Trades

Place limit or market orders on the CLOB. Long or short outcomes with full order book transparency.

> submit_order(side='YES', price)
STEP 04

Settlement

Smart contracts automatically resolve markets via oracle feeds. Claim winnings instantly.

> claim_payout(market_id)
