Frequently Asked Questions

How does Earn generate returns?
Our Earn protocol utilizes automated market making strategies and over-collateralized lending pools to generate consistent yield without directional market exposure.
What does "up to 18% APR" mean?
APR is dynamic and based on utilization rates of the liquidity pools. Historically, yields have ranged between 12-18% for stablecoin pairs.
What does 50% LTV mean?
Loan-to-Value (LTV) is the ratio of your loan amount to the value of your collateral. At 50% LTV, if you deposit $10,000 in Bitcoin, you can borrow up to $5,000.
What are the risks involved?
Smart contract risk and liquidation risk are the primary factors. We mitigate these through multiple audits and conservative LTV parameters.
