The AbstractApex Engine

1. Define Action Intent

Describe what needs to happen, not how. Submit a high-level intent JSON that is chain and wallet agnostic.

2. Negotiate Capabilities

The engine probes the user's address. Is it an EOA? Does it support EIP-7702? Are 7579 modules installed?

3. Execute & Upgrade

Transactions are compiled to the most gas-efficient format. Accounts are automatically upgraded if policies require it.
