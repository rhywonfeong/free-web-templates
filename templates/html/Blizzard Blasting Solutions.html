The Science

The Power of
Sublimation.

Dry ice blasting combines three distinct physical events to create a superior clean without abrasion.

Start your project
01

Kinetic Impact

Dry ice pellets are accelerated to supersonic speeds using compressed air. Upon impact, the kinetic energy transfers to the contaminant, loosening it from the surface.

02

Thermal Shock

At -78.5°C, the dry ice hits the contaminant. The extreme temperature difference causes the dirt layer to freeze and contract instantly, breaking the adhesive bond with the substrate.

03

Rapid Expansion

Upon impact, the solid pellets sublimate (turn to gas), expanding 700 times in volume in milliseconds. This mini-explosion lifts the contaminant away from the surface completely.

The process of aggressive surface preparation, which involves using steel grit or glass beads in an 80/20 mix with dry ice, is ideal for removing heavy rust, thick paint layers, and creating a surface profile.
