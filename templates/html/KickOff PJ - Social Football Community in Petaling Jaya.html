Our Story: Why We Started

KickOff started with a simple belief; that social football should be safe, affordable, and fun for all.

After years of being in the Malaysian social football scene, we noticed a concerning pattern. Violent players were continuously invited to sessions, prices kept rising, and somewhere along the way, casual football groups started feeling less and less like a community.

We thus decided to build the kind of football community we'd want to be part of. A place where people from all backgrounds could come together, play some fun, clean football, and leave looking forward to the next session.

Today, KickOff is home to more than 1,000 football enthusiasts across the Klang Valley, united by a shared love for the beautiful game.

Frequently Asked Questions About KickOff

New to KickOff? No worries. Here's everything you need to know before stepping onto the pitch.

What is KickOff?

Kick-Off is a social football community in Petaling Jaya, Shah Alam, and the Klang Valley, created for players who love the game and want to play regularly in a fun, welcoming environment. We organise weekly football and futsal sessions, including 5v5 futsal, 7v7, and 8v8 football games, for players of all skill levels. Whether you're a regular player, getting back into football, or trying it for the first time, Kick-Off is a place where you can enjoy the game, meet new people, and be part of a growing football community.

How do I join a KickOff session?

Joining a KickOff session is simple. First, send us a message on WhatsApp / IG and let us know which session you're interested in! Once you find a session that works for you, transfer payment via the multiple payment methods we offer to book your slot. Once confirmed, join the WhatsApp group to receive updates and session reminders. Finally, bring your footwear and drinks, and turn up early on the day. We'll take care of the rest!

Can I join alone, or do I need to bring my own team?

You don't need to bring a team—most of our players join on their own! KickOff is designed for anyone who wants to play regular football without the hassle of organising a full squad. Simply sign up, and we'll group everyone into balanced teams before each session, making it easy to meet new people and enjoy a great game from your very first visit.

Already have a team or a group of friends? They're welcome too! You can join together, and we'll do our best to accommodate your group while keeping matches balanced and enjoyable for everyone. To make things even better, both you and your friends will receive RM1 off your session fee when signing up together due to our group booking discount!

Are beginners welcome to play?

Absolutely! If you're willing to learn, have fun, and respect others on the pitch, you'll always be welcome. We balance our teams so beginners feel fully supported while experienced players still get a great sweat. We enforce a strict no-violence policy.

What's the session format like?

Our sessions are designed to be social, fun, and enjoyable while still giving everyone friendly competition. The exact format depends on the session.

For sessions with 3 teams, games are played in a rotating format: A vs B, B vs C, and A vs C - from the start of the session until the end. To keep things fair, goalkeeping duties are shared through a rotating goalkeeper system. This gives everyone plenty of game time and keeps the sessions moving.

What's the skill level like?

Kick-Off welcomes players of all skill levels, from complete beginners to experienced footballers. Our sessions are social, friendly, and inclusive, and we do our best to balance the teams so everyone gets a good game.

While everyone enjoys a bit of healthy competition, Kick-Off isn't a competitive league. We encourage fair play, respect, and good sportsmanship on and off the pitch. Whether you're here to improve your game, stay active, or simply have fun with friends, you'll fit right in.
