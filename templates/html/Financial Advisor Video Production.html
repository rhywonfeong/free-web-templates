Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be good on camera?

No. Our interview format is designed to pull out your best thinking in a natural way—clear, confident, and conversational.

Do you write scripts?

Most advisors perform better without memorizing scripts. We use prompts and structure. Creative Strategy packages include deeper planning.

Can this fit our compliance process?

Yes. We can align deliverables to your firm’s workflow. Final compliance review and approval remains the responsibility of the advisor/firm.

What platforms is this for?

Primarily YouTube. We can also format additional cuts for LinkedIn, Instagram, or TikTok if you want.
