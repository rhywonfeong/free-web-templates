25MPa Driveway Mix

A high-durability concrete blend engineered specifically for residential driveways and light vehicular traffic areas.

Product Overview

Our 25MPa Driveway Mix is formulated to resist abrasion and support the weight of passenger vehicles without cracking under normal load conditions. Unlike standard 20MPa blends, this mix includes a higher cement ratio for increased surface hardness, making it the ideal choice for exposed aggregate finishes or stenciled concrete.

Recommended Applications

Residential Driveways

Standard residential vehicle access.

Footpaths & Patios

High traffic pedestrian areas.

Shed Slabs

Foundations for light storage structures.

Carports

Covered parking areas.
