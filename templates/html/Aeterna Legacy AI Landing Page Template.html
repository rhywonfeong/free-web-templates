The Problem

What gets lost after we’re gone

Wills preserve assets, not presence. Most families have thousands of photos but very few hours of meaningful recorded conversation. When people perform for the camera, authenticity disappears.

The Solution

A legacy that feels like a conversation

Combine structured storytelling with gentle guided reflection. Aeterna aims to build an interactive archive that holds not just facts, but cadence, humor, and emotional context.
