Apps for Contributors

Beginners

Foap

Designed specifically for mobile photography. Upload directly from your camera roll and participate in brand "Missions".

Missions: Brands like Nivea post briefs; winners earn $50–$200+.

50/50 Split: Standard photos sell for $10, you keep $5.

Verdict

The easiest entry point for casual smartphone photographers.

Consistent Income

Adobe Stock

Integrates directly with Photoshop and Lightroom, putting your work in front of millions of professional designers.

Smart Tagging: AI suggests keywords automatically to save time.

33% Commission: High volume of professional buyers creates steady income.

Verdict

Best for high-quality, edited images and workflow integration.

Volume

Shutterstock

The largest customer base globally. While per-image earnings start low, the sheer volume of buyers is unmatched.

Tiered Earnings: Starts at 15%, increases up to 40% as you sell more.

Volume Game: Ideal for large portfolios (hundreds of photos).

Verdict

Essential if you have a massive library of content.

Artistic

500px

A community-first platform. "Pulse" algorithm helps new photographers get discovered based on quality, not just follower count.

High Royalties: Up to 60% commission for exclusive content.

Feedback Loop: Receive critiques and validation from other pros.

Verdict

Best for moody portraits, landscapes, and "fine art" photography.

Apps for Buyers

Shutterstock & Adobe

For Standard Needs

Best for designers needing high-quality, standard "stock" imagery like clean backgrounds, business settings, or specific objects.

Subscription / Credit Packs

EyeEm

For Authenticity

Recently acquired by Freepik. Ideal for buyers who dislike the "fake stock" look. Photos resemble high-quality, authentic social media content.

Authentic Mobile Look

Unsplash / Pexels

For Budgets

Excellent for personal projects or MVPs. Smaller selection than paid sites, but completely free to download.
