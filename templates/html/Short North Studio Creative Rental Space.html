Background

Designed for the creator , built for the professional .

Born from the need for a truly adaptable creative space in Columbus. We stripped away the unnecessary to provide an environment where light, acoustics, and layout serve your vision—not dictate it.

Whether you're shooting a campaign, recording a podcast, or hosting a workshop, the environment adapts seamlessly to your workflow.

Features

Built for flexibility.

Every element of Short North Studio has been thoughtfully designed to remove friction from your creative process.

Sound & Light

Exposed ceiling grids to manage sound. Directional and diffused lighting setups available on request to set the perfect mood for shoots or intimate events.

Modular Furniture

Movable pieces that can be arranged for a podcast set, a lounge party, or cleared entirely for a blank slate.

Equipment Ready

High-speed gigabit Wi-Fi ready for your team.

Heart of the Short North
