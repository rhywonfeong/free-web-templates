Financial Services

Legacy Core to AWS in 90 Days

A mid-market lender migrated its on-premise core systems to AWS with zero unplanned downtime, cutting infrastructure costs by 34%.

Read the full story
Client: A mid-market community lender running a 15-year-old on-premise core banking and loan-origination stack nearing end-of-support, with no disaster recovery site.
Challenge: Core banking and loan-servicing systems needed to move to AWS without disrupting active loan processing or creating a compliance gap during the transition.
Approach: Engagement opened with a Cloud Readiness Assessment mapping every core-banking dependency and third-party integration, producing a phased cutover plan sequenced around the lender's monthly close calendar. The Build & Test phase stood up a parallel AWS landing zone validated against a strict go/no-go checklist before a phased weekend-window cutover.
Result: Zero unplanned downtime during cutover, infrastructure costs cut 34% within two quarters, a documented DR posture the lender never had on-premise, and a 90-day timeline from kickoff to Operate handoff. The engagement transitioned into an ongoing Managed Works retainer after go-live.
Logistics

Failover Design That Held Up

Resiliency-first architecture kept a national logistics platform online through a regional outage, with recovery measured in minutes—not hours.

Read the full story
Client: A national logistics and freight-brokerage platform running real-time load-tracking and dispatch systems on a single-region architecture.
Challenge: A prior regional outage had taken the dispatch platform offline for hours, disrupting live shipments and triggering carrier SLA penalties.
Approach: Senior engineers redesigned the environment around multi-region failover, replicating the tracking database and dispatch services across two regions with automated health-check-triggered failover, then stayed on as the client's Engineering-as-a-Service team.
Result: During a later regional outage, failover completed in minutes instead of hours, with zero missed carrier SLAs — because the same architects who designed the failover logic were the ones responding to the alert.
Healthcare

Azure Migration Under Compliance

A regional provider moved patient-facing workloads to Azure while maintaining strict HIPAA compliance throughout every migration phase.

Read the full story
Client: A regional healthcare provider group migrating patient-facing scheduling workloads to Azure.
Challenge: The move had to maintain strict HIPAA compliance throughout, with zero tolerance for exposure gaps, while the provider's internal IT team was too thin to own the migration alongside daily operations.
Approach: Discovery catalogued every workload's PHI touchpoints against Azure's compliance-eligible services before any migration plan was finalized. The Build & Test go/no-go gate included a dedicated compliance review alongside the standard technical checklist, with rollout phased by department to keep clinical staff training manageable.
