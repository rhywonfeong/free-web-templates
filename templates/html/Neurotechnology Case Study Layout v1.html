Overview

Roga is an AI-powered neurotechnology platform combining wearable devices, mobile and web apps, and an online store designed to help users manage stress and anxiety. As engagement dropped significantly, I led a full redesign of the experience to reduce user friction, improve usability, and increase adoption across all touchpoints.

Problem / Challenge

  • Users found onboarding overwhelming and confusing, with fragmented flows that lacked personalization.
  • Previous research insights weren’t implemented, leaving critical UX gaps in the experience.
  • Engagement dropped as users struggled to connect devices and discover relevant content.
  • The business needed stronger engagement growth to drive adoption, retention, and revenue.

Solution (Process + Features)

Using a user-centered design approach, we streamlined the experience while ensuring each feature directly solved a pain point.

1. Research & Insights

Conducted stakeholder interviews, heuristic evaluations, and rapid UX research.
Analyzed previous usability studies and user interviews to validate findings.
Mapped user frustrations across onboarding, personalization, and device setup.
Identified gaps between existing research and actual product implementation.
Performed market research to understand competitors and positioning.

2. Key Design Features :

Simplified Onboarding Flow

Problem: Users dropped off early due to overwhelming onboarding.

Solution: Enabled guest mode, optional login, and stress-free personalization steps.

AI-Powered Stress Assessment (GAD-7)

Problem: Users struggled to interpret clinical results.

Solution: Introduced AI-generated interpretations, personalized recommendations, and session guidance → reduced stress and confusion.

Home & Contents

Problem: Engagement plateaued due to irrelevant content.

Solution: Personalized stress reports and AI-curated content; introduced smooth session player with journaling → increased retention.

Device Setup & Quick AI Sessions

Problem: Complex setup frustrated first-time users.

Solution: Step-by-step device setup and instant AI sessions with feedback → improved adoption.

Profile & Dashboard

Problem: Users lacked visibility into progress, reducing motivation.

Solution: Dashboard with performance analytics, mood tracking, badges, and history → boosted engagement and achievement-driven use.

Design System & Cross-Platform Consistency

Problem: Inconsistent visual language across apps and the store weakened the brand and disrupted the user journey.

Solution: Built a scalable design library to unify typography, colors, components, and interactions across mobile, desktop, and web. This ensured a consistent brand presence and smoother cross-platform experiences.

Website & Store Redesign

Problem: As traffic came from multiple touchpoints, the website/store flow felt disconnected from the product experience.

Solution: Redesigned the website and online store to align with the new design system, ensuring continuity in brand, usability, and conversion paths.

Challenges During Design

  • Time & Budget Constraints: Adopted rapid research methods using existing data.
  • Conflicting Research: Needed to merge old unimplemented findings with new insights.
  • Balancing Business vs. User Needs: Ensuring revenue growth features (subscriptions) didn’t conflict with stress-free experience.

Outcome / Impact

  • 64% increase in engagement.
  • 91% user satisfaction in usability testing.
  • 67% reduction in reported stress during sessions.
  • 34% increase in adoption, 68% retention, and 35% increase in conversion.
  • Positive user testimonials validated improvements in usability and overall experience.
