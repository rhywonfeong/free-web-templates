Our Practice

Patient-Centred Care in the Heart of Stafford

Welcome to mydentist Stafford. We love to help our patients smile, so the core of our work is our patient-centred approach.

We have facilities and dedicated clinicians to provide high-quality dental care, offering a full range of NHS and private dental services. We are a well-established practice with 5 clinicians and a hygienist providing care 6 days a week.

Based in the busy town centre on Eastgate Street, we're just a 1 minute walk from the bus stop and a 9 minute walk from Stafford train station.
