Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I sealcoat my pavement?
Generally, sealcoating is recommended every 2–3 years, depending on traffic, weather, and overall wear.
How long does it take to dry?
Typically, it takes 24 to 48 hours to dry completely. We recommend keeping vehicles off the surface during that time.
Can you repair cracks before sealcoating?
Yes! We offer crack filling and asphalt repair as part of our prep process to ensure a smooth, long-lasting finish.
Do you offer free estimates?
Yes! We offer free, no-obligation estimates for both residential and commercial projects.
