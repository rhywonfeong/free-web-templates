About Imperial Contracting

Imperial Contracting is a highly respected exterior specialist proudly serving Rantoul and the surrounding Central Illinois region since 2018. Led by Angie P. Rodriguez, our company was founded on a simple principle: deliver an "Imperial" standard of quality on every single job.

We focus heavily on utilizing durable, premium materials combined with uncompromising craftsmanship. We know that home improvements and storm damage repairs can be stressful.
