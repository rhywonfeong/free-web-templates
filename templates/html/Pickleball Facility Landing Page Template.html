Common Questions

Do I need to bring my own paddle?

No! We offer high-quality paddle rentals at the front desk. If you fall in love with the game, we also sell top brands in our pro shop.

What are the rates?

Community courts start from RM40/hour. Our exclusive Private Lounge courts start from RM70/hour. Rates vary by location and time.

Do I need to book in advance?

We highly recommend booking in advance, especially during peak evening hours and weekends, as slots fill up quickly.
