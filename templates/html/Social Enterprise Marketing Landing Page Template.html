Got questions?

Everything you need to know about the product and billing.

Who is Himayat Digital for?
Our packages are built specifically for local, location-based businesses that sell a physical product or service locally. If you're a small business owner trying to handle digital marketing on your own, or looking for an agency that understands diverse communities, we are built for you.
How is Himayat different from a regular digital agency?
Two ways: First, we combine powerful enterprise-grade AI tools with real, accessible community support (webinars, peer groups, events). Second, we are a Social Traders Verified Social Enterprise. Our commercial revenue funds employment pathways and digital inclusion programs for people facing systemic barriers. You get premium marketing; the community gets opportunity.
Can I upgrade my package later?
Absolutely. We designed our tiers so you can scale naturally. Start with Foundations or Growth to build your base, and upgrade to Accelerate or our fully Managed services when you're ready to hand over the keys.
What happens in the first month?
For self-service tiers, you'll receive immediate access to the platform, a sequence of onboarding videos to get your accounts connected, and invitations to our community groups and next live webinar. For Managed tiers, we kick off with a deep-dive strategy session and begin executing your setup deliverables (like website builds or logo refreshes).
What are Managed Service Packages?
Managed Services (Pro and Elite) are for business owners who want a completely hands-off approach. Instead of using the software yourself, our expert team runs your entire digital strategy—from creating social posts and managing reviews to running local SEO and Google Ads—so you can focus entirely on running your business.
