Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions from LA homeowners.

How long does a metal roof install take?

Most homes take 2–4 days on site once materials arrive. Weather and complexity can add time.

Will a metal roof make my home hotter?

No. With cool‑roof finishes and proper ventilation, metal roofs help reduce attic temps and AC use.

Are they noisy in the rain?

Installed over underlayment and decking, they’re comparable to asphalt roofs for sound.

What warranties do you offer?

Typically 25–50 years on materials and 10 years on workmanship. Details vary by product.

Do you handle permits and HOA approvals?

Yes. We prepare drawings, submit, and track approvals so you don’t have to.

Can you install over my existing roof?

Sometimes. We’ll assess structure, weight, and code before recommending overlay or tear‑off.
