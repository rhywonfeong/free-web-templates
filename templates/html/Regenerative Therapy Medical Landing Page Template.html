Where CPRT started

It began in a boxing gym in Irmo, South Carolina.

Terry runs Boxing Gym, where fighters living with Parkinson's disease throw punches at heavy bags to fight back against a disease that steals movement.Boxing-style training keeps people standing, moving and connected.

Over and over, his boxers came in with the same story: they'd found research on stem cell therapy that could change their lives — and quotes they'd never afford. Life-changing medicine, priced out of reach for the people who needed it most.

CPRT exists to close that gap with best-in-class medications, certified physicians, a real hospital and full travel coordination — bundled into one honest package.

FAQ

Questions patients ask us most

What exactly are MSC stem cells?
Mesenchymal stem cells are multipotent adult stem cells sourced from ethically donated, full-term umbilical cord tissue (Wharton's jelly) — not embryos. They regulate immune activity, reduce inflammation and signal your own tissue to repair.
Is stem cell therapy safe?
Every protocol is administered under physician supervision in a licensed hospital with full imaging, lab and emergency support on-site.
How many cells will I receive?
Between 100 and 250 million viable cells per dose depending on your protocol, verified by third-party lab testing.
How is it administered?
Via intra-articular injection, systemic IV infusion, or intranasally toward the central nervous system, depending on diagnosis.
Why is treatment performed in Mexico?
Regulatory frameworks allow higher cell doses and broader protocols than are currently available domestically, delivered in a licensed hospital just across the border.
How does the deposit work?
A non-refundable deposit reserves your protocol and is 100% reschedulable for life — it never expires.
