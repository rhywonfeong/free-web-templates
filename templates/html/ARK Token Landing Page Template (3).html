Frequently asked

How do reflections work?

On every transfer, 2% is distributed to holders, proportional to balance. No claiming, no staking—your wallet is the vault.

What is the locker stream?

3% is streamed to lockers. Lock longer to boost your multiplier and claim a larger share of emissions.

Is liquidity locked?

The protocol continuously adds to LP. Lock schedules and addresses are publicly viewable on-chain and via our Terminal.

Any risks I should know?

Crypto is volatile and experimental. This is creative lore, not financial advice. Research before you sail.
