Statement
No. 001

We cut for the body in motion. Every seam is an argument against the average — nothing here is decoration, the structure is the statement.

Small runs, open hems, visible construction. A garment should carry the record of how it was made and the freedom to be worn wrong.

The Studio

WE DRESS THE
DISTANCE BETWEEN
HABIT AND FORM.

OBLIQ° began in a two-room workshop with three rolls of mill-end wool and a rule: no piece leaves until it fails a full day of wear.

We pattern by hand, sample on real bodies and keep production under one hundred units per style. It's slower. It's also the only honest way we know.
