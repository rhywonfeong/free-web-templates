Meet Dr. Elena Rivas

Dr. Rivas is a board-certified orthopedic surgeon fellowship-trained in Sports Medicine and Joint Reconstruction. With over 15 years of clinical experience, she is dedicated to restoring patient mobility through personalized care plans.

She obtained her medical degree from Johns Hopkins University and completed her residency at the Hospital for Special Surgery. Dr. Rivas serves as the team physician for several local collegiate athletic programs.
