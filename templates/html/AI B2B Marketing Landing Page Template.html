Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from spam?
Spam is untargeted mass-blasting. We send personalized, relevant messages to verified contacts who match your ICP. Deliverability matters — burned domains don't generate leads.
What industries do you work with?
Any B2B company with a large addressable market (10,000+ contacts), high-value deals ($5k+ ACV), and an existing sales process. SaaS, agencies, professional services, manufacturing — if the TAM exists, we can reach it.
How long until I see results?
Infrastructure takes 14 days to build and warm. After launch, replies typically start within the first week. Meaningful volume compounds over 30-60 days.
What do I need to provide?
Your ICP definition, your offer, and someone to follow up on leads. We handle everything else — infrastructure, lists, copy, sending, monitoring.
What if the campaign doesn't work?
That's why we offer a free trial campaign. You see results before committing. If performance drops, we diagnose and optimize — you don't pay for leads that don't show up.
How do you charge?
Monthly tech fee (covers infrastructure) plus a performance fee per interested lead. You only pay for results. Specific pricing depends on your TAM size and volume requirements.
