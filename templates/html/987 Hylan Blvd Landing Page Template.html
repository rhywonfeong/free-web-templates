About this home

Fully detached high ranch single family home in the heart of Grasmere, located just around the corner from the Verrazano Bridge with convenient access to S53 bus stops and shopping centers.

This well-maintained property features a built-in garage with a private driveway accommodating multiple vehicles. The interior layout is designed for comfort, with each bedroom accommodating at least a Queen-sized bed—excellent for a large family or multi-generational living.

Notable features include 2-zoned heating, central HVAC, pristine hardwood flooring, and multiple entrances for added privacy. The building measures 27x52 on a 41x114 lot, totaling 2,808 sqft of interior living space.
