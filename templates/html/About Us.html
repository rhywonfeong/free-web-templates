Why Sleepal Exists

Sleepal was built around a simple but powerful insight: sleep is shaped as much by the daily routine and environment as it is by the individual.

From the beginning, privacy, comfort, and long-term usability were non-negotiable. Every design and technical decision was made with the bedroom in mind, so Sleepal supports sleep without competing for attention.

Instead of asking people to adapt to technology, Sleepal adapts to the user. By observing sleep contactlessly and responding through light, sound, and subtle guidance, it supports better sleep naturally over time.

  • Privacy, comfort, and usability
  • Adapts to the user
  • Non-negotiable bedroom focus

From Tracking to Improvement

Turning nightly signals into actionable change for healthier routines.

Predictive Digital Twin

Built on large-scale real-world data, the LLM-powered Sleepal AI Agent forms a per-user predictive digital twin to deliver CBT-I–inspired, AI-personalized coaching (evaluated with 30k+ users).

Sleepal also understands sleep posture and nighttime behaviors throughout the night, offering valuable insight for individuals with OSA or breathing disruptions — helping support calmer breathing and more restorative rest.

Smart Orchestration

Drives sleep-stage–aware orchestration of lighting, HVAC, and audio, along with a human-friendly smart alarm featuring out-of-bed auto-stop, behavior-adaptive timing, and gesture snooze.

To our knowledge, this is the first contactless bedside system to combine these capabilities, helping users build healthier routines and wake brighter.

Extensible Core

While focused on sleep, the software/AI stack remains extensible for broader wellness and safety scenarios in the future.

Privacy-First by Design

Anonymous Sensing

No optical camera, no photo/video recording - only anonymous heat patterns and radar.

Local-First Processing

Sensor data stays on-device. Only refined sleep features are sent to cloud AI for deep sleep insight.

Physical Privacy Button

One flick physically disconnects the thermal and mic arrays at the circuit level for 100% hardware privacy.
