Our Curriculum Overview

A Commitment to Excellence

TBWWIOC aims at offering all our students a broad and balanced curriculum that fosters creativity and critical thinking abilities to prepare them for the best social and cultural life.

Whether it is our books or hands-on training, we make sure each student gets personal attention to cope up and flourish in every subject for better scores and a brighter future.

Business & Entrepreneurship

Focused on Kingdom-centered enterprise creation, financial stewardship, and ethical business. Students learn to build businesses that generate wealth, create employment, and restore economic dignity.

Agriculture & Food Security

Emphasizes sustainable farming, land stewardship, and food sovereignty. Learners gain strategies to feed communities, protect natural resources, and advance Africa’s agricultural independence.

Humanities & Human Resources Development

Learners develop identity, ethics, and cultural renewal while acquiring skills in leadership, organizational development, and people management. This track nurtures humanity as the anchor of progress.

Naturopathic Medicine & Holistic Health

The study, training, scientific learning, discussion and practice create teachers and well-equipped laboratories help students explore, discover and experiment new things under the best supervision.

Precious Metals & Wealth Stewardship

Equips learners with knowledge of gold, silver, and other wealth assets as tools of economic freedom. Students gain practical strategies for investment, resource management, and generational wealth transfer.

Counseling, Communication & Conflict Resolution

Provides skills in emotional intelligence, restorative counseling, public communication, and conflict transformation. Learners become peacemakers and bridge builders in families, communities, and nations.

Real Estate, Land & Community Development

Covers property management, land ownership, urban and rural development with a Kingdom focus on justice, equity, and sustainability. Students learn to develop communities that thrive.

Leadership & Governance

Builds leaders with integrity, vision, and Kingdom accountability. Training includes governance systems, civic responsibility, and transformational leadership.

Hospitality & Service Excellence

Focuses on Kingdom-centered service, cultural diplomacy, and hospitality management. Students learn to create environments of excellence that honor both people and nations.

Humanitarianism & Philanthropy

Trains learners to design and manage humanitarian projects with accountability and impact. Emphasis is placed on giving as a Kingdom principle for restoring hope to broken systems.

Sustainability & Environmental Stewardship

Covers renewable energy, conservation, and sustainable development practices. Students are trained to protect creation while building models for future generations.

Public Speaking & Global Advocacy

Equips students with communication mastery, media training, and advocacy skills. Learners become confident voices for justice, truth, and the Kingdom agenda on global stages.

The Kingdom Agenda & Ministry Studies

Multi-dimensional studies that integrate biblical principles without religious tactics. Students learn to apply Kingdom values in business, governance, family, and society, advancing THE REBIRTH OF THE B.L.A.C.K. NATION.
