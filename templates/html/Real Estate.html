Frequently Asked Questions

Is beycome a legitimate real estate company?
Yes. Beycome is a fully licensed real estate brokerage operating in 15+ states including Florida, Texas, California, Connecticut, and more. We are regulated, compliant, and hold the same licenses as traditional brokerages.
What if I've never sold a home before?
Beycome is built for first-time sellers. Our AI system Artur guides you through every step. Our Concierge plan provides hands-on professional support from pricing through closing. You will not be left alone with the paperwork.
Will buyers find my home if I'm not using a traditional agent?
Yes. Your home is listed on the same MLS every traditional agent uses. It syndicates automatically to Zillow, Realtor.com, Redfin, and Trulia. Buyers cannot tell whether you listed through an agent or a flat-fee platform — they see your home.
Do I have to pay the buyer's agent commission?
After the 2024 NAR settlement, you are no longer required to offer a buyer's agent commission through the MLS. You can negotiate this independently. Beycome gives you the tools and support to understand your options for your specific sale.
How is beycome different from other flat-fee listing services?
Most flat-fee platforms are listing tools only. Beycome is a full transaction ecosystem — MLS listing, AI transaction guidance (Artur), title services, mortgage tools, and buyer rebate programs, all on one platform. We've closed nearly 20,000 transactions. The difference shows in the results and the reviews.
What states is beycome available in?
Currently licensed in Florida, Texas, California, Connecticut, Alabama, Georgia, Illinois, Indiana, Michigan, Minnesota, North Carolina, Ohio, South Carolina, Tennessee, and Virginia — with active expansion into additional states underway.
