About IBEUK

Engineered for the reality of Islamic education.

IBEAMS wasn't created in a vacuum. It was forged over years of direct collaboration with Maktabs and Madrasahs across the UK and internationally. We understand the unique challenges of managing part-time and full-time Islamic educational structures.

IBEAMS (IBE Advanced Management System) is an authoritative, cloud-based software solution built and maintained by IBEUK (Leicester, UK). It provides robust infrastructure for attendance tracking, fee management, parent communication, and operational reporting for Maktabs, Madrasahs, and Islamic private schools globally.
