The Object · One Tower

One tower. Four drivers.

The MONO-9 is a single 1,240 mm column of brushed aluminium and black lacquer. No grille, no ornament, nothing to soften the edge. Orbit it slowly, from full height to the driver array to the rear port.

Anatomy · Section 001

Every layer earns its place.

Cut the MONO-9 in half and there is nothing decorative inside. Bracing, crossover, magnet and dome, each solving one problem and adding none. Move the pointer to read the section.

ANATOMY

Nothing added. Nothing hidden.

The cabinet is not a box with drivers bolted on, it is a braced aluminium lattice poured with constrained-layer damping so the walls stay dead while the cones work. Every part inside answers to one measurement. If it did not lower the noise floor, it is not in the tower.
