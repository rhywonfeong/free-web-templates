Frequently asked questions

How do you measure cloud emissions?
We connect directly to your provider's billing and usage API (AWS Cost Explorer, GCP Billing, Azure Consumption). We map instance types and regions to real-time grid intensity data to calculate precise carbon output.
What types of carbon credits do you use?
We prioritize high-quality, permanent removal credits (Biochar, Direct Air Capture) over avoidance offsets. All projects are verified by Gold Standard or Verra.
Can I integrate this into my own product?
Yes. Our API is designed for "Carbon-Aware Computing". You can use it to schedule jobs when grid intensity is low, or show your users their own footprint.
