Outlier Discovery

We back outlier venture managers early, often before success is obvious to the broader market. We look for specific knowledge, not general exposure.

Concentration & Focus

We prefer small, focused, highly concentrated funds where every decision matters. We believe dilution is the enemy of returns.

Independent Judgment

We emphasize judgment, proximity, and acting before consensus. We rely on first-principles analysis rather than social proof.

Depth Over Breadth

Intentional concentration allows for deeper relationships and meaningful co-investment opportunities. We are partners, not just allocators.

Structural Shifts

We focus on emerging technologies and long-term structural shifts in the economy. We invest in the infrastructure of the future.

Discipline

Signal over noise. Action over optics. We maintain institutional discipline and respect for capital in all market cycles.
