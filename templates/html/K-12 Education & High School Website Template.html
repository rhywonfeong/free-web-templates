About New Model

A Heritage of Building Character.

Since our inception in 1962, New Model High School has been at the forefront of providing quality education. We believe in nurturing not just academic excellence, but moral integrity, physical fitness, and social responsibility.

  • State-of-the-art modern laboratories and smart classrooms.
  • Dedicated faculty focused on individual student progress.
