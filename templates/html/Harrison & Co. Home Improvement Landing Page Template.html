FAQs

Questions We
Get Asked Often

Practical answers to help you plan your project with clarity and confidence.

How long will my project take?
Timelines depend on scope and approvals. A kitchen renovation typically takes 3-5 weeks, while a full rear extension may take 8-14 weeks. We confirm a clear schedule before work begins.
Do I need planning permission?
Some projects fall under permitted development, while others require formal approval. We can guide you through the options, coordinate drawings and manage submissions with our architectural partners.
How do you manage budgets?
We provide transparent itemised estimates and keep you updated throughout the build. Any changes are discussed and approved before they affect the programme or final cost.
Do you provide a warranty?
Yes. Structural work is backed by a 10-year guarantee, and our workmanship is covered for 12 months alongside relevant manufacturer warranties for fixtures, fittings and finishes.
