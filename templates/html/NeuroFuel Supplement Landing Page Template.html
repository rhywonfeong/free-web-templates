Why Your Brain Feels Like It's Running on Low Power Mode

After age 40, your brain progressively reduces production of a protein called NGF — Nerve Growth Factor. NGF is the biological signal responsible for keeping neurons alive and forming new connections.

Coffee and generic vitamins don't address this root cause. They force a worn-out system to work harder without ever fixing what's broken.
