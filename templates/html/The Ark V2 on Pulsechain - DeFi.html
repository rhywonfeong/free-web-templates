Capabilities

What your agent handles from day one

Every OpenClaw agent ships with these capabilities pre-configured. No add-ons. No plugins. No extra cost.

📞
Booking & Scheduling

Checks real-time availability against your connected calendar, books appointments, sends confirmations, handles reschedules, and fires reminder messages at intervals you define. Works without a human in the loop at any point.

Can I book a cleaning for next Tuesday afternoon?
I need to move my 3pm appointment — what's available Friday?
Is Dr. Chen available this week for a new patient consult?
💬
FAQ & Live Support

Answers your 50+ most common questions instantly, in your voice. When something falls outside the knowledge base, it escalates with full context attached — your team never starts cold.

Do you accept Blue Cross insurance?
What's your cancellation policy?
How much does a crown cost without insurance?
🧾
New Client Onboarding

Guides new clients through your intake process automatically. Sends the right forms, answers first-week questions, delivers your welcome materials, and confirms everything is in place before their first appointment.

I just booked — what do I need to bring to my first visit?
Where do I send my X-rays from my previous dentist?
Can I fill out the new patient forms online?
📧
Email Triage & Auto-Reply

Sorts, categorizes, and responds to routine emails automatically. Urgent items get flagged and routed to the right person. Everything else is handled — your inbox goes from a source of dread to a clean priority queue.

Hey do you have any Tuesday openings next week?
I need to cancel my appointment tomorrow morning
Can you send me a copy of my last invoice?
🔔
Reminders & Follow-ups

Automated appointment reminders via SMS and email at the intervals you set. Post-visit follow-ups. Review request campaigns to satisfied clients. The repetitive touchpoints your team dreads — fully automated.

When something genuinely needs a human, your agent escalates — but with full context already attached. The transcript, the issue, the customer history. Your team never asks "what did they say?" again.

Common questions

Still wondering?

The questions people ask before they stop second-guessing and just start the trial.

What if my business is complicated? We have a lot of edge cases.

Good. The more complex your business, the more our managed approach pays off versus a DIY tool. We handle complicated escalation logic, multi-location rules, service-specific policies, and seasonal variations. Complex businesses are our sweet spot — not our edge case.

What happens when the agent gets something wrong?

We catch it before your customer does, most of the time. Our weekly review looks at every conversation that didn't resolve cleanly, and we fix the underlying knowledge gap or rule that caused it. You can also flag anything directly through your dashboard and we'll address it same-day.

How does my agent get updated when my prices or services change?

Just message us or use the knowledge base editor in your dashboard. We push the update within a few hours. For Growth and Scale plans, we proactively monitor your website and flag when something looks out of date — so you don't even have to remember to tell us.

Will it sound robotic to my customers?

No — and we've worked hard to make that the case. During your intake call, we calibrate your agent's tone to match how you actually communicate. It can be warm and casual, professional and direct, or anything in between. Most clients tell us customers can't tell the difference.

What integrations do you support?

Google Calendar, Acuity, Calendly, Mindbody, and most major scheduling tools. For CRM: HubSpot, Salesforce, and most small-business CRMs via Zapier. SMS: Twilio, your existing business number via call forwarding. Email: Gmail, Outlook, Fastmail. If you're on something unusual, ask — we've probably handled it.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Month-to-month, no long-term contracts. Cancel with 7 days' notice and we'll offboard cleanly — including exporting your full knowledge base so you keep everything we built. We'd rather earn your business every month than lock you in.

What does "managed" actually mean in practice?

It means a real person on our team reviews your agent's performance every week, makes tuning changes, updates the knowledge base when needed, and monitors for patterns that suggest a policy change is needed. On Growth plans, this is included. You don't have to log in to your dashboard unless you want to. Most of our clients check it occasionally for fun, not out of necessity.

Is my business data secure?

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We don't train AI models on your customer data. We're SOC 2 Type II compliant, HIPAA-ready for healthcare clients, and can sign a BAA on request. Customer conversations are stored for 90 days by default, configurable to your requirements.
