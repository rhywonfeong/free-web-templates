Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if I scale my fleet?
Our pricing scales with you. You only pay the monthly fee for active devices. As you pass certain volume thresholds (e.g., 500+ vehicles), volume discounts automatically apply to your account.
Are devices reusable?
Yes. When you sell or decommission a vehicle, simply remove the Movitronic hardware and install it in a new vehicle. You do not need to buy a new device.
What happens if a device breaks?
All hardware comes with a standard 2-year warranty. If a device malfunctions due to manufacturing defects during this period, we replace it free of charge.
Can I mix rental and car sharing?
Absolutely. Our platform supports hybrid fleets out of the box. You can designate specific vehicles for hourly car sharing and others for traditional daily rentals, all managed from the same dashboard.
Can I use my own payment gateway?
We natively integrate with Stripe for seamless payment processing. If you require a custom payment gateway, it can be arranged on the Enterprise plan via custom integration.
What are broker fees?
Movitronic charges no additional fees on bookings made through brokers or OTAs. You keep 100% of your negotiated rate, paying only your standard monthly vehicle platform fee.
How long does onboarding take?
Lite plans can be live in as little as 10 days. Pro plans with whitelabel apps typically take 30 days due to app store review processes. Enterprise deployments are timeline-dependent based on custom requirements.
Is support included?
Yes. All plans include standard email and ticket support. Enterprise plans include a dedicated account manager and priority SLA support.
