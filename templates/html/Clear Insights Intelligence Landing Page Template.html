01. Collect

Building the foundation

Continuous Collection

We monitor products over time. A product that was 4.5 stars six months ago and 3.8 today tells a different story than the rating alone.

Normalize & Cleanse

Our pipeline unifies "5 stars" and "9/10" into a single structure. Our taxonomy engine categorizes raw feedback by attribute (taste, value, shipping) before analysis begins.

03. Validate

Separating noise from truth

Not every complaint is a real problem. If you treat every complaint equally, you waste resources. We distinguish isolated incidents from systemic issues.

False Alarm Detection

Our system cross-references claims against product specs and consensus. A "half-empty bottle" might just be a large container for a specific volume. We flag these as perception issues, not defects.

Pattern Correlation

One "arrived damaged" is a fluke. Fifteen in a month is a fulfillment failure. We analyze clustering to verify severity.
