Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?
We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance to guarantee vehicle availability, especially for specific models. However, we do our best to accommodate last-minute requests.
What is your cancellation policy?
Cancellations made 24 hours prior to the scheduled pickup time are fully refundable. Cancellations within 24 hours may be subject to a cancellation fee.
Are gratuities included in the price?
Yes, our quoted prices are all-inclusive, covering fuel, tolls, and a standard gratuity for the chauffeur. There are no hidden fees.
Do you offer child seats?
Yes, child safety seats (infant, toddler, booster) are available upon request for a small additional fee. Please specify this when booking.
What areas do you serve?
Our primary service areas are the New York Metropolitan area (including Long Island, Westchester, NJ) and the Greater Washington DC area. Long-distance trips between these cities are also available.
Can I book by the hour?
Absolutely. We offer hourly service with a minimum booking duration (usually 3-4 hours depending on the vehicle type) for maximum flexibility.
