FAQs — Everything you need to know.

Why should I trust you to deliver these results?
Fair question — you don't have to just take my word for it. You only pay for results. If I don't deliver 15–25 qualified appointments within your first 60 days, you don't pay. I've sent millions of cold emails and built this system from the ground up, so the risk is on me, not you.
How long until I see results, and why does the guarantee run over 60 days instead of 30?
It takes about two weeks to warm up your inboxes properly — that's non-negotiable if we want your deliverability to hold up long-term instead of burning out fast. From there, volume ramps quickly, and most clients start seeing qualified appointments within 2 to 3 weeks of launch. Your first 60 days account for that full ramp and land you 15 to 25 qualified appointments total. From that point forward, once your system is fully warmed up and running at full volume, 15 to 25 per month becomes your ongoing pace.
What if the appointments don't meet my criteria?
You don't pay for them. We only charge for appointments that meet the specific criteria we agree on together — budget, need, intent, whatever matters to your business. Anything that doesn't meet that bar doesn't count toward your total.
How do you verify budget and intent before sending?
We use third-party data and intent signals — things like recent buying activity, growth indicators, or industry-specific triggers — to identify the businesses that are the best fit before we ever reach out.
What industries do you work with?
Any B2B industry with a clear ideal client profile. We customize targeting to match who you actually want to work with.
How many emails go out each month?
Typically 15,000 to 30,000, spread across enough inboxes to keep deliverability high — usually 500 to 1,000 per inbox per month.
What kind of response rate should I expect?
It varies by industry, but 2–5% is typical, and of those replies, roughly 30–50% end up qualifying. That's how we consistently land in the 15–25 range every month.
Do I need to do anything on my end?
No — we handle the infrastructure, the list, the messaging, the sending, and the qualification. All you do is show up to calls with people who are already a fit.
What if I'm already doing cold email and it's not working?
Then something in the system is broken — targeting, messaging, deliverability, or volume. We diagnose and fix all four. And if we can't beat what you're currently getting, you don't pay.
What does this cost?
Pricing is performance-based, typically $2,500 to $4,000 a month depending on volume and targeting — and again, you only pay for appointments that meet your criteria.
