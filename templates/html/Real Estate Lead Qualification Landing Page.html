How your team saves time on leads

Your team gets leads from forms, website, or any other source

If the lead is from a form, the system instantly analyzes their responses based on the rules specified by your team

AI scores each lead as hot / mid / cold by budget, timeline, location, and motivation.

Hot leads are routed to calendar booking + immediate team or agent alert with the full details of the lead.

Mid leads get a quick SMS clarification + routing to an agent/ISA or to an automated followup.

Cold leads are instantly nurtured and keeps them for future opportunities while out-of-area leads are sent to an agent or team for referral opportunites
