FAQs — Everything you need to know.

What if I'm already using Klaviyo?
We will audit your current setup to find what is broken. Most stores using Klaviyo only capture 20–30% of their potential email revenue; we optimize your system to capture the rest.
What if I don't have 800 cart abandonments?
We scale the system to your specific traffic volume. You will recover proportionally less if your traffic is lower, but our $100k revenue guarantee still applies.
What if I'm doing $200k/month instead of $75k?
The system scales up seamlessly. The more traffic and data your store has, the more revenue we can generate. The $100k guarantee is simply our baseline minimum.
What if I don't want to send 3 campaigns per week?
Then this program is not a good fit for you. Consistency is mandatory to hit our revenue numbers, and we require commitment to the scheduled campaign calendar.
How do you track the $100k in revenue?
We use Klaviyo’s native attribution tracking. We will set up a real-time dashboard so you can see the exact sales generated by each flow and campaign in black and white.
What if my website conversion rate is under 1.5%?
You will need to optimize your site's conversion rate first before onboarding with us. If your website fails to convert incoming traffic, email marketing cannot fix that underlying problem.
