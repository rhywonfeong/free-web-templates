Our Story

The Oldest Dock on LBI

Polly's Dock & Clam House is Beach Haven's best-kept secret — a rustic waterfront gem where the clams are caught just 400 yards from your plate. We're a family-run dock, restaurant, boat rental, and bait & tackle shop all in one. Grab a picnic table, bring your own brews (we're BYOB!), and watch the sun drop over Barnegat Bay.
