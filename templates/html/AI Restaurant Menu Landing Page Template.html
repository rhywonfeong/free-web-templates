Most Restaurant Menus Are Still PDFs

Many restaurants still share their menus as PDFs or images. These menus are difficult to read on phones, rarely include images of dishes, and are painful to update.

Not Mobile-Friendly

Customers have to pinch and zoom to read tiny text on PDF menus, leading to frustration before they even order.

No Food Images

Static menus lack visual appeal. Without appetizing photos, it's much harder to upsell your most profitable signature dishes.

Hard to Update

Changing a price or removing a sold-out item requires a completely new design file, export, and upload process.

The Solution
