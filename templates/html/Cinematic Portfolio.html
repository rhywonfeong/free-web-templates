About Me

Capturing moments defined by Emotion & Energy.

Ed_It_Shoots is a filmmaker dedicated to creating visuals that not only tell a story but Immerse Viewers in the experience. His editing style thoughtfully integrates narration, layered sound design, and dynamic color grading to enhance depth and amplify the impact of each frame.

Driven by a strong passion for the rave and festival scene, Ed has documented raw, authentic moments at major events across Asia such as S2O, Heineken Refresh, KLWP 2024, and Verknipt—transforming Real Life Chaotic Moments into compelling and unforgettable visual narratives.
