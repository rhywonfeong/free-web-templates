Relaxing Care Home

Sovereigncarehome is a residential care home offering relaxing assisted living, nursing, dementia, respite, palliative and rehabilitation care. Surrounded by greenery, this purpose-built nursing home offers excellent transport links to London and other surrounding areas so you can stay connected to your loved ones.

As you enter our care home, you can't help but notice our warm and welcoming lounges which are available to all of our residents and their loved ones. We have accommodation for up to 80 residents and provide stylish, fully furnished en-suite rooms complete with private terraces. All of our facilities are maintained to the highest standard so that our residents can enjoy a premium experience at Sovereigncarehome. We are dedicated to being the best relaxing care home and understand that a residential home is so much more than just its level of care. Whatever your loved one needs, our care team are always ready to help.

Our Care Services

Residential Care

Our award-winning residential care home offers exceptional care and support that is tailored to the needs of each resident, our care homes are designed to feel more like home than a hospital setting.

Dementia Care

We offer exceptional care and support delivered by our specialist dementia care team in a dementia friendly environment you can enjoy peace of mind that your loved one is being looked after in the best possible way, round-the-clock.

Respite Care

Whether you require short-term respite care for yourself, or you need to arrange it for a loved one, our premium services promise to deliver exactly what you need.

Nursing Care

At Sovereigncarehome, we offer expert nursing care tailored to individual needs, combining round-the-clock support with luxurious, hotel-style surroundings to give you complete peace of mind.

Palliative Care

We provide compassionate expert-led palliative care in a comfortable homely setting, ensuring dignity, comfort for every stage of life.

Rehabilitation

We offer rehabilitation through our respite care program, ideal for individuals recovering from surgery or requiring short-term support. Available for a minimum of one week, which allows residents to benefit from personalised care plans.
