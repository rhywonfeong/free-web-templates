FAQs

Is this really free?
Yes. There's no cost to you at any point. We're paid by the advisors we work with, never by owners, so you can explore your options with no fee and no obligation.
Do I have to sell if I take a call?
No. The intro call is just a conversation. You're never obligated to work with any advisor we introduce, or to sell at all. You stay in control the whole way.
Will this stay confidential?
Yes. We keep everything private. Your details are only shared with an advisor once you've agreed, and nothing is made public. Discretion matters as much to us as it does to you.
What kind of advisors will I be speaking to?
Sell-side M&A advisors who specialize in your industry and actively work with businesses like yours. We match you based on your sector and what you're looking for.
Do you work with my business?
As long as you're based in the US and your business is sellable, we can likely help. The best way to know for sure is a quick call, tell us about your business and we'll confirm whether we're a fit.
How quickly can you connect me with an advisor?
Depending on your industry, we can often start booking calls into your calendar within one working day. We'll confirm timelines on our first short call.
What if I'm not ready to sell yet?
That's fine. Plenty of owners just want to understand their options and what their business might be worth to the right buyer. There's no pressure to move on any timeline but your own.
What happens on the first call?
The first call is a short, informal chat with us. We just want to understand your business and what you're looking for, so we can match you with the right advisors.
