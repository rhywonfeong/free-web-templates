Frequently Asked Questions

1. Do I need previous repair or sewing experience?

No. Many people begin repairing clothing with little or no prior experience. Funalya tools are intended to make garment repair more approachable for everyday users.

2. What kinds of items can be repaired?

These tools can be used for repairing and reinforcing garments and textiles such as sweaters, socks, knitwear, clothing, and other fabric items with worn or damaged areas.

3. What's the difference between the 14-Hook and 21-Hook?

Both tools support textile repair and reinforcement. The primary difference is size, allowing users to choose the option that best suits their repair projects and preferred workflow.

4. Can I repair small holes?

Yes. Both tools can be used to address small damaged areas before they become larger problems.

5. Can these tools help reinforce thinning fabric?

Yes. In addition to repairing holes, they can be used to strengthen worn areas that may be vulnerable to future damage.

6. Are these tools only for knitwear?

No. While many users repair sweaters, socks, and knit garments, the tools can also be used on a variety of textile repair projects.

7. Will repaired garments look exactly like new?

Repairs are intended to restore usefulness and extend garment life. The appearance of the finished repair will vary depending on the fabric, damage, materials used, and individual technique.

8. Why repair clothing instead of replacing it?

Repairing can help extend garment life, reduce waste, preserve meaningful items, and potentially lower the need for replacement purchases.

9. Can these tools be used repeatedly?

Yes. They are designed for ongoing clothing maintenance and recurring repair projects.

10. How does clothing repair support sustainability?

Repairing garments helps keep usable textiles in service longer, reducing the number of items that are discarded due to minor damage or wear.
