Curated Details

What exactly is included in the fee?
The fee comprehensively covers your private helicopter transfer from Paris to Reims, opulent residency at the Royal Champagne Hotel, all breakfasts, the Michelin-starred dining experiences (inclusive of sommelier-curated wine pairings), the private Billecart-Salmon tasting, and your chauffeured return to Paris.
Are flights to Paris included?
International or domestic aviation to Paris is not included. Guests are respectfully asked to orchestrate their own arrival to our designated meeting point in Paris on the morning of April 24th.
What is the required attire?
The aesthetic is 'Smart Elegant'. Diurnal activities welcome refined, comfortable resort wear suitable for exclusive tastings. Evening engagements at Michelin establishments necessitate formal evening attire.
What is the cancellation policy?
Due to the highly bespoke nature of this curation—encompassing private aviation, exclusive estate buyouts, and rare culinary reservations—the investment is non-refundable upon confirmation. Comprehensive premium travel insurance is highly recommended.
