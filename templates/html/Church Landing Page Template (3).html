A Pastor Who Understands Where You Come From.

Born and raised in Jamaica, Pastor knows exactly what it feels like to pack up your life, move across the world, and miss the warmth of home. He knows the unique struggles of the diaspora—working long hours, navigating a new culture, and trying to hold onto your roots.

He started New Royal Ministry because he saw too many of our people feeling lost in massive, impersonal churches abroad. He wanted to create a space where the preaching is passionate, the prayers are fervent, and you never feel like just a number in a crowd.

Whether you're in London, Toronto, or New York, when you log on on a Sunday morning, you're stepping into a digital living room filled with family.
