Frequently asked questions

What is the typical onboarding timeline?
Our onboarding process typically takes 2 to 4 weeks. This includes deep discovery calls, technical setup of email infrastructure to ensure deliverability, and drafting the initial messaging sequences tailored to your specific buyer personas.

Do you guarantee meetings?
While we cannot guarantee absolute numbers due to market variables, we operate on performance-driven metrics and establish clear KPIs during the discovery phase. If targets aren't met, our team continuously optimizes until we hit the agreed-upon benchmarks.

What size companies do you work with?
We primarily partner with B2B companies ranging from Series A startups to Enterprise organizations. Our methodology works best for companies with high Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) and complex sales cycles.
