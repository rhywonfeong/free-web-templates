Yes, 100%. The LLC, the Amazon account, and all assets are fully owned by you. We operate as your exclusive management team on a performance-based fee structure.

Store setup and initial inventory staging typically takes 60-90 days. Revenue begins generating in month 3, with cash-flow stabilization usually occurring around months 6-8 as we reinvest early profits to compound growth.
