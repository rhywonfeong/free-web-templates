Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept insurance?
Yes, we are registered providers for most major insurance companies including AXA, Aviva, and Vitality. Please check with your provider for authorization before booking.
Is what I say confidential?
Absolutely. Client confidentiality is paramount. We adhere to strict BACP and GDPR guidelines. Information is only shared if there is a serious risk of harm to yourself or others, which will be discussed with you first.
How many sessions will I need?
This varies for everyone. CBT is often short-term (6-20 sessions), while deep-seated issues may require longer term work. We will review progress every 6 sessions to ensure you are getting value.
What is your cancellation policy?
We require 48 hours notice for cancellations. This allows us to offer the slot to someone else waiting for support. Late cancellations are charged at the full session rate.
