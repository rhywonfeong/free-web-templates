Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify if a remodeling contractor is legitimate?

Always ask for their state license number (for example, our license is ROC# 313636). You should verify this number through the local Registrar of Contractors website. Phoenix Home Remodeling is also BBB Accredited with an A+ rating and is a member of NARI and NKBA.

Does converting a tub to a walk-in shower decrease home value?

In most modern real estate markets, replacing an unused tub with a luxurious, easily accessible shower actually increases buyer appeal, especially in primary suites. However, real estate experts generally recommend keeping at least one bathtub elsewhere in the house to accommodate families with small children.

Why do you require a planning phase before giving a final price?

Basic contractors often provide ballpark numbers and adjust the price after demolition. By completing design and scope first through our Feasibility, Planning, and Design package, we provide a final real price before construction begins, preventing bait-and-switch pricing and scope confusion.
