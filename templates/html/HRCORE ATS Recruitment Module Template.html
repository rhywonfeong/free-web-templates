Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers.

Can I post to multiple job boards at once?

Yes, you can generate unique tracking links for LinkedIn, Indeed, and social media to see which source performs best.

How does the resume parser handle different formats?

Our AI supports PDF, Docx, and even image-based CVs, converting them into searchable structured data.

Can we set up a multi-round interview workflow?

Absolutely. You can customize stages for every role, such as "Technical Test," "Panel Interview," and "Culture Fit."

Can we store a talent pool for future roles?

Yes. Candidates who aren't a fit today can be tagged and archived for future openings, building your proprietary talent network.

Is there an approval flow for job requisitions?

Yes, managers can request a new hire, and it only goes live once HR or Finance approves the budget through automated routing.
