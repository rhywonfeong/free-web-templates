1. What Tatler Africa Is

Tatler Africa is a luxury cultural media platform focused on showcasing the people, places, and institutions shaping modern African influence.

The brand sits at the intersection of:

Luxury Culture Power Travel Business Society

Tatler exists to document and elevate Africa’s rising cultural and economic influence globally.

The narrative guiding the platform is: Influence is the new luxury.

Tatler Africa is not a trend page or entertainment account.

It is an authority platform focused on:

  • cultural power
  • leadership
  • luxury lifestyle
  • global African influence

The goal is to build a high-value audience ecosystem around these themes.

2. The Objective of the Page

Page Existence

Tatler Africa’s Instagram page exists to:

  • Build authority in African luxury culture
  • Grow a high-value audience
  • Support partnerships, events, and sponsorships
  • Establish Tatler as the leading luxury media voice on the continent

Revenue Support

The page supports revenue through:

  • brand partnerships
  • luxury sponsorships
  • editorial collaborations
  • events
  • membership communities

Social media is the top-of-funnel authority engine.

TATLER AFRICA — SOCIAL TEAM PRINCIPLES

(Emily & Leo Operational Responsibilities)

Tatler Africa is a high-visibility, high-expectation client.
The standard required is precision, ownership, and speed.
Both of you are responsible for ensuring the Tatler Africa page operates at the level expected of a global luxury media brand.

1

OWNERSHIP

Tatler Africa social execution is your responsibility.

This means you know what content is going out, understand why it is going out, and ensure everything meets Tatler quality standards. You should always be aware of:

  • the upcoming posts
  • the editorial context
  • the pillars and columns
  • the performance of recent posts

This account should be something you actively follow and understand, not something you simply upload content to. Treat the page as if you are responsible for its reputation.

2

COPYWRITING & EDITORIAL QUALITY

Emily

Ensure all posts read like editorial, not social media filler.

Includes: captions that flow naturally, readable on-screen text in Reels, structured carousel storytelling, clarity and authority in tone.

All copy must be grammatically correct, clean and readable, structured properly, aligned with Tatler’s editorial voice. There must be:

  • No spelling mistakes.
  • No sloppy captions.
  • No unclear messaging.

If something provided by editorial does not read well, fix it. Do not publish anything that feels careless.

3

DESIGN & TEMPLATE DISCIPLINE

Tatler operates with strict brand guidelines.

You must:

  • use the approved templates
  • follow layout rules exactly
  • maintain typography and spacing
  • maintain visual consistency

Do not modify templates without approval.
Tatler’s credibility depends on visual consistency.

4

CONTENT PRODUCTION

Emily

For each post you must:

  1. Receive the brief from Monday.com
  2. Source imagery from: Tatler content bank, editorial assets, public domain, partner content, royalty-free
  3. Insert content into Tatler templates
  4. Upload the completed asset to Monday.com
  5. Send for approval

Nothing should stall waiting for someone else. If assets are missing, find alternatives.

5

PUBLISHING & QC

Leo

Ensure that everything published is flawless.

Before scheduling/publishing, verify:

  • captions correct
  • spelling/grammar
  • tags & credits
  • collaborators
  • alt text included
  • format correct

Nothing should go live without final QA review. If something looks wrong, fix it before publishing.

6

ANALYTICS RESPONSIBILITY

Leo

Review performance every week.

Key metrics: saves, shares, reel completion rate, profile visits, follower growth, engagement quality.

Every week identify:

  • what performed best
  • what underperformed
  • what content type is resonating most

These insights help guide future content decisions.

7

DAILY COMMUNICATION

Tatler Africa requires active coordination. We will maintain a WhatsApp group for fast communication. Expect:

  • daily check-ins
  • quick responses
  • fast coordination when changes are needed

If something urgent happens, it will be handled through this group.

8

RESPONSIVENESS

This client requires speed and flexibility. Sometimes this may require:

  • quick changes to scheduled posts
  • reacting to editorial requests
  • adjusting captions or assets
  • responding outside normal hours

Occasionally this may require weekend work. Tatler is a high-demand client, and responsiveness is expected.

9

HANDOVER PROCESS

Evolving

We are still refining the ideal content handover process. The likely system will be:

  • bi-weekly editorial submission into Monday.com
  • content prepared and scheduled in advance
  • ongoing adjustments where necessary

Sandra will need to deliver content faster over time. Until then, flexibility is required.

10

THE EXPECTATION

Tatler Africa has the potential to become a major cultural platform.

The people working on this account should feel like they are building something important. This means:

  • paying attention to detail
  • understanding the brand
  • taking pride in execution
  • constantly improving the page

If the page becomes successful, the people working on it will be recognised for building it. Ownership now means recognition later.

ROOTED STANDARD FOR TATLER AFRICA

The expectation is:

high ownership • high attention to detail • strong editorial judgement • fast execution • constant awareness

The goal is simple:

Build Tatler Africa into the leading luxury cultural media platform on the continent.
