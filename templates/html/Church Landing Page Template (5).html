Our Story

Knowing Christ and making Him known.

We are more than just a Sunday gathering. COP Smyrna is a diverse, multi-generational family united by the transforming love of Jesus Christ. Rooted in the rich heritage of The Church of Pentecost, we are passionate about sound doctrine, fervent prayer, and genuine fellowship.

Our mission is simple: to help you encounter God, discover your purpose, and make a lasting impact in our city and beyond.
