Location

Discover Costa Adeje & Tenerife

Situated in the most premium area of southern Tenerife, you have world-class beaches, dining, and attractions right at your doorstep.

  • Premium Beaches

    Walk just 5 minutes to Playa del Duque, known for its golden sand and crystal-clear waters, or head to Playa Fañabé.

  • World-Class Attractions

    Siam Park, voted the world's best water park, is just a 10-minute drive away.

  • Exceptional Dining

    Surrounded by highly-rated restaurants. Enjoy fresh Canarian seafood at La Caleta.

  • Mount Teide & Nature

    A perfect base camp for exploring the island. Drive up to Teide National Park in under an hour.
