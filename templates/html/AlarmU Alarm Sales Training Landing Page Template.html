You're Competing Against Systems That Are Already Installed

Alarm sales has a unique challenge. Most of the homeowners you talk to either already have a system or think they don't need one.

"We already have ADT." "We've never had a break-in." "We can't afford it right now." "Not interested."

The Real Challenges You Face in Alarm Sales

Existing systems are your biggest competitor

Millions of homes already have security. Homeowners think switching is complicated. You need to know how to position your system as an upgrade and explain the switchover process.

Homeowners don't feel the urgency

Security is invisible until something goes wrong. You need to know how to create urgency without using fear tactics that make you seem pushy or manipulative.

Price objections kill deals

Alarm systems require monthly monitoring fees. You need to know how to frame the investment against what they're already spending and make the monthly fee feel insignificant.

The smart home conversation

Alarms are just the entry point. Most reps don't know how to upsell the full smart home package without being pushy. You need to know how to stack value and increase your ticket size.

Trust is everything

You're asking homeowners to let you install equipment in their home and give you access to their security. That requires trust. You need to know how to build credibility fast, position yourself as a professional, and make homeowners feel confident in their decision.
