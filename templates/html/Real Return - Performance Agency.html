Every major ad platform just changed the rules. Most agencies haven't noticed.

For the last decade, the value a digital agency provided was fairly simple: someone who knew how to work the platform better than you did. They knew the bidding strategies. They knew the targeting levers. They knew what buttons to press.

That era is over.

Meta and Google have handed the mechanical work over to AI. Campaign optimisation, audience targeting, bid management — the platforms now do this automatically, and they do it better than any human media buyer.

This isn't a threat. It's a signal.

The signal is this: the advantage has shifted upstream. The brands winning now aren't winning because they've found a smarter bidding strategy. They're winning because they have better creative, stronger offers and a structured system behind everything they do.

The question isn't whether your agency knows the platform. The question is whether they know how to build something the platform can actually scale.

AI didn't threaten our model. It validated it.
