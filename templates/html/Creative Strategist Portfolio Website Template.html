The Narrative

I bridge the gap between creative intuition and business strategy to build brand ecosystems that last.

Storytelling isn't just a skill for me—it's a lifelong connection to how humans understand the world. My background spans the full creative spectrum: from editing long-form documentaries to designing apparel and crafting high-velocity social content.

In a noisy digital landscape, resourcefulness is key. I pride myself on blending hard analytics with emotional creativity to shape narratives that don't just reach audiences, but move them. Whether I'm deep in the trenches of video editing, identifying core human motivations for a campaign, or designing a brand's visual identity, my focus remains the same: clarity, emotion, and impact.

I don't just execute; I engineer experiences. I translate complex data into compelling visual stories and turn abstract ideas into tangible assets across video, photography, and digital design.
