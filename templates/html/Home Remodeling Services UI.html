FAQ

Answers to common remodeling questions.

Do you provide permits and plans? +

Yes. We manage all drawings, engineering, and city coordination so your project stays compliant and on schedule.

Are you licensed and insured? +

Absolutely. We carry active licensing and full insurance, and we’ll provide documentation upon request.

What’s a typical project timeline? +

Smaller interior updates can be 2–6 weeks; kitchens/baths are often 6–10 weeks; additions/ADUs vary based on scope and city approvals.

Do you offer financing? +

We partner with financing providers for qualified projects. Ask during your consultation for current options.
