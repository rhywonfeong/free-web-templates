Our Story

Built for those who move differently.

LYRAUN was born from a simple belief: that movement is a form of self-expression. The idea is what you wear puts you ahead of others by staying focus on the work, the flow, and the moment.

Every stitch is considered. Every fabric is tested. We obsess over the details so you can move with confidence and intention—on the mat, on the run, or wherever the day takes you.
