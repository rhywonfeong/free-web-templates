Frequently asked questions

Find answers to common questions about Invoicely's features, billing, and usage.

General questions

What is Invoicely?

Invoely helps you create and send professional invoices, accept payments, automate reminders, and track revenue—without manual busywork.

How do I get started?

Sign up free, choose a template, add your business details, and send your first invoice in minutes. You can customize branding and taxes anytime.

Is there a free trial?

Yes—try all Pro features free for 14 days. No credit card required to start.

Subscription & billing

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major cards and bank transfers. For annual plans, we also support invoicing on request.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes. Cancel anytime from settings. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

What's the difference between plans?

Starter is great for individuals. Pro adds unlimited invoices, advanced branding, and automation. Enterprise includes SSO, audit logs, and premium support.

Features & usage

How many team members can I add?

Starter is single-user. Pro supports small teams, and Enterprise offers organization-wide seats with roles and permissions.

Can I export invoices and reports?

Yes. Export invoices as PDF and download CSV reports for payments, taxes, and revenue.

How secure is my data?

We use bank-grade encryption, regular backups, and role-based access controls. Two-factor authentication is supported for all accounts.
