Frequently Asked Questions

How does the credit system work?
Credits are consumed based on the complexity of the research task. A simple data lookup might cost 1 credit, while a deep competitive analysis involving multiple sources might cost 5-10 credits. Credits roll over month-to-month on annual plans.
How current is the data?
We do not rely on static databases. Our AI agents browse the live web in real-time when you make a request, ensuring the intelligence you receive is up-to-the-minute accurate.
What happens to our research data?
Your data is yours. We do not store your research inputs or results for our own training purposes. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, adhering to SOC 2 compliance standards.
Can I export the data?
Absolutely. All intelligence reports can be exported to CSV, Excel, or JSON. Enterprise plans also include direct API access and integrations with tools like Salesforce, HubSpot, and Slack.
