FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Ringyo AI

How does Ringyo AI work?
Ringyo uses advanced natural language processing to understand caller intent and respond with human-like voice synthesis. It integrates with your calendar to book appointments in real-time.
How long does setup take?
Most businesses are up and running in under 10 minutes. Our templates make it easy to customize the AI for your specific industry.
Can customers tell it's AI?
The voice quality is extremely high. While you can choose to disclose it's an AI assistant (recommended), many callers engage conversationally as if speaking to a human.
What happens if the AI can't handle a request?
The AI can be configured to take a message or forward the call to a human staff member if it encounters a complex query it hasn't been trained on.
