Frequently Asked Questions

What platforms do you develop for?
We specialize in Shopify and WooCommerce for e-commerce. For custom web applications, we use the MERN stack (MongoDB, Express, React, Node) or Next.js for high performance.
How long does a typical project take?
A standard e-commerce site takes 4-6 weeks. Custom development projects vary by complexity but typically range from 8-12 weeks. We provide a detailed timeline during the discovery phase.
Do you offer ongoing maintenance?
Yes, we offer several support packages that include security updates, bug fixes, content updates, and monthly performance reports.
