Frequently Asked Questions

How does Earn generate returns?
Returns are generated through interest payments from borrowers who over-collateralize their loans with crypto assets. The protocol facilitates this secure exchange of liquidity.
What does "up to 18% APR" mean?
APR varies based on market demand for liquidity. 18% represents the maximum historical yield achieved during peak utilization periods. Rates adjust dynamically.
What does 50% LTV mean?
LTV (Loan-to-Value) is the ratio of your loan to your collateral. At 50% LTV, if you deposit $10,000 worth of crypto collateral, you can borrow up to $5,000 in USDT.
What are the risks involved?
While all loans are collateralized, risks include smart contract vulnerabilities and extreme market volatility affecting collateral value. Please read our full risk disclosure.
