How giving works

A speculative token with a built-in mechanism to turn trading volume into transparent, verifiable public good.

1. Trade

Buy or sell $GIVE on Robinhood Chain. A small, fixed fee is applied to every transaction.

2. Pool

100% of collected fees accumulate in the public treasury smart contract.

3. Match & Donate

The community votes to disburse funds to match active campaigns on Daffy.

4. Verify

Every donation is recorded on-chain, verifiable by anyone at any time.

Frequently asked questions

How much of the fee goes to charity?

100%. The smart contract automatically routes the entire fee collected from buys and sells directly to the treasury wallet, which is solely used to fund nominated Daffy Campaigns. There is no team allocation or founder tax.

How are charities selected?

Any $GIVE holder can nominate a campaign currently active on Daffy.org. The community then votes on these nominations via off-chain gasless voting. The campaigns with the most votes receive funding matches from the treasury.

Is give.fund affiliated with Daffy or Robinhood?

No. give.fund is an independent, community-run project. It operates on the Robinhood Chain network and utilizes Daffy.org's public campaign infrastructure to facilitate real-world donations, but it has no official partnership with either entity.

Is this a registered non-profit?

$GIVE is a speculative memecoin. It is not a registered charity or 501(c)(3). However, the funds generated by the protocol are donated directly to verified 501(c)(3) organizations via the Daffy platform. Buyers of $GIVE are engaging in cryptocurrency trading, not making tax-deductible donations themselves.
