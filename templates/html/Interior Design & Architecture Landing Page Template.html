Hola, I'm Roxanne.
welcome to the family.

I believe your home should be as vibrant, welcoming, and beautifully complex as you are. Forget the sterile, beige boxes. We’re bringing soul, color, and undeniable personality back into interior design.

Drawing from my Mexican heritage, I design spaces that feel collected and alive. We mix handcrafted terracotta, rich dark woods, aged plaster, and splashes of deep cobalt to create homes that pull you in and invite you to stay awhile.
