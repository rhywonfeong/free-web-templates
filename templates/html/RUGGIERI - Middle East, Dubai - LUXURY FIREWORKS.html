Legacy

A Heritage of
Excellence Since 1739

RUGGIERI is not simply a fireworks company, it is a name rooted in history. Founded in the 18th century, RUGGIERI became renowned for pioneering innovations in pyrotechnics and staging spectacular performances for royal courts in Versailles, historic and national celebrations, and global audiences.

Over the centuries, the company evolved into a worldwide leader in fireworks, special effects, and multimedia productions, now part of the Etienne Lacroix Group, an international reference in advanced pyrotechnic technologies and safety systems.

Our expansion into the Middle East reflects a long-term commitment to the region — combining European craftsmanship, cutting-edge innovation, and strict safety standards with local expertise and regulatory compliance. We are here to build partnerships, not just deliver shows.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which countries do you operate in?

RUGGIERI Middle East operates across the region including the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman, and Bahrain. Wheras Ruggieri main office operates in Europe, US, Africa and Asia, for other locations our team will assess feasibility and regulatory requirements.

Are your fireworks shows compliant with Middle East regulations?

Yes. All RUGGIERI productions strictly comply with local regulations, permitting processes, and international safety standards. We work closely with relevant authorities in each country.

Do you handle permits and approvals?

Absolutely. Our team manages the full administrative and regulatory coordination, including permits, safety documentation, and authority approvals.

Can you combine fireworks with drones or lasers?

Yes. We specialize in multimedia shows integrating fireworks, LED or pyro drone swarms, lasers, lighting, and sound design.

Do you design custom shows?

Every RUGGIERI show is tailor-made. We design each production around your venue, audience, theme, and objectives.

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend early consultation, especially for large-scale or peak-season events. Availability depends on production complexity and calendar scheduling.

Is safety guaranteed?

Safety is our highest priority. Our licensed experts, highly trained engineering teams, and rigorously certified systems reflect an uncompromising commitment to audience safety and the protection of every venue we operate in. Our exceptionally high safety standards have led to our selection on multiple occasions to design and execute fireworks displays at UNESCO World Heritage Sites, meeting the strictest international compliance and preservation requirements.
