Questions

Answers before you ask.

How long until it is live?
Most builds go live within about 30 days. I handle the setup and integration; you spend a few hours reviewing and approving, not weeks managing it.
Is this HIPAA compliant?
Yes. Builds use HIPAA-aligned tooling and handling, with Business Associate Agreements in place wherever protected health information moves. Any specific legal question about your situation is one I route to your own counsel.
Is automated texting allowed under TCPA?
Outreach is consent-based and honors opt-out, structured around TCPA. Win-back and recall messages go only to patients who already have a relationship with your practice and have not opted out.
Will my patients dislike talking to AI?
Done well, most patients cannot tell, and they far prefer an instant answer at nine at night to a voicemail box. The system hands off to a human whenever it should.
Does it work with my scheduler and CRM?
It is built on top of the tools you already use, including Elation, Healthie, and most major schedulers and CRMs. Open-API systems integrate cleanly, and I will tell you upfront if yours has limits that create manual steps.
What if it does not work?
Then you pay nothing. If the system does not bring your practice at least 15 additional patients within 90 days of going live, you get a full refund.
