Questions

Frequently asked questions

How soon will I hear back?

We aim to review and answer requests within 24 hours on working days. For more complex work, we may ask a short follow-up question first.

Can I send photos of the problem?

Yes. Once we receive your request, we can invite you to send photos by email or WhatsApp. Clear photos often help us provide a more accurate quote.

Do you only handle woodwork?

Woodwork is where we began, but our network also handles many practical home tasks including drilling, mounting, minor repairs and installation work.
