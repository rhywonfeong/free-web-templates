Frequently asked questions

Don’t see your question? Call or email—we’re happy to help.

What IDs are accepted for notarization?

Valid government photo ID, like a CA driver’s license, passport, or military ID. It must be current or within California’s allowed window.

Do you offer Remote Online Notarization (RON)?

California notaries can’t do RON right now. We do in‑person notarization and can help with witnesses.

How many process‑serve attempts are included?

It depends on the package. Standard service includes multiple attempts at different times and days, with notes and photos when helpful.

What’s the turnaround for document preparation?

Many projects finish in 24–72 hours after we get your info. Rush options may be available.

Do you give legal advice?

We are not attorneys and cannot give legal advice. As LDAs, we prepare and file documents at your direction and provide self‑help services only.
