Common Questions

How do I submit footage?
We provide a dedicated Trello board and a Google Drive/Dropbox portal. Simply upload your raw files and fill out a quick brief card.
Can I pause my subscription?
Yes! If you have a slow month, pause your subscription and resume when you have more footage. No penalties.
Do you offer stock footage?
Absolutely. We have subscriptions to premium stock libraries (Storyblocks, Envato, etc.) and include them in your videos at no extra cost.
What software do you use?
We primarily use Adobe Premiere Pro and After Effects. We can provide project files upon request for an additional fee.
