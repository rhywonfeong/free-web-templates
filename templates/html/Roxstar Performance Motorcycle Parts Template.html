07 / Essential answers

Questions,
answered.

The details that matter before you choose an exhaust, confirm fitment, or place an order.

What's the difference between a slip-on and a full system exhaust? +

A slip-on exhaust replaces only the muffler and connects to the motorcycle's factory header, making it the simpler, lower-cost upgrade. A full system replaces the header, mid-pipe, and silencer together for a larger performance gain but a more involved fitment. Roxstar sources both options across its Dominator, Brock's Performance, and Scorpion ranges.

Do I need to retune or remap after fitting a performance exhaust? +

It depends on the system. A slip-on typically needs no remapping, since it does not significantly change backpressure. A full system changes airflow enough that fuel-injected bikes usually benefit from a remap or fuel controller to avoid running lean. Roxstar confirms the guidance per order.

How do I know an exhaust will fit my exact motorcycle? +

Every order is matched against the manufacturer's fitment table using your bike's make, model, and engine year before dispatch. Roxstar applies the same verification step across every brand it distributes.

Is Roxstar an authorised distributor, or are these grey-market parts? +

Roxstar Performance is an authorised South African distributor for every brand it carries, including Dominator Exhausts, Brock's Performance, and Scorpion Exhausts. Manufacturer warranties remain intact: 5 years on Dominator and a lifetime guarantee plus 50% crash-damage replacement on Scorpion.

Will a performance exhaust make my bike too loud or road-illegal? +

Roxstar checks the manufacturer's published tone and decibel data before recommending a system, so riders can weigh sound output against South African road-use requirements. Decibel figures vary by brand and silencer type; ask a fitment specialist about your specific model.

What's the difference between stainless steel and titanium exhaust systems? +

Stainless steel is heavier but more affordable and highly corrosion-resistant; Dominator systems use full stainless-steel construction. Titanium is significantly lighter and develops a heat-bluing patina over time, but costs more. It suits riders prioritising weight savings over price.

Does Roxstar sell performance parts beyond exhaust systems? +

Yes. Alongside its core exhaust distribution business, Roxstar sources performance parts for sportbike, Harley-Davidson V-twin, and adventure touring motorcycles, including custom solutions where a standard off-the-shelf part is not available.

Do you deliver outside Cape Town? +

Yes. Roxstar dispatches nationwide from Claremont, Cape Town—including to Johannesburg, Durban, and every other South African province—once an order has been sourced and fitment-verified.

How long does an order take from verification to dispatch? +

After verifying fitment against your make, model, and engine year, Roxstar sources the order through its established import pipeline and dispatches it from Claremont. The process aims to keep the wait to a minimum; a fitment specialist can provide a quote and current lead-time estimate for your part.
