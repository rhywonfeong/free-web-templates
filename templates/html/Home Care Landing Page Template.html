Frequently Asked Questions

Is it really 100% free?

Yes. If you or your loved one qualifies for Florida Medicaid or the Long-Term Care (LTC) MedWaiver program, the government pays us directly for the services. You will never receive a bill from us.

Do I need to be on Medicaid already?

Not necessarily. If you aren't sure if you qualify, contact us anyway. We can help assess your situation and guide you on how to apply for the necessary programs if you meet the income and health requirements.

How fast can care start?

If you are already approved through the MedWaiver program, care can usually start within 48 to 72 hours. We move quickly because we know how urgent finding help can be.

Can I choose my caregiver?

Absolutely. We try to make the best match based on language, personality, and needs, but if for any reason you want to switch caregivers, just let us know. Your comfort is our top priority.
