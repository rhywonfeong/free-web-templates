Rules & Regulations

THE CODE OF CONDUCT

Simple Rules. Strict Enforcement. Professional Standards.

Section 01
Account Specs
  • $50,000 Virtual Starting Balance
  • 3% Daily Loss Limit (Hard Breach)
  • 6% Maximum Overall Drawdown
  • MatchTrader or DXtrade Platforms
Section 02
Trading Rules
  • Minimum 3 individual trades required
  • No single trade > 50% of total profit
  • Highest % profit at end of round wins
  • All positions closed before round end
Section 03
Fair Play
  • Hidden trades in 1v1 (revealed at end)
  • Strictly one account per person
  • Automated anti-cheat scan every 10s
  • No HFT or latency arbitrage
Section 04
Tie-Breakers
  • Lowest maximum drawdown recorded
  • Highest win rate percentage
  • Shortest average trade duration
  • Most consistent lot sizing

WARNING: Any breach of the Code of Conduct results in immediate disqualification and a lifetime ban from all Hola Prime events.
