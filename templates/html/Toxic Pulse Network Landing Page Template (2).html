Sentinel Ground

Sentinel Brick is a public infrastructure system for future e-waste communities, using biosensor bricks to detect heavy metal pollution and respond through visual alerts and remediation materials.

01 / Context

Alaba Market Ground Zero

By 2036, unregulated e-waste burning near Alaba International Market has saturated the local atmosphere and soil with dangerous levels of lead, mercury, and cadmium. The Toxic Pulse Network was deployed to track these invisible threats in real-time, mapping the systemic flow of pollutants across Lagos to protect vulnerable communities.

02 / Infrastructure

System Architecture

Sentinel Brick

Biosensor pavement bricks embedded directly into urban roads. These nodes constantly analyze soil seepage and airborne heavy metals.

Civic Terminal

Public warning displays projecting localized toxicity data. Pedestrians receive real-time visual alerts before entering heavily polluted zones.

Response Platform

A centralized governmental dashboard aggregating grid data to deploy emergency environmental cleanup teams to critical areas.
