Scene 06 · FAQ

Answers in focus

As you open each question, the background softens and the answer steps into the light — like a rack focus between curiosity and clarity.

How do we begin a new project with SharpEye Films?

A quick call, a shared reference board, and a rough timeline.

We start with a discovery session — remote or in person — to map goals, constraints and references. From there, we build a treatment, schedule and budget, then lock the production timeline before moving into pre-production.

What budgets and timelines do you usually work with?

Scalable from lean social shoots to multi-day campaigns.

We work on projects ranging from fast-moving single-day shoots to complex productions with multi-city schedules. Tell us your budget, and we’ll design a production that respects it while maintaining craft.

Can you adapt content for multiple formats and platforms?

Yes — we plan framing and coverage for every final output.

Every project is planned with output in mind: 16:9, 9:16, square crops, cutdowns and language versions. We can deliver master files as well as ready-to-publish sets for your channels.
