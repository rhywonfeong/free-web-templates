Roots in Samokov,
The Potato Capital.

Nestled in the foothills of the Rila Mountains, Samokov is Bulgaria's historic potato capital. The unique microclimate and mineral-rich soil created a legacy that we have stewarded for over a century.

Today, as a third-generation farming business , we honor the traditions of our ancestors while embracing the future. We don't just grow potatoes; we cultivate a heritage of resilience and quality passed down through hands that have known this soil for 100 years.
