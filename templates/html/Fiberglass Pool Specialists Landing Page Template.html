    FAQ

    Good to know.

    How much does a fiberglass pool cost?

    Your quote depends on the pool size, site access, excavation, equipment and surrounding work. Tell us about your backyard and preferred model so we can define the scope and prepare a project-specific quote.

    How long does installation take?

    The schedule is specific to your site, permits, preparation and chosen scope. Your proposal will outline the installation stages and timing before you commit.

    Can I use a fiberglass pool in a cold climate?

    Tell us where your pool will be installed so we can review the site and climate requirements. Seasonal closing and winter care should follow the instructions supplied for your shell and equipment.

    What maintenance will my pool need?

    Plan for regular water testing, cleaning and equipment checks. We’ll discuss the care instructions for your selected pool and filtration system, including seasonal opening and closing.

    What warranty is included?

    Coverage depends on the selected shell, equipment and installation scope. Ask for the written warranty terms with your quote, including what is covered, any exclusions and care requirements.

    Why choose a fiberglass pool?

    A fiberglass pool uses a preformed, one-piece shell with an integrated interior finish. Compare available shapes, steps, site access and your preferred layout to decide whether it is the right fit for your project.
