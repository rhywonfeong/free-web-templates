01 We map the gaps

Where are the blocks in your customer journey? The leaks in your pipeline? The constraints on your BD capacity? We start by identifying where systematised workflows would make the biggest difference.

02 We capture your strategic knowledge

How do your best people actually think about accounts? What signals matter? What determines which offering to lead with? We capture that judgment through discovery sessions focused on the gaps that matter most.

03 We build around it

Knowledge systems that encode your team's expertise. Workflows that apply it to research accounts, select the right angle, and generate outreach that reflects your thinking, not generic AI patterns.

04 You focus on where your expertise adds real value

By making strategic proactive growth operational, you can finally focus time on what actually moves the needle: building relationships and sharpening your strategy.

05 It evolves

New angle resonates? New offering launches? Team member brings insight? That learning gets built into the system. It compounds over time rather than degrading.

"Which offering should we lead with?"

We systematise that diagnostic judgment so it runs across hundreds of accounts without losing the quality.

  • Have multiple distinct offerings or service lines
  • Need to determine which to lead with per account
  • Currently send generic "we do X, Y, Z" messaging

Relevance construction

"Why does what we do matter to you?"

Your expertise applies across diverse sectors — but making it relevant to each specific prospect requires diagnostic judgment that lives in your best people's heads. Prospects don't naturally recognise they need what you do.

We encode that diagnostic logic so each account receives outreach that demonstrates understanding, not just awareness.

This applies if you:

  • Have deep expertise applied across diverse, unrelated sectors
  • Prospects don't self-identify as needing your services
  • Junior BD can't replicate the quality of senior outreach

Qualification complexity

We encode that qualification logic so it surfaces the right accounts across your full addressable market.

  • Can't find qualified accounts through standard filters
