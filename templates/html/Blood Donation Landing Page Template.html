How RoktoChai Works

A streamlined process designed to eliminate delays and connect patients with donors seamlessly.

1. Post a Request

Fill out a simple form with blood group, location, and urgency level.

2. Smart Matching

Our system alerts eligible donors nearby via SMS and push notifications.

3. Connect & Save

Donor accepts, details are shared securely, and a life is saved.

Why trust RoktoChai?

We've removed the friction from blood donation. No more endless scrolling on social media or making desperate calls.

Verified Donors Only

Every donor profile is verified with phone numbers and eligibility checks to ensure safety.

Location-Based Routing

We only notify donors who are geographically close to the hospital to minimize travel time.

Privacy First

Your contact details are kept private until a match is mutually accepted by both parties.
