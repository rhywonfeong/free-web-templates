Our story

Born from getting lost in the market

In 2019, our founders lost a small fortune not to a bad trade — but to bad information. Twelve tabs, three exchanges, one phishing site. It wasn't the market that failed them; it was the map.

CoinCompass began as a weekend spreadsheet shared between friends. Today it's the guide we wish we'd had: one honest surface where data, education and self-custody live together — built on the belief that clarity, not hype, is what makes people wealthy over time.
