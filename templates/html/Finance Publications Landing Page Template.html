The Psychology of Wealth

A profound exploration into the behavioral forces that shape our financial decisions. Discover why we act the way we do with money, and how to build mental frameworks for lasting prosperity.

Wealth is rarely just a numbers game. It is deeply intertwined with human psychology, biases, and emotional triggers. In this authoritative volume, Dr. Vance dissects the hidden scripts that govern our financial lives, translating complex behavioral economic theories into actionable clarity.

Whether you are navigating turbulent markets, planning for intergenerational wealth transfers, or simply trying to optimize your monthly savings rate, understanding the psychological underpinnings of your decisions is the highest-leverage investment you can make.

Who this book is for
Designed for investors, financial planners, and individuals seeking to master the emotional components of money management. It serves as both a theoretical text and a practical field guide.
Key learning outcomes
Readers will learn to identify common cognitive biases like loss aversion and confirmation bias, build robust decision-making frameworks resistant to emotional shocks, and understand the structural psychology of market bubbles.
What you will learn
Over 12 comprehensive chapters, you'll dive deep into risk perception, the neurobiology of financial stress, and the architecture of choice in modern digital banking environments.
About the Author

Dr. Alistair Vance

A leading voice in behavioral economics, Dr. Vance has spent two decades researching how cognitive biases impact financial markets. He wrote this book to bridge the gap between academic research and practical wealth management.

Markets in Motion

A sweeping historical perspective on economic cycles, designed to give modern investors the context they need to weather volatility with confidence.

Markets do not move in straight lines; they breathe in cycles. Elena Rostova provides a meticulously researched yet highly accessible guide to recognizing where we are in the broader economic cycle.

By studying historical parallels without getting bogged down in academic jargon, readers will develop an intuitive sense for systemic risk and opportunity. This book is an antidote to short-term market anxiety.

Who this book is for
Ideal for portfolio managers, active investors, and anyone looking to move past daily market noise to understand the macro-forces driving asset prices.

Elena Rostova

Macroeconomist & Historian
