Do you wait at the destination?
Yes, drop/wait/return services are available. Please ensure to text 10 minutes before you are ready to leave so the engine can be warmed up.
What are your rates?
Rates are extremely competitive. Usually involves a polite "thank you", a hug, or occasionally picking up the tab for a drive-thru coffee.
