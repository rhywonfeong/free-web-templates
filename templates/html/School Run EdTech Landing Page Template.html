Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember information about your visit, like your preferences or how you navigated the site.

What cookies does School Run use?

We currently use two cookies on this site.

Cookie Name Purpose Duration
ph_* (PostHog) Analytics - helps us understand how visitors use the site, which pages are viewed, and whether people are completing the sign-up form Session / up to 1 year
cookieConsent Remembers whether you have accepted or declined cookies so we do not ask again 1 year

Why we use analytics cookies

We use PostHog to collect anonymous data about how parents and schools use this website. This helps us understand what is working, what is confusing, and how to improve the experience. We do not use this data for advertising or share it with third parties.

Your choices

You can accept or decline analytics cookies using the banner when you first visit the site. If you change your mind, you can clear your browser cookies and your preference will be reset on your next visit. You can also opt out of PostHog tracking directly at posthog.com/privacy .

Questions?

If you have any questions about how we use cookies, get in touch at support@schoolrun.io
