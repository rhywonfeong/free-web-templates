Feeding basics that keep it simple

Start with a consistent routine and adjust based on your pet’s energy, age, and body condition. If you’re switching foods, transition gradually.

Dogs
Match food to life stage; watch treat portions.
Cats

Grooming between appointments

A short brushing routine helps reduce matting and keeps the coat comfortable.

  • Brush gently — especially behind ears and under legs.
  • Keep nails trimmed to support comfort and mobility.
  • Nervous pet? Keep sessions short and reward calm behavior.
