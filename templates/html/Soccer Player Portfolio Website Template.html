About Parker

A standout athlete from York Community High School, Parker Gawne built a reputation as a high-impact player capable of changing the game from multiple positions. Whether deployed as a lethal forward or a lockdown outside back, his signature trait is exceptional speed and work ethic.

Parker played club soccer for Sockers FC Chicago for nine years, developing a sophisticated tactical understanding. His high school career was punctuated by deep postseason runs in 2018 and 2019, including memorable back-to-back hat tricks during his junior year breakout season.

Most recently, Parker has taken his talents to the semi-professional level with Wisłoka Chicago, scoring a crucial goal in the UPSL Midwest Central Play-off semifinal in July 2025.
