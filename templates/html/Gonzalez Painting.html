Frequently Asked Questions ❔

Everything you need to know about our painting services.

How long does a typical painting project take?
Most interior jobs take 2-4 days, while exterior painting usually takes 3-5 days depending on the size of your home and weather conditions in the Denver area.
Do you provide the paint or do I need to buy it?
We provide all professional-grade paint and materials as part of your estimate. We work with premium brands to ensure a long-lasting, high-quality finish, but we are happy to accommodate specific brand requests.
Are you licensed and insured?
We are not yet licensed and insured. With our gurantee your property is completely protected from start to finish throughout the entire project.
Do I need to move my furniture?
We kindly ask that you move smaller items and breakables before we arrive. Our professional team will carefully move your larger furniture to the center of the room and cover it completely with protective drop cloths.
How much will it cost to paint my house?
Every project is unique. The cost depends on the size of the area, the condition of the surfaces, and the type of paint used. We offer free, detailed estimates so you know exactly what to expect before we start.
Do you offer a warranty on your painting services?
Yes, we stand behind our work with a comprehensive warranty. We ensure high-quality materials and workmanship to guarantee long-lasting results.
Can you help me choose the right colors?
Absolutely! We offer color consultation services to help you find the perfect shades and finishes to match your style and enhance your home's aesthetic.
