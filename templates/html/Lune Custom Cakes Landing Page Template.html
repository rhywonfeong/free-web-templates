Everything you need to know.

We recommend booking 6-9 months in advance for peak wedding season (May-October). However, we occasionally have last-minute availability, so please don't hesitate to inquire.

Yes, we offer a selection of delicious gluten-free and vegan flavors. Please note our kitchen processes nuts, dairy, and gluten, so we cannot guarantee a completely allergen-free environment for severe allergies.
