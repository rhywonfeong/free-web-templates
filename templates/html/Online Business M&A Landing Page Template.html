02 — Process

How It Works

No Surprises Standard

Known risks are disclosed early. Clean documentation is required. Buyers get a consistent deal package every time.

1

Intake + Qualification

We review your high-level numbers, business model, and operational setup to confirm market viability. If you aren't ready, we tell you exactly why.

2

Standard Deal Package

We build a clean, standardized data room. Verified trailing 12-month financials, clear add-backs, traffic data, and early risk disclosures are organized for immediate buyer review.

3

Buyer Conversations

Your anonymized listing is presented to our vetted buyer list. Serious parties sign NDAs to access the full package and schedule management calls.

4

LOI → Diligence → Close

Because documents are prepared upfront, due diligence moves quickly post-LOI. We support both parties through final agreements and asset transfer.

03 — Fit Criteria

Who we represent.
