How to Choose the Right Security Service

Selecting the appropriate security provider requires careful evaluation of several critical factors to ensure optimal protection.

1

Licensing and Compliance

Ensure the company is registered with the Private Security Regulatory Authority (PSRA) and employs vetted, licensed personnel.

2

Experience and Training

Look for providers with a proven track record and operatives trained in close protection, first aid, and threat assessment.

3

Customization

Choose a firm that tailors services to your specific needs, whether for short-term events or long-term protection.

4

Reputation

Check reviews, testimonials, and references to verify reliability and customer satisfaction levels.

5

Cost Transparency

Opt for providers offering clear pricing structures and detailed service agreements with no hidden fees.

6

Local Knowledge

Select providers with deep understanding of Kenya's security landscape and regional risk factors.
