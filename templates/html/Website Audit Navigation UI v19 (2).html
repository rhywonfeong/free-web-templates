Priority Recommendations

Ordered by potential impact on conversion rate

1

Add Trust Badges and Security Indicators

High Impact

Your website lacks visible trust signals which can reduce conversion rates by up to 30%. Add SSL badges, payment security icons, and industry certifications near your forms and checkout areas.

Specific Actions:
  • Add SSL certificate badge in footer
  • Display payment security icons (Stripe, PayPal badges)
  • Add "Trusted by X customers" counter
  • Include industry certifications or awards
Estimated impact: +25-35% conversion rate Implementation time: 2-4 hours
2

Optimize Call-to-Action Buttons

Medium Impact

Your primary CTA buttons could be more prominent and action-oriented. Current buttons blend too much with the design and use weak action words.

Specific Actions:
  • Change "Learn More" to "Get Started Free" or "Start Your Trial"
  • Increase button size by 20-30%
  • Use higher contrast colors (current: #4984FF, suggested: #FF4444)
  • Add subtle animations or hover effects
Estimated impact: +15-20% conversion rate Implementation time: 1-2 hours
3

Enhance Social Proof Section

Medium Impact

Your testimonials section exists but lacks credibility indicators and variety. Only 3 testimonials are shown without photos or company information.

Specific Actions:
  • Add customer photos to testimonials
  • Include company names and job titles
  • Add star ratings or review scores
  • Include case studies with specific results
  • Add company logos of satisfied customers
Estimated impact: +10-18% conversion rate Implementation time: 3-5 hours
4

Add Urgency and Scarcity Elements

Low Impact

Create urgency to encourage immediate action. Your current design doesn't provide visitors with compelling reasons to act now vs. later.

Specific Actions:
  • Add limited-time offer banners
  • Show "X people viewed this today" counters
  • Add countdown timers for special offers
  • Include "Only X spots left" for limited offerings
Estimated impact: +5-12% conversion rate Implementation time: 2-3 hours
