Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set up table reservations?
Navigate to Tables & Floor section, click on any table, and select "Reserve". You can set the time, customer name, and party size.
How do I generate QR codes for tables?
Go to Menu & QR section, click "Generate QR" button. You can create unique QR codes for each table that link to your digital menu.
How do I process a refund?
Find the order in the Orders section, click the eye icon to view details, and select "Request Refund". Manager or Owner approval is required for refunds above ₹500.
