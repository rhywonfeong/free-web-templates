The Connecticut Climate

Brutal freeze-thaw cycles and coastal humidity.

If you live in Fairfield County, your home faces harsh New England winters and humid summers. Over time, that leads to cracks, warping, water damage, and energy loss.

Water damage is the #1 reason Connecticut homes need expensive repairs. When siding issues are ignored, small cracks let moisture behind your walls, insulation gets soaked, and wood rot spreads.

Warning Signs

  • Cracks or gaps
  • Warping or buckling
  • Fading or peeling
  • Interior water stains
Potential Impact

Energy Bill Spike

Damaged siding compromises insulation, driving up heating costs in winter.

Structural Rot

Moisture trapped behind walls causes frame rot that costs tens of thousands to fix.

Mold Growth

Hidden moisture creates the perfect environment for toxic mold to spread.
