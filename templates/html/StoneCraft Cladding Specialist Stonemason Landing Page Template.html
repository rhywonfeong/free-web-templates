A carefully planned installation process

Proper preparation and fixing methods are essential for stone cladding to perform and look its best. The process is transparent from first call through to final clean-down.

  1. 1

    Site visit & stone advice

    Measure-up, substrate inspection and discussion of how you want the finished cladding to look. Samples can be brought to your property or photos reviewed remotely.

  2. 2

    Detailed quotation

    Clear written quotation covering materials, labour, preparation work and timescales. Any optional extras such as lighting or copings are itemised.

  3. 3

    Preparation & fixing

    Substrate is prepared, level lines established and stone is set out to minimise cuts in visible areas. Appropriate adhesives, mechanical fixings and expansion joints are used where needed.

  4. 4

    Finishing & clean-down

    Joints are pointed or brushed clean to suit the style of stone. Surfaces and surrounding areas are washed down, with optional sealing if appropriate for the stone type.

Why choose a specialist stonemason?

Stone cladding isn’t just about fixing panels to a wall. A trained stonemason understands:

  • How stone behaves outdoors

    Allowing the substrate to breathe, avoiding trapped moisture and specifying appropriate sealing where needed.

  • Visual balance and coursing

    Keeping bed joints level, mixing pallets correctly and blending tones so the wall looks natural, not repetitive.

  • Long-term performance

    Specifying fixings and backing systems that stand up to frost, movement and changing regulations.
