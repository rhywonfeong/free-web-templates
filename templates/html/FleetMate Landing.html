Reservations

Booking & Capacity Management

Manage all bookings with ease, track cabin availability, view schedules, and move reservations across vessels with full control.

  • Calendar overview of all ships and departures (raster), with booking/payment statuses.
  • Cross-cabin view of all ships and cabins with drag & drop for guest relocation.
  • List of all individual & charter bookings – searchable, filterable tables with full details and editing.
  • Downloadable PDF of the guest manifest (boarding list), including any special notes like dietary needs or room requests.

Frequently asked question

Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions about FleetMate, implementation, and ongoing support.

Who is this ERP system designed for?
FleetMate is designed specifically for cruise agencies and operators. It supports booking & sales teams, operations managers, finance and admin staff who need one shared system to manage reservations, fleet capacity, payments, and guest communication.
Can we customize the system to fit our processes?
Yes. FleetMate is built to adapt to your workflows. During onboarding, we configure routes, pricing models, seasons, service levels, and user roles to match how your agency already operates.
What kind of support do we get after purchasing?
You get dedicated onboarding, training for your team, and ongoing product support. We assist with yearly updates (seasons, pricing, routes) and are available for questions as your operation grows.
Is the system cloud-based or do we need to install it?
FleetMate is cloud-based, so there’s nothing to install on your local computers. Your team can access it securely from any modern browser.
What happens after I request a demo?
Our team will contact you within 2–5 business days to schedule a video call. We’ll walk you through the system, show relevant workflows for your agency, and discuss next steps if you decide to move forward.
