Case Study Focus

B2B Onboarding Redesign

Reduced drop-offs and improved time-to-value for new users.

Role Senior Product & Visual Designer
Timeline 6 months
Team Product, Engineering, Leadership
Outcome
  • Improved onboarding completion by 23%
  • Reduced support tickets post-launch
  • Enabled faster iteration for future features

The Problem

The product had strong demand, but onboarding complexity was slowing activation and creating friction for new users.

This wasn’t just a UX issue — it directly impacted retention, support load, and sales confidence.

Role & Ownership

I led the redesign end-to-end — from problem framing and research synthesis to interaction design, visual systems, and handoff.

I worked closely with product and engineering to balance usability, feasibility, and long-term scalability.

Key Decisions & Trade-offs

Simplified the onboarding flow

Reduced steps while preserving critical setup requirements.

Prioritized system flexibility over visual novelty

Ensured patterns could scale beyond the initial release.

Chose clarity over cleverness

Avoided micro-interactions that added complexity without value.

Outcome & Reflection

The redesign improved activation, reduced confusion, and created a clearer foundation for future product growth.

If revisiting this today, I’d invest more time in testing edge cases earlier — especially for enterprise users.
