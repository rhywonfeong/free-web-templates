Connecting Roots to Global Impact

Founded in India over two decades ago, NDF has been a catalyst for change in rural and semi-urban landscapes. Our mission has always been simple: empower the individual to transform the community.

As our impact grew, so did our global family. The U.S. chapter was established to bridge the resources, expertise, and passion of the American diaspora with the on-ground realities in India. We ensure transparency, governance, and measurable outcomes for every dollar donated.
