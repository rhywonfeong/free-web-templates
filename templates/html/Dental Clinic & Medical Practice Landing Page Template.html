About Burton Dental Care

Caring For Your Smile Since Day One

Burton Dental Care is an NHS dental practice in West Didsbury, Manchester. We believe your teeth are precious and aim to offer affordable dental care to help you maintain and enhance your smile.

New patients of all ages are warmly welcomed. Whether you were recommended to us by a friend or relative, or you're simply searching for a dentist offering affordable but excellent quality dentistry, our friendly team will try our best to offer care that meets your needs.

Modern & Clean Surgeries

Our surgeries are modern, clean, and designed with patient comfort in mind.

Friendly, Professional Team

We provide personalised professional care to every one of our patients.

A Full Range of NHS & Private Treatments

We offer a complete range of NHS dental treatment, plus alternative private and cosmetic options where appropriate.

General Dentistry

Fillings, root canal treatment, extractions, and treatment of gum disease.

Crowns, Bridges & Veneers

Restore and strengthen your teeth with crowns, onlays, veneers, and bridges.

Dentures

Custom dentures and denture repairs to restore your smile and confidence.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic white fillings, cosmetic crowns, and professional teeth whitening.

Dental Implants

Private implant options to replace missing teeth with lasting results.

Hygiene Appointments

Scale and polish appointments to keep your gums and teeth healthy.
