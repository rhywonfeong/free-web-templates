Our Mission

Reduce disputes through absolute clarity.

We believe that business relationships thrive on trust, and trust requires a neutral environment where terms are clear, verifiable, and permanent.

Our Philosophy: The Neutral Witness

In traditional agreements, one party usually holds the pen, the platform, or the power. Middleman was built to act as an impartial observer—a digital witness that belongs to neither party but serves both equally. We do not judge the content; we simply ensure it is exactly what was agreed upon.

Why Middleman Exists

To eliminate the 'he said, she said' of digital transactions. By capturing agreements in a structured format, we prevent the ambiguity that leads to broken contracts and fractured relationships.

Our Trust Statement

We will never monetize your data, lock you out of your agreements, or introduce dark patterns that favor one party over another. Clarity and neutrality are our only products.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Middleman legally binding?

Middleman acts as a verifiable record keeper. While we don't provide legal advice, our timestamped, mutual-approval logs provide strong cryptographic evidence of intent and agreement.

What happens if someone changes their mind?

Once an agreement is active, it cannot be altered. To change terms, both parties must agree to draft and approve an amendment through the same transparent process.
