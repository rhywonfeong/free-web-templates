Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know what treatment is right for me?
Every journey begins with a comprehensive consultation. We use digital scanning and 3D imaging to assess your oral health, discuss your goals, and present a customized treatment plan tailored to your needs.
Are your procedures painful?
Patient comfort is our top priority. We utilize the latest techniques, modern anesthetics, and offer sedation options for anxious patients to ensure a pain-free, relaxing experience.
How long do porcelain veneers last?
With proper care and routine dental visits, our high-quality porcelain veneers can last 10 to 15 years or more. They are highly resistant to stains and incredibly durable.
