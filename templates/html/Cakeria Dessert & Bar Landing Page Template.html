About Us

Cooking will always be my passion in life. I studied pastry for 2 years and produced Cakeria which is loved by many people.

It started with me being on a sugar diet but wanting to eat cake, since then I have been exploring the recipes.

When I uploaded it to social media, it turned out to attract the attention of many people, especially those who were on a sugar diet too.

Made With LovE

Cakeria was founded in 2020 and has developed with love from the community. We provide delicious cakes with premium organic ingredients and are suitable for diets. 100% organic, 100% love.
