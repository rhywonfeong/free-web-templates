I’m AJ Galaviz, a graphic designer driven by pure passion and an obsessive curiosity for how ideas take shape visually. Everything I know has been learned through hands-on experience through experimentation, long nights, failed attempts, refinement, and an unwillingness to settle for “good enough.” Design, for me, was never about checking a box or following a prescribed path. It was about the pull to create and the need to understand how visuals can communicate something deeper than words alone.

How Can I Help?

Step 1

Understanding First

Before starting a project I like to take time to understand client goals, constraints, and vision before design begins. Clarity at the start leads to stronger results.

Clarify goals, constraints, and expectations
Identify what success should look like
Step 2

Strategic Direction

I help define a clear visual direction so decisions feel intentional, not reactive. This keeps the work focused and aligned.

Establish visual direction and tone
Align design decisions with the bigger picture
Step 3

Collaborative Refinement

Feedback is part of the process, not an afterthought. We refine together until the vision feels right.

Review, adjust, and refine through feedback
Strengthen the work without losing the vision

Finished With Intent

Deliver polished, ready-to-use design
