How quickly does the AI respond?

The AI responds instantly, typically within 10-30 seconds. This ensures you capture leads while their interest is highest, significantly increasing conversion rates compared to waiting hours for a human response.

Does it integrate with my scheduling software?

Yes! We integrate with most major gym management platforms including GymDesk, Mindbody, Spark, ZenPlanner, and more. The AI can check your real-time availability and book appointments directly into your calendar.

What happens if the AI doesn't know the answer?

While the AI handles 95% of inquiries autonomously, it knows when to escalate. If a question is too complex or specific, the AI will tag you or your staff to step in seamlessly and take over the conversation from the mobile app.

Is there a long-term contract?

No, we believe the results should speak for themselves. We operate on a month-to-month basis with no long-term lock-ins. We also offer a guarantee—if we don't generate bookings, you don't pay.

How long does onboarding take?

We can typically get your AI agent trained and live within 3-5 business days. We'll need some basic info about your gym (class schedule, pricing, FAQ), and we handle the technical setup.
