Frequently Asked Questions

EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW

Who can apply?

Founders, registered studios, digital agencies, and independent creators operating within the defined creative economy sectors looking to formalize and scale their business.

What sectors are eligible?

Eligible sectors include IT & Software, Gaming, Fashion & Design, Media & Film, Graphic Design, Music & Arts, Education Tech, and Commercial Photography.

What documents are required?

Standard business registration (or intent to register), a detailed business plan or pitch deck, and portfolio materials proving prior work or capability in your sector.

How long does the review process take?

Once all documentation is submitted, the expert panel typically reviews and issues a decision within 15 business days. You can track progress entirely online.

What benefits are available?

Key benefits include a 0% income tax rate, simplified customs procedures, access to modern infrastructure, and eligibility for state and partner growth funding.

What facilities can residents access?

Approved residents gain subsidized or free access to co-working spaces, high-speed connectivity, filming studios, sound labs, and meeting spaces in 15+ regional hubs.

Are startups eligible for grants?

Yes, highly innovative projects demonstrating clear export potential or high job creation can apply for direct ecosystem grants and accelerated VC introductions.

What happens after approval?

You receive a digital Resident Certificate, immediate activation of tax benefits, and an invitation to an onboarding session to access facilities and networking events.
