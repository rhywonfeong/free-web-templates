Our Mission

Redefining the patient experience with transparency.

At Lumina Dental, we believe a visit to the dentist should be restorative, not stressful. Founded by Dr. Sarah Jenkins, our clinic was built on the pillars of transparency, cutting-edge technology, and compassionate care.

We utilize low-radiation digital X-rays, intraoral cameras, and AI-assisted diagnostics to ensure you understand your oral health as well as we do. No hidden fees, no unnecessary treatments.
