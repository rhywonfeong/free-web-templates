Seijaku

One continuous walk. Through the entrance garden, over the threshold, and on through every room to the garden at the back — without a single cut.

IThe Genkan

Leave the world at the step

A slate tataki, a single cedar step and the scent of hinoki. Shoes come off here, and so does the day.

Tataki earth floor · Keyaki step beam · Hinoki lattice doors
IIThe Corridor

Light through paper

Shoji on the garden side turn afternoon sun into a soft, even glow that moves with the maples outside.

Cedar planks · Washi shoji · Kumiko lattice
IIIThe Zashiki

Twelve mats, one view

The formal room is measured in tatami. A tokonoma holds a scroll, a single seasonal branch and nothing else.

12 tatami · Tokonoma · Igusa rush · Juraku plaster
IVThe Tea Room

Four and a half mats

A low ceiling, a sunken hearth and just enough light. Everything here is smaller so that attention grows.

4.5 tatami · Sunken ro · Iron kettle
VThe Engawa

Neither inside nor out

The veranda runs beneath the eave: a metre of cedar between the rooms and the courtyard, warm underfoot by late afternoon.

Hinoki boards · Deep kawara eave · Kutsunugi stone
VIThe Courtyard

A garden held by the house

Moss, seven stones and a tsukubai. Stepping stones set the pace of the crossing, one deliberate step at a time.

Tsuboniwa · Moss · Granite tobi-ishi · Stone lantern
VIIThe Living Room

Old frame, new life

Exposed cedar beams, a wide timber floor and a hearth. Contemporary comfort inside a traditional frame.

Sugi beams · Irori hearth · Glazed doors to the courtyard
VIIIThe Bedroom

A room for sleeping

Tatami, a low bed and paper light. Nothing that does not belong.

Tatami · Andon lamps · Indigo linen
IXThe Zen Garden

Raked every morning

Eight stones set into moss, and gravel drawn into rings around each one. Nothing grows here that was not chosen.

Karesansui · Raked shirakawa gravel · Eight stones · Moss islands
XThe Bath

Hinoki and steam

A cypress tub facing the garden, filled to the brim. The wood scents the water, and the water darkens the wood.

Hinoki tub · Slate floor · Sliding glass to the garden
