About Mike Ferry

Mike Ferry founded The Mike Ferry Organization in June 1975 after eight years as National Training Director at Nightingale-Conant, where he was responsible for the sales activities of 1,300 people. He had studied under Earl Nightingale directly. He walked into real estate, saw an industry running on instinct and hope, and built the first coaching company in the history of the profession.

Today, Mike leads MFO alongside his wife Sabrina Ferry, who serves as President. Every coach on the MFO team is hand-selected by both of them. At 80 years old, Mike still runs events, still records weekly training, and still asks every agent who questions whether the system works: "Are you going to let an 80-year-old man outwork you?"

Does MFO work outside the US?
MFO coaching clients operate across North America, Mexico, and Europe. The system is built on sales fundamentals that don't change with geography — if people are buying and selling homes in your market, the system applies.
Will I actually work with Mike Ferry directly?
Mornings with Mike gives you a recorded 30-minute call from Mike personally every Monday. For in-person access, MFO's Superstar Retreats and Production Retreats are where you'll be in the room with Mike directly throughout the year.

Some of MFO's highest-performing clients joined coaching in their first six months. Learning the system correctly from the beginning produces significantly better results than unlearning bad habits later. The placement interview ensures you're matched to the right tier for where you are now.
What actually happens on the first call?
The first call is a placement interview — not a sales pitch. An MFO specialist reviews where your business is, what's getting in the way, and which coaching tier fits your goals. You leave the call with clarity on what the right next step is, whether that's MFO or not.
