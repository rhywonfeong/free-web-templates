About ADOM Academy

Transforming diagnosis through education, innovation, and a commitment to radiation-free medicine.

Our Purpose

ADOM Academy was founded to transform how the world understands and practices diagnosis. We believe true medicine begins with understanding the cause of disease—not simply identifying its effects.

Our mission is to reduce unnecessary radiation exposure, especially in children, and create a global shift toward safe, same-day, non‑radiative diagnostics. By protecting the body from harm, we protect both people and the planet.

Our Philosophy

At ADOM, we teach diagnosis at the core. Diagnostology unites anatomy, physiology, pathology, and the search for cause into a single science.

Students learn to see the body not just as images or organs, but as systems of balance, communication, and response. Diagnosis is not just a medical skill—it is the foundation of healing.

Our Pathways

ADOM was created for individuals seeking a new beginning—career changers, first-time healthcare entrants, and purpose-driven professionals.

Our programs provide direct, achievable pathways into medicine through ultrasound education and Diagnostology training. We train diagnostologists who are skilled in imaging and deeply committed to human and environmental health.

Our Legacy

Built on over two decades of leadership in ultrasound education and research—from the Gulf Coast Institute of Vascular Ultrasound (1999) to CURE—our history reflects a continuous mission: teach diagnosis as the heart of medicine and eliminate unnecessary radiation wherever it harms.

ADOM continues that legacy through Diagnostology.

Our Vision

A world where early detection is safe, accessible, and grounded in truth—where diagnosis restores understanding, reduces harm, and connects medicine back to its purpose: to heal.
