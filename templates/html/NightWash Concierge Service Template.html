Terms of Service & Liability

Last updated: Oct 2023

1. Pre-existing Conditions

Our driver will conduct a visual inspection upon collection. NightWash is not liable for structural degradation, latent stains that surface during washing (wicking), or pre-existing dye bleed. The digital waiver signed at collection supersedes prior agreements.

2. Dimensional Shrinkage

Natural fibers (wool, cotton) may experience minor dimensional changes (1-3%) during standard wet-wash processing. This is a normal characteristic of the material and not indicative of improper process.
