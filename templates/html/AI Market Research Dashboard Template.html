Strategist Action Plan

1. Team Integrations Push

Don't just add 50 integrations. Pick the 2 systems where notes become action (Slack & Task managers) and make the loop instant.

2. Optimize "Habit Moments"

Granola is clarifying paid tiers. Identify your own "habit moment" (what users do weekly) and make that the paid boundary fairly.

The winner won't be the best summary. It will be the tool that reliably turns talk into output (creates tasks, drafts emails, files notes automatically).
