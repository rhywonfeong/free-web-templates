Bio

Born in Belgium and raised across Africa, I discovered my passion for storytelling at 16, filming surfers in Pointe-Noire, Congo. Today, based in Cape Town, I craft cinematic visuals that capture emotion, energy, and authenticity.

Cinematic Approach

Film, for me, is like a dance, a way to make the viewer feel, guiding them into my universe where emotion, rhythm, and imagery move together, leaving a lasting resonance.
