Meet Dr. Ayesha Marwat

Dr. Ayesha founded Marwat Smile with a simple mission: to treat people, not just teeth. With over a decade of clinical experience and advanced training in cosmetic and restorative dentistry, she brings a gentle touch to complex procedures.

She holds a BDS from King Edward Medical University and certifications in Advanced Implantology. When you're in her chair, you're in the hands of a professional who prioritizes your comfort above all else.
