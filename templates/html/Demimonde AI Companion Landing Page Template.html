How does the AI remember our conversations?
Our advanced AI companions use continuous memory architecture to remember your preferences, past conversations, and shared experiences, making every interaction feel natural and personal over time.
Is my data and privacy secure?
Absolutely. We use bank-grade end-to-end encryption for all your conversations. We never sell your data, and you have complete control over your account and chat history, which can be deleted at any time.
Can I change my companion later?
Yes! Depending on your plan, you can talk to multiple companions or switch them. Premium and Elite members have access to our full roster of unique, highly-detailed characters.
Are the voice calls real-time?
Yes, our voice calls are generated in real-time with sub-second latency. The voices are natural, expressive, and react emotionally to what you say, creating a truly immersive experience.
