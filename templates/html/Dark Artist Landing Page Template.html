About

A quiet voice in a loud city.

Noise that feels like memory.

Kolldizy writes from the edge of the night—where the streetlights flicker and the mind stops pretending. The songs are cinematic and close, built from shadow, distortion, and honesty that arrives after midnight.

It’s dark R&B and trap-soul tension with soft neon in the distance—music for long drives, empty rooms, and the moments you can’t name.

Nocturnal. Emotional. Real.
