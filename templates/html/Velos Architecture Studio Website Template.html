Process &
Methodology

We don't just build; we subtract. Our process is a reductive journey from chaos to clarity, ensuring every line serves a structural or aesthetic purpose.

Phase 01

Schematic Void

Analyzing the site's negative space. We map light, wind, and movement before a single form is conceptualized.

Phase 02

Material Selection

Rigorous testing of raw materials. Concrete curing tests, steel weathering simulations, and glass transparency audits.

Phase 03

Construction & Subtraction

Overseeing the build with a focus on details. We remove excess, refining the structure until only the essential remains.
