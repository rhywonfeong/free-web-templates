Philosophy

Wabi-Sabi in form.

Embracing the beauty in imperfections and the transience of life. Our bonsai practice is deeply rooted in this worldview, honoring the twisted bark, the asymmetrical branch, and the quiet resilience of nature.

Each tree in our Bath sanctuary has been nurtured to express its own unique character, an ongoing dialogue between artist and nature that spans decades.
