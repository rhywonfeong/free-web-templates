How do you integrate technology without it being visible?

We collaborate with engineers from day one. Sensors, climate controls, and acoustic systems are embedded directly into the structural materials—cast within concrete walls, hidden behind acoustic plaster, or integrated into millwork—ensuring the aesthetic purity of the space is never compromised.
