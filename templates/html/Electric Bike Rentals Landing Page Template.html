SYSTEM DATA (FAQ)

Do I need a special license?
Yes, a valid two-wheeler driver's license is required for all models. For the Stratos X1 performance model, you must be 21+ years old.
How does charging work?
You can charge at any Volt Station for free. Alternatively, use any standard household socket with the provided portable charger. Our bikes also support fast charging at public EV networks.
What is included in the insurance?
Basic insurance covers third-party liability. The 'Daily Pro' and 'Subscription' plans include comprehensive coverage with zero depreciation and roadside assistance.
Can I use the bike for food delivery?
Absolutely. Our 'Aegis Cargo' model is specifically designed for B2B applications. We offer special weekly rates for gig workers.
