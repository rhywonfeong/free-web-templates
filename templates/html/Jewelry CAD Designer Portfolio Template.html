01 / The Intuitive Why

The Alchemy of Form & Function

Most see jewelry as an accessory. I see it as a mechanical challenge wrapped in an aesthetic dream. My process is a dialogue between two worlds: the tactile unpredictability of hand-carved wax and the mathematical perfection of Rhino and ZBrush.

Whether I'm calculating the tension of a trillion-cut setting or mapping the ergonomic flow of a high-jewelry collar, my goal remains singular: absolute structural integrity without sacrificing the poetry of the curve.

I design for the brands that demand heritage-quality craftsmanship delivered through a modern, scalable workflow.
