Are You
Present ?

Have you ever been looking at a picture or a beautiful view like this book cover and were completely unaware of the elements in front of you? This photo is called a man and his dog. Almost camouflaged on the back of the book is a man looking at the beautiful view of Long Beach, California, bluff. The day I captured this moment I was not aware of either the man or the dog. Until I looked in closely. I had to lean in, squeeze my eyes, focus, and purposely bring it into view. I was mesmerized by the ocean and the port on the horizon. Both living figures escaped my natural eye. I learned a valuable lesson that day. I had to learn to see outside of my field of view. I realized we must learn to see from more than one position. Not just looking to see one way. But seeking through insight versus using only the human ability to see a wide view. Called the big picture. This way we're not limiting life and how broad is the spectrum of how we can see life if we're present enough to become aware of all that life has for us. Life has more than one point and we need to be aware of the points that we are to follow. Showing the way at every stage and moment in life. The present. Awareness, and being present bring us understanding while on the journey. We may not agree or like what we learn or find out we must unlearn. BUT (Believers Understanding Truth) know that when we are present and aware the best parts of who we are will bring peace and love. Two main ingredients to produce a life of abundance.

That's what Stacey does. This is real, applicable, lasting change, giving you the tools you need for the life you want to achieve.
