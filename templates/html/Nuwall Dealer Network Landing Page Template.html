Frequently asked questions

Answers to common questions from new dealers. Still unsure? Reach out to our team and we’ll guide you personally.

Is there a minimum order to become a Nuwall dealer?
Yes, we maintain a reasonable opening order value to ensure serious partnerships and meaningful display at your store. The amount varies by city and category; our team will share details once you apply.
How long does verification and onboarding take?
In most cases, verification is completed within 3–5 working days of receiving your documents. In metros, onboarding can be even faster. Our team will keep you updated at each step.
Do you offer exclusive territories or regions?
In select markets we do offer structured territory protection based on performance, category, and store size. This is evaluated case by case in discussion with our sales leadership.
Is there a dedicated mobile app for dealers?
Yes. Once you are onboarded, you will receive login access to the Nuwall dealer app where you can browse live inventory, place orders, download creatives, and track shipments.
Can I sell Nuwall both online and offline?
We primarily work with physical stores and project partners to ensure high service quality. If you also sell online, please mention it in your application and our team will guide you on the policy.
