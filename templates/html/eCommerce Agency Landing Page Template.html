Frequently Asked Questions

What makes InteractOne different from other eCommerce agencies?
We’re a senior-level, U.S.-based team that values long-term partnerships over quick wins. No offshoring, no junior handoffs. We offer transparent communication, strategic thinking, and white-glove service - backed by 25+ years of experience building and growing complex eCommerce websites.
Do you work with both B2B and B2C merchants?
Yes. While we specialize in helping mid-market to enterprise B2B brands navigate complex catalogs, custom integrations, and long buying cycles, we also drive growth for high-volume B2C merchants—especially those on Shopify and Magento.
What platforms do you support?
We focus on Shopify, Magento/Adobe Commerce, and BigCommerce. We also have deep experience with third-party integrations, custom app development, and platform migrations.
Do you require long-term contracts?
Nope. We operate on flexible blocks of hours, so you only pay for what you need. No retainers, no surprises - just efficient, strategic work delivered with transparency. That said, many of our clients choose to stay with us for the long haul because the results speak for themselves.
Will I have a dedicated point of contact?
Yes. Every client engagement is managed by a Senior Project Manager who provides ongoing updates, meeting cadence, and visibility into priorities and progress.
Where is your team located?
We’re proudly headquartered in Cincinnati, Ohio, and our entire team is U.S.-based. No offshoring, ever. When you call or email, you’ll speak directly to someone who knows your business and your tech.
