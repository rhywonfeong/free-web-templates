What kinds of symptoms do you help with?

Many patients come in after trying multiple approaches. A deeper neurological assessment may help uncover patterns that were missed or not clearly explained before.

Not necessarily. Some patients come in because something still feels off even when testing has been inconclusive or their symptoms seem subtle but persistent.

No. Some patients are focused on chronic unresolved symptoms, while others want to improve balance, recovery, coordination, or neurological performance.
