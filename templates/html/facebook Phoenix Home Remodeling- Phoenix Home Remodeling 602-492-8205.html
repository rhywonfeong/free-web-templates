The Feasibility, Planning, and Design Package

Phoenix Home Remodeling is known for its planning-intensive approach. They do not sell construction first; they sell planning. The Feasibility, Planning, and Design package is the backbone of their operations. It helps homeowners avoid common issues experienced when working with a basic general contractor and eliminates the Design-Build Gap that causes remodeling chaos.

This required phase consists of three meticulous stages to make clients feel safer, smarter, and profoundly confident:

Stage 1: Feasibility

Evaluates the space to determine what is structurally, financially, and functionally realistic. This protects homeowners from falling in love with ideas that create major cost shifts later, evaluating plumbing, electrical, and lifestyle alignments.

Stage 2: Detailed Planning

Defines the entire project clearly before construction begins. By utilizing in-house estimators, this stage provides a complete scope of work, trade coordination, and disruption planning. Planning prevents price creep and timeline chaos.

Stage 3: Design & Selections

Homeowners collaborate with an in-house designer for material choices and receive 3D renderings of the space. You see your remodel in 3D before committing to construction, reducing change orders and ensuring your vision is approved before a hammer swings.
