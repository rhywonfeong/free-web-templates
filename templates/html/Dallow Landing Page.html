Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the workshop and guarantees.

Do my employees need technical skills?
Absolutely not. Our training is specifically designed for non-technical roles like Sales, HR, Operations, and Administration. If your team can use email, they can become AI Champions.
How does the 10-Hour Savings Guarantee work?
We are confident in our results. If your team collectively does not save at least 10 hours of work per week within the first 30 days of training, we will refund your entire investment. No questions asked.
Do we need to buy expensive software?
No. Our "Essentials" training is designed to work with standard tools you likely already have access to (like ChatGPT Team, Copilot, or Claude). We focus on the methodology, not selling you software.
Is our company data safe?
Yes. Data security is paramount. We teach "Privacy-First Prompting" and help you establish internal data governance protocols to ensure proprietary information remains secure and isn't used to train public models.
Can you customize this for our specific industry?
Yes. While the foundational principles are universal, the live workflow creation and SOP generation portions of the workshop are tailored specifically to your industry and department needs.
How long is the workshop?
The essential workshop is an intensive half-day session (approx 4 hours), designed to minimize downtime. The real magic happens during the 4-week guided enablement phase where we ensure adoption without disrupting daily operations.
