What Is Cimenza Microcement?

Cimenza microcement is a mineral-based coating system designed to create seamless, continuous surfaces.

It is supplied as a structured system combining primers, microcement layers and protective sealers to ensure consistent performance, durability and aesthetic quality.

A Complete Microcement System

Cimenza microcement is developed as a complete system rather than a single product. Each component is designed and tested to work together, ensuring reliable adhesion, performance and longevity.

Primers

Developed to provide stable adhesion on a variety of substrates.

Microcement Layers

Mineral-based layers that create texture, strength and surface character.

Protective Sealers

Final protective layers enhancing resistance and durability.

System Compatibility

All components are optimized to function as one integrated system.

Fine Grain

Rockbase S

Rockbase S is a microcement with an ultra-fine grain structure, designed for smooth, refined, and low-maintenance finishes. It creates a seamless and even surface with a modern, sophisticated appearance, while fully preserving excellent mechanical strength and chemical resistance.

Medium Grain

Rockbase M

Rockbase M is a microcement with a medium grain size, ideal as an intermediate layer or as a finishing layer when a balanced combination of texture and smoothness is desired. It delivers a versatile aesthetic that enhances the natural character of the surface while maintaining high strength and durability.

Coarse Grain

Rockbase L

Rockbase L is a coarse-grain microcement that combines high mechanical and chemical resistance with a more pronounced texture. It is used as a base layer or as a finishing layer for projects seeking a more rustic and natural appearance, and stands out for its reliable performance in demanding applications.

Structural Base

Rockbase XL

Rockbase XL is a microcement with the coarsest grain structure, developed to level surfaces and provide structural strength to the overall system. It is particularly suitable for substrates that require initial smoothing and maximum durability, while preserving the distinctive, natural appearance associated with the ROCKBASE system.

Key Characteristics

  • Seamless and joint-free surfaces
  • Durable and wear-resistant
  • Water-resistant when properly sealed
  • Suitable for walls and floors

ROCKBASE Application Method

The ROCKBASE system follows a structured application method designed to ensure optimal adhesion, durability and aesthetic quality. Below is an overview of the recommended application steps for professional use.

01
Step 01

Surface Preparation

Proper substrate preparation is essential to achieve a flawless and durable result.

The surface must be clean, dry, and free from dust, grease, loose paint, or any other contaminants that could negatively affect adhesion. All cracks, imperfections, or irregularities must be repaired prior to application.

Light sanding is recommended to increase surface roughness and improve bonding. After sanding, remove residues using a vacuum cleaner or damp cloth and allow the surface to dry completely.

02
Step 02

Mesh Installation & Primer

Mesh: The use of fiberglass mesh is recommended to reinforce the surface, reduce the risk of cracking and improve overall system durability, especially on uneven or unstable substrates. On smooth and stable surfaces, this step is optional.
Primer: Apply an even coat of PRIMER or PRIMERQUARTZ using a roller or brush to ensure optimal adhesion. Drying time: approximately 30–60 minutes, depending on environmental conditions.
03
Step 03

Base Layer – ROCKBASE XL or L

First coat: Apply ROCKBASE XL or L evenly using a flexible metal trowel. Allow to dry for 6–12 hours depending on temperature and humidity. Once dry, sand with 40-grit sandpaper to level the surface.
Second coat (optional): If required, apply a second coat following the same procedure to achieve a perfectly even base.
04
Step 04

Finishing Layer – ROCKBASE M or S

First coat: Apply ROCKBASE M for a slightly textured finish or ROCKBASE S for a finer, smoother appearance. Once dry, sand with 120-grit sandpaper.
Second coat: Apply a second layer of the same product. After drying, sand again to obtain a uniform, silky and natural final finish.
05
Step 05

Final Sealing & Protection

To protect the surface and ensure long-term system performance, apply two coats of VARNISH W. When increased water resistance is required, use VARNISH DSV instead.

Always respect the recommended drying times between coats.

Additional Recommendations

  • Always perform a test application to verify colour and product compatibility.
  • Apply the system under controlled conditions, between 10°C and 25°C, with moderate humidity.
  • Clean tools immediately after use to prevent material hardening.
