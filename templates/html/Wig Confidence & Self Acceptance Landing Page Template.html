About: hair loss rewrote my mirror before I had words for it.

Hair loss rewrote my reflection before I had a chance to process what was happening. I did not choose alopecia or chronic illness. I did choose not to disappear because of it.

When I began wearing wigs, I was overwhelmed and under-informed. Everything felt either sales-driven or unrealistic. I started sharing so another woman could step into this world without the same confusion and emotional weight I carried.

My faith in Christ steadied me through the quiet unraveling of identity that hair loss can bring. I believe you are not defined by the struggles you did not choose. You rise above them even on the days you do not feel like you are rising.

This space is for women who need permission to be seen again—deeply human, medically complicated, occasionally undone, and still fully present in their own lives.
