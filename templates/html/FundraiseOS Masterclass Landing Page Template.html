The Complete Roadmap

We've broken down the fundraising process into 6 actionable phases. No fluff, just execution.

Phase 01

Understand the investor's mind

Decode VC psychology. Learn what drives their decisions, how their funds work, and what they actually look for in a founder.

Phase 02

How fundable are you?

Honest self-assessment metrics. Benchmark your traction, market size, and team against venture-backable standards.

Phase 03

Design your fundraising strategy

Create your funnel. Identify target investors, set your timeline, and engineer the "FOMO" effect before you start.

Phase 04

Build a top 1% pitch deck

Narrative architecture. Slide-by-slide breakdown of decks that raised millions. Templates and design principles included.

Phase 05

Go live with your fundraising

Execution mode. Cold outreach scripts, warm intro protocols, managing the CRM, and handling first meetings.

Phase 06

Set the terms of your round

Closing the deal. Valuation, SAFEs, convertible notes, and term sheet negotiation. Don't lose equity unnecessarily.
