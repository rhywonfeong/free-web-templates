About Me

I’m a professional bagpiper based in Edinburgh, dedicated to bringing the unmistakable sound of the Great Highland Bagpipe to events across Scotland and beyond.

My piping journey began under the guidance of Callum Wynd—a highly respected soloist and Grade 1 pipe band player—whose mentorship instilled in me a deep respect for the tradition and technical precision of the instrument.

Whether performing at intimate gatherings or grand celebrations, I strive to deliver a performance that honors the heritage of the pipes while adding a memorable atmosphere to your special occasion.
