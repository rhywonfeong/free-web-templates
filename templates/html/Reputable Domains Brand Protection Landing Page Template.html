What's at Stake When Your Domain Gets Flagged

DNS or reputation errors can halt payments, email, and critical workflows—hurting revenue and trust.

Revenue Impact

Downtime Cost

Reputation Risk

Vendors and ISPs distrust your brand, leading to future deliverability issues.

Context

Why This Happens

Reputation ecosystem complexity

Automated filters and third‑party threat feeds can mistakenly classify legitimate domains as risky when:

  • New sending domains or fresh DNS changes resemble malicious patterns
  • Shared infrastructure or IP reputation drags good actors down
  • Over‑aggressive heuristics and stale feed data cause false positives
