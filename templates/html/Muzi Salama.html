Tilisi, Limuru

Sustainable urban development and community-centric living.

Nestled within the scenic landscape of Tilisi, Limuru, Muzi Salama stands as a testament to sustainable urban development. Developed by IHS Kenya, this innovative project spans 2.5 acres and is designed to address the growing demand for affordable yet high quality housing solutions in Kenya.

2 and 3-bedroom, master en-suite contemporary apartments.

A 15-Minute City

Tilisi is a revolutionary concept in Kenya. Designed as a walkable 15-minute city, Tilisi is a visionary 400-acre mixed-use development in Limuru, Kiambu County, Kenya. Designed as a self-sufficient community, Tilisi integrates residential, commercial, industrial, and recreational spaces.
