The Infrastructure of
Tomorrow Cannot Wait.

In an era of rapid geopolitical shifts, reliance on foreign infrastructure for our digital backbone is a calculated risk we can no longer afford. The data suggests a widening gap in domestic manufacturing capabilities that threatens our long-term sovereignty.

The American Horizon Coalition is addressing this by advocating for policies that fast-track permission reform and incentivize high-tech manufacturing on American soil.
