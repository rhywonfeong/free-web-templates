Our Installation Process

1. Surface preparation

Diamond grinding to ensure a porous, clean profile for maximum adhesion.

2. Primer application

Sealing the concrete to prevent outgassing and create a strong base bond.

3. Epoxy installation

Applying the core resin layers, including custom metallic pigments or flakes.

4. Protective topcoat

A clear, UV-stable polyaspartic or urethane coat for scratch and chemical resistance.

5. Final inspection

Thorough walkthrough to ensure the finish meets our strict quality standards.
