What Can SnapFind Do For You?

Effortless Item Cataloging
  • Quick Add: Add new items with description, location, and image.
  • Versatile Image Upload: Upload, drag & drop, or paste images; mobile camera supported.

AI-Powered Organization
  • Automatic Description Suggestion: AI generates concise item descriptions from images.
  • Intelligent Tag & Category Suggestions: AI proposes useful tags; re-trigger any time.
  • Duplicate Prevention: Detects similar items before adding to avoid clutter.
Intuitive Item Management
  • Clear Overview: View all items in a beautiful card layout.
  • Easy Editing: Modify details and trigger new AI suggestions anytime.
  • Safe Deletion: Remove items with confirmation.
Powerful Search Capabilities
  • Quick Access: Floating Action Button for instant search.
  • Text & Visual Search: Find by keywords or upload an image for AI-powered matching.
