About the Founder

MY NAME IS PJ PEARSON

The pool industry has always been in my blood starting with my grandfather who built pools in Fort Worth in the 1950's and 60's. My father and his brother each operate their own pool companies in Fort Worth.

I started working for my father's pool company right after I graduated college in 2003. I learned the basics of cleaning and service repairs but always kept my dreams in renovations. Then I worked for a major builder in Collin County where I developed my skills and knowledge of pool renovation and proper construction practices.

Today, I operate my company based on a simple principle: take care of each customer so they can spend time with their family enjoying their pool.
