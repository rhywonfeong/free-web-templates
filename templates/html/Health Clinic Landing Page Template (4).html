Conditions We Treat

Focused, evidence-based care for injuries and chronic conditions — delivered through a coordinated, multidisciplinary model.

Precision Diagnosis, Not Protocols.

At Legacy Injury & Wellness, we treat a range of musculoskeletal and neurological conditions commonly seen after motor vehicle accidents, workplace injuries, and chronic strain.

Our approach emphasizes accurate diagnosis, individualized treatment planning, and coordinated care — not one-size-fits-all protocols.

Lower Back Pain

Common injury-related complaint involving muscular strain, disc injury, joint dysfunction, or nerve involvement.

  • Identifying the root cause
  • Restoring mobility & stability
  • Reducing inflammation
  • Supporting long-term recovery

Neck Pain

Resulting from trauma like whiplash or repetitive strain, often associated with headaches or stiffness.

  • Structural assessment
  • Targeted chiropractic care
  • Rehabilitation planning
  • Ongoing re-evaluation

Herniated Disc

When disc material presses on nearby nerves, causing pain, numbness, or weakness in extremities.

  • Reducing nerve compression
  • Improving spinal mechanics
  • Functional recovery
  • Imaging coordination

Sciatica & Leg Pain

Pain radiating from the lower back into the hip or leg due to nerve irritation or compression.

  • Addressing irritation source
  • Restoring movement patterns
  • Reducing functional limitation

Spinal Stenosis

Narrowing of the spinal canal placing pressure on nerves, leading to pain or weakness.

  • Conservative care strategies
  • Mobility & stability support
  • Progression tracking

Peripheral Neuropathy

Affects nerves outside the brain/spinal cord causing burning, tingling, numbness, or weakness.

  • Detailed clinical evaluation
  • Supportive care strategies
  • Coordinated treatment

Migraine & Headaches

Headaches and migraines may originate from spinal dysfunction, muscular tension, or nerve irritation — particularly in the cervical spine.

Identifying mechanical contributors Reducing frequency & intensity Improving quality of life
