Speaking-first method

Real conversations, from your very first lesson

No lecture halls, no silent worksheets. Every session is built around talking — debates, roleplay, and stories — so fluency comes from doing, not memorizing.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How is SOL different from an app or a regular course?

Apps teach you to tap; courses teach you to listen. SOL is built around speaking — small live groups, real coaches, and a community that keeps you coming back. Progress tracking wraps it all together.

What if my English is very basic?

Perfect — we run groups from A1 upward. Your placement test guarantees you're with peers at exactly your level, so nobody feels behind and nobody feels bored.

Are lessons online or in person?

Both. All lessons and clubs run online, and members in the city can join in-person speaking clubs and events at our club space every week.

How fast will I see progress?

Most members feel noticeably more confident within 3–4 weeks, and 94% reach the next CEFR level within 6 months on the Fluency plan.

Can I try before paying?

Yes — the placement test and your first full group lesson are free, no card required. Join, feel the vibe, then decide.
