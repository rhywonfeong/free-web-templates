True Passion

A family tradition passed down through three generations.

A story of milk, fruit, and memory.

Monello began with a simple memory: the taste of freshly churned gelato on a hot summer afternoon in Sicily. It wasn't neon-colored or overly sweet; it was pure, creamy, and tasted exactly like the ingredients it was made from.

We brought that philosophy back home. No pre-made mixes, no artificial colorings. Just fresh local milk, ripe seasonal fruits, and premium nuts roasted to perfection.

Every scoop we serve is a tribute to the artisans who taught us that patience is the most important ingredient of all.

Marco & Elena
