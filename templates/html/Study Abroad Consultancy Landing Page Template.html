FAQs

Quick answers to common questions.

How soon will I get a call after I submit?

Typically within 24 hours (often sooner). For urgent cases, use the WhatsApp button to connect instantly.

Do you help with scholarships?

Yes. We guide you on eligibility, documentation, and timelines based on your chosen university and intake.

Can I apply without IELTS?

In some cases, yes—depending on country, university, and your academic background. We’ll suggest suitable pathways.

Is my data safe?

Yes. This demo uses session storage only. For production, connect your CRM with secure server-side handling.
