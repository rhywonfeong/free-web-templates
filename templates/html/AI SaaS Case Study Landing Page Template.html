Overview

Outdated and unmonetized.

RiseAngle is an AI-powered video generation SaaS platform designed to help creators produce short-form videos for TikTok and YouTube using trending, viral-style templates.

The original version of the platform was outdated, not mobile responsive, and lacked a clear monetization structure. Despite growing interest in AI content tools, the product was not converting users effectively or generating sustainable revenue.

Problem Statement

Friction in the funnel.

The core challenges were usability, scalability, and monetization. The business needed a mobile-first rebuild that simplified the AI complexity while creating a clear path to recurring revenue.

Research & Strategy

Understanding the creator.

We conducted competitor analysis across AI video and creator tools to benchmark onboarding flows, pricing structures, automation features, and user retention tactics. Validated user personas were created based on short-form creators who prioritized speed, automation, and clear ROI.

Solution — Phase 1 (MVP)

Mobile-First MVP

Phase 1 focused on delivering a clean, mobile-first MVP experience. Users could select a trending template, click generate, and receive a fully rendered AI video within minutes. A modern Tailwind-based design system ensured consistency.

Mobile-First Video Generation Interface

Solution — Phase 2

Autopilot & Monetization

Phase 2 introduced Autopilot, allowing users to automate content creation. To support revenue growth, we designed subscription-based tiers and credit packs. The pricing model was positioned around output value rather than technical AI usage.

Conclusion

By rebuilding the platform with a mobile-first mindset and aligning UX with business goals, RiseAngle evolved from an underperforming tool into a scalable, monetizable SaaS product. Implementation of analytics tracking (PostHog) enabled data-driven iteration and roadmap planning for future releases.
