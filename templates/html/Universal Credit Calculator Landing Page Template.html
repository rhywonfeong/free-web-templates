Understanding your claim

Work Allowance

If you have children or limited capability for work, you can earn a certain amount before your Universal Credit is reduced.

Housing Element

Assistance with rent payments is calculated based on your location and the number of people living in your home.

Taper Rate

For every £1 you earn over your work allowance, your Universal Credit payment reduces by 55p.
