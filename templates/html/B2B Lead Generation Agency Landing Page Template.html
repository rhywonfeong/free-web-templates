FAQs — Everything you need to know.

What exactly does the Tech Fee cover?
The Tech Fee covers your access to our system while we work together. It pays for the hard costs of domains, lead lists, inboxes, and the sending tool. It is an operational cost, just like paying rent or a software bill. It is not refundable.
Do I own the domains and inboxes if we stop working together?
No. We keep all domains, inboxes, campaigns, and systems to ensure security. If the deal ends, your access to the system ends.
How do you define a qualified lead?
You define it. During our start up call, you tell us the exact job titles and company profiles you want to target. Qualified means the company is the industry and size you told us, and the person is the job title you told us, and they have said they are interested in what you do.
What happens if a lead does not show up to the meeting?
You do not pay for them. The performance fee is only for meetings that actually happen. If they do not show up, you are not billed.
How much time will my team need to spend on this?
Almost zero. You help us define your target market on the first call. You keep your calendar updated. We handle one hundred percent of the daily work. You just show up to the sales calls.
What will my ROI be?
So that you can actually see what results you can expect, you can use the same ROI Calculator I have used earlier. You will find it on our website.
What is required from me?
Everything is fully done for you. Just take the calls. We handle everything else.
What if I have never run cold email before?
Not a problem. We handle everything, even if it is your first time.
