The Design-Build Difference: Planning Before Pricing

Phoenix Home Remodeling is known for its structured pre-construction process that drastically reduces change orders and surprises. We operate strictly on the design-build business model. All of our homeowner clients begin by purchasing our comprehensive Feasibility, Planning, and Design package.

This phase differentiates us from standard general contractors who often provide vague ballpark numbers and use rough allowances that lead to bait-and-switch pricing. We do not sell construction first; we sell planning. Homeowners do not commit to construction until:

  • The design is fully complete, including comprehensive floor plans and 3D renderings of the space.
  • All material selections are made, from cabinet refacing styles and drywall finishes to hardware upgrades and grout lines.
  • Scope is clearly defined with no gaps in structural feasibility or plumbing adjustments.
  • A final fixed construction price is presented, eliminating mid-project price increases.

Our in-house designers, estimators, and construction professionals collaborate internally. There is no separation between the design and build teams, which completely eliminates finger-pointing and costly on-the-fly re-engineering.

Interior Project Types, Price Ranges & Timelines

Budgeting accurately is a critical step in the feasibility phase. Understanding realistic numbers helps align design expectations with reality. Below are our standard investment ranges for common interior spaces:

Primary Bathroom Remodel

  • Standard: $40,000 - $60,000 (4-6 weeks)
  • High-End/Larger: $50,000 - $75,000 (4-7 weeks)

Includes complete renovation with quality fixtures, custom tile work, vanity layout changes, lighting, and shower systems. Shower size and plumbing adjustments are significant cost factors.

Kitchen Remodel

  • Small Kitchen: $40,000 - $55,000 (4-7 weeks)
  • Large/High-End: $75,000 - $100,000+ (5-7 weeks)

Cabinetry is a primary cost driver. We only utilize high-quality cabinet options. Countertop materials, energy-efficient appliances, and under-cabinet lighting complexity impact final investments.

Entertainment Wall Remodel

  • Without Cabinets: $15,000 - $25,000
  • With Custom Cabinets: $20,000 - $30,000

Includes custom cabinetry, electric fireplace integration, wall finishing, and shelving. Electrical requirements and stacked stone vs. tile selections affect pricing.

Laundry Room Remodel

  • Standard: $10,000 - $25,000
  • Timeline: 2-3 weeks

Space size, plumbing complexity, sink additions, flooring updates, and specialty storage cabinet configurations drive the final price.
