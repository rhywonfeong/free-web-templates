Which cities are supported?

Pulse is live in 40+ cities across Europe, North America, and Latin America, with new places added every month based on waitlist demand.

How do you know where people are?

We combine opt‑in, privacy‑preserving location signals, venue data, and local curator reports — never selling or sharing personal data.

During beta, Pulse is free. Later, we’ll keep a generous free tier and offer a plus plan for power users and frequent travelers.
