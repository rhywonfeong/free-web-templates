Our Philosophy

Redefining the modern grooming ritual.

LINO was born from a simple belief: the start and end of your day deserve the highest quality materials. We meticulously source long-staple Aegean cotton and partner with heritage mills to create pieces that aren't just functional, but fundamentally superior.
