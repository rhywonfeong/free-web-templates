01 · What is Visiblie OS?

An AI-visibility operating system: the Claude setup, skills library, workflows and MCP integrations Visiblie uses to run generative engine optimization for its own clients, installed into a partner’s own Claude, Slack and Notion workspace. It ships as three Claude plugins covering pitching, auditing and delivery, and runs on the partner’s own API keys rather than a hosted dashboard.

02 · What is generative engine optimization (GEO)?

The practice of making a brand appear and be cited accurately in AI-generated answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude and Copilot. Unlike SEO, which optimizes for ranked links, GEO optimizes for inclusion in a synthesized answer — dependent on entity clarity, citable source coverage and consistent factual signals across the web.

03 · How do you measure AI visibility?

By testing a fixed set of buyer-intent prompts across multiple AI engines and recording whether a brand is mentioned, how it’s characterized, and which sources the engine cites. Visiblie OS registers that prompt set once, then re-tests against the same set over time, so the change is attributable rather than anecdotal.

04 · What are the alternatives to AirOps for AI visibility work?

The main platforms are AirOps, Profound, Otterly, Relevance and Rankability, all sold as hosted subscriptions with their own dashboards. Visiblie OS is a different category: an operating system licensed into a team’s existing Claude, Slack and Notion rather than a platform to log into — no per-seat rent and no roadmap lock-in.

05 · Can you do AI visibility work using Claude on its own?

Partly. Claude can research a brand’s AI presence and draft optimization work competently. What it lacks is persistent memory of prior engagements, quality gates defining when work is finished, and benchmark data to score against, so every session restarts from zero. Visiblie OS supplies those three layers and runs on top of Claude rather than replacing it.

06 · Do AI visibility tools need access to my company data?

Most hosted platforms require uploading positioning documents, competitor intel and client data to the vendor’s cloud. Visiblie OS does not. It runs inside your own Claude workspace on your own API keys, reading your company brain from your own Notion or Drive. Scoring logic stays server-side behind Visiblie’s MCP; customer data never leaves their side.

07 · How much does AI visibility software cost?

Hosted platforms typically run from a few hundred to several thousand dollars per month depending on seats and tracked prompts. Visiblie OS is priced as a setup fee plus a monthly minimum, deliberately below AirOps-tier, with a revenue share for agencies reselling to clients. Founding partner pricing is set during the call and held for the life of the partnership.

08 · Why does our AI SaaS bill keep climbing?

Most AI SaaS products are built on foundation models and marked up on top of token cost, and much of that pricing was venture-subsidized during 2023 to 2025. As subsidies ended, real inference costs passed through to customers, often multiplied. Running the operation on your own API keys removes that markup layer entirely.

09 · Can we extend it with our own skills?

Yes. Visiblie OS runs as skills, prompts and MCP connections inside Claude rather than behind a fixed interface, so teams already building their own MCPs can add to it directly. Visiblie’s scoring logic and benchmark thresholds stay server-side, but everything around them is yours to build on.

10 · Why only a handful of founding partners?

Because each install is done by hand and we stay close for eight weeks afterward. One more would mean doing all of them badly. The cohort is deliberately mixed — one agency, one in-house growth team and one revenue-share candidate — so the feedback covers different surfaces and different books.
