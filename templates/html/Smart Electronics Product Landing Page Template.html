The Find My Network

If you leave something far behind, like at the beach or the gym, the Find My network — hundreds of millions of iPhone, iPad, and Mac devices around the world — helps track down your AirTag. And it's designed to protect your privacy every step of the way.

Precision Finding

If your AirTag is nearby, your iPhone can lead you straight to it with Precision Finding. You'll see the distance to your AirTag and the direction to head in. (On supported iPhone models).

Built-in Speaker

Play a sound on the built-in speaker by going to the new Items tab in the Find My app, or say "Hey Siri, find my wallet."

Lost Mode

Just like your other Apple devices, AirTag can be put into Lost Mode. When it's detected by a device in the network, you'll automatically get a notification.

Long Battery Life

AirTag is designed to keep going more than a year on a standard battery you can easily replace. Your iPhone lets you know when it's time.

Water & Dust Resistant

Rated IP67 (maximum depth of 1 meter up to 30 minutes). Dropping it in a puddle is no big deal.

NFC Support

Set it up so someone can get your contact info by tapping your AirTag with an NFC-capable smartphone.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Apple AirTag?
Apple AirTag is a small, elegantly designed tracking device that helps you keep track of personal items using Apple's Find My app and network.
What can I use AirTag for?
You can attach or place it with everyday essentials like keys, bags, wallets, luggage, backpacks, and other personal belongings you want to keep track of.
Does AirTag have a battery?
Yes, AirTag uses a standard, user-replaceable CR2032 coin cell battery that is designed to last more than a year.
Is AirTag water resistant?
Yes, AirTag is rated IP67 under IEC standard 60529, meaning it offers splash, water, and dust resistance (maximum depth of 1 meter up to 30 minutes).
Does AirTag work with Android?
AirTag is designed primarily for Apple's Find My ecosystem (requires iPhone, iPad, or iPod touch). However, if an AirTag is in Lost Mode, someone with an NFC-capable smartphone (including Android) can tap it to see the owner's contact information.
Can I use AirTag for people tracking?
No. AirTag is designed for tracking personal items, not for tracking people without consent. Apple has built-in proactive features to discourage unwanted tracking.
