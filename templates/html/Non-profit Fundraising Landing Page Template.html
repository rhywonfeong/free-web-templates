Project #1

Stem Cell Therapy for Veterans & First Responders

In 2025, the Perinatal Society partnered with the Sabot Foundation to provide Veterans badly in need of stem cell therapy treatments in the United States.

In our first year, we treated 25 Veterans with full body stem cell and exosome therapies. We aim to fully treat each person rather than provide a single vial.

Immediate Need

We currently have 3 Veterans in pain who are awaiting our next round of stem cell therapies.

Project #2

Stem Cell Therapy for Autistic Children

Fundraising for a Phase I Clinical Trial to test the safety of Muse MSC stem cell therapy. Partnered with the White Lotus Global Endowment.

The Objective

Complete Phase I clinical trial by 2027. Use data to create access to Right to Try 2.0 and the Right to Try law in Montana.

The Families

Initial pool drawn from Veteran families with autistic children. Once the safety study is complete, protocol access will expand to all families.
