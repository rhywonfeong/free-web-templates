From Creative Agency Leader to Global Advisor

Juliet started her career at one of the most admired creative agencies in the world, BBH . She set up BBH's integrated agency in 1987, leading it for 14 years. In this high-performance environment, she learnt exactly what it takes to be outstanding.

This experience prompted her move into headhunting. In 2007, she co-founded Grace Blue on the belief that finding and supporting the right talent is the most critical factor in business success. Grace Blue grew to be a global organisation respected for developing transformational leadership talent.

Two decades later, Juliet trained as a business coach with the prestigious Meyler Campbell programme. Now leading Grace Blue's business coaching practice, she combines 20 years as an agency practitioner and 20 years as a C-Suite headhunter to help clients develop effectively within their organisational environments.
