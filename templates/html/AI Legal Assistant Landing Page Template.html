Cheating and Dishonestly Inducing Delivery of Property

Based on your description, the situation aligns with the provisions for "Cheating" under Section 318 of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), which corresponds to Section 420 of the legacy Indian Penal Code (IPC).

आपके विवरण के आधार पर, यह स्थिति भारतीय न्याय संहिता (BNS) की धारा 318 के तहत "धोखाधड़ी" के प्रावधानों से मेल खाती है।

  • The core element involves deception and fraudulently inducing someone to deliver property.
  • The offense is cognizable and non-bailable.
Read Original Statute Text

"Whoever cheats and thereby dishonestly induces the person deceived to deliver any property to any person, or to make, alter or destroy the whole or any part of a valuable security... shall be punished with imprisonment of either description for a term which may extend to seven years, and shall also be liable to fine."
