Why the tongue? Why now?

The tongue is already part of oral cancer screenings and systemic health conversations. The limitation has never been relevance — it’s frequency. Seeing changes over time creates context without interpretation.

THE GAP

Patients struggle to explain when symptoms started or what changed between visits. This friction slows down discovery and creates documentation gaps.
