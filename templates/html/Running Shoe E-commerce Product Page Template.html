Designed to Break Records

The AeroStride Vapor Pro isn't just a shoe; it's a piece of precision engineering. Born from the insights of the world's fastest marathoners, it combines our lightest, most responsive foam with a full-length carbon fiber plate.

The upper uses a new AtomKnit material that is steamed and stretched to be incredibly lightweight while holding minimal water and enhanced breathability. The outsole design has been updated based on data from hundreds of runners to place traction exactly where it's needed for different grip angles.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this shoe true to size?

Yes, the Vapor Pro fits true to size. If you have wide feet, we recommend going half a size up.

Can I use these for daily training?

While durable, these are designed as racing shoes. For daily training, we recommend our Pegasus line to preserve the life of the Vapor Pro.

How do I clean them?

Hand wash with mild soap and cold water. Air dry away from direct heat.
