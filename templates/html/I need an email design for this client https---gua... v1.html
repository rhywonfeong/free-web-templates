Dear Friend in Christ,

In the stillness of August, the Church honors the Immaculate Heart of Mary, a heart wholly united to God open to all who seek refuge, mercy, and hope. At the Shrine of Our Lady of Guadalupe, this sacred month offers us a renewed invitation to bring our needs and sorrows to the heart of Mary, confident that she brings them before her Son with a mother’s tender care.

If you carry a burden, a hope, or a prayer of thanksgiving, we invite you to share it with us. Your intention will be placed near the altar and remembered in our daily Masses and Rosaries.

About the Shrine

The Shrine of Our Lady of Guadalupe in La Crosse, Wisconsin, is a place of pilgrimage dedicated to Our Lady and her merciful intercession. We welcome all who seek a deeper encounter with Christ through sacred beauty, silence, and prayer. Whether you visit in person or from afar, you are part of this spiritual family.

To learn more, visit guadalupeshrine.org.
