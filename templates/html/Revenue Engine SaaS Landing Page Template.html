04 / Scope of work — what's included

Four workstreams.

01

Landing Page & Funnel Design

The pages a visitor actually moves through.

  • Homepage — the freedom-platform hub, built on the calm, cinematic direction from your blueprint.
  • Free Community opt-in page — the top-of-funnel lead capture.
  • Boot Camp sales page ($999) — full conversion page: outcome, curriculum, proof, objection handling, checkout.
  • Mentorship application page ($5,000+) — qualification + booked-call funnel.
  • Supporting pages — thank-you / confirmation steps and member welcome / onboarding entry.
  • Mobile-first, conversion-led layout with copy direction included alongside design.
02

Email & SMS Flow Architecture

The sequences that warm, convert, and retain on autopilot.

  • Full flow mapping — trigger, timing, content purpose, and conversion goal for every email.
  • Copywriting for each sequence, in the restrained no-hype voice the brand runs on.
  • Design direction and build inside the chosen ESP — not just copy handed off.
  • Segmentation logic — who enters each flow and under what conditions.
  • Detailed in §05.
03

Automations & Pipeline

The invisible wiring that routes every member to the right place.

  • Pipeline stages — lead → free member → Boot Camp buyer → Mentorship applicant → Mentorship member.
  • Tagging & segmentation by tier and engagement, so messaging stays relevant.
  • Application routing & booking integration for the Mentorship funnel.
  • Payment & enrollment triggers — purchase fires onboarding, access, and the right next flow.
  • Free → paid escalation rules that move warm leads toward the next rung automatically.
04

Launch & QA

Proving the machine works before a dollar depends on it.

  • End-to-end testing — every CTA, every checkout, every flow trigger, on mobile and desktop.
  • Trust-killer sweep — no dead buttons, no typos, no broken steps (the stuff that's hurting the current site).
  • Handoff & walkthrough so you know exactly what's running and how.
