Ref: L-2023-V4

1. Manufacturing Warranty
RX Orthotics warrants that all custom orthotic devices will be free from defects in materials and workmanship. The polypropylene shell is warranted against breakage for the lifetime of the patient.

2. Top Covers
Top covers and soft tissue supplementation (padding) are warranted for six (6) months from the date of shipment against normal wear and tear.

3. Adjustments
Fit adjustments are provided at no charge for 90 days following the shipping date. This includes narrowing, grinding, or adding arch fill. Major structural changes requiring a new shell are not considered adjustments.

For specific returns, please contact support prior to shipping devices back to the lab.
