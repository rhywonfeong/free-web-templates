Our Story

Rooted in the UK.

The Revival Fellowship has been active in the UK for decades, quietly building communities where everyday people can discover the extraordinary power of God together.

We believe that faith isn't meant to be complicated or strictly institutional. While each of our local fellowships operates independently to best serve its specific area, we remain deeply united in our core beliefs and shared purpose.

We place a strong emphasis on genuine community, practical faith, and real-life transformation. When you visit any of our meetings, you won't find heavy religious rituals or exaggeration. Instead, you'll find a grounded, human approach to exploring what the Bible means for our lives today.

Part of a Worldwide Fellowship

What began in Australia has steadily grown to span multiple continents. The UK & Ireland fellowships are part of this wider global body, sharing a profound unity in doctrine, spirit, and purpose. No matter where you travel in the world, the fellowship feels like home.

What Makes Us Distinct

Bible-Centred Teaching

We skip the heavy theological wording and traditions. Instead, we focus on clear, straightforward teaching directly from the scriptures that anyone can understand.

Active Faith

We believe faith isn't just a Sunday morning activity. It's a dynamic, everyday experience that shapes how we live, work, and treat others throughout the week.

Community & Support

We are a family. We support one another through life's ups and downs with genuine care, ensuring nobody has to walk through difficult seasons alone.

Personal Experience of the Spirit

We embrace the biblical promise of receiving the Holy Spirit. It's not just historical—it brings transformative power and comfort to our everyday lives.

Leadership & Structure

Our local fellowships are led by dedicated pastors and ministry teams who view their roles as service-focused, not hierarchical.

Rather than operating in isolation, there is strong collaboration, shared resources, and close fellowship between churches. This ensures a steady, trustworthy environment where everyone is supported to grow in their faith.
