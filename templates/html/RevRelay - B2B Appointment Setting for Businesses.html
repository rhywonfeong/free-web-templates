FAQs — Everything you need to know.

How do you ensure the meetings are actually qualified?
We use a multi-step qualification process including budget confirmation, decision-maker verification, and timeline validation before any meeting gets booked on your calendar.
What if prospects don't show up to the calls?
You don't pay. Our model is purely pay-per-show. If a prospect no-shows, reschedules and fails to attend, or turns out to be unqualified, you owe us nothing for that meeting.
How is this different from other lead generation agencies?
Traditional agencies charge monthly retainers whether they deliver results or not. We only get paid when you get qualified prospects on sales calls. Our incentives are completely aligned with your success.
How quickly can you start generating meetings?
Most clients see their first qualified meetings booked within 3-4 weeks. Full pipeline flow typically develops by week 6-8.
What industries do you work with?
We specialize in B2B companies selling high-ticket services or products ($3K-$50K+) that require sales conversations to close deals.
