Frequently Asked Questions

Does it need to be refrigerated?

No, Berry Seoul is a shelf-stable product that stays fresh at room temperature, making it ideal for international travel. Keep it away from direct sunlight and extreme heat.

Where can I find the pop-up store?

Follow our social media or check the website for current pop-up locations. We frequently rotate through major shopping districts and tourist hotspots in Seoul.

Is the packaging sturdy enough for a long flight?

Absolutely. Our boxes are specifically designed to be compact yet durable to withstand the rigors of travel. The inner structure prevents the dessert from moving or getting damaged.
