Common Questions

How much does it cost to import a car?
Total cost includes purchase price (CIF), Import Duty (25%), VAT (16%), IDF (3.5%), and RDL (2%). We provide a detailed breakdown before purchase.
Do you offer financing in Eldoret?
Yes! We partner with leading banks and SACCOs to offer financing up to 80% with repayment periods of up to 60 months. Interest rates start from 12%.
Are the cars inspected?
Absolutely. Imported cars undergo QISJ inspection in Japan. Locally used cars undergo a comprehensive multi-point mechanical check at our Eldoret yard.
Do you accept trade-ins?
Yes, we accept trade-ins. Bring your vehicle for a free valuation, and use the value towards your new purchase or import order.
