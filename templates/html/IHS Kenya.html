About Us

At International Housing Solutions (IHS), we finance the development of energy efficient, affordable housing communities.

We recognize the need to both create viable housing investments and provide broader community infrastructure with easy access to schools, shops and work opportunities.

2005 Established

The IHS Group is a pioneer in the green affordable housing sector and has built a strong reputation by successfully financing the construction and delivery of new homes while building sustainable communities.

We have established long-term partnerships with key stakeholders, which allows us to bring together the necessary elements for creating successful investments in the affordable housing market. Furthermore, our relationships have enabled us to create a strong pipeline of prospective investments in the regions we operate in.

Core Pillars

Investment

The IHS Group ("IHS") invests in the delivery of green affordable housing projects, designed to be energy and water efficient, which generate social and environmental benefits beyond simply addressing housing needs. Through our investment in climate-friendly affordable housing, our projects help to create jobs, grow skills and stimulate economic development.

Asset Value $450M+

Fund Management

IHS manages investment vehicles that pool capital aimed at delivering attractive risk-adjusted returns to our investors while also delivering social and environmental benefits. We provide a holistic approach to Fund, Portfolio and Asset Management all the way from inception right through capital raising, deal sourcing, project delivery, and investment exit.

Property Management

IHS Property Management ("IHS PM"), an affiliate of IHS, provides hands-on property management and administration services for all IHS investments.
