06 · The atelier

Built slowly.
Made to last.

Aperture began in 1987 when cabinetmaker Elias Vale was asked to create a single oversized oak door for a coastal home. Its apparent simplicity demanded every discipline he knew.

Today, his daughter Mara leads a multidisciplinary team of designers, engineers, joiners, and metalworkers united by the same belief: usefulness and beauty should never be separated.
