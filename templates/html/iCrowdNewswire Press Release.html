The Mechanism

Active Marketing, Not Passive Publishing

iCrowdNewswire doesn't stop at putting your release on the wire it puts paid media advertising behind it on Google, Facebook, and social, so the release is actively marketed to the exact audience you choose.

You target by geography, industry, age, and interest, and every release goes out in 9 languages to reach audiences English-only distribution misses.

Because views are driven by paid campaigns, the audience is high-value and the figures are auditable not random clicks.

And it's self-service and pay-as-you-go: no membership fees, no word overages, no contract.

Founder Story

Common Questions

Doubt Removal

How is this different from a normal press release wire?

A standard wire publishes your release and reports potential reach. We add paid media advertising on top, actively driving real, targeted audiences to it and the viewership is auditable, not estimated.

Do I have to sign a contract or pay membership fees?

No. It's pay-as-you-go with no contract, no membership fees, and no word-overage charges you pay per release.

What if I don't have time to format the release?

Use the full-service option upload what you have and the team formats and distributes it for you, including rush service.

Can my release actually reach non-English audiences?

Yes. Every release is distributed in 9 languages, which matters because about half of global digital searches happen in languages other than English.

What do I actually get to show for it?

A detailed report with web placements, social impressions and clicks, and keyword analytics the kind of auditable proof you can hand a client or executive.
