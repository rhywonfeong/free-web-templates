Frequently asked questions

Who is executive coaching for?
Executive coaching is designed for C-suite leaders, VPs, and high-potential directors who wish to elevate their performance, navigate transitions, or address specific leadership challenges.
What is the typical duration of an engagement?
While every engagement is tailored, typical coaching programs last between 6 to 12 months. Advisory projects are scoped based on organizational needs and may range from weeks to ongoing retainers.
Do you offer services in languages other than English?
Yes, Aspireon is a fully bilingual consultancy. We offer all our coaching and advisory services in both English and French to accommodate diverse leadership teams.
