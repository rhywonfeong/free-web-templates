The Soul

Meet Alice & her wings.

"Ali di Miele" is a play on my life. It captures the wings of the bees I tend to, and the nickname I've carried since childhood.

Based in Novate Milanese, I manage every hive alone. My philosophy is simple: don't rush. No pasteurization, no chemical shortcuts. Just raw honey as it was intended to be — seasonal, vibrant, and pure.
