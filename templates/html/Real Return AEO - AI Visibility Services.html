HOW SEARCH HAS CHANGED

The Old Search vs. The New Reality

Traditional Search

  • Customer types query → sees 10 blue links → clicks through to websites
  • You rank page one → get traffic → make the pitch on your site
  • Keywords, backlinks and page speed determined who won
  • The customer did the comparing - you just had to show up
  • Optimising for robots crawling your HTML

AI-Driven Search

  • Customer asks question → AI gives direct answer → customer acts on it
  • AI recommends you specifically → customer arrives pre-sold, pre-qualified
  • Authority, structure, citations and expertise signals now decide who wins
  • The AI does the comparing - if you're not in its dataset, you're invisible
  • Optimising for language models that assess credibility and clarity
