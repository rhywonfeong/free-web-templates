Who We Are

A therapeutic home for musical connection, healing, and community.

Expression Concert Music Foundation C.I.C is a community interest company using music and creative arts to support emotional wellbeing across the life span. Our practitioners combine clinical music therapy, community arts practice, and trauma-informed care to create safe, inclusive spaces for healing.

Who we support

Children, young people, adults, and older adults navigating anxiety, grief, neurodiversity, long-term health conditions, and social isolation.

How we work

Evidence-based interventions, co-designed programmes with communities, and reflective practice rooted in compassion, cultural awareness, and lived experience.

Therapeutic Outcomes

Reduced anxiety, improved mood, strengthened emotional regulation, and increased sense of belonging.

Spaces

Schools, NHS and healthcare settings, hospices, community halls, online sessions, and creative studios.

Approach

Person-centred, strengths-based, culturally responsive, with clear safeguarding, supervision, and evaluation.
