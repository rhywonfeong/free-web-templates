FAQ's

Healthy Way, answered.

Is Healthy Way Vegetable Oil cholesterol free?

Yes. Healthy Way is 100% all natural, cholesterol free and naturally low in saturated fats — a lighter choice for everyday Jamaican cooking.

Can I use Healthy Way for deep frying?

Absolutely. Its high smoke point makes it perfect for frying festival, plantains and chicken without breaking down or burning your spices.

What sizes does Healthy Way come in?

From the Likkle Gill (110ml) right up to 1 Gallon — including 180ml, 300ml, 400ml, 750ml and our best selling 1 Liter.

Where can I buy Healthy Way?

Healthy Way is available islandwide at Hi Lo, MegaMart, PriceSmart, Shoppers Fair, General Food Supermarket and wholesale distributors across Jamaica.

Simply create a wholesale account or send us a wholesale inquiry — our distribution team in Kingston will get you set up.
