Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Fleetlytics.

Is Fleetlytics compliant with PH invoicing and VAT?

Yes—issue VAT-inclusive invoices and official receipts with customizable series, plus withholding support.

Can I manage cars, vans, motorcycles, and EVs together?

Absolutely. Create categories, rates, and maintenance schedules per vehicle type—track utilization across the entire fleet.

How secure is my data?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, with role-based access, audit logs, and daily backups.

Do you offer migration from spreadsheets?

Yes—concierge onboarding is included for Growth and Pro plans. We’ll import vehicles, rates, and active bookings.

Does Fleetlytics support branches and user roles?

Yes. Pro includes multi-branch management with granular roles and permissions for owners, managers, dispatchers, and accountants.

Is there a free trial?

Starter is free for up to 10 vehicles. You can upgrade or cancel anytime. We also offer a 14‑day Growth trial on request.
