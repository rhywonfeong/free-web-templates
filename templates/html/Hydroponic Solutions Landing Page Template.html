Frequently Asked Questions

Is hydroponic produce organic?

It is better than organic. We use mineral-rich water solutions without soil, eliminating soil-borne diseases and the need for pesticides.

Cost of commercial setup?

Costs vary by automation level. Generally starts from $10/sq.ft. Contact us for a precise quote based on your area.

Does it require high maintenance?

Less labor than traditional farming, but requires daily monitoring of pH and EC levels (15-20 mins/day).
