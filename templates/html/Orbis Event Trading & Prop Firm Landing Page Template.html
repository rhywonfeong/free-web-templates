Frequently Asked Questions

What is Orbis and how does it work?
Orbis is a revolutionary market platform merging the liquidity of prediction markets with the scaling of proprietary trading firms. Traders can speculate on real-world events, build a verifiable track record, and earn access to significant trading capital.
What is the difference between an Evaluation and a Straight to Funded account?
Evaluation accounts require you to pass a dedicated testing phase to prove your edge and risk management skills before receiving capital. Straight to Funded accounts bypass this phase, providing immediate access to live capital for experienced traders ready to execute their strategies instantly.
How quickly can I get funded?
You can get funded in as little as a few days. Once you meet the profit targets and adhere to our risk management rules in the evaluation phase, your account will be upgraded to a funded status upon swift review.
How many accounts can I have?
To ensure optimal risk management and maintain a high standard of trading, each trader is permitted to manage up to 5 distinct accounts simultaneously. This allows you to diversify your strategies across different market categories safely.
