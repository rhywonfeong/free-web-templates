Our mission & vision

We believe that time in nature changes lives. HolaCamp builds welcoming experiences where people grow skills, confidence, and belonging—supported by thoughtful programs and simple, human-centered tools.

Mission

Make outdoor learning accessible and delightful through inclusive programs, supportive communities, and tools that empower staff and campers alike.

Vision

A global network of sustainable, tech-enabled camps where every person can feel safe, inspired, and connected—to self, others, and the planet.

Hiring process

Clear steps, timely updates, and structured feedback.

  1. 1. Apply

    Upload your resume/portfolio and answer a few role-specific prompts.

  2. 2. Recruiter chat

    30 minutes to learn more about you and answer questions.

  3. 3. Panel interviews

    Meet cross-functional peers; structured questions, no surprises.

  4. 4. Practical task

    Short, paid exercise mirroring real work. Clear feedback.

  5. 5. Offer

    We aim to decide within a week of final interviews.

Do you hire remotely?

Yes. Many roles are remote within overlapping time zones. Some operations roles are hybrid/on-site.

Do you sponsor visas?

In select cases and regions. Mention your situation in your application.

How should I prepare for interviews?

Review the role’s scope, prepare examples with outcomes, and bring questions about how we work.

What’s your timeline?

We respond within two weeks of application and move fast after interviews.
