InnerClass helps you gently shift from pressure-based motivation toward curiosity and vision, at your own pace.

Study vision board (5 minutes) Your manifestation pillar in one page

Create a simple vision board just for your studies. It’s not about making it perfect—it’s about seeing your future self every time you sit down to learn.

1. Future moment

Write or paste 1–2 images of you after your studies helped you: exam day, dream job, helping someone.

2. Study feelings

Choose 3 words you want to feel while studying (calm, focused, proud…) and add them big and clear.

3. Today’s actions

List 1–3 small actions for tonight that match your vision: “20 mins math,” “review notes,” “ask 1 question.”

Keep this page near your desk. Before you start homework, look at it, take one slow breath, and choose just one action from your board.
