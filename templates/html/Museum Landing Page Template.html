Olney

Heritage Museum

Preserving the rich tapestry of local history, bringing the stories, artifacts, and legacy of our community to life for future generations.

Mission Statement

To preserve, protect, and present the history and cultural heritage of Olney, ensuring that the legacy of our past informs and inspires our community today and tomorrow.

About Museum

The Olney Heritage Museum was conceived as a way to make local history accessible to everyone, bridging the gap between historical preservation and community engagement.

Our collections offer a journey through the pivotal eras of our town, featuring works, artifacts, and personal histories that shaped our region.

We strive to create a balance between a modern educational environment and a deep respect for the historical context of the items we curate.
