Our Story

Nearly 30 Years of Trusted Care

Greater Washington Dentistry began with one dentist's commitment to gentle, judgment-free care for kids — Dr. Shohreh Sharif, a Howard University-trained pediatric specialist and Diplomate of the American Board of Pediatric Dentistry. Nearly three decades later, that same standard carries forward with Dr. Joon Kim and Dr. Samon Nazemian, serving families across our Merrifield and Fair Oaks locations.

Nearly 30 years. Three doctors. One unwavering commitment to your family's smiles.
