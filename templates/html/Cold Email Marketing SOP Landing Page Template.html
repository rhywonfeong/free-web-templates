The Three Pillars of Cold Email

Before any execution, understand that cold email success depends on getting three pillars right. If your system is not working, diagnose which pillar is weak. If it is working, find the weakest one and improve it.

1. Technical Infrastructure

Domains, DNS records, mailbox config, warm-up, software setup. Follow the recipe exactly.

2. List Building

Finding leads who are a good fit. Verifying emails. Qualifying with AI. Avoid spam reports by emailing relevant people.

3. Offer & Copy

Does your offer solve a real problem? Is it interesting? Will the copy pass spam filters and earn trust?

Key Principle

Fundamentals beat fancy AI every time. Only layer in advanced tactics (AI personalization, signal workflows, Clay enrichment) after the three pillars are solid.

1

Technical Infrastructure Setup

Goal: Build the mailbox infrastructure that lands in the inbox, not spam. Budget: ~$3/month per mailbox using a reseller.

Step 1.1 — Purchase Domains

  • Buy domains on Spaceship (recommended) or similar registrar. Cost: ~$2–$9 per domain per year.
  • Use variations of your primary domain. Example: if your company is AcmeSales.com, buy AcmeSalesHQ.com, TryAcmeSales.com, GetAcmeSales.com, etc.
  • Buy 1 domain per 3–4 mailboxes. If you need 20 mailboxes, purchase ~5–7 domains.
  • Do NOT use your primary business domain for cold email. If it gets flagged, your entire business email is compromised.
  • Set up URL forwarding so each cold email domain redirects to your main website (permanent 301 redirect).

Step 1.2 — Configure DNS Records

For each domain, configure the following DNS records. Use EasyDMARC.com to scan and verify.

Record Purpose How to Set Up
MX Mail exchange routing Point to Google (or Microsoft) mail servers. Priority 1. Added during mailbox setup.
SPF Sender authentication TXT record. Use EasyDMARC SPF generator. Host: @ (default).
DKIM Email signing TXT record. Generated in Google Workspace Admin > DKIM authentication.
DMARC Policy enforcement TXT record. Host: _dmarc. Use EasyDMARC generator. Policy: quarantine or reject.
CNAME Custom tracking domain Host: inst (for Instantly). Value provided in your Instantly account settings.
Validation

After adding all records, scan with EasyDMARC.com > Tools > Domain Scanner. All four records (MX, SPF, DKIM, DMARC) must show green/valid before proceeding.

Step 1.3 — Create Mailboxes

  • Use Google Workspace (recommended). Highest inbox placement at 87.2%. Use a reseller/setup service at ~$3/month per mailbox instead of the retail $8.40.
  • Microsoft 365 is second best at ~75.6% inbox placement. Use as a supplement to Google, not a replacement.
  • SMTP/Custom IP services (Mailin, Inframail) are cheapest but riskiest. Only use after you have a working system with Google. Unmanaged IPs can burn all your mailboxes at once.
  • Create 3–4 mailboxes per domain. Use realistic names: john@, sarah@, mike@. Avoid generic names like info@ or sales@.
  • Set up profile pictures and signatures on each mailbox to appear human.

Step 1.4 — Connect to Instantly AI & Warm Up

  • Add all mailboxes to Instantly AI. Go to Email Accounts and connect each one.
  • Enable warm-up (fire emoji = ON). Set to increase by 1–2 per day until it matches your daily campaign send limit (e.g., 10).
  • Reply rate: 30–100%. All values in this range work.
  • Enable advanced settings: weekends only, read emulation, warm custom tracking domain. These emulate human behavior.
  • Warm up for 14–21 days minimum before sending any cold emails.
  • Leave warm-up always on — even while campaigns are running. Warm-up emails offset spam complaints.
NEVER DO THIS

Do not use open tracking or click tracking in cold emails. Do not use unsubscribe links in Email 1. Do not forward cold email replies to your primary inbox. All of these destroy deliverability.

2

List Building & Lead Qualification

Goal: Build a hyper-targeted list of leads who are actually a good fit for your offer. The quality of your list directly determines your reply rates and spam complaint rates.

Step 2.1 — Define Your ICP

Document the following:

  • Industry/niche: Specific industries you serve.
  • Job titles: Who is the decision maker?
  • Company size: Employee count/revenue range.
  • Geography: Targeted regions/countries.
  • Technologies: Tools suggesting they need you.
  • Pain points: Specific problems your offer solves.

Step 2.2 — Build List (Apollo.io)

  • Go to Apollo.io > People Search. Use recommended filters.
  • Always set Email Status = Verified .
  • Use company keywords to refine by company description.
  • Avoid Apollo’s signal filters, buyer intent, and revenue filters (inaccurate data).
  • Export as CSV with necessary contact and company info.

Step 2.3 — Verify Your Emails

Run all emails through Million Verifier (~$37 per 100K). Remove invalid/risky emails. For catch-all domains (often 50%+ of list), use Scrubby or Find Email. Bounce rate must stay under 3%.

Step 2.4 — AI Lead Qualification (Recommended)

Use AI to review each lead. This step alone can 2–3x your positive reply rate. Expect to remove 30–50% of your list.

Prompt Template

"You’re an expert sales assistant. Review this lead’s company info and decide if they’re a good fit for my offer. My offer: I help [X] with [Y]. Company info: [insert]. Output YES or NO only. No explanations."
Tools: Clay (best), Sheet Magic, or Instantly’s built-in AI templates.
3

Craft Your Offer & Write Copy

Step 3.1 — Position Your Offer

Must solve a specific problem, be clear under 10 seconds, and aim to get a hand-raise, not a sale on Email 1. If boring, use repositioing:

Loss Leader Offer high-value service near cost to get a foot in, then upsell.
Trojan Horse Frame as partnership or intro, not a direct sale.
Front-End Offer Pull out the single most desirable component and lead with it.
Reverse Lead Magnet Do the work BEFORE emailing. Show specific results.

Step 3.2 — Write Your Copy (Triple Tap)

Section Purpose Rules
1. Preview Text
(Subject + First Line) 		Gets the email opened. Only thing seen before opening. Keep subject 1–4 words. First sentence = personalized or curiosity. No spam words.
2. Body Builds interest. Positions offer. Short (2–4 sentences). Mention specific pain. Social proof. No links in Email 1.
3. CTA
(Call to Action) 		Gets the reply. Single action desired. Soft, low-commitment. "Worth a quick look?" Never ask for a meeting in Email 1.

Step 3.3 — Copywriting Rules

DO

  • • Write like a human texting a colleague (short, casual).
  • • Use personalization variables ({{firstName}}).
  • • Keep Email 1 plain text only (No links/images/HTML).
  • • Use spam word checkers before launching.
  • • Test deliverability (GlockApps) before going live.

DON'T

  • • Use opt-out links in Email 1 (Use soft opt-out in Email 2+).
  • • Use spam words: free, guarantee, act now, click here.
  • • Use open/click tracking links.
  • • Send links in Email 1.
  • • Write long emails. Shorter delivers better.
4

Campaign Setup & Launch

4.1 — Email Sequence

Recommended: 3-email sequence.

Email 1: Plain text. No links. Short. Focus on inbox/open.
Email 2 (Day 3-5): Follow-up in thread. Link allowed. Soft opt-out.
Email 3 (Day 6-10): Breakup/new angle. New pain point. Opt-out language.
