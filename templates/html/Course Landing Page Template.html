Frequently Asked Questions

Is this course for service-based or product-based businesses?
Both! Strategic partnerships work incredibly well whether you are selling physical products, digital goods, or services. The principles of audience alignment and value exchange remain the same.
How much time do I need to dedicate to the course?
The video lessons are designed to be punchy and actionable—about 2-3 hours total. However, the real work happens in the workbook and execution. You have lifetime access, so you can move at your own pace.
I have a tiny audience. Can I still do partnerships?
Absolutely. Partnerships aren't just about audience size; they are about audience quality and the creative value you bring. We have an entire module dedicated to figuring out your unique leverage, even if your follower count is small.
