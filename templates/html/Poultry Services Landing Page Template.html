About the Farm

Established in 2018, Shahbaz Poultry Services has grown from a visionary concept into a standard-bearer for quality poultry farming in the region. We are not just a farm; we are an institution dedicated to the science of bird health and ethical farming practices.

Our primary mission is to ensure the highest level of hygiene and biosecurity. We understand that the quality of poultry output is directly related to the environment in which birds are raised. Through rigorous management and continuous improvement of our facilities, we maintain a disease-free, stress-free environment for our flock.
