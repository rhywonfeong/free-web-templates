Recruiting Periods Explained

Contact Period

Coaches can have face-to-face contact with you or your parents, watch you compete, and visit your high school. Communication is fully open.

Evaluation Period

Coaches can watch you compete and visit your school, but cannot have face-to-face conversations with you off-campus.

Quiet Period

Coaches can only talk to you on their college campus. They cannot watch you play or visit your high school.

Dead Period

No in-person contact allowed anywhere. Coaches cannot watch you play. Phone calls and emails are still permitted.

Based on 2024-2025 NCAA Women's Lacrosse Rules
