How is your MoneySign® assessed?

Determined through the Five-Factor Model of personality, widely known as the OCEAN framework. It draws on specific facets that most directly influence financial behavior.

OCEAN
O

Openness

Captures the desire and ability to seek new experiences, think unconventionally.

Financial Implication Shapes how receptive a person is to newer investment options.
C

Conscientiousness

Reflects capacity to follow through on goals, exercise self-discipline.

Financial Implication Drives savings habits, long-term decisions, holding to a plan.
E

Extraversion

Indicates how readily a person draws energy from social interaction.

Financial Implication Decisions shaped more by peer behaviour than independent analysis.
A

Agreeableness

Measures how cooperatively and compassionately a person relates to others.

Financial Implication Shapes weight given to partner's preferences or advisor recommendations.
N

Neuroticism

Considers tendency toward impulsive behaviour, emotional instability.

Financial Implication Higher susceptibility to reactive decision-making during volatility.
