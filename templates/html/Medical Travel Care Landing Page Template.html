Frequently asked questions

If you can’t find the answer, message us—we reply quickly.

How fast can I get an appointment?

Most GP visits are same‑day. For specialists and imaging, we typically arrange within 24–48 hours.

Do you help with insurance paperwork?

Yes. We prepare invoices, medical reports, and forms needed for claims or direct billing where available.

Which languages do you support?

English, French, and Arabic. Translation is available during appointments if needed.

Can you arrange hospital admissions?

Absolutely. We check bed availability, coordinate with specialists, and handle admission logistics.

What if I’m outside major cities?

Message us. We often support nearby areas with telemedicine, transport, or partner clinics.
