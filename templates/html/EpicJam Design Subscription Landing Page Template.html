Frequently asked questions

Why not just hire a full-time designer?
A senior designer costs $100k+ annually, plus benefits. And you might not have enough work to keep them busy all the time. With EpicJam, you can pause your subscription whenever you don't have requests.
Is there a limit on requests?
No limits! Once you're subscribed, you can add as many design requests to your queue as you'd like, and they will be delivered one by one.
How fast will I receive my designs?
On average, most requests are completed in just two days or less. However, more complex requests (like a full landing page) can take longer.
What if I don't like the design?
No worries! We'll continue to revise the design until you're 100% satisfied. We don't stop until it's perfect.
