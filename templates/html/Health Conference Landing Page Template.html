About The Health Series

The Health Series is a year-round ecosystem of healthcare conferences, exhibitions, and policy dialogues that bring together diverse stakeholders to strengthen health systems across Africa. Each event is curated to drive measurable impact—from preventive wellness to maternal health, cross-border collaboration, and healthcare financing.

Organized By HCA Consults

Driving Impact Through Expertise

HCA Consults is a leading event management and consulting firm with strong expertise across healthcare and sustainability sectors in Africa. The company has successfully executed the Lagos Health Summit, Nigeria Healthcare Investment Forum, and the Pan-African Health Alliance Summit, convening leaders from government, private sector, development agencies, and academia.
