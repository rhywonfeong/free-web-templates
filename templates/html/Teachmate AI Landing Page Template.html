Frequently asked questions

What is a multi-agent platform?
Unlike a standard chatbot, a multi-agent platform uses specialized AI "agents" that pass context to one another. For example, a Planner Agent creates a syllabus, and a Creator Agent automatically turns that syllabus into weekly lesson materials without you having to reprompt it.
Can I use it for university courses?
Yes! Teachmate is designed for both K-12 and Higher Education. Our agents can parse advanced academic texts, generate university-level discussion prompts, and align with complex higher-ed grading rubrics.
Does it help students too?
While primarily an educator tool, Teachmate includes a Student Tutor Agent workflow. Educators can set up parameters and share a safe, contained AI tutor link with students to help them practice concepts step-by-step.
Can I upload my syllabus/PDFs?
Yes, Pro users can upload PDFs, Word docs, and text files. The AI will read your existing materials to ensure outputs (like quizzes or slide decks) match your specific course content.
How do you handle privacy?
We default to privacy-first. Your prompts and uploaded materials are not used to train global public AI models. For institutions, we offer administrative controls and data processing agreements.
Can an institution deploy it?
Absolutely. Our Institution plan includes custom onboarding, administrative dashboards to manage seats, usage analytics, and standard SSO integrations.
