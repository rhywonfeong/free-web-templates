Dr. Bernardt Beukes

Dentist (BChD, Dentistry)

Dr. Beukes was born on 20 January 1985. He is the youngest of three children and grew up in Uitenhage.

He matriculated in 2003 and graduated from the University of the Western Cape near Stellenbosch with his degree in dentistry in 2008. He completed his community service year in Humansdorp.

In 2010, Dr. Beukes joined Dentists@Health in Graaff-Reinet, where he became a director of the company in 2012 up until now.

His scope of practice includes the full spectrum of general and surgical dentistry as well as orthodontic treatment. His dedication to patient care shines through in his thoroughness in dental treatment and strives to obtain healthy smiles for all of his patients.

He loves spending time in nature and that makes Graaff-Reinet the perfect place!

Surgical Dentistry Orthodontics

Dr. Anzette van Zyl

Dentist (BChD, Dentistry)

Dr. Anzette van Zyl is a qualified dental surgeon with a distinctive interest in paediatric dentistry and facial aesthetics. She grew up in Swakopmund, Namibia where she matriculated from Namib High School.

She then proceeded to study at the University of the Western Cape where she graduated in 2013. Her community service year was completed in Graaff-Reinet and this is where she met her husband and subsequently decided to lay down roots.

Dentistry is in an exceedingly modernizing era, with different techniques and materials constantly being developed. It is for this reason Dr. van Zyl routinely participates in workshops and courses.

She completed a post graduate course in administering Botulinum toxin and dermal filler injections, and fell in love with the industry of facial aesthetics. She also aims to further her Orthodontic studies. She is very proud to be affiliated with Dentists@Health.

Paediatric Facial Aesthetics Botox & Fillers

Dr. Elriëtte van Huyssteen

Dentist (BChD, Dentistry)

Originally from the small town of Kareedouw in the Eastern Cape, Dr. Elriëtte van Huyssteen completed her schooling there before pursuing her dental degree at the University of the Western Cape.

During her community service year in Graaff-Reinet, she developed a deep connection with the town and its people, where she also met her husband.

Passionate about dentistry and committed to serving the community, Dr. Van Huyssteen takes particular joy in working with children. She is currently advancing her expertise with further studies in orthodontics. Her goal is to ensure that every patient enjoys a comfortable, pain-free experience.
