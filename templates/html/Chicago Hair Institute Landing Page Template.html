Surgical Expertise

We specialize in ultra-refined follicular unit grafting, ensuring the most natural hairlines and maximum density possible.

Compare Procedures

FUE (Follicular Unit Excision)

Individual graft extraction using precision microsurgical punches. Ideal for patients who wish to wear their hair very short.

Ultra-Refined Grafting

Dr. Konior is famous for his "invisible" scar closures. This method yields the highest number of high-quality grafts for maximum density.

Hairline Lowering

A specialty of Dr. Nadimi. Surgical advancement of the hairline to reduce forehead size, often providing instant density results.

Repair & Corrective Work

We frequently correct pluggy, unnatural results from other clinics. We refine hairlines and camouflage old scarring.

Eyebrow Restoration

Artistic placement of single hairs to restore density and shape to eyebrows thinned by over-plucking or genetics.

Beard Transplant

Restoring facial hair density using FUE to transfer follicles from the scalp to the beard or mustache area.
