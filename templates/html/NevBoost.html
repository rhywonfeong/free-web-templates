How we run it

Most "meetings booked" offers skip the gate and jump into volume. We run a short Week 0 diagnostic first. It locks the four things that determine whether outbound produces real meetings:

  • Who you are actually targeting this month
  • What the first message is really claiming
  • Whether you will consistently land in inbox
  • How you will measure meeting quality, not just calendar fills

Then we execute for you the outbound sprint using the agreed guardrails.
