03. The Audit

Key UX Problems

Weak Brand Storytelling

The fun, tropical theme is lost in standard website layouts.

Navigation Confusion

Adult and kids services are mixed, causing cognitive overload.

Mobile UX Issues

Unresponsive elements and poor spacing on smaller screens.

Booking Flow Unclear

Calls to action are buried beneath dense paragraphs of text.

🐒

About the Brand

Forest Salon is a premium themed space aiming to make grooming enjoyable for children and relaxing for adults.

  • Theme-based setting
  • Tear-free experience
  • Safe, ethical products

06. The Shift

Before vs After Website

Before
  • Dense text copy masking the brand vibe.
  • Kids and Adult services mixed in one long list.
  • Hidden booking button requiring scrolling.
  • Poor mobile spacing and tiny tap targets.
After
  • Visual storytelling with themed illustrations.
  • Clear segregation via tabbed service UI.
  • Sticky 'Book Now' CTA always accessible.
  • Mobile-first components and generous padding.
