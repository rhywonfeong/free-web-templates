FAQ

Who can participate?

Anyone 18+ with a passion for building. Whether you're a developer, designer, or domain expert in finance/crypto — you're welcome. No prior blockchain or ZK experience required.

Is this hackathon in-person only?

Yes. This is a fully in-person event in Manhattan, NYC. No remote participation.

Do I need a team?

No. You can register solo. We'll have a team formation session Friday evening. Teams of 2-5 are recommended.

Do I need to use Unlink and Monad?

Yes. Your project must integrate Unlink's privacy layer and deploy on Monad.

Code of Conduct?
