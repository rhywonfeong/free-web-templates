Frequently Asked Questions

How does TFL differ from Ho:YAG lasers?

TFL uses a diode-pumped fiber source emitting at 1940nm, closer to the water absorption peak than Ho:YAG (2100nm). This allows for lower energy per pulse but much higher frequencies, resulting in finer dusting and less retropulsion.

Is a special power outlet required?

No. Unlike high-power Ho:YAG systems that often require 3-phase power, the Lumina TFL operates on a standard domestic power outlet (100-240V), making it easy to move between ORs.

What fibers are compatible?

The system is optimized for use with our proprietary silica fibers ranging from 150µm to 600µm. The 150µm fiber is ideal for flexible ureteroscopy to maximize irrigation flow.

Is this system approved for BPH treatment?

Yes, the system is indicated for incision, excision, resection, ablation, vaporization, coagulation, and hemostasis of soft tissue in urology, including BPH enucleation (ThuLEP).
