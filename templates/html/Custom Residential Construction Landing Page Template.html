Welcome to Bihn Construction

Bihn Construction was founded with a clear vision: to bring timeless homes to life — built with integrity, guided by faith, and crafted to endure.

After managing multimillion-dollar projects in New York, Brandon Bihn and his family relocated to Upstate South Carolina to build a company rooted in purpose — one that values relationships over transactions, and excellence over shortcuts.

At Bihn Construction, we believe building a home is more than a project, it’s a partnership. Our approach is simple: understand your vision clearly, collaborate intentionally, and deliver results that stand the test of time. Each home we build reflects a commitment to meticulous craftsmanship, enduring quality, and a client-first mindset.

From steep terrain to complex architectural demands, we take on what others avoid — because where others hesitate, we build.

Serving premier communities across Upstate South Carolina, including The Cliffs, The Reserve at Lake Keowee, and beyond, Bihn Construction is proud to create honest, timeless homes that leave a lasting legacy.
