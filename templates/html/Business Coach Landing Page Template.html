About Me

I started working at 15, delivering newspapers. By 20, I had started my first business. Over the next 27 years, I grew it into a company with 25 employees, managing teams, vans, office staff and demanding client work.

My experience spans home-service and civil-engineering projects. I understand the pressure of keeping work coming in, getting jobs completed properly, managing people and making sure there is money left at the end of the month.

The turning point was learning to work ON the business rather than only IN it.

“Do I own this business, or does the business own me?”
“I will work as hard as I have to, so that I can eventually do as little as possible.”
