SFDR Disclosures

Sondo Capital integrates sustainability risks into its investment decision-making process in accordance with the Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation (SFDR). We believe that evaluating environmental, social, and governance (ESG) criteria is vital to identifying resilient, future-proof founders and technologies.

Our fund focuses on backing teams that align with long-term sustainable growth. While we remain a generalist pre-seed and seed fund, avoiding strict exclusion mandates outside standard international sanctions, we actively monitor our portfolio's impact and encourage continuous improvement in governance and societal contributions.
