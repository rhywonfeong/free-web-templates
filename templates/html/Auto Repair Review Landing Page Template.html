Items worth checking

Carsys highlights wording, pricing structure, or missing detail that may deserve a follow-up question.

Line item from document

"Labor – System Diagnostics (3 hrs)"

€360.00 Breakdown Needed

Why Carsys flagged this

The estimate does not specify which diagnostic tests are included in the 3 hours of labor.

Carsys Note: 3 hours of diagnostic labor is a significant block of time. The estimate does not specify which systems are being tested or what diagnostic path is being taken. It is reasonable to ask for a breakdown of the specific tests this covers.

Line item from document

"Misc. Shop Supplies"

€45.00 Worth Clarifying

Why Carsys flagged this

The charge is generic and not itemized, which makes it harder to understand what is included.

Carsys Note: "Shop supplies" is a common catch-all charge, often used for smaller materials. However, €45 is relatively high without itemization. It is reasonable to ask which specific materials this charge covers for a brake job.

Line item from document

"Brake Pads Set (Front)"

€85.00 Looks Standard

Why Carsys flagged this

This item appears straightforward, but one detail may still affect value and warranty.

Carsys Note: This pricing structure appears standard. One useful follow-up is whether these are OEM (original) brake pads or an aftermarket brand, since that can affect longevity and warranty.

Questions to ask the workshop

Use these plain-language questions to get clarity before you approve the work or make a payment.

You can use these as-is or adapt them when speaking to the workshop.

Prepared from this sample review
  • 1

    "Can you break down the specific diagnostic tests that are included in the 3 hours of labor?"

  • 2

    "Could you clarify what specific materials the €45 'Shop Supplies' charge covers?"

  • 3

    "Are the front brake pads quoted OEM (original) parts or an aftermarket brand?"

  • 4

    "Is this total of €750 an estimate, or is it a guaranteed price assuming no other issues are found?"
