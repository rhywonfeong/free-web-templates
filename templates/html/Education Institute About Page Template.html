The Blackwood Worldwide Institute of Change (TBWWIOC) stands as a beacon of transformative education, rooted in the Kingdom Agenda and dedicated to empowering generations through knowledge, healing, and leadership. Founded and overseen by Dr. Angela Harris, TBWWIOC is an accredited institution recognized by the U.S. Department of Education and international bodies, with authority to extend accreditation to partner institutions worldwide.

Dr. Angela Harris is a robust academic leader, holding a certification PhD that grants her authority to accredit institutions of learning. Philosophy

Our Core Mandate

The Kingdom Agenda is the foundation of every program. It is a Christlike mandate without religious tactics—a call to live with truth, justice, service, and excellence in all things. Every discipline—Business, Agriculture, Health & Wellness, and Ministry—is tied together by one unshakable anchor: the Humanities.

At B.L.A.C.K.W.O.O.D, Humanities is central, because it is through understanding humanity that we:

  • Restore broken identities
  • Rebuild culture with dignity
  • Revive purpose and accountability
  • Lead nations back to their original intent

"This vision is inseparable from the Pan-African Agenda – THE REBIRTH OF THE B.L.A.C.K. NATION. The Institute also operates through powerful acronyms and growth systems that equip Kingdom people to live, lead, and transform with clarity and purpose."

Our Vision

TBWWIOC seeks to restore broken identities, revive hope, and cultivate leadership in every area of human development. Through departments such as Humanities, Mental Health, Business & Economics, Entertainment, Advocacy, Health & Wellness, and Naturopathic Sciences, we provide innovative pathways for students to thrive intellectually, spiritually, and economically. The Institute exists not only as a college but as a movement, grounded in the belief that education is the foundation for lasting transformation.

As TBWWIOC grows, partnerships with educators, visionaries, and cultural leaders will continue to expand. Our focus remains on equipping students with the skills, wisdom, and courage to lead in their communities, to unite Africa and the Diaspora, and to Make Africa Great Again.
