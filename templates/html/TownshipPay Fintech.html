Our story

TownshipPay was founded in 2022 by Lerato Nkosi, a Letlhabile-born product leader who saw first-hand how cash slows down township businesses. After years working in payments analytics, Lerato came home with a simple idea: give every spaza shop, salon and street vendor the same fast, secure tools as big retailers—without the paperwork and hardware.

Our mission is to unlock economic mobility in South Africa’s townships by digitising transactions, building reliable cashflow data, and opening doors to responsible credit. We partner with community organisations and local banks to build trust and deliver value from day one.

Lerato Nkosi
Founder & CEO
We’re building with and for the community—pilots started in Letlhabile and Mabopane, expanding across North West and Gauteng.

Financial insights for SA SMEs

Data-driven context, trends and practical tips for township operators.

Stats
  • SA township businesses process R100bn+ annually — Source: FinMark Trust, 2023
  • SMEs are major contributors to jobs and GDP in SA — Source: IFC, 2018
  • Card and QR acceptance among informal merchants has been rising — Source: FinMark Trust, 2022
Trends
  • Mobile, hardware-free acceptance (Tap-to-Phone) lowers setup barriers.
  • Data-backed lending improves access to responsible working capital.
  • QR and wallet rails expand choice for commuters and youth segments.
Tips
  • Start with Tap-to-Phone to avoid hardware costs.
  • Reconcile daily: separate stock float from personal spend.
  • Use instant settlement to restock faster before weekends.
  • Track your top items and peak hours—adjust staffing and stock.
