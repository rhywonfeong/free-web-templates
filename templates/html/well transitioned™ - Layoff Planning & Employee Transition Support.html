Risk, coordination, readiness

Layoffs carry more than a human cost.

Poorly planned workforce reductions can create operational, reputational, and compliance risk. From notice requirements and severance coordination to employee communications and documentation, the details matter.

well transitioned™ helps your team coordinate the process, prepare leaders, and work alongside HR and legal counsel so the layoff is executed thoughtfully and responsibly.

Notice & Timing Considerations

Help teams prepare for notice requirements, timing considerations, communication sequencing, and key execution dates.

Severance & Separation Coordination

Coordinate the operational details surrounding severance, separation materials, benefits information, and employee handoffs.

Documentation & Communication Readiness

Prepare leaders, documentation, employee communications, and internal teams before the first conversation begins.
