UK-Specific Interview Coverage

We don't do generic interview prep. Our entire curriculum is built around the specific demands of UK medical schools.

MMI Stations

Timed station practice with real UK-style prompts, clear structures, and targeted feedback to sharpen your answers under pressure.

Panel Interviews

UK panel interview coaching focused on personal statement, motivation, and portfolio - plus confident delivery and follow-up handling.

Medical Ethics

UK ethics frameworks applied to realistic scenarios: consent, capacity, confidentiality, safeguarding, and resource allocation - explained simply.

Role-Play & Empathy

Interacting with actors/examiners to demonstrate soft skills. Breaking bad news and dealing with angry patients.

Motivation for Medicine

Structuring your "Why Medicine" and "Why this University" answers to be personal, specific, and non-cliché.

Abstract & NHS Hot Topics

High-yield NHS themes and UK healthcare debates - plus how to discuss research abstracts clearly (methods, results, limitations) in interviews.
