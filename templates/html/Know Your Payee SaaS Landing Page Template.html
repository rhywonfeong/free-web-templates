KYXstart's Know Your Payee (KYP) solution is specifically engineered to address the critical need for payment security and fraud mitigation within the US financial ecosystem, while maintaining global reach. The platform delivers instant, real-time validation of destination bank accounts, directly combating rising US threats like Authorized Push Payment (APP) fraud and Business Email Compromise (BEC).

Leveraging a single, high-performance API, KYX matches the intended recipient's name against the bank account details before funds are released, drastically reducing misdirected payments. By providing a scalable, compliant, and fraud-focused layer of trust, KYX empowers US banks, fintechs, and enterprises to enhance operational efficiency.
