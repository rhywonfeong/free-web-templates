Fundamentals

Swing Basics

Before you chase distance, build a swing that repeats under pressure. Most "swing flaws" are just setup flaws in disguise.

  • The Grip

    Your only connection to the club. Neutral is usually best.

  • Setup & Posture

    Athletic and balanced. Not too stiff, not too slouchy.

  • Alignment

    Train your eyes. If you aim right, you'll swing left.

  • Tempo

    Smooth is fast. Rushed is erratic.

Troubleshooting

Common Mistakes

Identifying your fault is half the battle. Here are the usual suspects ruining your scorecard.

Early Extension

Hips moving toward the ball

#1 Issue

Losing your posture at impact forces your hands to flip, causing blocks and inconsistent contact.

  • Symptom: Standing up at impact
  • Symptom: Thin shots / Topped ball
  • Drill: The Chair Drill
POWER KILLER

Over-the-Top & Slice

Steep downswing path

The classic move that robs you of distance. You swing across the line, sending the ball curving weakly to the right.

  • Symptom: Weak fade / Slice
  • Symptom: Pulls straight left
  • Drill: Split Hand Drill
