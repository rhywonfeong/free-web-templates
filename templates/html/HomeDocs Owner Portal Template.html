Construction Agreement

A high-level overview of the terms of your construction contract.

Prepared by: Skyline Builders Owner: Jamie Lee

This document summarizes the key terms of the construction agreement for your home at Maple Ridge · Lot 27. It is provided here for quick reference and does not replace the signed legal contract.

Use the section list on the right to jump to specific topics like allowances, schedule, and warranties that were agreed upon during the construction phase.

Scope of Work

The builder agrees to construct a single-family residence according to the approved plans and specifications dated Jan 18, 2025. All labor, materials, permits, and inspections required to deliver a move-in ready home are included unless specifically excluded in the contract.

Allowances & Selections

Certain items—such as appliances, lighting fixtures, and flooring—were assigned dollar allowances at the time of contract. Final selections are documented in the Interior Finish Selections document and replace the initial placeholders.

  • Appliance package allowance: $7,500
  • Lighting fixtures allowance: $4,000
  • Flooring allowance: $18,000

Payment Schedule

Payments were tied to construction milestones (foundation, framing, rough-ins, finishes, and completion) rather than fixed dates, ensuring that funds were only requested after work was completed and inspected.

Warranty Overview

The contract references several warranty documents which are all available in this portal: workmanship and materials, systems (mechanical, electrical, plumbing), and structural coverage. For details about what is covered and for how long, open the 10-Year Structural Warranty and related warranty files from the left panel.
