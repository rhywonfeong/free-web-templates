09 / Questions

The player
details.

01 Where can I buy these games?

You can purchase our featured titles on major digital storefronts including Steam, Epic Games Store, and console marketplaces.

02 Are there physical editions available?

Yes. We partner with premium distributors to create limited-run physical editions complete with artbooks, soundtracks, and collectibles.

03 Do these games support modding?

Many of our titles feature built-in mod support and Steam Workshop integration, allowing the community to create and share new content.

04 What are the system requirements?

Most indie games are highly optimized to run on a wide range of hardware. Minimum and recommended specs are listed on each game's store page.

05 How often do games get updated?

Developers release regular patches for bug fixes and balance changes, with major content updates occurring on a seasonal roadmap.
