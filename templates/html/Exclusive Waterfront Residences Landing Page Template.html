Frequently Asked Questions

Clarity on fees, visas, taxation, and investment returns when buying property in Abu Dhabi’s waterfront communities.

What is the ADM Fee Waiver?
ADM refers to the Abu Dhabi Municipality registration fee, typically a percentage of the property price. A 2% ADM Fee Waiver means this cost is covered, significantly reducing your upfront expenses when purchasing a property.
Is property more affordable in Abu Dhabi compared to Dubai?
In many prime and waterfront segments, Abu Dhabi can offer more space and newer communities at price points that are often more competitive than similar areas in Dubai. At the same time, yields and lifestyle amenities remain highly attractive, especially in Saadiyat and Yas Island.
Can I get a UAE visa if I buy property in Abu Dhabi?
Property ownership at certain minimum investment thresholds can qualify you for residency options in the UAE, subject to current regulations. Visa rules are updated periodically, so our advisors can connect you with immigration specialists for the latest requirements.
Is investing in UAE real estate a smart decision?
The UAE offers political stability, strong infrastructure, tax advantages, and a growing expatriate population. Waterfront and prime communities in Abu Dhabi combine lifestyle appeal with rental demand and long-term capital appreciation potential, making them compelling for both yield and growth-oriented investors.
Do I have to pay property tax in Abu Dhabi?
There is currently no recurring property tax in Abu Dhabi, which is a key advantage compared to many global cities. Buyers typically pay one-time registration fees and ongoing community service charges, some of which may be waived for promotional periods in select projects.
Can I resell my off-plan property before it’s completed?
Many developers and authorities allow resale of off-plan properties after a certain percentage of payment has been made or construction milestones are achieved. Policies differ by project, so it’s important to review the specific terms before purchase if early exit flexibility is important to you.
Why is now a good time to buy in Abu Dhabi?
Abu Dhabi is in a growth phase with major cultural, tourism, and infrastructure projects underway, particularly on Saadiyat and Yas Island. Limited prime waterfront supply, attractive payment plans, and promotional incentives like ADM and service charge waivers are creating a compelling entry window.
Are off-plan developers in Abu Dhabi trustworthy?
Abu Dhabi’s leading developers, including those behind Saadiyat and Yas Island communities, are highly regulated and have strong delivery track records. Payment schedules are often milestone-based, and escrow frameworks provide additional safeguards for off-plan buyers.
Why invest in Abu Dhabi’s off-plan market over other global cities?
Abu Dhabi offers a rare mix of waterfront masterplans, favourable tax treatment, modern infrastructure, and political stability. Compared to many established global cities, buyers benefit from newer inventory, competitive entry prices for prime locations, and developer-backed payment plans that reduce capital strain during construction.
