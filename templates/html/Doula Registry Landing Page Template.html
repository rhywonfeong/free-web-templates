Learn about Doula Support

Gentle Doula Matching for Birth, Postpartum & Beyond

Led by Melanie, an experienced doula and educator, this registry helps you find the doula who fits your values, personality, and support needs—so you feel held from pregnancy through the fourth trimester.

Personalized Matching

Share your preferences, values, and support needs. We gently match you with doulas who feel like the right emotional, cultural, and communication fit.

Pregnancy & Birth Support

Receive steady emotional, physical, and informational support throughout pregnancy and birth—always within your medical team’s plan.

Fourth Trimester Care

Gentle, grounded support as you adjust to life with a newborn—emotional check-ins, practical guidance, and space to be honest about how you’re feeling.

Trusted, Vetted Doulas

Every doula in the registry is personally reviewed by Melanie for experience, training, references, and commitment to respectful, inclusive care.

Support for Every Stage of Your Journey

Whether you’re just beginning to explore doula care or preparing for postpartum, we’ll meet you exactly where you are.

A calm, informed path into birth and early parenthood

Melanie has walked alongside hundreds of families through pregnancy, birth, and the fourth trimester. This registry is her way of sharing that steady presence—by connecting you with doulas who offer grounded, respectful support that centers your values and choices.

  • Compassionate, non-medical support that works alongside your clinical care team
  • Education and preparation that help you navigate choices with more clarity and ease
  • Thoughtful matching that considers communication style, lived experience, and family structure
