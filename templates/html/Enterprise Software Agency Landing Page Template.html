Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about our process and collaboration.

How do you handle project pricing?
We offer flexible engagement models. For well-defined scopes, we provide fixed-price contracts. For ongoing development or projects with evolving requirements, we utilize a dedicated team (Time & Materials) approach to ensure maximum flexibility and transparency.
What technology stack do you use?
We primarily focus on modern, scalable technologies. For frontend, we use React, Next.js, and Tailwind CSS. Our backend stacks typically involve Node.js, Python, or Go, supported by PostgreSQL and managed cloud infrastructure on AWS or Google Cloud.
How long does a typical project take?
Project timelines vary based on complexity. A standard MVP typically takes 3 to 4 months from discovery to launch. Enterprise-scale applications or complex platform migrations can span 6 to 12 months, delivered in continuous iterative phases.
Do you provide maintenance after launch?
Yes, we offer comprehensive SLA-backed support and maintenance contracts. This includes proactive monitoring, security updates, bug fixes, and continuous feature development to ensure your software remains performant and secure.
