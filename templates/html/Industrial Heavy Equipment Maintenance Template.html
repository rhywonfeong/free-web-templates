Why IMM

Operational Excellence. Zero Fluff.

Founded in 2012 in Arizona, Independent Mine Maintenance operates on an employee-owned basis. We align our success directly with the reliability and output of your operations.

Our specialized team is capable of executing complete equipment rebuilds, managing large plant outages, delivering responsive field service, and performing precision on-site machining.

Our core promise is simple: improved machine availability, reduced downtime, enhanced production metrics, and lower operating costs.

Core Capabilities

Industrial Maintenance Services

Field Service

Comprehensive troubleshooting and repair for hydraulic systems, air systems, and diesel engine diagnosis.

Equipment Rebuilds

Complete overhauls for hydraulic and electric mining shovels, rotary blasthole drills, sonic drills, and top hammer drills.

Crushing & Conveyor

Maintenance of crushing systems, conveyors, crusher chutes, jaw crushers, cone crushers, and liner package installation.

Welding & Fabrication

On-site welding, stainless steel and aluminum repair, vehicle/equipment frame repair, tank repair, and structure modifications.

Precision Machining

Specialized precision field machining and on-site line boring ranging from 1.5 inch to 12 inch diameters.

Preventative Maintenance

Structured preventative maintenance programs, scheduled inspections, and equipment/structure relocations.

Standards

Quality, Safety, and Compliance Built Into Every Job
