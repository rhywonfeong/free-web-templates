About Smile Oral Care

Affordable, world-class dental care designed for you.

At Smile Oral Care Clinic, we believe everyone deserves a healthy smile. We offer a comprehensive range of dental services including fixed and removable orthodontic appliances (braces) to correct alignment, dental implants to replace missing teeth, and aesthetic veneers.

Our facility in Gusau is equipped with modern technology to ensure your scaling and polishing, teeth whitening, and general procedures are comfortable and effective.
