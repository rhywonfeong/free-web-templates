Elvin Guri

Elvin remembers the exact day he came to Bulgaria for the first time – 26 August 1992. He spent four years at AUBG and in 1996 double majored in Economics and History. Economics – because his father thought it would be a smart choice and History – because that was what he wanted (he even thought of getting a PhD in History).

In January 1998 he moved to the Bulgarian office of EBRD. In November 2001, Elvin together with Ivan Hristanov found JetFinance, "with four people and five computers." For seven years their business has moved up to become one of the largest non-banking consumer credit companies in the region.
