Common questions

What if the camera doesn't read my plate?

Our LPR technology is 99.9% accurate. However, if there is an issue, you can scan the QR code at the gate with your app, or enter your pin manually to gain access.

Is my credit card information secure?

Absolutely. We use bank-level encryption and partner with Stripe for payment processing. We never store your full credit card details on our servers.

How do I get a receipt?

Receipts are automatically emailed to you after every session. You can also view and download your full parking history and invoices directly within the app.
