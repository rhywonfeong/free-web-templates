Investment Climate & Incentives

Where capital meets opportunity. Bangladesh offers a structured, time-bound incentive framework designed to support technology-driven investment.

Hi-Tech Park Benefits

  • 100% income tax exemption for the first 7 years.
  • 70% exemption for years 8 to 10.
  • VAT relief on specified production inputs and services.

IT & ITES Incentives

  • Income from ITES remains tax-exempt via banking channels.
  • Locally produced software subject to a reduced 5% VAT rate.
  • R&D incentives for AI, machine learning, and innovation.

Investor Facilitation

  • BanglaBiz digital gateway unifying OSS platforms.
  • BIDA One Stop Service covering 142 services from 47 agencies.
  • FDI Heatmap targeting structured sector growth.
