Frequently Asked Questions

Does "White Label" mean my customers never see Viirtue?

Correct. Your customers interact with your brand, your domain, and your support team. Viirtue operates strictly in the background.

Who handles taxes?

In the pure White Label model, you are the carrier of record and responsible for taxes. However, we offer integrated tax calculation engines to make this process easier for you.

What if my team gets stuck on a technical issue?

While you handle Tier 1 support, Viirtue provides comprehensive Tier 2 and Tier 3 engineering support to help your team resolve complex issues.

Is there a monthly minimum?

Yes, White Label partners typically have a monthly revenue commitment to access wholesale pricing. Contact us for specific details.
