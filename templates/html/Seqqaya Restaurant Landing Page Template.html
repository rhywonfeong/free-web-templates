Our Story

Authentic &
Modern Vibes

Located on the vibrant Boulevard Mohamed V, Seqqaya brings a fresh perspective to dining in Sefrou. We blend traditional Moroccan hospitality with a modern, airy atmosphere.

From our famous Khlea with aged beef to our wood-fired pizzas and signature Tacos Blanco, every dish is crafted with quality local ingredients and served with a smile.
