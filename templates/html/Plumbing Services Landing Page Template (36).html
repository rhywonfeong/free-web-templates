Trenchless Pipe Lining (CIPP)

We utilize Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) technology to rehabilitate underground sewer lines without excavation. By inserting an epoxy-saturated liner into the existing pipe and curing it, we create a brand new, seamless, joint-free pipe within the old one.

  • Bridges gaps and seals cracks permanently
  • Prevents future root intrusion 100%
  • Improves flow efficiency

The Trenchless Process

How we restore your pipes in a single day without destroying your yard.

01

Video Diagnosis

We run a camera through the line to identify the exact cause of the problem (roots, cracks, offset joints) and measure the length required for the repair.

02

Clean & Prep

Using high-pressure hydro jetters and mechanical descalers, we clean the pipe down to its original wall to ensure the new liner adheres perfectly.

03

Liner Installation

The epoxy-saturated liner is inverted into the pipe using air pressure. It expands to fit the pipe walls tightly and cures in place within a few hours.

04

Final Inspection

We perform a final video inspection to verify the installation quality and flow. We provide you with a copy of the video and your warranty documentation.

Why technology matters

Traditional digging vs. Modern lining

For decades, the only way to fix a broken sewer pipe was to bring in a backhoe, dig a trench through your yard (and sometimes your living room), replace the pipe, and then spend thousands repairing the landscape damage.

Our trenchless pipe lining technology eliminates this destruction. The liner creates a structural pipe within the old host pipe that is often stronger than the original PVC or clay.

We are one of the few specialists in Michigan dedicated specifically to these advanced methods, ensuring you get the most modern solution available with equipment designed for residential access.
