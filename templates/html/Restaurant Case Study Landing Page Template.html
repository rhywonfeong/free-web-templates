The Problem

The "Taste Gap"

In F&B and hospitality, the product is sensory — but your brand is often not. Most businesses don't have a "design problem." They have a perception problem.

Visual Mismatch

The visuals don't match the experience. People judge before they taste.

Template Syndrome

You look like a template brand. Customers assume the food is one too.

Price Ceiling

You don't feel premium enough to charge premium. So you compete on offers.

Inconsistency

Menu says one thing, packaging says another, Instagram says nothing.

I close that gap — by translating what you actually are into an identity people trust.

Systemic Failure Analysis

Why most F&B brands get stuck

Failure Mode 01

The "Logo-Only" Trap

You get a logo. Then you're left alone to figure out menus, packaging, signage, and social. Result: brand falls apart under pressure.

Fix: Build a system, not a symbol.
Failure Mode 02

Solving for "Aesthetic"

Your branding looks good on one post… but doesn't work on a menu, label, or takeaway bag. Style with no structure.

Fix: Design for touchpoints.
Failure Mode 03

"Figure It Out Later"

F&B moves fast. So branding gets piecemealed. You spend more, get less, and nothing feels cohesive.

Fix: Foundations first.
