Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a service take?

A Basic Service usually takes about 1-2 hours. A Standard Service takes roughly 3 hours. We aim for a rapid turnaround to minimize your downtime. You can drop it off in the morning and it's usually ready by lunch!

Do I need to book in advance?

We highly recommend booking a few days in advance to secure your spot. However, if it's an emergency or you need a quick oil change, message us on WhatsApp and we will do our best to fit you in same-day.

Can I contact you on WhatsApp?

Yes! WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach us. Simply click any "Chat on WhatsApp" button on this site, or message us directly at +353 87 962 0558. We reply quickly with transparent quotes and availability.

Do you offer same-day service?

Yes, for Basic Services and quick inspections, we offer same-day service depending on our morning schedule. Contact us early to reserve a slot.

Are there hidden charges?

Never. We pride ourselves on transparent pricing. Before we proceed with any additional repairs discovered during a service, we will contact you, explain the issue, and give you a firm quote. You are always in control.
