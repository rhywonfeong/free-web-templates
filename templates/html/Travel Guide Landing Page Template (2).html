Field Notes / 2021-2023

Written by Someone Who Worked There.

I spent three consecutive summer seasons working in operations within Yellowstone National Park. Living in employee housing gave me a perspective that visitors simply cannot get: I saw the daily traffic patterns, the parking lot saturation times, and the recurring mistakes that cost tourists hours of their vacation.

This guide is a compilation of the operational knowledge used by staff to navigate the park efficiently. It is factual, data-driven, and built to solve the specific logistical bottlenecks of the Greater Yellowstone Ecosystem.
