Frequently asked questions

Can I import my data from other apps?

Currently, we support CSV imports from major platforms like Strong and Hevy. You can find the import tool in your account settings.

Is there a mobile app available?

Yes, GymFlow is available on both iOS and Android. Your data syncs seamlessly across the web platform and mobile apps.

Do I need to pay to create custom exercises?

No, creating custom exercises is available on the free Starter plan. The Pro plan adds unlimited custom multi-week routines.

How do you calculate 1RM?

We use the Brzycki formula by default to estimate your One Rep Max based on your logged sets, though you can change the formula in settings.
