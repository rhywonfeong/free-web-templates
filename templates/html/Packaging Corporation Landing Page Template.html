About Great Packaging

Great Packaging started in 1991 in Tianjin, China with two facilities dedicated to paper and reusable packaging. In less than 30 years, we have become a leading packaging partner, working with the largest brands across different countries and market segments.

To better serve our global clientele, Great Packaging International Division was created in 2016 to guarantee tailored service and impeccable execution on every project. Clients can now work with local market experts at our sales offices in Madrid, Miami, Montreal, and Santiago de Chile.

As our clients’ needs have expanded, so has our production capacity. We now count on a global production network spread across Europe and Asia. Once your product is ready, we can coordinate delivery and storage across the map.
