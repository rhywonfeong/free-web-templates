Prepare Before You Start the Wizard

This guide walks you through each section of the wizard, helping you gather the right information and present it professionally.

Before You Begin

Only use the wizard when you have a specific deal you're ready to present to lenders. Each submission uses one of your available presentations. Take time to gather all your information first.

Documents to Gather First

Property Documents

  • Purchase contract or LOI
  • Property photos
  • Inspection report (if available)
  • Title report

Financial Documents

  • Bank statements (2-3 months)
  • Proof of funds for down payment
  • Tax returns (1-2 years)
  • Credit report (optional but helpful)

Entity Documents

  • LLC operating agreement
  • Articles of organization
  • EIN letter
  • Certificate of good standing

Deal Analysis

  • Comparable sales (3-5 comps)
  • Renovation budget/scope of work
  • ARV calculation support
  • Timeline/project schedule

Exit Strategy

Your plan for completing and exiting the deal

Fix-and-Flip Strategy

  • Focus on quick turnaround timeline
  • Emphasize comparable sales for ARV
  • Show clear profit margin (aim for 15%+)
  • Detail your contractor/team experience

Buy-and-Hold / BRRRR

  • Show rental income projections
  • Include refinance plan details
  • Demonstrate cash flow after refi
  • Highlight property management plan
