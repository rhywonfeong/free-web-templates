Trading is not a gamble.

Today, my mission is simple: to help dedicated individuals escape the rat race, achieve absolute financial freedom, and learn how to navigate the markets independently. I built this mentorship to provide exactly what I wish I had when I started. No fluff, just pure edge.

Can I cancel my subscription at any time?
Absolutely. Subscriptions are billed monthly through Whop, and you can cancel your membership at any time with zero hidden fees. Your access will simply expire at the end of your current billing period.
Can I copy your trades?
Do not copy Shah Zaib's trades. The goal of the livestreams is to help you learn how to read price action. Any trades taken on your account is 100% your responsibility.
Do I need prior trading experience?
No. The Inner Circle Mentorship starts from ground zero (broker setup, basics) and progresses to advanced institutional algorithmic trading models.
What time are the live trading sessions?
Live trading streams occur during the New York session, typically starting around 8:00 AM EST. (Monday - Friday). Educational masterclasses and weekly chart reviews are held on weekends. All sessions are recorded and archived for those who cannot attend live.
What markets do you actively trade?
Our primary strike zones are in Forex (XAU/USD), Indices (NAS100), and major Crypto (BTC, ETH). However, the liquidity and order flow concepts taught apply universally to any highly liquid financial market.
How do the signals work?
Signals are dispatched in real-time via our dedicated private WhatsApp community, Instant WhatsApp notifications for high-probability setups sent directly to your phone, No lag. Includes Entry price, Stop Loss, and multiple Take Profit zones.
Can I join the community without a consultation?
Yes! Follow @shahzaibxtrader on Snapchat, Instagram and X (Twitter), or join our WhatsApp and Telegram channels for free market updates, trading insights, mindset & psychology sessions, and more.

No. All analysis is educational and for informational purposes only. Always do your own research.

No, Shah Zaib does not offer refunds. All sales are final.
