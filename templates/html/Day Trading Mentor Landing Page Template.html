FAQ

Is this financial advice?
No. Everything I share is for education only. I show how I think and trade. You are responsible for your own decisions and risk.
Do you guarantee profits or funded accounts?
No. There are no guarantees in trading. I give you structure, tools and my experience, but your results depend on your work, discipline and decisions.
I am a beginner. Is this for me?
If you are completely new to charts, it may feel intense at first. If you understand basic candlesticks and timeframes and are willing to learn, you can still get a lot out of it. I focus on clarity, not fancy terms.
Do you share live signals?
In the free Discord I share bias, chart ideas and sometimes trades I am interested in. The focus is on understanding why, not on copy paste signals. My paid offers are about systems and education, not pure signal selling.
How is the course different from the free Discord?
The free Discord gives you a feel for how I think about the market. The course is structured, step by step training that builds a complete intraday framework, with deeper explanations, examples and tools.
What exactly do I get when I buy the course or tools?
You get access to the material through Whop. That can include video lessons, PDFs, templates, journals and calculators depending on the product. Each offer clearly lists what is included on its Whop page.
How long do I have access after I purchase?
For one time purchases like the course, ebooks and tools, you get ongoing access as long as the product is active on Whop. For mentorship or memberships, access follows the billing period you choose.
What platforms and tools do I need?
You will need a charting platform like TradingView, a broker or prop firm of your choice, and basic tools like Google Sheets or Notion if you use my templates. Everything else is explained inside the material.
Can I get a refund if it is not for me?
Check the refund policy on each Whop product. In general, digital products are hard to refund once accessed, but I want people to feel treated fairly. If there is a real issue, contact me and we will talk.
How much time do I need to put in for this to work?
There is no shortcut here. You should expect to study the material, journal your trades, and review your performance consistently. Think in months and years of development, not days. The goal is to become a serious trader, not just pass one lucky challenge.
