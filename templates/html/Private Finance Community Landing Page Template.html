Good financial conversations are rare.

Personal Finance Club exists to make them possible.

"Most financial communities in India exist to sell you something—a policy, a fund or a course."

PFC was built on the opposite premise: that meaningful financial discussions should exist independent of products, commissions and sales incentives.

Every member goes through a profiling and verification process conducted by the 1 Finance team.

A meaningful share of applicants are turned away—not to create scarcity, but because the quality of the conversation depends on who’s in the room.

The people who get in are working professionals, founders and individuals making important financial decisions every day.
