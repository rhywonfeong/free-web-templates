Hey, I'm Monique.

I'm Fraser Valley's first Indo-Fijian stand-up comedian—which basically means I can roast my husband in two distinct cultures.

I'm a mom, a Christian, and someone who found her way through the storm and came out laughing. My comedy is clean, honest, and hits close to home—touching on marriage, parenting, faith, recovery, and all the messy, beautiful stuff in between.

I've performed at comedy clubs, churches, fundraisers, and yes, I've delivered mental health talks that are now being watched in over 90 countries.

"Two Characters, One Mic"

My signature comedy style where I play both sides of the conversation—because sometimes the funniest person to talk to is yourself.
