Support

Common Questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Email us at support@optic.app.

How do you verify sellers?
Toggle

We use a combination of ID verification and community reputation. Sellers must link active portfolios or social profiles to be approved for the marketplace.

Are the sample images edited?
Toggle

We flag all images. Users can upload "Straight out of Camera" JPEGs or RAW files. Edited photos are clearly marked so you know what you're looking at.

Does Optic take a commission on sales?
Toggle

Optic Pro members pay 0% fees on sales. Free accounts are charged a standard 5% transaction fee to cover payment processing and platform costs.

Do you support analog/film gear?
Toggle

Yes! We have a dedicated section for vintage glass and film bodies, including a database of lens mount adapters.
