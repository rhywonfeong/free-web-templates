Questions? Answered.

Everything you need to know about setting up your channel.

Do I need a mobile app to use this?

No! SendPush works via Web Push standards (PWA) and integrates with existing wallet passes. You can also integrate our SDK into your existing app if you have one.

Does it work on iOS and Android?

Yes. We support all modern browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox) on both iOS (16.4+) and Android devices natively.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. You can cancel your subscription at any time from your dashboard. You'll keep access until the end of your billing cycle.
