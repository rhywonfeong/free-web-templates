The story of Aura isn't about teeth. It's about people.

For decades, visiting the dentist has been synonymous with stress, clinical smells, and a feeling of being rushed. We knew there had to be a better way. Aura was founded on a simple principle: healthcare should be empathetic.

Our mission is to shift the narrative of dental care from fear to empowerment. We practice "slow dentistry"—taking the time to truly listen to your concerns, explain every step of the process, and ensure you feel completely in control of your health journey.

From our whisper-quiet instruments to our carefully curated, sensory-friendly environment, every detail has been chosen to help you exhale the moment you walk through our doors.

Common questions

Do you accept my insurance?
We are in-network with most major PPO insurance plans. As a courtesy, our team will verify your benefits before your appointment and file claims on your behalf. We also offer flexible financing options for those without insurance.
I have severe dental anxiety. Can you help?
Absolutely. Our clinic was designed specifically with anxious patients in mind. We offer a 'comfort menu', longer appointment times so you never feel rushed, and various sedation options to ensure you feel completely relaxed during your visit.
What should I expect at my first visit?
Your first visit is about getting to know you. We'll have a conversation about your dental history and goals, take comprehensive digital x-rays, perform a gentle exam, and usually complete a routine cleaning. No pressure, just a thorough assessment.
Do you see children?
Yes! We love treating families. We recommend bringing your child in for their first "happy visit" around their first birthday. We focus on making it a fun, educational, and positive experience to build good habits early on.
What if I have an emergency after hours?
We reserve time in our daily schedule for emergency appointments. If you experience severe pain, trauma, or a broken tooth after hours, our phone line directs you to an on-call doctor who will assess your situation and arrange prompt care.
