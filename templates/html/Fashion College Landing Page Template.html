NIFT-TEA College of Knitwear Fashion, Tirupur is a specialized fashion institution focussed on knitwear design, apparel production and textile industry education. The college offers structured undergraduate programs supported by experienced faculty, modern laboratories and strong industry collaboration. Situated within India's leading knitwear manufacturing cluster, students gain practical exposure to real-time garment production, export operations and professional career pathways in the apparel and textile sector.
