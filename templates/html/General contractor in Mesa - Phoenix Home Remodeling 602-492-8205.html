The Feasibility, Planning & Design Package

This phase is required for all PHR projects. It is not an optional add-on and it is not a free consultation. It is the foundation of the Design-Build model and the primary mechanism that prevents common contractor nightmares. We do not sell construction first; we sell planning. Homeowners do not commit to construction until the design is complete, selections are finalized, scope is clearly defined, feasibility is confirmed, and a final price is presented.

The process is broken down into three critical stages:

Stage 1: Feasibility

We determine what is structurally, financially, and functionally realistic before design advances. This involves layout evaluation, plumbing and electrical assessment, lifestyle alignment, and risk identification. Feasibility protects homeowners from falling in love with ideas that create major cost shifts later.

Stage 2: Detailed Planning

We define the entire project clearly before construction. This includes the complete scope of work, trade coordination planning, construction sequencing, disruption planning, and homeowner portal setup. Planning prevents price creep and timeline chaos.

Stage 3: Design & Selections

We align aesthetics, function, and homeowner expectations. Homeowners collaborate with our in-house designer to finalize material and finish selections, layout refinements, lighting, and storage planning. You will receive 3D renderings to approve before a hammer swings.
