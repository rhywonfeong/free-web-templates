Due Diligence Checklist

Zoning & Entitlements

  • As-of-right lots/units, density, setbacks
  • ANR vs full subdivision + approvals
  • Prior plans/studies indicate “real” project

Utilities & Infrastructure

  • Sewer/water vs wells/septics feasibility
  • Power/gas at boundary? Extension costs
  • Road standards + off-site improvements

Physical Site Constraints

  • Wetlands/flood zones reduce buildable area
  • Ledge/rock risk → test pits/surveys
  • Easements/ROW: confirm each lot buildable

Permitting & Community

  • Hearing timeline, opposition, appeal risk
  • Impact fees/inclusionary requirements
  • Approvals drive critical path

Costs & Contingency

  • Horizontal: roads, drainage, grading, utilities
  • Soft/carry: engineering, legal, taxes, interest
  • Contingency: 15–20% for unknowns
  • Test bid assumptions against local subs
