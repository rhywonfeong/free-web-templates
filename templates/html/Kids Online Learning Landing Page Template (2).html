Got Questions?

Here are some common questions parents ask us.

What equipment does my child need?
Just a laptop, tablet, or desktop computer with a reliable internet connection, a webcam, and a microphone. Most standard devices work perfectly fine.
How long are the classes?
Our classes are designed to match children's attention spans. They typically last between 40 to 45 minutes, packed with interactive activities to keep them engaged without screen fatigue.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes, absolutely. We believe in our service, so there are no long-term lock-in contracts. You can cancel or pause your subscription at any time from your account settings.
Are the teachers qualified?
All our educators go through a rigorous vetting process. They are certified professionals with specific experience in early childhood or elementary education, and undergo thorough background checks.
