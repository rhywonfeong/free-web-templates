Who We Are

Cultivating wellness through community
in the heart of Bashundhara.

Yoga Freehand Exercise and Meditation (YFM) is a complimentary health awareness club. We believe that wellness should be accessible, communal, and holistic. Located in Bashundhara R/A, Dhaka, we offer a sanctuary away from the noise.

Our Mission

To provide an inclusive space where residents can engage in daily exercises under expert guidance.

Our Vision

A healthy, active community that values both physical vitality and mental peace.

Our programs are designed for everyone—from beginners taking their first breath on the mat to experienced practitioners looking to deepen their flow. We combine traditional yoga practices with modern freehand exercises to create a balanced routine.
