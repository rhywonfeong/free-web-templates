Frequently Asked Questions

What exactly is the supervision model?
Every therapist at Moji Health works with a licensed clinical supervisor. They meet regularly (weekly or bi-weekly) to review cases, discuss treatment strategies, and ensure you’re getting evidence-based, high-quality care. It’s like having two experts working together on your progress instead of just one.
Does supervision cost extra?
Absolutely not! Supervision is built into our model at no additional cost to you. You pay your standard insurance copay and get two experts for the price of one.
Can I see who the supervisor is before I book?
Yes! Transparency is key. When you browse therapist profiles, you’ll see their supervisor’s credentials, experience, and specialties right there. You know exactly who’s on your care team from day one.
Why is supervision better than traditional therapy?
Two licensed professionals collaborating means better treatment plans, faster breakthroughs, quality assurance, and multiple perspectives on your challenges. If your therapist gets stuck, they have expert backup. You get accountability and oversight that solo therapists can’t provide.
Is my therapist less experienced because they have a supervisor?
Not at all! Supervision is a professional best practice, not a sign of inexperience. Many seasoned therapists continue working with supervisors throughout their careers because collaboration makes better outcomes. It’s about excellence, not inexperience.
Will I ever meet the supervisor?
Supervisors work behind the scenes reviewing cases and consulting with your therapist. Your sessions are with your dedicated therapist, but you have peace of mind knowing a senior clinician is also invested in your progress.
What if I don’t feel the supervision is helping?
You can always switch to a different therapist-supervisor team. We believe the right fit matters, and you should feel confident in everyone working on your care.
