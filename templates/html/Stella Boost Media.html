FAQs — Everything you need to know.

How fast can we expect results?
Most clients can expect to see their first replies and booked appointments within 3 to 5 weeks of launch, after the infrastructure is built, warmed up, and the first campaign goes live. Month two is typically when volume and consistency builds significantly, and by month three the system is fully optimized and generating predictable appointment flow.
Do you work with any industry?
We work exclusively with B2B businesses selling high ticket services worth $10,000 or more, including commercial contractors, medical practices, specialty service providers, and similar businesses where the buyer is a professional decision maker. If your business fits that profile we will let you know on the strategy call whether we can take you on.
What is the guarantee exactly?
Our model is built around performance. You pay a flat monthly fee to cover the hard costs of running the system, and beyond that you only pay per appointment that actually shows up to the call. No shows cost you nothing. Unqualified leads never hit your calendar. The guarantee is built into the pricing structure itself, we only make meaningful money when you get results.
How much of my time is required?
Almost none. During onboarding we need about 30 to 60 minutes of your time to understand your ideal client, your target geography, and your offer. After that the entire system runs without you, we build it, manage it, handle every reply, qualify every lead, and book every call. Your only job is showing up to the appointments we put on your calendar.
