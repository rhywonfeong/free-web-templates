About Brian Icenhower

Brian Icenhower began his career as a real estate attorney - trained at California Western School of Law and the University of Southern California's Marshall School of Business - before moving into brokerage operations in the early 1990s.

Over the following three decades, he personally built and ran production-focused real estate offices across California and the Midwest, and opened escrow companies, title companies, a real estate licensing school, a property management division, a commercial operation, and a mortgage company.

He founded Icenhower Coaching & Training after building every system he now teaches - not to teach what he had studied, but to codify what he had operated. He is a best-selling author and a nationally recognised keynote speaker for the largest real estate organisations in the United States. ICC operates today as one of the largest real estate coaching organisations in North America.

Frequently Asked Questions

I've heard of coaching but I've never heard of ICC. Why is that?

ICC is known primarily through agent and broker referral networks, TheRealEstateTrainer.com, and the Real Estate Agent Round Table community — not through advertising. The agents who benefit most from ICC tend to find it through a peer recommendation or by searching for a specific system problem they are trying to solve. The free diagnostic call is the fastest way to evaluate whether the programme is right for you without any prior familiarity.

Does ICC take every agent who applies, or is there a selection process?

ICC coaches agents, team leaders, and broker-owners at every level of production — from new licensees to teams doing $100 million or more annually. The diagnostic call determines which programme is the right fit for your specific situation and production stage, so the coaching plan is built around where you actually are, not a generic starting point.

What makes ICC's approach structurally different from motivational or accountability coaching?

ICC coaching installs documented systems — activity trackers, scoreboards, training protocols, recruiting frameworks — rather than delivering general advice or motivational structure. Every ICC coach has personally sold 1,000 or more units before coaching anyone, so the systems they install came from their own operating experience, not from theory. The difference is that you leave each call with something built into your business, not just a plan to think about.

What markets and geographies does ICC serve?

ICC coaches agents, teams, and brokerages across the United States and Canada. The coaching programmes and systems are designed for North American real estate markets broadly — Brian Icenhower operated offices in California and the Midwest, and ICC's client base spans both coasts, the Sun Belt, and major metro and regional markets across the country.

What actually happens on the free consultation call?

The call is a 30-minute diagnostic — your coach reviews your current production numbers, your lead sources, and how your time is currently allocated, then identifies the highest-leverage gap in your operation. There is no pitch and no obligation. You leave with a clear picture of what your business needs next, whether or not you decide to continue with coaching.
