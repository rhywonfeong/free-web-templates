The Shift

Flipping the model.

Safari booking has traditionally been backwards. Most brands start with the destination, the luxury lodge, or the rigid itinerary.

Join Africa starts with the guide. Because the guide shapes what travellers see, how they experience it, and what they ultimately take away.

Join Africa helps travellers choose the right verified guide before they book, matching them by passions, language and travel style, then connecting them to a licensed operator to plan the trip.

Key Narrative

Two people. Same safari. Two stories. Dramatise how radically different a safari is depending on the guide.
Birders don't need a driver. They need a specialist. Speak directly to specific passions.
The biggest mistake people make? Booking the trip before the guide.
