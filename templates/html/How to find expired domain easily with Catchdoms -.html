What are expired domains ?

Expired domains are web addresses that previous owners failed to renew. They often come with established backlink profiles, existing traffic, and years of SEO authority already built in.

Why are they difficult to find?

The market is saturated with spam. Finding a clean, high-authority domain requires sifting through millions of dropped names daily, checking for toxic backlinks, and verifying archive history. It is manually impossible.
