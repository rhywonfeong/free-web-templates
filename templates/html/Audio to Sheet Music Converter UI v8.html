Frequently asked question

Everything you need to know to get started with confidence.

Can I use Ivory on mobile devices or tablets?

Yes, Ivory is available on mobile devices and tablets for your convenience. However, for the best user experience and full functionality, we recommend using Ivory on a tablet or a laptop.

What is the accuracy of the transcription? Is it for piano only

We offer the best possible transcription for piano solo recording only. However, the transcription may contain errors, and you have the option to correct any existing mistakes afterward.

Do you offer a free version, and what features does it include?

Ivory offers a free version to its users; however, this version has limitations. You can only transcribe the beginning of pieces for free, and only from YouTube. Additionally, playback of existing pieces on the platform is limited to the beginning of the tracks.

What subscription plans are available, and what’s included in the paid version?

There are two subscription options: monthly and yearly. With an Ivory subscription, you can transcribe as many tracks as you want and access all the features of the app without any limitations.

How and in what format can I export my transcription?

Currently, you can export your recorded tracks in MIDI (notes), PDF and MusicXML (score) format.

Where to ask my questions?

You can contact the Ivory team via email, but the most popular support channel today is Discord. Feel free to join our server for a quick and tailored response to your needs.
