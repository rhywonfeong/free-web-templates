01 // The Friction

Enablement is currently guessing.

Traditional training relies on sporadic workshops and subjective manager feedback. There is no control group, no isolated variables, and no way to stress-test a rep’s capability before they burn a lead.

The ramp time plateau.

Without a safe environment to practice specific scenarios, reps practice on your prospects. This extends ramp time and creates a permanent ceiling on quota attainment.

We moved sales readiness from knowledge verification to behavioral stress-testing.

Knowing the pitch deck is not the same as navigating a hostile procurement negotiation. SellSim measures the latter.

02 // The Mechanism
01

Environment Configuration

We ingest your call recordings, objection handling playbooks, and buyer personas. The engine constructs a digital twin of your typical sales interaction, calibrated to your specific difficulty curves.

02

Active Simulation

Reps engage in voice-based, real-time scenarios. The simulation reacts dynamically to tone, pacing, and argument structure. It is not a decision tree; it is an adaptive adversary.

03

Diagnostic Output

Post-simulation analysis provides object-level data on performance gaps. Managers receive a heatmap of competency rather than a completion certificate.

The Performance Layer

SellSim acts as the middleware between your LMS and your CRM. It validates that the training in the LMS has actually resulted in the behavioral capability required to update the CRM.

This creates a closed-loop system where enablement strategy is dictated by simulation failure rates, not anecdotal requests.

03 // Differentiation

Scalability of Experience

A manager can only roleplay for 3 hours a week. SellSim can run 3,000 concurrent simulations instantly. We decouple practice volume from management bandwidth.

Objectivity of Feedback

Human feedback is biased by recency and relationship. The engine evaluates strictly on the syntax of success defined by your top performers.
