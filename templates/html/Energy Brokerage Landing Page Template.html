Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about switching energy providers.

Is there a fee to use your service?

No, our service is 100% free for consumers. We are paid a small commission by the provider you choose, and this does not increase your rate.

Will my power be interrupted during the switch?

Absolutely not. Your local utility company will continue to deliver your power, maintain the lines, and respond to emergencies exactly as they do now.

How long does it take to switch?

The switch typically takes 1 to 2 billing cycles to take effect. You will be notified by your new provider once the transition is complete.

What happens when my contract expires?

We will proactively reach out to you before your contract expires to review current market rates and help you renew or switch to a better plan to avoid costly variable rates.
