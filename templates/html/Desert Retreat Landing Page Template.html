The noise stops here.
We handle the rest.

Modern life demands constant attention. Here, we ask for none of it. We guide you through the valleys of Sinai. We prepare the meals. We set the pace. You don't need to look at a map. You just need to put one foot in front of the other.

01. The Rhythm

Walking, not rushing.

We wake with the sun. We walk while the air is fresh. The pace is slow, adapted to the group. This is not an endurance test; it is a moving meditation. The landscape changes every hour—from granite cathedrals to soft dunes.

  • 3-4 hours of gentle walking per day
  • Frequent pauses for tea and silence
02. The Care

Everything is prepared.

You arrive at camp, and the fire is already lit. Fresh meals are cooked over open flames using local ingredients. Bedouin tea is always brewing. We create a comfortable space in the middle of nowhere, so you can focus on resting.

  • Freshly cooked vegetarian/traditional meals
  • Unlimited water, herbal tea, and coffee
03. The Night

A billion stars.

As the sun sets, the desert becomes silent. We gather around the fire for simple conversation or quiet contemplation. Sleep happens under the open sky (or in tents if you prefer), wrapped in heavy blankets, watching the Milky Way.

  • Zero light pollution
  • Mattresses and heavy blankets provided

Safety through presence.

The desert is vast, but you are never alone. We know every rock and every valley. Your safety comes from our preparation and our deep knowledge of the terrain.

Constant Guidance

We are with you from the moment you step out of the car until you return. We manage the route, the weather, and the supplies.

Small Groups

We keep groups small (max 8 people) to ensure everyone receives attention and the atmosphere remains calm and intimate.

Logistics Handled

We have 4x4 support nearby for supplies and emergencies. We carry the heavy loads; you only carry your day pack.

Note: We do not use aggressive guarantees. We rely on experience, local knowledge, and common sense to manage risk effectively.

Common Questions

Do I need hiking experience?
No. The walking is gentle and adapted to the group's ability. If you can walk for a few hours in a city, you can do this. We take plenty of breaks.
Is it suitable for solo travelers?
Absolutely. Many of our guests come alone to disconnect. The group environment is safe, welcoming, and conducive to both solitude and community.
What about phone signal?
Signal is intermittent. In the deep desert, there is none. We encourage you to switch off, but our guides have communication channels for emergencies.
What is the cancellation policy?
We are flexible. Cancel up to 48 hours before the trip for a full refund. We understand plans change.
