What Makes Our Microneedling Regenerative?

Regenerative Microneedling stimulates controlled dermal micro-injury, initiating fibroblast proliferation and increased collagen synthesis. When combined with targeted actives such as peptides and PDRN, this enhances epidermal regeneration and skin density.

PDRN

(Polydeoxyribonucleotide)

Derived from salmon DNA and celebrated in Korean skincare, PDRN is prized for its ability to repair damaged cells, calm inflammation, and hydrate deeply, supporting your skin's natural regeneration at a cellular level.

Exosomes

Tiny messengers rich in growth factors and peptides that supercharge your skin’s healing response. They enhance collagen production, reduce inflammation, and improve recovery, leaving skin smoother, stronger, and more balanced.

Retinoids

A gold-standard in dermatology, retinoids refine skin texture, brighten tone, and help stimulate long-term collagen renewal. Infused through microneedling channels, they work more effectively for visibly radiant results.

How soon will I see improvements?

Collagen Induction Therapy stimulates the body’s own regenerative collagen production. It takes time for this process to become visible and patients should expect to see significant improvements within three to six weeks after each treatment. The full effect will be achieved within a few months.

What should I expect during the treatment?

This treatment, performed under topical anesthesia, is administered via very fine needles creating tiny pin-pricks in the skin. Post-procedure, the skin appears flushed and may swell for a few days, similar to a moderate sunburn. Healing stimulates new skin cells, collagen and elastin production.

How many sessions are recommended?

To achieve maximum benefits from Collagen Induction Therapy, we recommend a course of three to six treatments, spaced out in a minimum of 1 to 2 week intervals. Other options include one treatment per month or 6 treatments every 6 months.

How to prepare for this treatment?

For optimal results, a daily pre-treatment protocol is recommended. Studies have shown that using topical Environ Vitamin A and C for a minimum of 8 weeks prior to a medical needling treatment, can enhance growth and thickness of the epidermal layers.
