Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can we expect results?

The first 3 weeks are setup. We buy domains, warm inboxes, and build your list. Emails start sending in week 4 and the first calls follow shortly after. The 90 day guarantee clock starts when sending starts.

What is the guarantee exactly?

15 completed calls with qualified prospects in 90 days. Qualified means a decision maker who matches the target profile we agree in writing before launch. If we fall short, you get your money back.

Do you work with any industry?

We work with B2B businesses selling to other businesses in the US. On the first call we map your market together. If I don't think I can deliver you qualified sales meetings I will let you know on the free call.

How much of my time is required?

One 30 minute call to map your market and one round of approving the email copy. After that you only get involved when a prospect books in.

Will this hurt my domain or land my emails in spam?

No. Your company domain never sends a cold email. We build separate sending domains, warm them for 3 weeks, and keep volume low per inbox so deliverability stays healthy.
