05 — Peace of Mind

Our Health Guarantee

We are deeply committed to raising healthy, well-socialized puppies. Our comprehensive health guarantee is designed to give you complete peace of mind as you welcome your new Maltipoo family member.

30days

30-Day Physical Exam

Every puppy should be examined by a licensed veterinarian within 30 days. If a previously undetected congenital physical condition is diagnosed according to the terms of the guarantee, we will work with the family to provide the appropriate resolution.

7days

7-Day Viral Illness

If a licensed veterinarian diagnoses a qualifying contagious viral illness within 7 days of pickup, the guarantee applies according to our health policy, ensuring your puppy's early well-being.

2years

2-Year Genetic Health

Every puppy is covered for qualifying hereditary or congenital genetic conditions for up to two years, providing additional peace of mind for new families investing in their companion.
