What is Solar Battery Storage?

Solar battery storage is an advanced energy system that captures and stores the excess electricity generated by your solar panels during the day, allowing you to use that free, clean energy at night or during grid blackouts.

When we talk about battery storage for solar, we're talking about true energy independence. Instead of sending your excess daytime solar power back to the grid for a tiny feed-in tariff — only to buy it back at premium rates at night — a home solar battery storage system lets you keep the power you produce. Whether you need a DC-coupled system for a new solar installation or an AC-coupled solar panels battery like the Tesla Powerwall to retrofit your existing panels, Queenshine Electricals designs systems that perfectly match your household's energy demands.

How It Works: The Daily Cycle

Your system is fully automated — intelligent software always uses the most cost-effective power source without you lifting a finger.

1

Morning — Generate

Your solar panels power your morning appliances — coffee makers, toasters and lights.

2

Midday — Store

Surplus energy is redirected to charge your battery to 100% instead of being exported to the grid.

3

Evening — Discharge

As the sun sets and demand spikes, your system instantly switches to stored battery power.

4

VPP — Earn

Join a Virtual Power Plant and earn premium feed-in tariffs when your retailer draws from your battery to stabilise the grid.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add a battery to my existing solar panels?

Yes, absolutely. If your inverter is hybrid-ready, we plug in a compatible DC-coupled battery directly. For older inverters, we use an AC-coupled solution like the Tesla Powerwall with its own built-in inverter — no need to replace your entire system.

How long does home solar battery storage last?

Modern batteries last 10–15 years. Our Tier-1 brands use Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) or NMC chemistry, engineered for thousands of deep cycles. Tesla, Sigenergy and Duracell all come with a 10-year warranty guaranteeing 60–70% capacity retention.

Will a battery power my house during a blackout?

Yes. Your system uses an Emergency Power Supply to isolate your home from the grid. We can configure Essential Circuit Backup for fridge, lights and Wi-Fi, or Whole Home Backup for larger systems — keeping heavy appliances like AC running even in a full neighbourhood blackout.

How do I claim a solar battery rebate?

Rebates are claimed at point of sale through accredited installers. As CEC Accredited, we automatically calculate and deduct your STC discount from your invoice. We also monitor Queensland's Battery Booster grants and advise you on every rebate your property qualifies for.
