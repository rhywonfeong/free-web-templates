Role Overview

Precision work that matters

Technicians at QRS support institutions that rely on equipment to conduct research, process clinical samples, and maintain controlled environments.

The work requires technical skill, attention to detail, and professional communication. If you have HVAC fundamentals, we will teach you the specialized side of the work.

See requirements

Core Responsibilities

  • Equipment Maintenance

    Repair and maintain laboratory and medical equipment across universities, hospitals, and research facilities.

  • System Diagnostics

    Diagnose refrigeration and mechanical systems using foundational HVAC knowledge to identify root causes.

  • Regional Travel

    Travel within the Pittsburgh region to client locations. Home-based starts reduce commute stress.

  • Client Communication

    Communicate clearly with lab staff regarding findings, timelines, and completed work.

  • Autonomous Work

    Work independently with support from an experienced technical team when needed.

What we look for

We seek individuals who want to build a long-term, technically skilled career in a growing specialty area. Your foundation in HVAC is the starting point.

HVAC/Refrigeration training or experience
EPA Universal Certification (Required)

Ability to pass background & drug screening
