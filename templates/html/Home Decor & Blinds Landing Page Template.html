Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer trade pricing?
Yes. We are independently owned with no franchise fees or storefront overhead. That means we can offer trade pricing on Hunter Douglas, Norman, and Alta products that franchise competitors cannot match. Contact us for a trade pricing sheet.
What is your typical turnaround time?
2 to 3 weeks from final measure to installation for most projects. Rush orders can sometimes be accommodated depending on product availability.
Do you handle commercial properties?
Yes. We supply and install window treatments for apartments, HOA communities, office buildings, medical offices, and new construction projects across El Paso County.
What brands do you carry?
Hunter Douglas, Alta Window Fashions, Norman Window Fashions, Timber Blind Metro Shade, and Lafayette Interior Fashions. Full product range including shutters, blinds, shades, and motorized options.
Do your treatments help with energy efficiency?
Yes. Honeycomb and cellular shades are specifically designed for insulation and are ideal for Colorado's dry climate and temperature swings. Motorized shades can also be timed to block heat in summer and let in warmth in winter.
What warranty do you offer?
All Hunter Douglas products come with a lifetime warranty. We handle all warranty claims and repairs in-house.
