Frequently Asked Questions

How can one company do both IT and Adventure Tourism?
We operate as a collective of specialized experts. Our Tourism division is led by field experts in adventure and wellness, while our IT division is run by seasoned software engineers. We offer "End-to-End" solutions—building both the resort and the booking system that runs it.
What is a "Detailed Project Report" (DPR)?
A DPR is the blueprint of your entire project. It includes financial feasibility, technical design, environmental impact assessments, and operational plans. It is essential for securing bank loans, attracting investors, and obtaining government clearances.
Do you provide staff for wellness centers?
Yes. Under our Wellness Tourism vertical, we offer "Manpower Supply." We recruit and train qualified therapists, naturopaths, and yoga instructors to ensure your service quality matches your infrastructure.
