Frequently asked questions

Can I try Verdant before paying?

Yes. Every paid plan starts with a 14-day free trial and no credit card is required.

Can I change plans later?

You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime. Changes apply at the start of your next billing cycle.

What counts as a data source?

A data source is any connected warehouse, SaaS tool, database, sheet, or API endpoint that Verdant syncs from.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes. Annual plans include preferred pricing. Enterprise contracts can be tailored to procurement and security needs.
