Is something wrong?

Don't risk it. If you see these signs, give us a call immediately.

Power Outage

Partial or full power loss when the neighbors still have lights means an internal electrical failure.

Burning Smell

Acrid, fishy, or burning plastic smells near outlets or panels indicate overheating wires.

Sparks or Heat

Outlets that feel hot to the touch or spark when you plug something in are a serious fire hazard.
