Frequently asked

How does species identification work?

A compact model runs on-device, classifying frames and tracking movement. Images never leave your feeder unless you enable cloud backup.

Is it weatherproof?

Yes. IP65 enclosure, heated lens, and drainage channels keep optics clear in rain and snow.

Will it work without internet?

Core features run offline. Clips and analytics sync when connectivity returns.

Can I share clips?

Yes—create a circle and share automatically for selected species or time windows.
