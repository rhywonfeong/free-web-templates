Hardscaping questions from Columbus homeowners, answered.

If you do not see your question here, include it in your estimate request and we will cover it on our first call.

How much does hardscaping cost in Columbus? + −

Costs vary based on size, access to the yard, and materials. Simple walkway or small patio projects may start in the low thousands, while full outdoor living spaces with walls and fire features are higher. After a short site visit, we provide a detailed written estimate so you know exactly what to expect.

How long will my project take from start to finish? + −

Most patios and walkways are completed within 3–7 working days once we are on site. Larger outdoor living areas with retaining walls, steps, and lighting can take 2–3 weeks. We give you a start window and estimated completion date before we begin and keep you updated if weather shifts the schedule.

Will my new hardscape require much maintenance? + −

Properly installed hardscapes are low‑maintenance. We recommend periodic sweeping, occasional power washing, and joint sand refreshes as needed. For many projects, we also offer sealing and maintenance services and can recommend a simple schedule based on your materials and shade.

Are you licensed and insured to work on my property? + −

Yes. Columbus Hardscape Pros is fully licensed and carries both liability and workers’ compensation insurance. We can provide proof of insurance and any required permits for your project, especially for structures, gas lines, or electrical work that involve inspections.

Which parts of Greater Columbus do you serve? + −

We serve most of Central Ohio, including Columbus neighborhoods such as Clintonville, German Village, Grandview, and Hilliard, plus suburbs like Dublin, Powell, Westerville, Grove City, and Pickerington. If you are not sure whether you are in our service area, send your address with your estimate request and we will confirm quickly.
