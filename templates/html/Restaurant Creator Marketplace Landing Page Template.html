Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Test Feature work?
You pay the $299 platform fee and provide 3 dates. We match you with a vetted local creator. They visit, enjoy a meal (comped by you), and deliver 1 Reel + Stories within 5 days of the visit.
Can I choose the creator?
To keep the process fast and affordable, our admin team selects the best match based on your restaurant's style and the creator's niche. For Paid Campaigns, you get more input on selection.
What cities are you in?
We are currently active in Montreal, Toronto, Hamilton, and Winnipeg. We are expanding to Vancouver soon.
