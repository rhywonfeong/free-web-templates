The Problem & Design

Discreet daily dignity

The disabled face immense challenges in daily navigation and fluid communication. Traditional assistive devices are often bulky, drawing unwanted attention and causing stigma.

OptiCap’s design philosophy prioritizes a 'lowkey' aesthetic. By utilizing a structured baseball cap chassis, the technology fades into the background, empowering the user without defining them.

Stigma-Free

Financial Viability

High impact, low overhead

By utilizing existing inventory and smart power management, prototype unit costs are kept exceptionally low, proving extreme financial soundness.

Zero Waste

Utilizing high-quantity items from existing inventory (Sensors, Buzzers, Batteries), making the prototype cost well under ₹1000.

Solar Efficiency

Adding a 5V/2W solar panel extends battery life by 30-40% during outdoor use, eliminating the need for expensive, heavy batteries.

Modular Design

With 50+ sensors in stock, broken components can be hot-swapped in seconds without replacing the entire cap architecture.
