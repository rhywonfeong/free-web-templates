How does Stable Sync work with my existing vet or farrier?

Invite them by email or share a secure link. Providers can review records, add notes, and confirm appointments from any device—no install required.

Can I use it for multiple barns or owners?

Yes. Create separate workspaces for each barn or ownership group while keeping a single profile for each horse. Permissions ensure the right people see the right data.

Is my horse’s data secure?

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. You control access at the horse, barn, and provider level and can revoke sharing links at any time.
