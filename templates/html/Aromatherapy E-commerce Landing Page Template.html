Your Questions, Answered

Is the oil safe for pets?

We recommend diffusing in well-ventilated spaces and keeping pets out of the room while the diffuser is running. Some essential oils can be irritating to cats and dogs in high concentrations. Always consult your vet if unsure.

How long does the diffuser run for?

The diffuser runs for up to 6 hours on continuous mode or can be set to intermittent misting. It features an automatic shut-off when the water runs low for complete peace of mind.

Is the packaging recyclable?

Yes! Our packaging is designed with sustainability in mind. The outer box is fully recyclable, and our amber glass bottles can be reused or recycled.

How long will one 10ml bottle last?

When used nightly (6-8 drops per session), one 10ml bottle lasts approximately 30-40 uses—around a month of restful sleep.
