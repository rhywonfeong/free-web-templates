The Logic of Length

Traditional skirts cut the leg line at the wrong point. We engineered a specific cut for shorter inseams to maximize visual elongation.

The Iliac Crest Fit

Instead of choking the smallest part of your waist, our skirt sits lower, right under the widest part (the iliac crest). This extends the visual torso line while eliminating the "muffin top" effect, creating a straighter, longer vertical axis.

Calculated Micro Hem

Every inch of fabric hides leg length. We removed the excess. By exposing the upper thigh, we force the eye to travel a longer distance from shoe to hem. Essential for stumpy proportions looking for redemption.

The 'Le Compte' Cut

Confidence is key. The back hem is curved slightly upward to frame the glutes rather than flatten them. It provides a cheeky hint of the under-butt, lifting the visual center of gravity and making legs appear to start higher.

Invisible Utility

Sculpting Fabric

Utility meets Vanity.

Most micro skirts are flimsy pieces of cloth. Ours is a structured garment. It doesn't ride up, it doesn't twist, and it actually holds your essentials.

The Stealth Pocket

Sewn into the lining on the right hip. Completely invisible from the outside, but deep enough for an iPhone. Go bag-free.

Premium Ponte Roma

Double-knit fabric with 4-way stretch. It smooths the hips and thighs while maintaining a sharp architectural shape. No flimsy transparency here.

Low-Rise Engineering

Sits securely on the hips. The waistband is reinforced to prevent rolling, ensuring the skirt stays exactly where it flatters you most.
