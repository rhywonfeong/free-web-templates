I've been told to eat whole grains, but my blood sugar spikes after bread. Why?

This is a common myth. "Whole grain" bread often has a Glycemic Index similar to white sugar due to how finely the flour is milled.

The Agewell Approach:

  • Switch to sprouted grains or almond flour alternatives.
  • Pair carbohydrates with healthy fats (avocado, nuts) to blunt the spike.
  • Go for a 10-minute walk immediately after eating.
