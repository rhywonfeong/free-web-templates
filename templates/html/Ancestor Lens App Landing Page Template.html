Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a DNA test?
No. Ancestor Lens does not analyze biological DNA. We use advanced computer vision and AI to analyze visual patterns in your facial features and match them with historical and ethnic datasets.
Do I need special equipment?
No. You just need your smartphone camera or an existing photo. The app guides you through taking the perfect picture for analysis.
Is my data secure?
Yes. Your privacy is our priority. Photos are processed securely for the purpose of the analysis and are not sold to third parties.
Who is this app for?
Anyone curious about ancestry, heritage, and identity who wants a visual, fun, and immediate way to explore their roots without waiting for lab results.
