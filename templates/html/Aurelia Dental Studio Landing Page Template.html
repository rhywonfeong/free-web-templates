Common Questions

Everything you're wondering, answered.

Will my treatment hurt?

Comfort is our specialty. Between gentle techniques, modern anesthetics, and sedation options, most patients tell us the visit was far easier than they expected — many feel nothing at all.

How much will it cost?

You'll receive a clear, itemized quote before anything begins — no surprises, ever. We also offer 0% financing plans so you can start now and pay comfortably over time.

Do you accept my insurance?

We work with most major insurance providers and handle the paperwork for you. Our team will verify your benefits before your first visit so you know exactly what's covered.

What is recovery like?

Thanks to minimally invasive techniques, most patients return to their normal routine the same or next day. You'll leave with a personal aftercare plan and our direct line — day or night.

What happens at my first appointment?

Just a relaxed conversation. We'll listen to your goals, take a quick comfortable scan, and show you your options — including a preview of your possible new smile. No pressure, no obligations.
