Cool things you didn't know about bananas

They are technically berries, they can improve your mood, and they make the ultimate natural workout fuel.

🧠

Mood Elevators

Bananas contain vitamin B6 and tryptophan, which help your brain produce serotonin—instantly brightening your vibe.

🏃‍♂️

Natural Power Gel

Packed with potassium and complex carbs, athletes use them to sustain energy, prevent muscle cramps, and power through.

🍰

The Baking Swap

Mashed banana acts as a perfect 1:1 vegan substitute for eggs or butter in muffins, cakes, and pancakes to lock in moisture naturally.

🌿

Berry Interesting

Botanically speaking, bananas are actually classified as berries, while the majestic "banana tree" is technically a giant herbaceous plant!

💡 Pro-tip hack

Want to speed up or slow down ripening?

Wrap banana stems in beeswax wrap or foil to slow down the release of ethylene gas, keeping them fresh longer. Want them ripe fast? Place them in a paper bag with an apple overnight!
