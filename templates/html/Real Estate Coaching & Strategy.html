About Jeff Glover

Jeff Glover started in real estate at 19 years old, selling 30 homes in his first year. Within two years he was running training and recruitment for a 17-office, 600-agent Coldwell Banker firm which is where he found his real passion: making other people better at this business.

In 2008 he founded Glover Agency, now the number one real estate team in Michigan. A few years later he built Glover U because he couldn't find a coaching programme run by someone still actively in production. He built the programme he wished existed and staffed it with coaches who are all currently selling at high volume.

He also founded Titleocity to reinvent the title experience for agents and consumers, and runs Glover's Heroes, a foundation that returns 20% of his commission to teachers, nurses, veterans, police, and first responders in Michigan communities.
