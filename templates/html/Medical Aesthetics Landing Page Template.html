The Protocol

The Madden Sax
Signature Consultation

A calm, comprehensive process designed to align clinical possibilities with your personal aesthetic goals, ensuring clarity and comfort before any treatment begins.

01

Listening & Assessment

We begin by discussing your concerns, lifestyle, and aesthetic history. A thorough facial assessment follows, analyzing structure, symmetry, skin quality, and dynamic movement to understand your unique anatomical canvas.

02

Custom Treatment Plan

Rather than standardized packages, we architect a phased, individualized plan. This encompasses immediate enhancements and long-term collagen preservation strategies, clearly outlining expectations and timelines.

03

Clinical Execution

Treatments are performed in a serene, private environment prioritizing safety and precision. We use only premium medical-grade products and advanced techniques to ensure minimal downtime and elegant outcomes.

04

Ongoing Refinement

Aesthetics is a journey, not a singular event. We schedule follow-up assessments to monitor progress, refine results, and adapt your skincare regimen to maintain your investment over time.
