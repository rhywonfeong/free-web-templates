FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a minimum order?

No — order one piece or ten thousand. Pricing scales with quantity, but there's no bulk requirement to get started.

How fast is rush production?

24-hour turnaround is available on select items and production locations. Flag your deadline on the quote form and we'll confirm what's possible.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes — production and delivery across 65+ countries, with local production available in 35 U.S. cities.

Can I get a free company store?

Yes, for qualifying accounts. Mention it when you request pricing and we'll set it up alongside your first order.

What file types do you accept for logos or artwork?

AI, EPS, PDF, PNG, SVG, and JPG. If you're not sure your file will work, upload it anyway — we'll flag anything that needs adjusting.

Can I get samples before ordering in bulk?

Yes — request samples directly on your quote form and we'll include them with your pricing response.
