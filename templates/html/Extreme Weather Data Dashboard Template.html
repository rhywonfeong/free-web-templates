What’s driving the increase?

  • Warmer air holds more moisture, intensifying rainfall.
  • Shifting jet streams and stalled systems amplify storms.
  • Expanded wildland‑urban interface increases exposure to wildfire.
  • More assets in risk‑prone areas raise losses.
