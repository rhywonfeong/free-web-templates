Frequently asked questions

How fast will I get my website?
Most websites are completed in 5 business days. More complex projects with additional pages may take slightly longer.
How does the process work?
Simple: choose a template, complete our questionnaire with your content and photos, pay your deposit, and we get to work. You'll receive a preview link for review, we make revisions, then launch.
Who designs the websites?
TONG Studio is run entirely by Bonnie Xu. You work directly with me throughout the process — no outsourcing, no handoffs.
What if I don't have professional photos?
No problem! We can work with what you have, source quality stock images, or recommend affordable photography options.
What about domain and hosting?
First-year domain registration is included for standard .com.au or .com domains. Hosting is paid separately (~$10-15/month direct to your host). We handle all the technical setup so you don't have to.
