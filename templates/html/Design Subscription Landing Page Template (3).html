Frequently asked questions

Is this really unlimited?
Yes — you can submit unlimited requests and revisions. Work is completed based on your plan capacity and queue.
Why only 1 task at a time?
Because focused execution gets better results. Instead of making everything slow at once, we complete work in a structured order.
Why only 2 hours per day?
This keeps the model efficient, sustainable, and more affordable than hiring internally — while still delivering consistent progress every working day.
Can I get things done faster?
Yes. If you need faster output or parallel work, you can add another plan for more capacity.
Do you use AI to generate the designs?
No. We use professional design tools, but the creative work is handled by real human designers.
Do I own the final files?
Yes. Once paid, all final approved design files belong to you.
Do you work on weekends or holidays?
No. Work runs on weekdays only.
