About me

The Tour Leader

"Forget law books and courtroom dramas—my true passion lies in giving you the ultimate Moroccan adventure. While my family was busy trying to convince me to don a lawyer's suit, I was dreaming of donning a tour guide's hat and leading travelers through the mesmerizing maze of Morocco. And guess what? I traded legal briefs for breathtaking views and became a tour leader instead. It's like my dream job was waiting for me to stumble upon it!"

For over six years, I've been the ultimate Moroccan treasure hunter, guiding folks through ancient souks, epic deserts, and stunning landscapes.

Fluent in English, French, and my native languages, I'm basically a walking, talking cultural encyclopedia with a side of charm. I'm known for my infectious energy, easygoing vibes, and a humor that's been described as 'so good it should come with a warning label.' You can count on me to greet you with a grin as wide as the Atlas Mountains and stories as rich as a Moroccan feast.

When I'm not sharing the wonders of my country, you'll find me volunteering at an orphanage—giving back to my community is something I'm truly passionate about. "After all, life is a journey, and I'm here to make yours unforgettable!"

The Social Worker

Helping others has always come naturally to me. Being raised in a small village in the Atlas Mountains, I was aware of the need to help others and became involved with many volunteer projects. In 2015, while attending university in Marrakech, I met Mr. Hansjörg Huber, a Swiss philanthropist who founded an orphanage in Morocco. This meeting changed my life.

When my mentor Hansjörg passed away in September 2022, it left a huge hole in my heart but I am determined to keep his legacy alive. I will continue to find creative ways to bring awareness to the important work that Atlas Kinder does so that every child gets the love, support and education they deserve.
