Frequently Asked Questions

What is your minimum ad spend?
We generally work with B2B SaaS companies spending at least $5,000/month on ads, or those ready to ramp up to that level to see statistically significant results.
Do you create the ad creatives?
Yes. We have an in-house design team that handles static visuals, carousels, and video editing. You don't need to provide anything but your brand assets.
Which platforms do you focus on?
LinkedIn Ads and Google Ads (Search & YouTube). For B2B SaaS, these are the highest intent channels. We occasionally test Meta for retargeting.
How do you track attribution?
We set up offline conversion tracking with HubSpot or Salesforce. This allows us to feed data back to the ad platforms about which clicks turned into qualified deals.
