The Venue

Dundonald Links

Nestled on the Ayrshire coast, Dundonald Links offers a par-72 championship course that blends traditional links heritage with modern luxury. Home to the 2026 ISPS HANDA Women's Scottish Open, it presents a strategic challenge amidst rugged dunes and undulating fairways.

  • Gailes, Ayrshire, Scotland
  • 7,100 Yards, Par 72
  • Co-sanctioned by LPGA & LET

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the gate opening times?

Gates open at 7:00 AM each day of the tournament. The first tee time is approximately 7:30 AM.

Is parking available on site?

Yes, free public parking is available adjacent to the course. VIP parking passes are included with Hospitality packages.

Can I bring a mobile phone?

Mobile phones are permitted but must be kept on silent at all times. Photography is allowed on practice days, but restricted on tournament days.
