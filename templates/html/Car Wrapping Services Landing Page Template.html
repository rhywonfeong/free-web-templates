Paint Protection Film

Invisible shield for your car

Investing in Paint Protection Film (PPF) is the best step to preserve your car's value. Our polyurethane films provide extreme resistance to mechanical damage, stone chips, and chemicals while maintaining perfect clarity.

  • Self-healing effect

    Minor scratches from brushes or keys disappear on their own when heated by the sun.

  • Paint protection (UV & Chemicals)

    Prevents color fading from the sun and damage from bird droppings or insects.

  • Preserving car value

    The original paint remains intact, just like when the car left the showroom.
