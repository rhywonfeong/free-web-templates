Use & Care

Essential maintenance for performance.

How to Clean

Airtight Container

Place your Helix inside a sealed plastic container large enough to submerge it.

The Mix

Fill with 60% warm water, 20% isopropyl alcohol, and 20% white vinegar.

Add Salt

Add 2 tablespoons of table salt to provide gentle abrasion during cleaning.

Soak & Agitate

Seal. Rotate or shake gently. Soak for 60-90 minutes.

Rinse & Dry

Use Q-tips for tight areas. Rinse thoroughly and air dry completely.

Using Your Helix

Keep It Clean

Browning in the chamber = time to clean. Dirty glass slows airflow and cooling.

Grind Matters

Medium to coarse works best. Too fine slows the draw.

Pack Light

Medium grind. Medium tamp. Let it breathe.

Draw Restricted?

Flame should dip when you inhale. No dip? Clear the bowl hole with a toothpick.

Creator Tip

Play it like an instrument. Smooth, steady carbing keeps the smoke cooler and tastier.
