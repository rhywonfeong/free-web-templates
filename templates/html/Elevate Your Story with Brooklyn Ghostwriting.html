Frequently Asked Questions

What is ghostwriting?

Ghostwriting is a professional writing service where an experienced writer creates content on behalf of a client while the client receives full authorship credit.

Will I own the rights to my manuscript?

Yes. Once your project is completed, you retain full ownership rights to your book.

Do you sign NDAs?

Absolutely. We maintain complete confidentiality and are happy to work under non-disclosure agreements whenever requested.

Can you write in my style?

Yes. Our writers carefully study your voice, tone, and personality to create a manuscript that sounds authentically yours.

How long does the process take?

Every project is unique, but timelines are established before work begins so you'll always know what to expect.

Do you help with publishing?

Yes. We provide editing, formatting, cover design, publishing guidance, and book marketing support.
