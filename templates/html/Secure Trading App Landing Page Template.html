Frequently asked questions

Have more questions? Read our full documentation or contact support.

Do both people need CardTrade?
Yes. To ensure our secure escrow and verified identity systems work, both the buyer and seller must have active, verified CardTrade accounts. Registration only takes a few minutes.
How does the payment holding work?
When a buyer commits to a purchase, their funds are held in a secure, neutral account. The seller is then notified to ship. Funds are only released to the seller once the buyer receives the item and confirms it matches the description.
What happens if something goes wrong?
If an item arrives damaged or not as described, the buyer can open a dispute before funds are released. Our dedicated resolution team will review evidence from both parties and mediate a fair outcome, up to a full refund.
How does shipping insurance work?
Sellers are responsible for purchasing adequate shipping insurance through their chosen carrier. CardTrade requires tracking information for all transactions to utilize our escrow system effectively.
Is this like PayPal Goods & Services?
It's better. While G&S takes a percentage of your sale, CardTrade is completely fee-free for transactions. Furthermore, our platform is built specifically for collectibles, with dispute resolutions handled by people who understand the hobby, not generalized support reps.
