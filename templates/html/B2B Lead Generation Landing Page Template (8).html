Frequently Asked Questions

How is this different from other lead generation services?
Most agencies use generic spray-and-pray approaches that waste everyone's time. We focus on identifying businesses with real pain points and crafting messaging that resonates with decision-makers who specifically need your type of service. Quality over quantity.
What if the prospects aren't qualified?
You only pay the $300 performance fee for booked and qualified discovery calls. If someone shows up unprepared or unqualified, there's no charge for that appointment. We eat the cost of unqualified leads.
How quickly will I see results?
Most campaigns start generating booked calls within 3–4 weeks of launch, typically delivering 2–4 qualified conversations per month. Some clients see their first booked call within 10 days.
What's included in the monthly fee?
The monthly operations fee covers prospect research, email infrastructure, campaign management, reply handling, and calendar integration. You only pay the performance fee when we deliver qualified calls that show up.
Do I need to provide lists or messaging?
No. We handle all prospect research and create messaging specifically for your service and ideal client profile. You simply provide input on who you serve best and we handle everything else.
