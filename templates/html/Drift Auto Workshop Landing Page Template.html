FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How much does an oil change cost?

An oil change with an oil filter starts from 100 PLN. The final price depends on the car model and the type of oil used. All prices in our list are estimates – you will get an exact quote after contacting the workshop or visiting us.

Do you offer a warranty on paint jobs?

Yes, we provide a written warranty on all paint and restoration services. We use premium materials, which allows us to ensure the durability and resistance of the coating for many years. Details are agreed upon individually depending on the scope of work.

What are your opening hours?

Drift workshop in Mysłowice is open Monday to Friday from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM. The workshop is closed on weekends. We recommend booking an appointment by phone in advance.

Can I get a free repair quote?

Yes, preliminary valuation and consultation are free. You can come with your car for inspection or send us photos via the contact form – we will prepare a detailed cost estimate. The decision is yours.

Do you repair every car?

We specialize in complex repairs, classic restorations, and tuning projects. We do not repair engines or vehicles after major accidents requiring frame straightening – but we can recommend trusted workshops for those specific needs.
