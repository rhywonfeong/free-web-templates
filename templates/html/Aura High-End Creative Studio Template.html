The Methodology

01 Discovery & Audit
We begin by dismantling your current digital footprint. Analyzing analytics, heatmaps, and user recordings to identify friction points and missed conversion opportunities.
02 Wireframing Logic
Structural foundation before visual noise. We map out the skeletal framework of every viewport, ensuring logical flow and hierarchical clarity before styling begins.
03 Visual System
Translating brand identity into a scalable component library. Typography, spacing rules, and interaction patterns are codified into a strict design language.
04 Motion & Interaction
Adding tactile feedback through GSAP and custom WebGL shaders. Motion isn't decorative; it directs attention and explains spatial relationships.
