Frequently asked questions

How fast can PodPress generate a newsletter?

Most episodes under 60 minutes are processed in less than 2 minutes. You'll get a notification as soon as it's ready.

Do I have to write show notes manually?

Not at all. PodPress generates comprehensive show notes, timestamps, and key takeaways automatically. You can edit them before publishing if needed.

What formats can I export?

You can export as raw Text, Markdown, HTML (for emails), or push directly to integrated platforms like Ghost or WordPress.

Which languages are supported?

We currently support English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, and 10 others.
