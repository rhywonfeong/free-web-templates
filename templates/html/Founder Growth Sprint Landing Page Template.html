FAQ

Who is this sprint for?

Founders and lean teams with a real product in market (or ready to launch) who are tired of “hoping” launches work. You don’t need huge traffic, but you do need the willingness to move fast and ship during the 30 days.

What happens on the 30‑minute demo call?

We’ll quickly map your current offer, pricing and funnel, then show you where we’d focus in a sprint. If we can’t clearly point to revenue‑relevant changes, we won’t invite you in.

How does the guarantee work?

You pay 50% to reserve your spot. If by the end of the sprint you genuinely feel it wasn’t worth it, you don’t pay the remaining 50%. We’d rather protect the relationship than squeeze the invoice.

How much of my time does it take?

Expect 1–2 focused hours per week for working sessions and reviews. The goal is to move quickly without derailing your actual job: running the product.
