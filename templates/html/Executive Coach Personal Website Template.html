Frequently Asked Questions

How long is a typical coaching engagement?
Most engagements last between 3 to 6 months. We typically meet bi-weekly for 60-minute sessions. However, every journey is unique, and we can tailor the duration to your specific goals.
Do you offer sessions in Polish?
Absolutely. I am a native Polish speaker and fully fluent in Czech and English. I conduct sessions in whichever language allows you to express yourself most comfortably.
What is the difference between coaching and therapy?
Coaching focuses on the future and taking action to achieve specific goals, while therapy often deals with the past and healing emotional wounds. While I use psychological frameworks, our work will be forward-looking and results-oriented.
