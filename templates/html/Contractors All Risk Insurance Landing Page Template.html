Contractors All Risks Insurance (CAR) offers comprehensive protection against loss of damage in respect of the contract works, construction plant, equipment and machinery, as well as against third-party claims in respect of property damage or bodily injury arising in connection with the building and execution of a construction project.

A “Contractors All Risk” policy may be taken up by the investor or principal or by the contractors engaged in a project, including all subcontractors.

This insurance provides an "all risk" coverage, of which in principle every risk is covered, unless it is specifically excluded under the policy.

Key Protections

  • Construction Works Damage to property & materials
  • Third Party Liability Injury or property damage
  • Equipment Construction plant & machinery
