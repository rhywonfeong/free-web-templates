Common Questions

How long does acne treatment take?
Results vary by severity. Typically, visible improvement is seen in 4-6 weeks, with complete clearance taking 3-6 months depending on the protocol (oral medication, peels, or laser).
Is PRP hair treatment painful?
We use numbing agents to make the procedure very comfortable. Most patients describe it as a mild pricking sensation. Downtime is minimal.
Do I need a consultation before laser treatment?
Yes. Dr. Hiteshree examines your skin type and concerns to ensure the laser settings are safe and effective for you specifically.
