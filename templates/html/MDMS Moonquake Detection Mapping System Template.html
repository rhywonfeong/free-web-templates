01
The Challenge

The Moon Is Not Silent

As humanity prepares for long-term lunar exploration, understanding the Moon's seismic environment is critical. Current knowledge remains limited, leaving uncertainty around moonquakes, subsurface conditions, landing zones, and the structural safety of future habitats.

Without a comprehensive seismic map, mission planners must make important decisions with incomplete information.

Deployment

Arrival & Transport

Mission Value

Turning Signals Into Intelligence

MDMS transforms small movements beneath the lunar surface into actionable knowledge for science, exploration, and future lunar infrastructure.

Safer Landing Zones

08 Mission Plan

A Phased Path to Deployment

Phase 1: Concept & Engineering

System design, instrument prototyping, and environmental qualification for lunar survivability.

Phase 2: Mission Integration

Assembly, testing with launch vehicle providers, and final software verification.

Phase 3: Lunar Operations

South Pole landing, LTV deployment sequence, and commencement of the primary science mission.
