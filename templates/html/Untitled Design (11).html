HOW BAIL WORKS IN NORTH CAROLINA

A clear, six-step process.

You do not need every detail before contacting Affordable Bail Bonds.

Start a bail request

Release times vary based on the jail, booking process, court requirements, holds and individual circumstances. A specific release time cannot be promised.

01

Someone Is Arrested

The detention facility completes booking.

02

Bond Conditions Are Determined

The court or judicial official determines release conditions.

03

Contact a Bail Bondsman

Provide the person's name, detention location and any available bond information.

04

Review Terms

The agent explains applicable premium, required agreements and any collateral requirements.

05

Bond Is Posted

When the case is eligible and requirements are completed, the bond can be posted.

06

COSTS & PAYMENT

Bail Bond Costs & Payment Options

North Carolina law limits a bail bond premium to no more than 15% of the face value of the bond.

Actual premium, payment arrangements and collateral requirements depend on the individual bond and must be confirmed by the bail bondsman. No financing or payment arrangement is guaranteed.
