Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work on non-WordPress sites?
No. We specialize exclusively in WordPress to ensure mastery over the platform's nuances and deliver the best possible results.
Do you alter the design or content?
Our work is strictly backend performance optimization. We do not change your design unless a specific element is critically harming performance, in which case we consult you first.
Is this process safe for WooCommerce?
Absolutely. We implement strict exclusion rules for dynamic pages like cart, checkout, and account areas to ensure functionality remains flawless while static content is accelerated.
What is the turnaround time?
Standard optimization engagements are completed within 5–7 business days. Complex bespoke builds may require additional time for testing.
