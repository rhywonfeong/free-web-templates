Our Origin Story

CoFee was built from a simple purpose: fee collection shouldn’t be a burden. We saw how businesses and institutions struggled with recurring payments—endless reminders, manual records, late fees, and constant follow-ups.

Our goal has always been clear: remove the stress of fee collections so institutions can focus on what truly matters. With CoFee, payments are automated, transparent, and secure. No chaos, no chasing. Just a smooth system that works in the background.
