What is the minimum civilization size?

Arxon engages civilizations from 200 million inhabitants upward. Below this threshold, infrastructure needs can typically be addressed with commercially available systems. Above it, civilizational-grade engineering is required — and that is exclusively our domain.

02

How does the 500-year guarantee work?

All primary systems carry a 500-year operational guarantee against design failure. If a system we built fails due to a design defect within that period, Arxon deploys reconstruction teams at no additional cost to your governance.

03

Can you integrate with existing grids?

Yes. Phased deployment is standard protocol. We map and integrate into your current system topology rather than replacing everything simultaneously. The transition point is engineered to be the safest moment in the project cycle.
