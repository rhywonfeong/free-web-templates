The Ultimate Storage Guide

How to keep your premium nuts fresh, crunchy, and potent for 6+ months.

Cool Environments

Keep away from direct sunlight or stoves. Refrigeration extends life dramatically without losing texture.

Dry Conditions

Moisture is the enemy of crunch. Ensure your storage area maintains low humidity levels.

Airtight Containers

Once the vacuum seal is broken, transfer contents to glass jars with tight rubber seals to prevent oxidation.
