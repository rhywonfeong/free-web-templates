The Flawless Canvas

Process

  1. 01 Prep skin with a hydrating, silicone-free primer.
  2. 02 Apply a sheer skin tint using warmth of fingers.
  3. 03 Micro-conceal only where needed.
  4. 04 Set T-zone lightly with translucent powder.
Eyes 30 Sec

Soft Sculpted Eye

Process

  1. 01 Sweep a neutral contour shade through the crease.
  2. 02 Press a soft shimmer onto the center lid.
  3. 03 Smudge brown liner at the outer lash line.
  4. 04 Apply one coat of lengthening mascara.
Lips 60 Sec

The Blushed Lip

Process

  1. 01 Exfoliate and heavily hydrate lips.
  2. 02 Line subtly outside the vermilion border.
  3. 03 Dab a cream blush onto the center of lips.
  4. 04 Diffuse edges with a clean fluffy brush.
