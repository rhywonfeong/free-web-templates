General Care

  • Get plenty of rest to help your immune system.
  • Stay hydrated by drinking water or clear broths.
  • Use a humidifier to ease breathing.

Suggested Medicines

Paracetamol OTC

For mild fever and body aches. Reduces pain and temperature. Follow package instructions carefully.

Cough Suppressant OTC

E.g., Dextromethorphan. To soothe a dry or sore throat. Look for non-drowsy options during the day.

Antibiotics Rx Only

Only if a bacterial infection is diagnosed by a doctor. Not effective for viral infections like the cold.
