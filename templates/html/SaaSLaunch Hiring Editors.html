Frequency Asked Questions

How are editors paid?

We pay per project/deliverable. Payments are processed bi-weekly via Wise or Direct Deposit.

What tools do you use?

We use Slack for communication, ClickUp for project management, and Frame.io for feedback and approvals. You must be proficient in Premiere Pro or After Effects.

Is this a full-time role?

This is a contract role with flexible volume. Some editors take on 5 videos a week, others take 20. It depends on your capacity and quality.
