Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need prior coding knowledge?
Zero. None at all. We start from the absolute basics of how the web works and move into WordPress. You won't need to write custom code to build professional, highly-functional websites.
What language is the course taught in?
The core curriculum and portal are primarily in easy-to-understand English, tailored for the Indian audience. Community discussions and live Q&A sessions are often in conversational Hinglish for maximum clarity.
Can I actually make a business out of this?
Absolutely. Over 40% of the internet runs on WordPress. Small to medium businesses constantly need sites, redesigns, and maintenance. We teach you not just how to build the site, but how to package, price, and sell those services.
How long do I have access to the course?
Lifetime access. You pay once and keep the content forever, including all future updates to the curriculum as WordPress evolves.
What's the difference between WhatsApp Group and Direct Access?
The Thor Academy Community (Premium tier) is a lifetime group channel where you can ask questions, and either Ankush or peers will answer. Direct Access (Platinum tier) means you have Ankush's personal WhatsApp number for private, prioritized consultation for 6 months.
What is the refund policy?
We offer a 7-day no-questions-asked money-back guarantee. If you log in, review the material, and feel it's not the right fit for you, just email support within 7 days of purchase for a full refund.
