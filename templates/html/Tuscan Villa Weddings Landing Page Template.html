Frequently asked

How far in advance should we book?

9–14 months is ideal for villa availability and vendor holds. I also take select shorter‑notice weddings when dates align.

Do you work with our budget?

Yes—during discovery, we frame priorities and propose options that keep design and logistics in balance.

Are tastings and site visits included?

I coordinate chef tastings and villa walk‑throughs in person or virtually, depending on your travel plans.

Which cities do you arrange transport from?

Florence, Rome, and Milan—with custom options for Pisa and Bologna as needed.
