Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer Cash on Delivery (COD)?

Yes! We offer COD all over India. However, for orders under ₹499, a small shipping fee may apply. Prepaid orders usually ship faster!

How long does delivery take?

We ship within 24-48 hours. Delivery usually takes 3-5 business days for metros (Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi) and 5-7 days for other locations.

Is the jewellery anti-tarnish?

Most of our premium collection is 18k gold plated stainless steel, which is water-resistant and anti-tarnish. Check the product description for specific material details.
