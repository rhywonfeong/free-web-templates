Yes. We listen to missed-call events via integrations or webhooks, then trigger WhatsApp using your approved templates.

Can we use live agent availability instead of fixed hours?

Absolutely. Presence from the dialer or helpdesk can drive auto-responses so messages match reality.

How fast are follow-ups sent?

Typically within 5–15 seconds, with retries and deduplication to avoid spam.

Do you support WhatsApp templates?

Yes—message templates with variables, locales, and approvals are included.
