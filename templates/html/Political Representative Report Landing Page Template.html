Key Achievements & Contributions

Leadership and Public Service: A Legacy of Impact in Jubbaland

As a dedicated and elected representative of the Waamo constituency in Jubbaland State, Hon. Hassan Ismail Abdi has demonstrated strong leadership, integrity, and measurable impact across governance, education, healthcare, infrastructure, and community development. His work reflects a deep commitment to public service, regional development, and community empowerment.

Leadership & Governance

  • A principled, incorruptible, and patriotic leader who consistently defends the rights and interests of his constituency.

  • Served with integrity under multiple federal administrations, ensuring his region remains represented and protected.

  • Advocates for political fairness, regional inclusion, and constitutional reform.

Education Development

  • Sponsored 20 students in Kismayo, fully covering tuition fees; all successfully completed Bachelor’s degrees and are now employed in regional ministries.

  • Facilitated international scholarships for 30 students to study in China, Russia, and Egypt, achieving Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees.

  • Enabled local access to national examinations in Afmadow and Dhobley, eliminating the need for travel.

  • Supported establishment of primary, intermediate, and secondary schools across Afmadow and Dhobley districts.

Employment & Economy

  • Enabled over 100 individuals from his community to secure employment in Mogadishu across federal institutions, NGOs, and international agencies.

  • Created employment opportunities for more than 50 individuals in Kismayo working in Jubbaland ministries and development organizations.

  • Promoted trade and mobility by supporting transportation between Kismayo, Afmadow, and Dhobley despite security challenges.

Infrastructure & Community

  • Led construction and rehabilitation of water systems (wells, boreholes, and small dams) in Afmadow, Dhobley, Dagalamo, Wadajir, Diif, and Qoqani.

  • Initiated development plans for a modern district center (Towfiiq) including schools, hospitals, and public infrastructure.

  • Advocated for development and operational improvement of Dhobley Airport.

Healthcare & Social Services

  • Strengthened hospitals in Dhobley and Afmadow, ensuring availability of essential medical services.

  • Developed MCH centers and supported healthcare infrastructure across Waamo areas.

  • Facilitated medical treatment for injured soldiers and civilians in Mogadishu.

Security & Stability

  • Worked closely with Jubbaland security forces to enhance peace, safety, and stability.

  • Supported injured personnel through coordination of medical care and recovery support.

Institutional Strengthening

  • Supported formation and capacity-building of district-level administration including health, education, and finance departments.

  • Played a key role in establishing and strengthening district councils in Afmadow and Dhobley.

Conclusion: A Vision of Stability

The representative’s leadership is defined by integrity and tangible results. From providing medical care for injured soldiers at Digfeer Hospital to ensuring students can sit for national exams in their home districts, his impact is felt at every level of society.
