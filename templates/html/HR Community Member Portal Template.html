Community Guidelines

Code of Conduct

These guidelines exist to ensure a positive, collaborative, and safe community for all HR leaders. By maintaining these standards, we create a space where meaningful conversations thrive.

5 min read
—
Last updated: Oct 2024
01

Be Respectful

Treat everyone with dignity and respect. Do not use abusive, discriminatory, or harassing language. Our community thrives on mutual respect and the understanding that we all come from different backgrounds and experiences.

Every member of this portal is a professional seeking to better their craft. When we communicate with empathy and openness, we elevate the entire network.

"Respect is the foundation of any thriving professional community."

  • • Good practice: Approach disagreements with curiosity rather than hostility. Assume positive intent in every interaction.
  • • Things to avoid: Personal attacks, condescending tones, and exclusionary language that marginalizes others.

Keep conversations constructive and appropriate for a professional setting. Avoid personal attacks, aggressive tones, or unprofessional conduct.

As HR leaders, you set the standard for communication within your own organizations. We expect the same level of decorum and professional polish here.

Best Practice

Before posting, ask yourself if your comment adds value to the discussion or simply restates an existing point. Constructive dialogue pushes the conversation forward.

02

Professional Communication

03

Protect Confidentiality

Do not share sensitive company data, personal information of others, or any confidential material without explicit consent. Trust is paramount in a community of HR leaders.

We often discuss delicate workplace scenarios. When seeking advice, anonymize details to protect your organization and its employees from unnecessary exposure.

  • • Always: Redact names, exact job titles, and identifying company markers when discussing specific employee relations cases.
  • • Never: Share proprietary documents, compensation data, or internal policies without written permission.

Avoid using the community for unsolicited self-promotion, sales pitches, or mass messaging. Keep the focus on sharing genuine HR value.

This space is designed for learning and peer support, not lead generation. While networking is highly encouraged, it should happen organically through meaningful contributions rather than direct solicitation.

"Value is created through shared knowledge, not aggressive sales tactics."

04
