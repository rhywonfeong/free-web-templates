Where Texture Becomes Play

Every batch is mixed in its own small window. Cloud creams are whipped light and packed the same afternoon; butter slimes rest a day so the stretch settles. We follow the feel, not a fixed clock.

Each jar records the hands that made it. Small differences stay visible because a batch is handmade, not machine poured.

Before you ask

Common
Questions

How do drops work?
New textures are released weekly as each batch finishes curing. Members get an early access window before the public drop; no subscription is required.
What is in the slime?
Every jar is handmade with skin-safe PVA glue, activator, clay and cosmetic-grade colour and scent. Full ingredient lists are printed on each label.
Can I visit the studio?
The studio is intentionally small, so workshops are booked in advance. Same-week places occasionally appear in the calendar.
Where do you ship?
We ship to most of Europe, the United Kingdom, Canada and the continental United States. Slime travels only within safe temperature windows.
