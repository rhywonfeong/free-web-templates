The Architect

Jason Steele

Rooted in the dramatic landscapes of the Arizona desert, Aperture Design is an architecture practice that balances raw organic forms with refined modernist precision. Founded by Jason Steele, the firm approaches every project with a quiet confidence—avoiding fleeting trends in favor of intentional, site-specific solutions.

With training from the Frank Lloyd Wright School of Architecture, the firm's ethos is deeply connected to material honesty, natural light, and the seamless integration of indoor and outdoor environments.

  • LEED AP Certification
