What this is

I work as a spiritual medium and psychic reader. During a session, I receive information through images, impressions, and direct knowing - sometimes about your life and patterns, sometimes from those who have passed on.

What comes through may include ancestors, loved ones, animals, spirit guides, or other beings - known or unknown to you. I cannot guarantee who or what will come through. I only share what is present.

I don't prepare in advance.

I don't follow a script.

I don't ask questions beforehand.

You simply sit with me, and what needs to be seen flows through.
