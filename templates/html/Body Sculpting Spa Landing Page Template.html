Frequently Asked Questions

How does Red Light Therapy work?
Red Light Therapy uses specific wavelengths of light to penetrate the skin, stimulating fat cells to release their contents, which are then naturally processed by the body.
Is it safe and FDA-cleared?
Yes, our treatments utilize FDA-cleared technology that is non-invasive and considered very safe for most individuals.
What results can I expect?
Results vary by individual, but many clients see noticeable inch loss and skin tightening after a series of sessions.
Who is NOT a candidate for these treatments?
Pregnant women, individuals with active cancer, or those with certain photosensitivity conditions should avoid these treatments. Please consult with us.
How do I book my first session?
You can book directly through our website or give us a call. We recommend starting with our trial session.
