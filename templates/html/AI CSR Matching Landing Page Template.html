The Connection Process

How It Works

A seamless 5-step process to bridge the CSR funding gap. We replace manual searching with intelligent AI matching.

Step 1

Create Profiles

CSR Heads and NGOs create accounts. NGOs authenticate using official credentials like NGO-DARPAN to establish immediate platform trust.

1
2
Step 2

Post Needs & Projects

NGOs post detailed community projects. CSR heads post funding opportunities outlining their social objectives, budgets, and cause areas.

Step 3

AI Embeddings Analysis

The platform's AI analyzes both project descriptions and corporate opportunities using advanced semantic embeddings to understand true intent.

3
4
Step 4

Ranked AI Generation

The platform generates ranked matches using cosine similarity, providing a percentage score and a clear explanation of the alignment reasoning.

Step 5

Connect & Collaborate

Users browse recommendations, review the AI’s logic, and seamlessly connect to begin their CSR partnership and fund impact.
