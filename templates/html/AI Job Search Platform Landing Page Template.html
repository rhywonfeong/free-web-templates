AI Interview Preparation

Generate tailored interview questions and structured answer guidance based on the company, role, and job description you provide.

Sample question set

These are examples. Your questions will adapt to each role.

Walk me through a recent product you launched end-to-end. What were your goals and how did you measure success?

Evaluates ownership, outcome focus, and decision-making.

Suggested answer structure

  • Context: Briefly describe the product, audience, and business constraint.
  • Goals: Quantify the primary success metrics (activation, retention, revenue, etc.).
  • Approach: Explain your discovery, prioritization, and trade-off decisions.
  • Execution: Outline collaboration with design, engineering, and stakeholders.
  • Outcomes: Share concrete results and what you would do differently next time.

Tell me about a time you had conflicting feedback from stakeholders. How did you move the product forward?

Tests stakeholder management and communication.

Suggested answer structure

  • Situation: Describe the stakeholders and nature of conflict.
  • Options: Outline the trade-offs you considered.
  • Decision: Explain the principle or data you used to decide.
  • Communication: Show how you brought stakeholders along.
  • Outcome: Summarize impact and learning.

How do you decide what to build next when you have limited engineering capacity?

Explores prioritization frameworks and product sense.

Suggested answer structure

  • Inputs: Users, business goals, tech constraints.
  • Framework: RICE, impact vs. effort, or similar model.
  • Validation: How you test assumptions pre-build.
  • Communication: How you set expectations with stakeholders.
  • Iteration: Adjusting priorities based on results.
