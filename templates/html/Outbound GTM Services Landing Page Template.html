Spam is untargeted, mass-blasted garbage. We send personalized, relevant messages to verified contacts who fit your ICP. Deliverability matters to us — burned domains don't generate leads.

Any B2B company with a large addressable market (10,000+ contacts), high-value deals ($5k+ ACV), and an existing sales process. SaaS, agencies, professional services, manufacturing.
How long until I see results?
Infrastructure takes about 14 days to build and warm. After launch, you'll typically see replies within the first week. Meaningful volume compounds over 30-60 days.
What if the campaign doesn't work?
That's why we offer a free trial campaign. You see results before committing. If we scale together and performance drops, we diagnose and optimize — you're not paying for leads that don't show up.
How do you charge?
Monthly tech fee (covers infrastructure) plus a performance fee per interested lead. You only pay for results.
