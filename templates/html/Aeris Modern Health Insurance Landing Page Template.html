Common questions

Everything you need to know about the product and billing.

What does "$0 deductible" actually mean?
It means exactly what it sounds like. With our Complete plan, you don't have to pay thousands of dollars out of pocket before your insurance starts covering costs. We pay for your covered care from day one.
Can I keep my current doctor?
Aeris operates on a broad, national PPO network. There's a very high chance your current doctor is already in-network. You can easily search our provider directory before signing up to verify.
How do claims work?
For in-network care, your doctor bills us directly—you just pay your copay at the visit. If you go out-of-network, you can snap a picture of your receipt and upload it to the Aeris app. We typically process and reimburse these within 3-5 business days.
Is dental and vision included?
Dental and vision are fully included in our Max plan. For Essential and Complete plans, they can be added on for a small, transparent monthly fee during checkout.
