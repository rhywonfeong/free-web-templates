For the past 30 years, I have been an entrepreneur based in London, working independently across property, business, and investment. My professional life has been defined by curiosity, creativity, and an ongoing commitment to growth — both personal and professional. Alongside my business activities, I have been an active investor in technology and cryptocurrency since 2014.. Equally important to me has been a two-decade-long personal and spiritual journey, which has deepened my understanding of self-awareness, purpose, and human connection. This ongoing exploration led me naturally to psychosynthesis coaching, a discipline that integrates the practical structure of coaching with Jungian philosophy and a holistic understanding of the human experience. As a psychosynthesis coach in training, I aim to create a reflective, holistic and accountable series of sessions with my coaches. — helping clients explore not only their goals but also the deeper values and aspirations that give their lives meaning.
