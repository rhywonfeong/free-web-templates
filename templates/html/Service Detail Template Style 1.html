Signs You Need HVAC Repair

Your HVAC system tells you when something's wrong—if you know what to listen for. Catching problems early saves you money and prevents uncomfortable breakdowns in extreme weather.

Common warning signs

  • • Grinding, squealing, or banging sounds indicate worn bearings, loose parts, or failing motors
  • • Could mean a clogged filter, ductwork leak, or blower motor issue
  • • Low refrigerant, compressor failure, or thermostat calibration problem
  • • System turns on and off rapidly—often an electrical or sensor fault
  • • Sudden spike in utility costs suggests inefficient operation
  • • Refrigerant or condensate drain issues need immediate attention

If you notice any of these symptoms, call us for a diagnostic. Most repairs cost far less than emergency replacements.

HVAC Installation Costs

A new HVAC system is a significant investment, but modern equipment pays for itself through energy savings and reliable comfort. Here's what influences the final price:

Factors that affect installation cost

  • • We perform Manual J load calculations to size your system correctly
  • • Higher-efficiency models cost more upfront but deliver lower monthly bills
  • • Existing ducts in good shape save thousands
  • • Multi-zone systems and WiFi thermostats add comfort and control
  • • Required by local code—we handle all paperwork

Typical price ranges:

  • • Standard efficiency central AC: $5,000–$8,000
  • • High-efficiency heat pump: $7,000–$12,000
  • • Ductless mini-split (multi-zone): $4,500–$10,000
  • • Gas furnace replacement: $3,500–$6,500

Every home is different. Request a free in-home assessment for an accurate quote.

HVAC Maintenance Schedule

Regular tune-ups extend equipment life, prevent costly breakdowns, and keep your system running efficiently. We recommend seasonal maintenance—spring for cooling, fall for heating.

What our tune-up includes

  • • Clean or replace air filters
  • • Inspect and clean condenser/evaporator coils
  • • Check refrigerant levels and look for leaks
  • • Test thermostat calibration and controls
  • • Lubricate motors and inspect belts
  • • Clear condensate drain lines
  • • Measure airflow and temperature differential
  • • Tighten electrical connections
  • • Inspect ductwork for leaks or damage

Our annual or bi-annual plans include priority scheduling, discounts on repairs, and extended warranties.
