About the Founder

Stuti Shah

Stuti Shah is a Delhi-based fashion and textile professional with over 15 years of experience in garment production, fabric sourcing, pattern making, and bespoke apparel design. A graduate of the National Institute of Fashion Technology (NIFT) and a Textile Engineering professional, she brings together deep technical knowledge and an intuitive understanding of Indian aesthetics.

Her work reflects a refined balance of tradition and contemporary elegance, with particular expertise in Indian textiles and regional crafts. Stuti has previously worked with production houses associated with FabIndia and has led her own design studio under the label SACHIS Designs in Gujarat and New Delhi.
