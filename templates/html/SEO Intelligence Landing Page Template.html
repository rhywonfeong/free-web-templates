Protocol FAQ

What defines Semantic SEO?
Semantic SEO optimizes for topics, entities, and context rather than isolated keywords. It aligns your content with the Knowledge Graph, helping search engines understand your authority.
How do you approach Technical Audits?
We execute a 100+ point inspection covering crawlability, indexation, Core Web Vitals, schema markup, and JS rendering to ensure a flawless technical foundation.
Is local SEO relevant for non-brick-and-mortar?
Yes. Service Area Businesses (SABs) need local visibility to dominate specific geographic markets, even without a customer-facing storefront.
Timeline for results?
While technical fixes can yield quick wins, significant organic growth typically compounds over 3–6 months depending on market competition and authority baselines.
