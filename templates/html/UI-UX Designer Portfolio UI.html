AI Design Assistant

Overview

Nebula is an AI-assisted workspace that helps product teams go from brief to validated concepts faster. I led product design, interaction patterns, and brand system—shipping an MVP in 12 weeks.

Role

Lead Product Designer

Scope

Research, UX, UI, Prototype, Brand

Timeline

12 weeks

Problem / Challenge

Product teams lose time aligning on direction and exploring divergent options. Knowledge is siloed across tools, and handoff backtracks are common—slowing delivery and reducing quality.

Solution (Process + Features)

We introduced an AI “copilot” that ingests brief + market signals to propose structured directions, flows, and content blocks. The workspace allows quick synthesis, alignment rituals, and instant prototypes.

  • Brief-to-Flows generation with guardrails
  • Design tokens + live styles for rapid theming
  • Comments, mentions, and async approvals
  • Export to Figma and code-ready components

Challenges During Design

  • AI trust and explainability

    Added rationale previews, confidence markers, and human-in-the-loop checkpoints.

  • Scope control

    Narrowed MVP to 3 killer moments; backlog framed as extensions.
