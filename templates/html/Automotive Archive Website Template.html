The 1982 'Stratos' study was never intended for production. It existed solely to test the limits of wedge-shaped design and active downforce systems in a highly controlled environment.

Kept within the secret vaults of the R&D department for nearly two decades, it has recently been meticulously restored to its original clay-model specifications, revealing details previously thought lost to history.
