About AYUBA FADDAH SOLID FOUNDATION

AYUBA FADDAH SOLID FOUNDATION is a nonprofit organization based in Nigeria, working to remove financial and structural barriers that keep children out of school. We focus on underprivileged children who are at risk of missing out on basic education due to poverty, lack of classroom spaces, or limited access to learning materials.

By partnering with local communities, schools, and leaders, we identify children who are excluded from formal education and provide practical support so they can enroll, attend, and thrive in school. Our work is centered on transparency, accountability, and long-term impact for children and their families.

Our Mission & Programs

Mission Statement

Our goal is to raise funds to provide classroom spaces, educational materials, and school enrollment support for children who are currently excluded from formal education due to poverty. Every donation helps us create learning opportunities - from community classroom funding to supporting school supplies for underprivileged pupils. Your contribution will directly support the AYUBA FADDAH SOLID FOUNDATION children education relief fund program for children in Nigeria.

We translate this mission into practical, community-centered programs that remove the barriers preventing children from starting and continuing their education.

Classroom Construction & Community Classroom Funding

We support communities where children lack safe and adequate classroom spaces. This includes:

  • Supporting community-led classroom construction projects.
  • Improving existing learning spaces for safety and comfort.
  • Providing simple infrastructure for temporary learning centers.

School Enrollment Support

Many children stay out of school because their families cannot afford registration costs. We provide:

  • Assistance with basic enrollment and registration fees.
  • Support for documentation and placement into nearby schools.
  • Collaboration with school administrators to keep children enrolled.

School Supplies & Educational Materials

We ensure children from low-income families have the basic materials needed to participate in school:

  • Exercise books, writing materials, and basic textbooks.
  • School bags and simple learning resources.
  • Targeted support for children at risk of dropping out.

Transparency & Use of Donations

We are committed to using donations responsibly and for clear educational outcomes. As we grow, we will publish simplified reports showing how funds are allocated across:

  • Classroom construction and learning spaces.
  • Enrollment support for children in need.
  • School supplies and educational materials.

Donors, partners, and stakeholders will be able to request additional information by contacting us directly at contact@ayubafaddahsolidfoundation.name.ng .
