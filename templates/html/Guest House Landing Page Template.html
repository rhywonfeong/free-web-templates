Welcome to Hampton House

An Oasis of Peace in Centurion

Originally built in 1953 in Clubview, Centurion, our well-appointed guest house offers a sanctuary from the city's hustle. Managed with personal care by Ms. Liezl & Amelia, we invite you to experience true "home away from home" hospitality.

Our guests stay in one of five spacious luxury rooms, each uniquely named after a Scottish Island. Every room features a private bathroom and an additional outside entrance leading onto our lush garden or courtyard, ensuring your absolute privacy and comfort.
