Frequently Asked Questions

How much ad spend do I need to start?
We typically recommend a minimum ad spend of $3,000/month to allow the algorithms enough data to optimize properly and generate a meaningful return on investment. However, this varies by industry.
Do you guarantee results?
While we cannot guarantee specific revenue numbers due to external market factors, we do guarantee that we will build a high-converting system and manage your campaigns actively based on data to aim for your target CPA (Cost Per Acquisition).
How does the AI agent integration work?
We custom-train an AI model on your specific business data, FAQs, and sales qualifying questions. We then integrate it into your website or social media messaging to instantly respond to inquiries, book meetings, or filter out unqualified leads 24/7.
How long does it take to see ROI?
Most clients start seeing qualified leads within the first week of a campaign going live. However, the first 30 days are typically focused on testing and optimization. The highest ROI usually stabilizes between months 2 and 3 as the funnels mature.
