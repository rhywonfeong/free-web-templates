Frequently asked questions

New to the studio or curious about digital perms, treatments or colour? Start with the most common questions below.

Do I need to book in advance?
We recommend booking 1–2 weeks ahead for evenings and weekends. Midweek daytime appointments are often available sooner.
Do you offer both men’s and women’s cuts?
Yes. Our stylists work with all hair types and lengths, including short, mid and long hair, as well as gender‑neutral cuts.
How do I know which service is right for me?
You can book a complimentary 15‑minute consultation or send us photos of your current hair and inspiration. We’ll recommend the safest and most effective option.
What should I do before my appointment?
Arrive with dry hair in its natural state and avoid using heavy oils or treatments 24 hours before any colour, perm or straightening service.

Not sure which service to book?

Share a little about your hair and we’ll get back to you within one working day with recommendations and availability.
