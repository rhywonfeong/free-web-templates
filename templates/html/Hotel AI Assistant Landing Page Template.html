Frequently Asked Questions

How long does setup take?

Most hotels go live within 45 days, following our structured onboarding process: 24h sign up, 5 day spec, 10 day V1, and rigorous QC.

Will this replace my staff?

No, Dextr.ai is designed to support your staff, not replace them. It handles the repetitive queries and late-night calls so your team can focus on the guests currently at your property.

What if the AI doesn't know the answer?

The AI learns your property details during setup. If it encounters a complex issue it can't resolve, it politely takes a message or forwards the call to a designated staff member/manager immediately.

Does it integrate with my PMS?

Yes, we integrate with major Property Management Systems including Oracle, Cloudbeds, and Mews. During your demo call, we can confirm specific compatibility.
