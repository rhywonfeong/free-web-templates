Questions We Hear Often

Do I need to be Muslim to attend?
Not at all. Our Soup Kitchen, community BBQs, and many events welcome everyone. We believe in serving all our neighbors regardless of faith.
How do I enrol my child in Madrasa?
Visit our Madrasa page and complete the online enrolment form. Classes run weekdays and weekends, with morning options available.
Are there programs specifically for women?
Yes. We run regular Sisters' Bayaans, Tamil-language talks, and a Girls Youth Club. Our expansion will include a dedicated women's prayer space.
How do I volunteer?
Text "VOLUNTEER" to 07572116952 or visit us after any prayer. Soup Kitchen, event setup, Madrasa assistance—there's always something you can help with.
