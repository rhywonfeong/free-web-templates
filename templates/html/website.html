Our Heritage

A fusion of coastal traditions

Chez Musty is born from a passion for authentic culinary experiences. We seamlessly combine the timeless recipes of authentic Italian cuisine with the rich, vibrant seafood traditions of Morocco.

Every dish is crafted with meticulous attention to detail, using only the freshest locally-sourced ingredients, handmade pastas, and our signature homemade sauces.

Whether you are here for our renowned fresh fish selections, authentic pizzas baked to perfection, or simply to enjoy the warm, welcoming family atmosphere, you are always treated like family at Chez Musty.
