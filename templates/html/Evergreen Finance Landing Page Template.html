WTFinance

WTFinance

Content education and guided brokerage funnel, feeding the Evergreen ecosystem with better‑informed customers and safer referrals.

WTFinance sits under Evergreen but outside the core product line. It is our content engine and broker funnel, designed to build trust by educating, not just selling credit.

  • Supports Moneyboat+ with guides on borrowing, repayment and budgeting.
  • Strengthens MoneyLighthouse by helping declined customers navigate next steps responsibly.
  • Creates a brand‑neutral space where customers can compare lenders clearly and safely.
  • Positions Evergreen as a long‑term financial companion rather than a one‑off lender.

In the journey map, only Moneyboat+ and MoneyLighthouse connect directly to WTFinance, ensuring guidance is focused where customers need the most support.

Slides 03 & 06 · WTFinance placement

WTFinance: the education and broker‑funnel layer under Evergreen.

WTFinance sits beneath Evergreen Finance, outside the core product line. It powers education, confidence and responsible referrals for customers across our journeys.

WTF

Education & broker funnel

WTFinance

Evergreen sub‑brand

Simple, brand‑neutral guides and tools that help customers make sense of borrowing, budgeting and credit choices. WTFinance is designed to feel independent and trustworthy, while still feeding the Evergreen ecosystem.

Core principles

  • Clear, jargon‑free explanations of borrowing, repayment and budgeting.
  • Transparent lender comparisons and referrals, never “hard sell”.
  • Safe space for declined customers to understand options and next steps.

WTFinance connects directly to two key Evergreen journeys, with dotted‑line support for others over time.

Moneyboat+ MoneyLighthouse

Lending journey

With Moneyboat+

Guides & tools
  • Explain affordability, credit limits and repayment options before customers commit.
  • Support budgeting choices so customers use flexible credit well.

Decline journey

With MoneyLighthouse

Guided alternatives
  • Help rejected customers understand why, and what they can do next.
  • Offer clean, responsible comparisons rather than typical broker tactics.

Brand value

WTFinance builds trust by educating first and referring second—supporting Evergreen’s ambition to be a true financial companion.

Stronger brand credibility
