The Artist Behind the Movement

With over two decades dedicated to the art of dance, Alin Tudorie has established himself as a pillar in the Bachata community. His journey began with a passion for Latin rhythms and evolved into a lifelong career of teaching, performing, and innovating.

Alin's unique methodology blends the traditional roots of Dominican Bachata with the fluid complexity of Moderna and the connection-focused techniques of Sensual style. He believes that dance is not just movement, but a language spoken without words.
