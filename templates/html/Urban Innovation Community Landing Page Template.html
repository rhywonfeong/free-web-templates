What IKEA puts on the table

We are not networking. We exchange real assets for real contribution.

Category Performance Data

Anonymised sales and consumer preference data — available before you design, not after.

The Store as a Live Testbed

Real foot traffic in Copenhagen for testing concepts, prototypes, and services in actual context.

Structured Consumer Cohorts

Pre-recruited, defined test panels — available for validation sessions without partner recruitment costs.

Portfolio Owner Access

Presentations go directly to decision-makers — not filtered through an events team or workshop room.

DNS+ Pipeline Entry

The formal pathway for community contributions to enter exploration and business case processes.

Supplier Pathway & Go/No-Go

A defined commercial route: Pilot → KPI evaluation → formal decision → contract. Time-bound.

Initiatives & Access Points
Briefed Design Sprint
Data + Decision Access
+

IKEA publishes quarterly briefs for each active portfolio area. Designers receive a Category Intelligence Pack (anonymised sales and preference data) before starting. Submissions go directly to the portfolio owner for a formal decision.

Quarterly brief · 6-week sprint
Danish Design Center and KADK as institutional entry points.
Customer Validation
Consumer Cohort
+

For shortlisted designs, IKEA facilitates a 2-hour testing session with a pre-recruited consumer cohort in the Copenhagen store. The designer observes and receives the full structured feedback report.

1 session per shortlisted design
Studio Access
Tools + Materials
+

15–20 active design members at any time. Includes 2 days/month of studio prototyping access, materials library, and IKEA's supplier sample archive. Conditional on submitting at least one active brief per quarter.

Rolling monthly access

What Makes This Different

This is not a network

Typical communities offer inspiration. This model offers access to IKEA's real assets in exchange for real contribution.

Typical communities offer
  • × Inspiration and keynote talks
  • × Networking without structure
  • × High-level knowledge sharing
  • × Visibility and logo placement
  • × Open-ended collaboration with no output
IKEA Xplore offers
  • ✓ Access to real category and behavioural data
  • ✓ Real customer testing with a defined cohort
  • ✓ Direct access to portfolio owners
  • ✓ A structured pathway to partnership
  • ✓ Clear decision timelines (go/no-go)

"It is not about being part of something."

It is about access to IKEA's real assets in exchange for real contribution.
