The Subnet Marketing Gap

Strong technology needs a strong market story.

Bittensor subnets are building important products, but technical progress alone does not create awareness, adoption, or sustained demand.

Development vs Positioning

Founders understandably prioritise product and protocol development, while positioning, storytelling, and brand-building often receive less attention.

Unclear Value Prop

Communications frequently highlight technical milestones without clearly explaining who the product serves, what problem it solves, or why it matters now.

Audience Saturation

Most subnets compete for attention within the same Bittensor audience, limiting access to new users, partners, talent, and capital.

Translating technical innovation

Long-term growth requires reaching broader crypto, AI, enterprise, and mainstream audiences. A strong brand helps a subnet become easier to understand, easier to trust, and easier to choose.
