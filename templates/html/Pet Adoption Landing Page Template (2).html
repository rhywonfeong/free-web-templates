About Bella

Bella is a sweet, gentle soul looking for a quiet home where she can blossom. While she can be a little shy at first meeting, a few treats and some patience will quickly win her over. She loves to curl up on the sofa for movie nights and enjoys leisurely walks in the park.

She gets along well with other calm dogs but would prefer a home without very young children as sudden loud noises can startle her. She is house-trained and walks beautifully on a leash.
