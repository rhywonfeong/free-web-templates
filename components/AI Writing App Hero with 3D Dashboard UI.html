# Launching Lexora 2.0

Great writing doesn’t start from scratch. With Lexora, your past campaigns, tone, and structure all live in one intelligent workspace. Every new piece of content feels consistent, on-brand, and ready to ship.

## The Problem

Content teams are drowning in Google Docs, Slack threads, and endless revision cycles. We realized that the tool shouldn't just be a blank page—it should be a partner that knows your context. When you write "Our mission," Lexora knows exactly what that means.

  • - Instantly references your last 50 campaigns to maintain continuity.
  • - Automatically flags language that drifts from your established style guide.
  • - Edit with your team and AI agents simultaneously in the same doc.
