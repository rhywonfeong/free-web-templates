04 — Process

The same four moves, every time.

No proprietary methodology. Just an order of operations that keeps decisions reversible for as long as possible.

01 Listen
02 Frame
03 Shape
04 Release
Week one

Listen

Conversations with the people who use the thing and the people who maintain it. I write down contradictions before I write down solutions.

Week two

Frame

One page that states the problem, the constraint and the measure of success. If we can't agree here, no amount of pixels will rescue it.

Weeks three to six

Shape

Screens, states, edge cases, motion. Reviewed twice weekly in short sessions so the work never drifts a fortnight off course.

Handover

Release

Files, tokens and a recorded walkthrough. Two weeks of build support included, because handover is a period, not a moment.
